How it works

The earliest signs of a deal are buried in municipal PDFs. We dig them out.

01 · COLLECT

Agendas, every day

Agendas, packets, minutes and hearing notices from township, borough and city boards, plus the Lehigh Valley Planning Commission's regional reviews. Collected daily, scanned pages included.

02 · READ

AI pulls out the deal facts

Project name, address, parcel, owner, applicant, request, zoning, acreage, square footage, stage and the vote, pulled straight from the record with a link to the source document.

03 · TRACK

One project, one timeline

Every appearance of a project across boards and months is linked into a single record, so you see where it stands and what's next.

Coverage

Starting in Pennsylvania. Built to go nationwide.

Every municipality runs its own boards, posts its own PDFs and uses its own website. ZoningTrack is built to read them all the same way, so new counties can be added quickly.

Our first region is live: Lehigh and Northampton counties, collected directly from municipalities and from the Lehigh Valley Planning Commission's regional reviews. Pennsylvania comes next, county by county.
