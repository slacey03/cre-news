About ZoningTrack Lehigh Valley, Pennsylvania

AboutUs

Zoning and real estate intelligence for the Lehigh Valley.

Zoning agendas, hearing notices, and meeting minutes hold the earliest signs of what's coming to a corridor, but they're buried in PDFs across dozens of township websites. ZoningTrack reads them for you.

It monitors municipalities across the Lehigh Valley and close surrounding regions for changes and news articles, summarizes every document with AI, flags what matters for real estate, and alerts you.
