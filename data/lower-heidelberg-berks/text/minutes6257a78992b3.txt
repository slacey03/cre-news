CALL TO ORDER – SEPTEMBER 15, 2026 SUPERVISORS BUSINESS MEETING 
At 3:01 p.m. Chairperson Paul Prutzman called the September 15, 2026 meeting of the Lower 
Heidelberg Township supervisors to order with a pledge to the flag and roll call. Present: 
Chairperson Paul Prutzman, Vice Chair Cheryl Johnson, and Craig Lutz, Member. Also in 
attendance: Brian Cole, Township Manager, Matt Clay, Road Foreman, Police Chief Chris 
Stouch, Jenn Frasso, Secretary/Treasurer, Glenn Bertolet, Kraft Municipal Group, Ryan Rhode, 
Kraft Engineering, Jim McCarthy, McCarthy Engineering and Jonathan Long, Solicitor. 
Taking minutes was J. Frasso, Secretary/Treasurer

office to Bryan Ditzler, with Mr. Lutz offering a second. Motion passed unanimously.

unanimously. 

Sewer Engineer: Jim McCarthy from McCarthy Engineering presented the report on activity in 
August. 
Engineer: Ryan Rhode, Township Engineer presented his report. 
Mr. Lutz made a motion to approve conditional approval of the final land development 
plan for the Clay/Motus Land Development, with Mrs. Johnson offering a second. Motion passed 
unanimously. 
Mr. Lutz made a motion to approve conditional approval of the preliminary plan for the 
Calvary Bible Church expansion, with Mrs. Johnson offering a second. Motion passed 
unanimously. 
Mrs. Johnson made a motion to approve the Starr Road Culvert payment application 
from Empire, with Mr. Lutz offering a second. Motion passed unanimously. 
Mr. Prutzman made a motion to approve the 2026 Guide Rail Project payment 
application from Minnici, with Mrs. Johnson offering a second. Motion passed unanimously. 
Mrs. Johnson made a motion to award the ADA Project at Connecticut and Fairwood to 
Forino Co., with Mr. Lutz offering a second. Motion passed unanimously.
