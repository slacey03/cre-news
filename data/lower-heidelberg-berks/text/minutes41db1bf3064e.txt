July 21, 2026 
CALL TO ORDER – JULY 21, 2026 SUPERVISORS BUSINESS MEETING 
At 3:00 p.m. Chairperson Paul Prutzman called the July 21, 2026 meeting of the Lower 
Heidelberg Township supervisors to order with a pledge to the flag and roll call. Present: 
Chairperson Paul Prutzman, Vice Chair Cheryl Johnson, and Craig Lutz, Member. Also in 
attendance: Brian Cole, Township Manager, Matt Clay, Road Foreman, Police Chief Chris 
Stouch, Jenn Frasso, Secretary/Treasurer, Glenn Bertolet, Kraft Municipal Group, Ryan Rhode, 
Kraft Engineering, Jim McCarthy, McCarthy Engineering and Jonathan Long, Solicitor. 
Taking minutes was J. Frasso, Secretary/Treasurer 
PUBLIC COMMENTS 
Several people attended to question the status of the closed Heidelberg Bridge, which is on 
Heidelberg Township and Lower Heidelberg Township property. 
Megan Thompson of Cherry Bekaert presented the 2025 Audit Report 
Mr. Prutzman made a motion to approve the 2027 J.P. Mascaro contract extension, with 
Mrs. Johnson offering a second. Motion passed unanimously.

Kris Moore of Gaul Rd. questioned the supervisors about her property being subject to

investigating.
