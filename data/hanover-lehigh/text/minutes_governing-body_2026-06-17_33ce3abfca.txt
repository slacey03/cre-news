Courtesy of the Floor:   
 
Louis Daveski with  JW Development at 795 Roble Rd. addressed the Board at this time.   
This evening he addressed JW 4 at Lot 3, 1112 American Parkway.  They are under  
permit and currently under construction for 210,000 sq. ft. speculative warehouse.  At the  
start of our project,  he came in to meet the Manager to introduce himself.  At that time,  
he was reminded that construction is from 7:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.  He comes before the  
board this evening to ask for a variance on the start time from concrete pours only for a  
floor slab.  They thought they would be able to do their day pours, which they attempted  
on Monday.  The weather is hard to control at this time of year.  They ended up losing  
mostly their entire pour due to the weather.  They put their best effort to keeping with the  
7:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m. hours but are struggling with it.  They would like to have the hours  
be 2:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m.  They feel they would be able to finish if they start earlier.  Their  
site construction is complete and the sound wall has been installed, which is the barrier  
between themselves and the apartments off Irving Street behind them to the east.  They  
would need to do five concrete pours over three weeks, weather permitting and truck  
availability.  The pours would be from 2:00 a.m. – 7:00 a.m.   Everything else throughout  
should be done in the daytime. 
 
Motion:  I recommend we approve JW Development’s request for a total of five concrete  
pours to occur between the hours of 2:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. with the final pour to be on or  
before from July 15.   
 
Heimbecker, Rossi:  Moved and Seconded 
Heimbecker, Rossi, Woolley, Paulus:    Aye  Unan.
