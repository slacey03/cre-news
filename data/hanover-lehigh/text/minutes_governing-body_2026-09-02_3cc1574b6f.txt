Will Hummel, Assistant Fire Chief of Han-Le-Co Volunteer Fire Company, appeared  
before the Board this evening to inform them that as of this past Sunday, their Chief  
Keith DeAngelis stepped down and  he will be acting as the Interim Fire Chief for the  
remainder of 2026.   
   
Courtesy of the Floor:   
 
Ryan Meyer, Director of Planning and Programming, with the Lehigh-Northampton  
Airport Authority appeared before the Board to inform them of recent projects going on  
at the Airport.  At this time, he is requesting a reduction in the Letter of Credit in the  
Escrow amounts that were previously presented.  The Attorney stated that if Council  
should agree to this reduction, it should be conditioned upon the review of the Engineer,  
the Solicitor and Township administrative staff with the bulk of it being the Engineer’s  
review of the project itself.  The Engineer’s recommendation after discussions with the  
Manager and the Solicitor is to roughly do the $1,400,000 security.   
 
Motion:  I move we approve the reduced Letter of Credit request from the Lehigh- 
Northampton Airport Authority to the amount of $1,400,000 conditioned upon review by  
the Engineer, Solicitor and Township staff and a reduced Escrow amount to be reviewed  
by the Engineer, Solicitor and Township staff and final approval subject to the September  
16, 2026 Council Meeting.   
 
Heimbecker, Rossi:  Moved and Seconded 
Heimbecker, Cruz, Rossi, Paulus:     Aye  Unan.   
 
Mark Thomas, a resident on Sherwood Street who was joined by Jean Rossi also a  
resident of Sherwood Street, addressed the Board regarding a Township email list which  
was previously discussed at Council’s February 18 Meeting which was suggested by  
another resident of the Township.  After discussions, the decision was made at that time  
that the current method of communicating with our residents specifically the Township  
Calendar, the annual newsletter, Facebook page and the website.  It was determined that  
those methods were sufficient. They come before the Board to ask for that decision to be  
reconsidered.  They suggested utilizing Constant Contact, which is utilized by  
neighboring Townships and Boroughs.  The Manager will research this further and report  
back to Council.
