1. Public Hearing – Proposed 2027 Operating, Capital, Liquid Fuels, Water and 
Sewer Budgets (Review, Discussion and Vote) 
 
 The Manager presented the preliminary Proposed 2027 Operating Budget which 
she and Staff put a lot of time and effort into putting together.  The Manager 
stated that she is pleased to report that it is a balanced Budget with no tax 
increase.  The following is the Manager’s overview of the Budget.   
 
 LIQUID FUELS 
 
 We take our street lights out of the Liquid Fuels monies we receive and our 
turnback monies and allocation we receive.  As you know, Liquid Fuels monies 
can only be used for certain things.  To keep it cut and dry for the Township and 
for when the auditors come, we pay our street lights out of that.  The last audit we 
had on Liquid Fuels was completed January of this year for calendar year 2024.  
The Manager is pleased to report that as we did earlier when we received the 
findings back in all significant aspects complied with the criteria with PennDOT 
publication.  We received a very good review from our audit. 
 
 WATER 
 
 Reimbursable Management Services, the 2026 amount is zero due to it being in 
our Bill List later this evening.  That will be up at $45,833.51.  Regarding Legal 
Fees which is the last line item on this page.  We have not spent any Water legal 
fees to date this year in this Budget.   
 
 There are a couple of changes proposed.  Under Reimbursable Maintenance Labor 
will change since it is on this evenings Bill List.  Computer Office Supplies is up 
at $10,000 which includes the Asset Management Program that we are initiating 
which will get split three ways, i.e. one-third Water, one-third Sewer and one-
third General Fund/Highways.  In Capital Reserve-Water System Improvements, 
we proposed $100,000 when going over the Capital Reserve Budget back in July.

 You will see that our Expenditures are way lower than our Receivables.  We will

 SEWER 
 
 The Reimbursable Management Services will be in the Bill List this evening 
making a change in this line item.  Regarding Sewer Usage.  The Manager 
explained to Council some issues going on with the Borough of Catasauqua and 
that our Public Works Director had found some errors that they had which 
included an $80,000 mistake and some administration changes at the Borough.  
As practice has it, the 4th quarter billing of a given year is paid in the beginning of 
the following new year and the 1st, 2nd and 3rd quarters of that new year is paid in 
that new year.  We were never billed in 2026.  We are working with the Borough 
to rectify this and hopefully get it squared away so nothing spills over into next 
year.  Even with these projections of what we will need to pay, we are well within 
the Budget for this year as well as our future proposed Budgets for 2027.   
 
 CAPITAL RESERVE 
 
 There aren’t many changes in this Budget.  We reduced the Water from $100,000 
to $50,000.  With all the proposed projections for 2027 including the personnel 
changes that were discussed earlier, we still have extra money that we can beef up 
some monies to put into Capital.  I would like to add another $1,000,000 to the 
Sinkhole Fund.  We were at $175,000 and I’d like to change that to $1,175,000.  
For Future Road Construction, in July’s discussions we funded $1,000,000 to this 
line item.  We’d like to add $1,000,000 to this fund bringing this line item to 
$2,000,000.  For Building Maintenance, when we met in July it was proposed to 
fund this line item $300,000.  We have added an additional $300,000 to bring this 
line item to $600,000.  In all, we are putting $2,300,000 back in and taking 
$50,000 out in Water.  Water is a separate fund.  To explain things further, once 
these extra monies are placed in Capital they are also put into PLIGT, where we 
get a higher interest rate than putting  it into a bank.   
 
 GENERAL FUND 
 
 We are projected to start 2027 with a $3,000,000 beginning balance.  This 
allowed us to put monies into the Capital Reserves to come to a balanced Budget.   
 
 As of our last Bill List on October 7 and our Revenues at that point after we paid 
our bills on October 7, our Revenues were at 98% and our Expenditures were at 
51% for the year.  We are very keen on watching what we spend and what we 
take in.  As you know, some of these numbers are projections we pull from

 Planning and Zoning has been increased to $7,000.  We are proposing $35 per

 Code Enforcement Salaries.  We are proposing $22.00 per hour for the lead of the

Councilmembers proposed the lead at $25.00 and the starting salary for the other 
two should be $19.00.  The Chairman said they would be deciding as a Board on 
salaries after they meet in Executive Session.   
 
 The Manager proposed a 5% increase for all employees with the exception of the 
Public Works Supervisor which should be bumped up to $120,000.  The Public 
Works Assistant Supervisor is being proposed at $90,000 to start.  The Manager’s 
succession plan for the Public Works Supervisor position to get position 
advertised and have them start at the beginning of the year so that they get as 
much time with the present Public Works Supervisor until his end date.  A new 
position being added is the Water/Sewer Lead.  Our projection for that is to have 
someone be in the lead position who the Public Works Supervisor and Public 
Works Crew Chief will assign a second person to go with them to get more water 
and sewer done.  The Crew Chief will be responsible until we bring in the 
Assistant Supervisor who will take over the water and sewer and they will do the 
assignments for that and oversee it.   
 
 For Administration, the Manager proposed 5% for everyone across the Board with 
the exception of the Township Clerk going from $74,000 to $81,000.  The 
Manager proposed this increase because she sees her as her Assistant Manager as 
far as every day work.  She is the go to person when the Manager is not available 
and it needs to be reflected her pay.  The Manager proposed $145,000 for herself 
reason being just trying to stay in line with her male counterparts in this position 
is other municipalities.  Some do less because they are not doing Zoning or 
Planning.  They have an Assistant Manager or Deputy Manager.  They do not do 
purchasing.  The Manager said she takes her role very seriously as Council can 
see she puts in time and effort with just the Budget alone.  She is the go to person 
at the Township.  She said she keeps the best interest of not only the residents but 
also the business sector.  She’s built a great rapport with not only the airport but 
also the shopping centers and the state police.  We get a lot covered in our 
municipalities that they don’t get covered in other municipalities.  She is 
proposing what other area manager’s are receiving in their salaries.  She reiterated 
that it is a balanced Budget.  No increases in taxes.  Since she has been the 
Manager at the Township, there have been no major issues like you see at other 
municipalities in the neighboring communities.  She’s put a lot of checks and 
balances in place.  Some of the other municipalities have gone to a four-day work 
week for their employees.  They give longevity pay.  They give up to six weeks 
vacation.  They give a healthcare incentive, i.e. an employee may want to go on 
their spouse’s healthcare and we give them say $600 a month which would save 
us $2,400 per month, but they have to show that they are on their spouse’s plan.  
This may be something we might want to look into if it benefits us.  It’s a win win 
for us.  Our healthcare goes down and the employee still has healthcare.   Some 
municipalities are giving sick day bonuses.  If an employee doesn’t use their sick

 Since there are two Council members absent this evening, the Board decided to
