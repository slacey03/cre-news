1. Melissa A. Wehr 
 Township Manager 
 
 1. The Manager addressed questions from the last meeting from the Vice Chairman.  
Jeanne McNeill’s office said they are no longer doing the tire recycling.  They did do 
the electronic recycling but it became too expensive to continue.  The Manager put 
together a flyer which will be posted on the Township’s website informing everyone 
that Staples, Best Buy and Goodwill takes all electronic recycling.  According to 
several different sources, lawn tractor tires are not recyclable and should be put in the 
regular garbage.   Regarding the grass clippings and tree shrub trimmings, disposal 
instructions are on the Township calendar on the bottom of Page 15.  They should be 
three feet in length and bundled and tied.  If a residential garbage/recycling pickup is 
missed, J.P. Mascaro would like to hear from the resident and they will come back for 
pickup.  Their phone number is also on the Township calendar.   
 
 2. The Township received notification that we did receive the 2024 Recycling Grant 
in the amount of $25,637.03.  We should be receiving that from the County within the 
next four to six weeks.  
 
 3. PennDOT was contracted with LVPC for traffic counts on the 13th and 14th on 
Schoenersville Rd. between Airport Road and Old Airport Road.

 5. The Manager requested an Executive Session regarding personnel matters after

 6. The Manager asked to get photographs of Council, the Solicitor and Engineer at

A Councilmember mentioned that as he was taking his usual walk around the Sherwood  
Park area, the street signs and poles look like they need some maintenance.   The  
Manager noted that the Public Works Supervisor advised her of same and it will be taken  
care of within the next couple of weeks. 
 
The Vice Chairman thanked the Manager for putting together the FLOCK presentation as  
requested.  There has been a lot of chatter on the internet and FOX news regarding  
deflocking as a prank set to take place this Halloween.  He would like for the Township  
to post something well before Halloween that there will be a penalty for any damage  
done to equipment belonging to the Township.  The Attorney stated that the FLOCK  
owns the cameras and the Township owns the data.  It would be vandalism of someone  
else’s property and a typical Title 18 Vandalism or Criminal Mischief charge.  The  
charge would likely be a disorderly conduct charge.  The Attorney stated he will check  
what that charge would be and the Township could post it as preventative maintenance.   
 
The Vice Chairman is concerned that the incident to a resident’s property and fence at  
Ulster and Pennsylvania Avenue could be intentional.  The incident happened twice.  The  
Attorney stated that since it is private property, the Township is limited to what they can  
do other than road restrictions.  A discussion ensued.
