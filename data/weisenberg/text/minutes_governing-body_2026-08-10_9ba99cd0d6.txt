Weisenberg Volunteer Fire Department Comments 
Justin Oswald , Fire Chief , informed the Board that they ran 32 calls for the month of July 2026.  He reviewed the Fire 
Department’s comment letter dated 8/10/2026 regarding the activity of the fire department for the month of  July and 
upcoming events, grants and membership.   Cetronia EMS also provided their call volume for July 2026.  The 20 th 
anniversary of the fire department went really well.  The new cascade unit will be coming next week.  Bylaws and SOG’s are 
going to be paused until a future date. 
 
Weisenberg Community Center – Advisory Committee Comments 
Conestoga Buildings - Payment App#13 
Motion made by Richard Bleiler , seconded by Antony Werley  to approve the payment release of $ 33,003.00 for the final 
work performed by Conestoga Buildings.  Brian will hold off the payment until the end of the week to have the final punch 
list completed.  Motion carried.  3-0 
 
The open house event was a huge success, and we had a great turnout of vendors and residents.  
Final payment for the turf area will also be held until Brian has confirmed the punch list on that is completed.   
 
Future Events / Users 
A meeting will be held  with all the directors and coaches of NYAA on Thursday the 28 th regarding scheduling.  The turf will 
be open  to general membership on Thursdays & Sundays and  NYAA can schedule Monday Tuesday Wednesday Friday 
evenings and Saturdays.  The Board realizes this is an ongoing  conversation they will be having  regarding scheduling and 
they will monitor the needs of residents and sports together.   Brian is looking at netting the walking trail surrounding the 
turf area so that residents will still be able to walk t he perimeter of the turf area.  NYAA is also interested in installing a 
batting cage in the turf area (retractable or installed onto the ceiling ) and they would be responsible for the cost of the unit.  
The Board agreed to rent half the turf out during the AM hours Monday thru Friday.    
 
EAC Comments 
Marty stated the car show had about 43 cars, music and vendors .  He received a lot of positive feedback and the car show 
was a huge success.  Resident (Trish Knoblauch – 4587 Loch Valley Road) was a tremendous  help and will be voted into 
the EAC for membership. 
 
Roadmaster’s Comments 
Anthony Werley informed the Board that they are continuing with shoulder mowing grass and working at the community

was completed.  They raked the ball field and replaced a pipe on Blacksmith Road.    Wednesday and Thursday they will be 
at the Northwestern Lehigh Rec Fields to help set up Night in the Country an d then tear down on Monday.
