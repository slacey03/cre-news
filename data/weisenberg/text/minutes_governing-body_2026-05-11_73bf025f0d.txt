Weisenberg Volunteer Fire Department Comments 
Justin Oswald , Fire Chief , informed the Board that they ran 19 calls for the month of April 2026.  He reviewed the Fire 
Department’s comment letter dated 5/11/2026 regarding the activity of the fire department for the month of  April and 
upcoming events, grants and membership.  Cetronia EMS also provided their call volume for April 2026.   
 
Weisenberg Community Center – Advisory Committee Comments 
Conestoga Buildings - Payment App#10 
Motion made by Richard Bleiler , seconded by Antony Werley  to approve the payment release of $ 112,420.00 for work 
performed by Conestoga Buildings.  Motion carried.  3-0 
 
Community Center Scheduling Software – EZ Facility 
Brian Carl informed the Board regarding the scheduling/membership software for the community center activities.  Several 
members of the advisory committee sat in on a presentation with EZ Facility and liked the simplicity and ease of the 
software.  Motion made by Anthony Werley, seconded by Richard Bleiler to approve the purchase of the software to 
schedule the activities and membership for the community center  up to $169.00 per month.  Motion carried.  3-0 
 
LV Chamber Grant Applicant - Benches 
Brian Carl reviewed with the Board an available grant through the Lehigh Valley Chamber to order and install permanent 
benches in front of the community center.  This is a matching grant 50/50.  Motion was made by Richard Bleiler, seconded 
by Anthony Werley to apply for a matching grant (up to $2000.) for the purchase of 4 permanent benches for the 
community center.  Motion carried. 3-0 
 
Phone System 
Brian Carl addressed the Board with a request for a new phone system for the offices at the community center.  We 
currently have an outdated phone system  that is not made anymore .  He has spoken to Altek and they recommended a 
cloud base  VoIP phone system that will be cheaper than our current phone system.   Motion made by Anthony Werley, 
seconded by Richard Bleiler to approve the purchase of a new phone system for $4 79.20 and $59.80 per month through N -
Cloud Connect Net Carrier.  Motion carried. 3-0 
 
EAC Comments 
Marty Rakaczewski informed the Board of the success of the past Earth Day event.  Roadside litter has been consider ably 
declining with the help of the EAC, surrounding warehouses and local businesses.  Marty supplied photos of the event and 
thanked Brian Carl for the supplies and support along with the Board of Supervisors.  Marty is also working with Brian and 
the Fire Department with the efforts of the open house events.   A flyer for the car show is in the process so they can have a 
successful event at the soccer fields. 
 
Roadmaster’s Comments 
Anthony Werley informed the Board that they finished scratch paving this month.  They are currently working at the 
community center and  performing equipment maintenance.    Stock piling stone for the upcoming paving project and oil & 
chipping.  Shoulder mowing will begin shortly. 
 
Engineer’s Comments 
Matthew Rieger, PE advised the Board with and update regarding the advanced left turn arrow at the Golden Key Road and 
West Hills intersection.  Currently PennDOT is stating that it doesn’t meet the  warrant for this request.  We are still working 

 
 
on this request and hopefully PennDOT will come out and field view this intersection at peak hours.  The Lilly project that i s 
taking place in Upper Macungie (for the next couple of years) along with the I -78 widening project was discuss ed.  Both of 
these projects will impact the traffic within the Township.   The intersection at Old 22 and Kecks Road was also mentioned 
to PennDOT as there have been several accidents at this intersection recently. 
 
The bid documents have been put together for the procurement of the Blacksmith Road culvert.  These were sent to Low 
Volume Dirt and Gravel Road for comments . This will go out to bid in the next couple of weeks with an award to follow.  
They are also going through the DEP permitting process for the culvert.
