Old Business  
Blacksmith Road Culvert – Bid Award 
Josh Frey, Engineer – Barry Isett & Associates reviewed with the Board the (5) sealed bids received for the Blacksmith Road 
culvert replacement.  CMR Construction, Inc. was the lowest bid @ $180, 542.80 with an allowance of $11,350.00 to be 
used at the discretion of the owner.  Motion was made by Anthony Werley, seconded by Richard Bleiler to award the bid to 
CMR Construction, Inc. for their bid of $180,542.80 with an allowance of $11,350.00.  Motion carried.  3 -0
