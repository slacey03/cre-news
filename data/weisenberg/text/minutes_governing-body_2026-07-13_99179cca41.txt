Weisenberg Volunteer Fire Department Comments 
Justin Oswald , Fire Chief , informed the Board that they ran 25 calls for the month of June 2026.  He reviewed the Fire 
Department’s comment letter dated 7/13/2026 regarding the activity of the fire de partment for the month of  June and 
upcoming events, grants and membership.   Cetronia EMS also provided their call volume for June 2026.  Discussion was 
also held regarding a capital project the fire department is considering.  Information and (3) quotes were given to the Board 
to review on resurfacing the concrete floor with an epoxy -style coating & antiskid materials in the following areas. ( station 
engine bay, SCPA room, ge ar extraction room and turn -out room) The proposed cost is approximately $50,000.00 .  The 
Board did not have any objections to the capital project to resurface the concrete floors.

Treasurer's Comments  
Jeff Stauss informed the Board that  the Go Fund Me account monies have been emptied and instead of closing the 
account it is now renamed as “Community Center Membership & rentals”.   
 

 
 
Discussion was held regarding credit card fees for memberships and rentals.  It was decided that the fees would increa se 
by $5.00 in order to not charge a processing fee on credit cards.  Senior citizen fees will stay the same.  Motion made by 
Richard Bleiler, seconded by Anthony Werley, to increase the membership fees and rental fees by $5.00 in order to cover 
the costs of credit card transaction fees.  Motion carried.  3 -0 
 
Jeff Strauss also informed the Board that we are currently  at $17,250.00 into legal fees regarding Independent Road.
