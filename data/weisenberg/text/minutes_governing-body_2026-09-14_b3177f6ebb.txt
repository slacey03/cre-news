Subdivisions & Land Development  
Linda Gorgas – 2395 Seipstown Road – Lot Line Adjustment – Prel./Final Plan Dated 8/6/2026 
Linda Gorgas and Jordan Woods (Colliers Engineering & Design) were present to review the lot line adjustment plan with 
the Board.  Linda Gorgas abstained from commenting/voting.  The plan proposes revising the boundary lines , common to 
the two lots , approximately 30 feet to the south .  The  proposed acreage of each lot is: (Lot#1) 3.44 acres (Lot #2) 4.61 
acres.  Ryan Kern of HRG Engineering provided a comment letter date d September 10, 2026 with a few min or plan 
corrections.  Motion made by Richard Bleiler, seconded by Anthony Werley to approve the lot line adjustment upon the 
completion of the comment letter provided by HRG dated September 10, 2026.  Motion carried. 2 -0

Weisenberg Volunteer Fire Department Comments 
Justin Oswald , Fire Chief , informed the Board that they ran 23 calls for the month of August 2026.  He reviewed the Fire 
Department’s comment letter dated 9/14/2026 regarding the ac tivity of the fire department for the month of  July and 
upcoming events, grants and membership.   Cetronia EMS also provided their call volume for August 2026.  Fall Fire 
Prevention planning is underway and the 2027 operating and capital budget will be ready for the October Board of 
Supervisors meeting for their review. 
 
Weisenberg Community Center – Advisory Committee Comments 
Purchase Sports Netting 
Brian Carl reviewed with the Board a quote for netting within the turf area of the community center.    A quote for perimeter 
netting (material only) around the interior turf area with net flap doors (3) 4’ wide x8’ high and (2) 7’6” wide x 8’ high was 
received for $10,145.00.  Motion made by Anthony Werley, seconded by Richard Bleiler to approve the quote for the netting 
surrounding the turf area to AllSports.  Motion carried 3-0 
Brian Carl also reviewed with the Board a quote for a batting tunnel (1) 12’ wide x 12’high x 55’ long ceiling mounted power 
operated at the cost of $23,855.00 installed.  The Board deci ded to hold off on the approval regarding the batting cage for 
additional options. 
Brian also coordinated with Northwestern Elementary School PTO to hold “Trunk or Treat” here at the Community Center 
in the turf area  where they can set up tables.   Trick or Treat falls on October 31 st this year and volunteers Valarie and Ellen 
are looking into a possible craft and Halloween movie for the kids.   
Brian also suggested a FaceBook page for the Community Center with Val and Ellen being admins for that account.  The 
Board was in favor of a FaceBook page for the Community Center.
