PUBLIC COMMENT 
 
 None.  
 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES 
 
The minutes of the meeting of June 1, 2026, were distributed to the members for their review and 
comment.  After discussion, a motion to approve the minutes was made by D. Dietrich and seconded by B. 
Hamm.  The motion was unanimously carried and the minutes of the meeting of June 1, 2026, were 
approved. 
 
 
OLD BUSINESS   
 
 Bittner Subdivision – Red Road / Lynn Township  
 No one present at the meeting.  No action taken. 
 
 Albright’s Mill – New Building 
 Discussion regarding the Site and Drainage Plan for Building No. 6. 
 After discussion, a motion was made by A. Zawada and seconded by B. Hamm to approve the Site and  
Drainage Plan of Albright’s Mill Building No. 6.  The motion was unanimously carried and the Site and  
Drainage Plan for Albright’s Mill Building No. 6 was approved. 
   
 J. Moriarity – Cell Tower 
 Discussion regarding a proposed cell tower on J. Moriarity’s property. 
 Hearing is continuing.  No decision as yet. 
  
Zoning Concerns:  Solar ordinance discussion, data centers, Trexler Village Historic District 
 Solar Ordinance discussion, data centers and Trexler Village Historic District.  Discussion regarding  
 the Trexler Village Historic District and setting limitations to keep it small.  Also discussed  
Data Centers and J. Body’s document entitled “Data Centers, Proposed Ordinance Provisions, 
Draft 6/01/26.

Northeastern Berks Joint Comprehensive Plan  
Survey questionnaire, existing land use, demographics, housing, economics. 
J. Body, Township Engineer, informed the Planning Commission that he attended a meeting regarding the 
Northeastern Berks Joint Comprehensive Plan in mid-June.  Discussion at the meeting was primarily about 
the demographics of the Township.    
 
 
NEW BUSINESS 
 
 Zettlemoyer Poultry Barns 
 No one was present at the meeting, but plans have been mailed previously to Albany Township 
and were accepted for review tonight.  After discussion a motion was made by A. Zawada and seconded by 
P. Bauscher to approve the Asher Zettlemoyer Ag Operation plans for review.  
 
Hawk Mtn. Sanctuary

ADJOURNMENT 
 
There being no further business to come before the Commission, a motion to adjourn was made by P.
