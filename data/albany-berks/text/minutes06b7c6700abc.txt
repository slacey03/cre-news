CALL TO ORDER  
 
A regular meeting of the Albany Township Board of Supervisors was called to order at 7:35 PM by Board

Absent:  Board Member Joshua Rabert, Roadmaster Ryan Rex 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT 
 
Elizabeth Mammen reported that she contacted PennDOT requesting the speed limit on Route 143 be reduced to 
40 MPH between Lenhartsville and Hawk Mountain Road.  PennDOT replied that Albany Township would need 
to submit a request for a traffic study, and she asked if the township would be willing to do that.  The Board 
responded the request will be discussed with Engineer Joe Body. 
 
Scott Clouden provided an update on the Kutztown Public Library, explained the significant revenue lost due to 
the elimination of passport processing, discussed fund raising concerns and efforts, discussed providing library 
usage statistics, and asked if a link to the library could be added on the township website.

Public Parking on Township Property for Kempton Fair 
 
A motion was made by Charles and seconded by Chris to allow public parking on township property during 
the Kempton Fair on June 18-20, 2026, contingent on: 
a) receipt of a Certificate of Insurance indicating acceptable coverage limits and granting Albany Township 
additional-insured status, 
b) township property will be restored to same condition as before the event by the Kempton Community 
Center, and 
c) Albany Township reserves the right to revoke this permission if conditions are deemed unsuitable. 
 
Vote Call:  Chris – Yes, Josh – Absent, Charles – Yes 
 
Berks County Community Foundation Grant Award:  Albany Township was awarded a grant for the collection 
of scientific data and mapping to petition PA DEP to designate three Kittatinny Ridge streams as Exceptional 
Value/High Quality (Pine Creek, Manor Creek, and Ontelaunee Creek). 
 
A motion was made by Charles and seconded by Chris to accept the grant from the Cathy and Doug Weaver 
Fund of Berks County Community Foundation in the amount of $1,500.00 to be used for stream water quality
