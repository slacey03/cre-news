Hawk Mountain Sanctuary Visitor Center Construction: Conditional Use application, fee payment, and plans

Act 537 Sewage Plan Draft: The public comment period was September 1-30.  The plan will be adopted at the 
November 2nd meeting and submitted to DEP for a 180-day review period.

Northeastern Berks Joint Comprehensive Plan: The public survey is open through October 29.  The last 
meeting on September 10 covered the topics of Community Facilities / Services and Energy Conservation.  The 
next meeting will be held on October 8 at the Maxatawny Township municipal building at which time all previous

Comcast Broadband Expansion: Progress has been delayed due to Comcast reassigning the cable installation

NEW BUSINESS

A motion was made by Josh and seconded by Charles to approve the 2027 Minimum Municipal Obligation 
(MMO) contribution to the Pension Plan of $4,001. 
Vote Call:  Chris – Yes, Josh – Yes, Charles – Yes 
 
Salt Shed Security Camera: 
A motion was made by Charles and seconded by Josh to install a security camera to monitor activity at the 
new salt shed, and to accept the quote received from Dynamic System Solutions for the camera 
installation in the amount of $712.00. 
Vote Call:  Chris – Yes, Josh – Yes, Charles – Yes 
 
State Local Share Account (LSA) Grant Application: The Kempton Fire Company is requesting the board 
submit a grant application for the purchase of upgraded fire equipment.  Applications are due November 30th.  
The board agreed to investigate the requirements and process for submitting a grant application, to be decided at 
the November 2nd meeting. 
 
Ambulance Run Cards: KFC Fire Chief Matt Brett was in attendance to request adding this urgent topic to the 
agenda. 
A motion was made by Josh and seconded by Charles to add to the agenda discussion about updating 
ambulance run cards. 
Vote Call:  Chris – Yes, Josh – Yes, Charles – Yes 
 
Matt recently learned that Cetronia Ambulance will pull its resources out of the New Tripoli station on 
November 14th which will require realigning ambulance coverage and updating run cards.  This change will 
require converting all data into the county's new format.  Matt is requesting the township purchase a 
software program for $250 to facilitate conducting time and distance studies and arranging this data into 
the accepted format. 
A motion was made by Charles and seconded by Josh to purchase the Fire CAD Box Apparatus 
Generator program per Insight Service LLC quote for $250.00 
Vote Call:  Chris – Yes, Josh – Yes, Charles – Yes
