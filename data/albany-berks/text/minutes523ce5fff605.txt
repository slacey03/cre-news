Northeastern Berks Joint Comprehensive Plan:  The public survey is open through October 29.  The last 
meeting on September 10 covered the topics of Community Facilities / Services and Energy Conservation.  The 
next meeting will be held on October 8 at the Maxatawny Township municipal building at which time all previous 
sections will be reviewed for revision. 
 
Comcast Broadband Expansion:  Underground installation is currently underway on Pine Creek Road.  To date 
13 street opening permits have been issued and 7 applications are pending.

NEW BUSINESS 
 
Zettlemoyer Chicken Barns (230 Quaker City Road):  Nothing was presented to the board for discussion. 
 
Hawk Mountain Sanctuary Visitor Center Construction: HMS President Sean Grace, Board Member George 
Cauffman, and Director of Stewardship Todd Bauman presented plans for the proposed reconstruction of the 
Visitor Center.  Per the Zoning Ordinance, this project is permitted as a conditional use.  They were instructed to 
submit a conditional use application, fee payment, and plans prior to the October 5th meeting at which they are to 
present to the Planning Commission. 
 
Act 537 Sewage Plan:  The draft plan has been completed which recommends septic tank pumping 
management.  The draft plan was submitted on August 31 to the Berks County Planning Commission for 60-day 
review period.  The public comment period is September 1-30.  After the review period the plan will be adopted at 
the November 2nd meeting and submitted to DEP for a 180-day review period. 
 
Wireless Facilities Ordinance:  Postponed for consideration at a later meeting.
