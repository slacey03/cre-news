PUBLIC COMMENT 
 
 None.  
 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES 
 
The minutes of the meeting of August 3, 2026, were distributed to the members for their review and 
comment.  After discussion, a motion to approve the minutes was made by P. Bauscher and seconded by 
B. Hamm.  The motion was unanimously carried and the minutes of the meeting of August 3, 2026, were 
approved. 
 
 
OLD BUSINESS   
 
 Bittner Subdivision – Red Road / Lynn Township 
After discussion, a motion was made by A. Zawada, and seconded by B. Hamm to approve the Bittner 
Subdivision.  The motion was unanimously carried, and the Bittner Subdivision was approved.   
 
Zettlemoyer Poultry Barns 
Discussion regarding construction of three poultry barns, plus a manure storage building and drainage 
facilities. 
   
 J. Moriarity – Cell Tower 
 Construction of a cell tower on his property 
Conditional Use was granted by the Supervisors, and they will be submitting a Land Development Plan  
in the near future. 
  
Zoning Concerns:  Solar ordinance discussion, data centers, Trexler Village Historic District 
Discussion regarding Data Centers. J. Body, Township Engineer, distributed to the Planning Commission  
members a document for their review entitled, Data Centers, Proposed Ordinance Provisions, Draft 
9/14/26.

Northeastern Berks Joint Comprehensive Plan 
Survey questionnaire, community facilities, existing land use, demographics, housing, economics. 
J. Body, Township Engineer, informed the Planning Commission that the Northeastern Berks Joint 
Comprehensive Plan Committee held a meeting last week and is currently looking at community facilities 
and services.  
 
 
NEW BUSINESS 
 
 Hawk Mountain Sanctuary Visitors Center  
The development, design and site plan for the proposed Hawk Mountain Sanctuary Visitor Center was 
presented and discussed with the Planning Commission.  
 
Note: 
A motion was then proposed to add Frank and Sebastian Stelmach to the Agenda. 
A motion was made by B. Hamm, seconded by P. Bauscher, and unanimously carried, and Frank and 
Sebastian Stelmach were added to the Agenda. 
 
Frank and Sebastian Stelmach 
Subdivision Plan 
The Stelmach’s presented a Subdivision Plan to the Planning Commission for review. 
After discussion, a motion was made by D. Dietrich, seconded by P. Bauscher and unanimously carried to 
accept the Stelmach Subdivision Plan for review. 
  
 
ADJOURNMENT 
 
There being no further business to come before the Commission, a motion to adjourn was made by P. 
Bauscher and seconded by A. Zawada. The motion was unanimously carried and the meeting adjourned at 
9:10 p.m.
