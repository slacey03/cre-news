
The regular meeting of the Hereford Township Board of Supervisors was held in person at the municipal building and via Zoom. Those present were Supervisors John Membrino, Albert Ciccarone, Karla Dexter and Secretary Hannah Edwards.

Guests: Harold Albitz, Jen Sacks, Eric Miller, Stephanie McCann, Bryan Leets, Bryanna McCann, Dean Marks, Mark Levengood, Cliff Kirchner, Josh Borowski, Matt Lorish, Elwood Harper

Mr. Membrino called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m. 

Minutes 
Approval of the minutes of May 5th, 2026 BOS meeting 

John Membrino made a motion to approve the minutes of May 5th, 2026 BOS meeting. Karla Dexter second this motion. All in favor, motion passed.

Mr. Membrino made a motion and Mr. Ciccarone second to pay the bills and approve the Treasurer Report. All in favor, motion passed.

Fire Companies

Hereford Fire Company- Mr. Borowski, reported 14 calls for the month. He also mentioned additional information on the radios. Weblink of information on cost, the most current updated pricing, there were meetings since the last estimate. The current estimate, which was the cheapest, was APX 6000. The reason they are cheap is due to being discontinued, and overstock. Josh goal is to purchase next level up which will cost $150,000.00 which is a rough estimate. The radio he is looking at is APX 8000, which will not be out of “date” after a year or so. The APX 6000 has the greatest discount due to stock abundance, if Josh had a preference he would want the basic radio. He isn’t looking for anything that is touch screen, he wants bare basic models. From what Brian mentioned the pricing could change again but there is no set cost yet, the batteries, mic, chargers and other items do not come with the radio. Josh would like to speak with a representative to get a good idea of what is needed vs what Josh wants for his equipment. 
John mentioned the numbers would be $50,000.00 a year for 6 years which doesn’t include interest.
