Approve/ Not Approve Ordinance 2026-01: Vacating Kahler Road 
Mr. Membrino made a motion to approve Ordinance 2026-01, vacation of Kahler Road as a public road. Mrs. Dexter second this motion. All in favor, motion passed. 
Mr. Ciccarone wanted to address the “the line question”. He mentioned the bottom line would be to keep expenses down and taxes down. The biggest part is road maintenance; the money we received from the state isn’t touching our expenses to maintain the roads. Between the amount of salt/ man hours/ diesel and safety, the cost is not matching and or equaling out. 
Mr. Johnson question – if diesel is a concern, why are we not conserving the diesel and speed vs the expectation of payout vs level of completion. 

Discussion / Action: Winter Maintenance Agreement 
Mr. Membrino made a motion to authorize the solicitor to notify Penn Dot in writing of our specific intent to not accept the auto renewal of the winter maintenance contract and that we do not intend to sign or approve the new contract received and consider the existing agreement terminated.
The township trucks will still assist Penn Dot roads in need, he doesn’t want Fire dept or EMS stuck on our roads in the township.
