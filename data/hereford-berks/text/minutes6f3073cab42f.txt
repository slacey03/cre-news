Personal Appearances
Rick Rachor with Stonewood Landscaping- potential Airburner Chestnut St 
Rick mentioned his goal is to purchase the land for sale on Chestnut Street. He is proposing to purchase the land and place an airburner on the property to burn logs, stumps and trees. The pollution is extremely minimal; there will be biochar left over from the burn which can be used in topsoil. The one to be purchased will burn 8 tons of stumps an hour. The air burner is created to look like a 40-yard dumpster and keeps the smoke inside the container. The company is well known, there is one in Saylorsburg that is functioning currently. The wood chips grinder uses more diesel than this burner. The property for sale is 5 acres, wants to put the air burner there and a single standing shed. The machine simply starts like a match and lighter, the air curtain burner burns 100 tons wood waste in an hour.  The major difficulty will be the zoning, which is currently used for mixed use, where a variance would be needed.  Mr. Membrino noted there may be a lot of push back to a variance request in the MU district for that lot since it is used as a residential zoning area. Mr. Membrino mentioned there is a very small lot with a steep incline that could give some issues with the placement.  
Ms. McConnell noted it would be a permitted use within the Industrial Zoning District.  Rick wants to know if the township would welcome this idea in industrial zone. Mr. Membrino mentioned by right of law it is absolutely allowed to be placed in the industrial zone and wouldn’t require a zoning hearing board meeting.
