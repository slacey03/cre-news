Joint Comprehensive plan Discussion 
Mr. Membrino mentioned he got a call from District Township, he wants to discuss our joint plan. Longswamp has dropped out of their plan and now District is working with Topton Borough and Rockland, will our plan need to be renewed in 2028? The purpose of the phone call is to see if we are interested in joining their group for a new plan? The county used to fund the plan and current townships have noted the county isn’t paying for the plan like they used to. District got a grant to help fund the comprehensive plan from PA Economy. District, Rockland, Topton and Hereford would be in the group to join the plan. Gene mentioned you cannot have too many joint plan participants, which allows for more protection when it comes to any type of challenge.

Data Center Conversation 
Hereford Township is in a joint planning commission with Washington Township. This allows uses to be allowed in one Township or the other, and not necessarily in both.  In this case, Washington Township has adopted a data center ordinance allowing Data Centers in certain zoning districts and therefore Hereford is not obligated to allow them.  
We had two residents in the township coming to address the Data Center – Tammy and Slyvia. 
Mr. Orlando wants to confirm that he is very confident due to the joint comprehensive plan that Hereford Township will not face a legal challenge to have a data center placed in Hereford Township.
