
The regular meeting of the Hereford Township Board of Supervisors was held in person at the municipal building and via Zoom. Those present were Supervisors John Membrino, Albert Ciccarone, Karla Dexter and Secretary Hannah Edwards.

Guests: Harold Albitz, Jen Sacks, Eric Miller, Stephanie McCann, Bryan Leets, Bryanna McCann, Dean Marks, Mark Levengood, Cliff Kirchner, Josh Borowski, Matt Lorish, Elwood Harper
Zoom: Candace Perry, Irene Donovan

Mr. Membrino called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m. 

Minutes 
Approval of the minutes of June 2nd, 2026 BOS meeting 

John Membrino made a motion to approve the minutes of June 2nd, 2026 BOS meeting. Karla Dexter second this motion. All in favor, motion passed. 

Payment of Bills and Approval of the Treasurer’s Report
Mr. Membrino made a motion and Mr. Ciccarone second to pay the bills and approve the Treasurer Report. All in favor, motion passed.

Personal Appearances – No Report

Fire Companies

Seisholtzville Fire Company- Mrs. Sacks reported 9 calls for the month of May. She also noted the fire police will be attending the Huffs Union Church event. 
Hereford Fire Company- Mr. Borowski, reported 8 calls for the month. He also noted nothing new regarding the radios. There was a phone conversation with a sales representative. He plans on sending over some demos to the fire house to see what they like best. The one radio will be a touch pad; Josh forwarded an email regarding questions and answers that most townships are bringing up regarding the radios and functionality. Josh doesn’t have a defined direction on which radio he would select. Josh would prefer the 6,000 or the 8,000 radios which are all button-based and not touch pads.  Albert questioned if there would be any in the demo and Josh mentioned no. Josh thinks the 6,000 choices would be valuable and cheaper. Josh said the main thing to focus on the fact that the 6000 will be the first to go and then the 8000 would be the next to go. John mentioned the life span of the 6000 would be 10 years based on the information. Josh mentioned he would bring the radios to the next meeting if the board would be interested in seeing the radios. The most expensive is the 8,500 which is a 3-band radio, josh mentioned the township only needs a product with one band. The touch screen cost is between 6000 and 8000 radios. 
Albert mentioned which radio would Josh feel most comfortable with, Josh mentioned the 6000 radio would work just fine for their need and request. 
Mr. Membrino wants to know what the total cost would be for the township. Currently the guess price would be 300,000.00 for the two-township combined. Mr. Membrino noted the township needs to budget for the 2027 cost and would like an exact list of what both fire houses are requesting. 
Dean completed the documents for the relief association so Hereford can receive their funds from the state.
