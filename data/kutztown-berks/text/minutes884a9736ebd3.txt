   Borough of Kutztown  
 Planning Commission  
Minutes of September 14, 2026 
      
 
A regular meeting of the Borough of Kutztown Planning Commission was held on Monday, September 14, 2026, 
in the Kutztown Borough Municipal Building Multi-Purpose Room.  
  
The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. by Mr. Robert Weller, Chairperson. The members present were: 
Mr. Daniel Fogarty, Ms. Patricia Snyder, Mr. Joel Seidel, and Mr. Chad Master.  Mr. Drew (Brad) Myers was 
absent.   Also present was Ms. Michele Lopez, Code Enforcement Officer, and Ms. Carolann Bartik, Recording 
Secretary.    Public Attendance: Mr. Eric Boyer, Borough resident, Ms. April Crossley representing Homes of 
Grace and Elliot Comparsa representing C2C Design Group 
                                  
 PUBLIC COMMENTS ON NON-AGENDA ITEMS 
Ms. Lopez introduced Ms. April Crossley and Mr. Elliot Comparsa to Planning Commissions stating that they are 
interested in developing the Silfies lot and they wanted to give an informal presentation. 
 
Planning Commissioners reviewed the plans which include a section for adults 55 and older and veterans and 
another section for adults with autism, which would be purchased by their parents for the autistic adults that 
require assistance.  She added that there would also be a community center in the plans that would be available 
to Kutztown Borough residents.  The community center would also have onsite resources such as Berks Encore 
and Friend, Inc. 
 
There was a lengthy discussion regarding this project.   
 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES                    
Commissioners reviewed the August 10, 2026 Planning Commission meeting minutes and the July 13, 2026 
Planning Commission Workshop minutes.  A motion was made by Ms. Snyder and seconded by Mr. Master to 
approve August 10, 2026 Planning Commission meeting minutes and the July 13, 2026 Planning Commission 
Workshop minutes, as written.   The motion passed by unanimous vote. 
 
REPORTS                  
Ms. Lopez reviewed the August Community Development report with Planning Commissioners.  There were no 
questions or comments. 
 
Ms. Lopez mentioned that she did receive the Dollar General zoning application.  She added that she has signed 
the application and has forwarded it on to Mr. Matt Davenport for his review.  Ms. Lopez stated that this is the 
Dollar General from Maxatawny moving into the Borough of Kutztown.   
 
 
 
 
 

ACTION ITEMS  
•      Conditional Use Procedures and Standards Ordinance 
Planning Commissioners reviewed the revised Conditional Use Procedures and Standards Ordinance.  Following 
a brief discussion, Planning Commissioners noted to add the definition of “Conditional Use”  to the Zoning 
Ordinance as a defined term. 
 
Motion by Mr. Fogarty and seconded by Ms. Snyder to recommend the Conditional Use Procedures and 
Standards Ordinance be sent to Mr. Keith Mooney, Borough Solicitor, to make the necessary changes and then 
forward to Borough Council for approval.  The motion passed by unanimous vote. 
 
•      Section 225-33.3 review Chapters 75, 168, and 225  
Following a brief discussion, Planning Commissioners agreed to discuss this at the Planning Commission 
Workshop meeting.
