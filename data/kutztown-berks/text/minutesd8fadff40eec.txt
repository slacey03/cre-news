 
Borough of Kutztown  
Planning Commission  
Minutes of October 12, 2026  
 
A regular meeting of the Borough of Kutztown Planning Commission was held on Monday, October 12, 
2026, in the Kutztown Borough Municipal Building Multi-Purpose Room. 
The meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. by Mr. Robert Weller, Chairperson. The members present 
were:  Mr. Daniel Fogarty, Mr. Joel Seidel, Mr. Chad Master, and Mr. Drew (Brad) Myers.   Also present 
were Ms. Michele Lopez, Code Enforcement Officer, and Ms. Carolann Bartik, Recording Secretary. 
Public attendance:  Mr. Jonathan Rogers representing Keystone Consulting Engineers; and Mr. Mike 
Heffner and Mr. Steven Curry, Borough of Kutztown residents       
        
PUBLIC COMMENTS ON NON-AGENDA ITEMS  
Ms. Lopez introduced Mr. Mike Heffner, property owner, and Mr. Jonathan Rogers of Keystone 
Consulting Engineers, who presented preliminary sketch plans for three parcels on East Main Street. 
Mr. Heffner proposed consolidating the parcels, totaling approximately nine-tenths of an acre, to 
accommodate a building for the display and storage of sports collectibles, a museum/showroom 
component, and a limited retail operation. Mr. Heffner indicated that retail hours and initial visitor 
traffic would be limited. 
The sketch showed a building footprint of approximately 7,000 square feet, with portions potentially 
reaching two stories and no residential use. The site layout depicted 24 parking spaces, subject to final 
design and accessible space requirements, with primary customer access from James Alley by way of 
South Kemp Street. Mr. Rogers stated that the proposed design would reduce existing impervious 
coverage, maintain or improve drainage conditions, provide screening near neighboring residences, 
and retain substantial green space.  
Commissioners discussed emergency vehicle turning and access requirements, including access from 
East Main Street or a reinforced grass emergency lane, and recommended consultation with the 
Borough Fire Marshal.  This was an informal discussion; no application was approved or action taken. 
 
Mr. Steven Curry presented photographs and expressed concern about the condition of the Kutztown 
Borough Community Park basketball court and ballpark bleachers. Mr. Curry noted that local youth 
have been using Kutztown University facilities instead. Commissioners explained that Public Works 
more appropriately addresses park maintenance.  Mr. Curry was encouraged to forward photographs 
for distribution to appropriate officials and to attend the upcoming Community Development and 
Public Safety Committee meeting. No formal action was taken.

REPORTS  
•  From the Community Development Office 
 Ms. Lopez reviewed the September Community Development report with Planning Commissioners.  
She reported that the proposed Dollar General relocation from Maxatawny Township to Kutztown 
remains in process, with a sign permit still outstanding at the time of the meeting. 
Ms. Lopez reported that the overgrown property at 225 Greenwich Street has been mowed and that 
the Borough will invoice the homeowner. She also reported that two Zoning Hearing Board matters 
had been approved. One, involving Mr. Pete Smith, included a restriction discussed at the meeting 
limiting each apartment to one tenant; parking provisions for the residential and ground-floor retail 
uses were also discussed. 
 
ACTION ITEMS  
•  Selection of Planning Commission Vice Chairperson  
A nomination from Mr. Weller for Mr. Daniel Fogarty to serve as Planning Commission Vice 
Chairperson was made and seconded by Mr. Seidel. Following a brief discussion, Mr. Fogarty accepted 
the nomination. Planning Commissioners all voted in favor, and Mr. Fogarty was elected Vice 
Chairperson. 
•  Conditional Use Procedures and Standards Ordinance  
Ms. Lopez presented a revised redlined draft of the Conditional Use Procedures and Standards 
Ordinance incorporating comments from the Berks County Planning Commission (BCPC) and review by 
Borough Solicitor Mr. Keith Mooney. The principal revisions clarified procedural requirements and 
added deadlines, including specified 10-day and 45-day periods. 

 
A motion was made by Mr. Seidel and seconded by Mr. Myers to recommend forwarding the revised 
Conditional Use Procedures and Standards Ordinance to Borough Council for approval.  Ms. Lopez 
stated that she will provide both a redlined and a clean copy. The motion passed by unanimous vote.  
Ms. Bartik will prepare the recommendation letter for Borough Council and will forward to Planning 
Commissioners for approval. 
•  Section 225-33.3 – Alternative Energy Systems  
Planning Commissioners continued review of Section 225-33.3, focusing on small wind energy systems. 
Commissioners expressed agreement with a maximum height of 50 feet measured to the top of the 
blade, a minimum one-acre lot requirement, and retention of the 1:1 setback provision discussed 
previously.  
•  Review of Chapters 75, 168, and 225  
Following a brief discussion, Planning Commissioners decided to continue the discussion of Section 
225-33.3 and review Chapters 75, 168, and 225 at the upcoming Planning Commission Workshop 
meeting. 
 
OLD BUSINESS/UPDATES  
•  Joint Comprehensive Plan  
With Ms. Snyder being appointed to Kutztown Borough Council, she had previously mentioned that she 
would continue to participate in Joint Comprehensive Plan meetings.  Ms. Bartik will contact Ms. 
Snyder for the latest chapter versions and place them in the Planning Commission SharePoint materials 
for Commissioners to review. 
 
NEW BUSINESS  
•  Public Comment Rules and Meeting Procedures  
Commissioners reviewed public-comment rules and a resolution governing Borough Council meetings 
that had been distributed for awareness. Members discussed the distinction between Borough Council, 
Borough committees, and the Planning Commission, as well as the value of a Commission-specific 
policy for public participation, speaking limits, and managing large public turnouts. Ms. Lopez indicated 
that she would review procedures used by other municipalities and coordinate with the Borough 
Solicitor.  Members expressed an interest in having a policy for review before the end of the year. 
•  Set agenda for the October 26, 2026 Planning Commission Workshop meeting, if necessary  
Commissioners agreed that the October 26, 2026 workshop agenda would include further review of 
the proposed amendments to Section 225-33.3 and Chapters 75, 168, and 225.   
Updated documents will be placed together in the October 26 workshop folder on SharePoint ahead of 
the meeting. 

 
OFF AGENDA  
Members discussed the possibility of creating additional public parking on underused privately owned 
parcels, including property near James Alley on the east side of the Borough and an area near West 
Walnut Street. Ms. Lopez noted that Borough representatives had previously spoken with the owners 
of those properties, but that owners had not indicated a willingness to sell.  
 
ADJOURNMENT  
With no further business to discuss, a motion was made by Mr. Seidel and seconded by Mr. Myers to 
adjourn the meeting.  The motion passed by unanimous vote.  The meeting was adjourned at 9:15 p.m.
