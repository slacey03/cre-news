Workshop Topics  
 Section 225-33.3 review alongside Chapters 75, 168, and 225 
Planning Commissioners reviewed the proposed amendments to Section 225-33.3 
(Alternative Energy Systems).   
 
Planning Commissioners also reviewed Chapter 225-27.1 regarding the keeping of 
domestic hens. 
 Joint Comprehensive Plan Chapter reviews 
Ms. Snyder mentioned that she will still be one of the two people representing the 
Borough of Kutztown on the Joint Comprehensive Plan even though she is now on 
Kutztown Borough Council.   
Ms. Snyder stated that she and Mr. Engelhardt will review the chapters before they get 
adopted and once they are in their second review she can post them in the Planning 
Commission SharePoint so Planning Commissioners can also review them.   
Planning Commissioners reviewed the Natural Features, Agriculture and History Chapter 
of the Comprehensive Plan. 
 Conditional Use Procedures and Standards review letter from Berks 
County Planning Commission 
Mr. Weller mentioned that the Conditional Use Procedures and Standards Ordinance is 
already on the Borough Council agenda for October, however, they just received the 
BCPC review letter and questioned how to follow through.  Ms. Lopez reminded 
Planning Commission members that BCPC is a recommending body, and they are not 
required to take their recommendations unless they agree with them.  Ms. Lopez 
recommended that they move forward with the Conditional Use Procedures and 
Standards Ordinance and they can always amend at a later date.
