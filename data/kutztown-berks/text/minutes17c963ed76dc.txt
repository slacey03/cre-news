12928 
 
September 15, 2026 Council Meeting Minutes 
 
Mayor Schlegel administered the oath of office to Patricia Snyder. 
Keith Mooney called for a Public Hearing to consider an Ordinance to amend 
Chapter 225 – Data Centers and Data Center Accessory Uses. After noting that the Public 
Hearing was advertised in the Reading Eagle and posted at Borough Hall, he opened the 
floor for questions. 
Matt Senseny stated that he thinks the decommissioning process is a good clause 
and asked if there is a possibility to make it retroactive. Mr. Mooney replied that while it 
cannot be retroactively applied, it can be done moving forward. 
Steve Curry asked if the use of water and electric is allowed. Mr. Mooney replied 
that you cannot be exclusionary, so we can only put in rules and regulations about water.  
Judy Wildman asked if there are any current applications and was told that there 
are not. She told Borough Council that she thinks they are doing the right thing and she 
appreciates it. 
Angie Lutz asked where the Industrial Zone is located, and the Mayor responded 
that it is from this location (Train Station) to Moyer Lumber. 
There being no more questions from the public, Mr. Mooney asked for questions 
or comments from Borough Council. 
Dr. Mace asked if it is a recommendation or requirement to use a closed-looped 
system? Mr. Mooney replied that it is a requirement if on municipal water and it is all 
based on conditional use. 
 Dr. Mace also asked about the decommissioning plan and wanted to know an 
estimate of the bond. Mr. Mooney explained that it would be based on the project and 
could increase every five years.

required to be made into the Police Pension Fund for the calendar year 2027, by each 
full-time police officer of the Borough of Kutztown, now or hereafter eligible for 
participation in the Police Pension Plan for the calendar year of 2027, as provided for in 
§33-8, of the Code of the Borough of Kutztown, Berks County, Pennsylvania.
