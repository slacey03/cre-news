RESOLUTION NO. 4-2026 
 
A RESOLUTION OF THE BOROUGH OF KUTZTOWN, 
COUNTY OF BERKS, COMMONWEALTH OF 
PENNSYLVANIA AUTHORIZING AND DIRECTING 
KEVIN SNYDER, PRESIDENT OF THE BOROUGH 
COUNCIL, AND ANY OTHER APPROPRIATE 
BOROUGH OFFICIALS, TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT 
BETWEEN THE BOROUGH OF KUTZTOWN AND 
UNIPOWER, LLC, INC. FOR THE PROVISION OF 
SERVICE AND MAINTENANCE UPON THE 
BOROUGH’S UPS POWER SUPPLY FOR THE 
BOROUGH’S COMPUTER SYSTEM. 
 
             WHEREAS, the Borough of Kutztown has as a UPS battery back-
up system to power its computer systems in the event of a power outage 
(the “UPS System”); and 
 
WHEREAS, the Borough Council of the Borough of Kutztown has a service and 
maintenance contract to keep the UPS System in good working order; and 
 
WHEREAS, UniPower, LLC has made a proposal to the Borough of Kutztown 
for the provision of service and maintenance for the UPS System; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Borough Council of the Borough of Kutztown desires to enter 
into a service and maintenance agreement with UniPower, LLC. for the UPS System; and  
 
WHEREAS, UniPower, LLC is ready, willing and able to provide said service 
and maintenance coverage for the Borough’s UPS System. 
 
NOW, THEREFOREE, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED by the Council of the 
Borough of Kutztown, County of Berks, Commonwealth of Pennsylvania that Kevin 
Snyder, Borough Council President, and any other appropriate Borough officials, are 
hereby authorized and directed to execute and enter into an agreement with UniPower, 
LLC to provide service and maintenance for the Borough’s UPS System.   
 
 ADOPTED by the Council of the Borough of Kutztown, Berks County, 
Pennsylvania, in lawful session duly assembled this 18 th  day of August, 2026. 
 
Passed by unanimous vote.

              WHEREAS, the Borough of Kutztown owns and operates a water

 WHEREAS, the Borough Council of the Borough of Kutztown desires to enter

into a comprehensive coverage plan with Hach Service Partnership for the Borough’s 
water analysis equipment; and  
 
WHEREAS, Hach Service Partnership is ready, willing and able to provide said 
comprehensive coverage for the Borough’s water analysis equipment.

Borough of Kutztown, County of Berks, Commonwealth of Pennsylvania that Kevin 
Snyder, Borough Council President, and any other appropriate Borough officials, are 
hereby authorized and directed to execute and enter into an agreement with Hach Service 
Partnership to provide a comprehensive coverage plan to service and maintain the 
Borough’s water analysis equipment.
