  Borough of Kutztown  
 Planning Commission  
Minutes of August 10, 2026    
      
 
A regular meeting of the Borough of Kutztown Planning Commission was held on Monday, August 10, 2026, in 
the Kutztown Borough Municipal Building Multi-Purpose Room.  
  
The meeting was called to order at 7:04 p.m. by Mr. Robert Weller, Chairperson. The members present were: 
Mr. Daniel Fogarty, Ms. Patricia Snyder, Mr. Joel Seidel, and Mr. Chad Master.  Mr. Drew (Brad) Myers was 
absent.   Also present was Ms. Michele Lopez, Code Enforcement Officer, and Ms. Carolann Bartik, Recording 
Secretary.    Public Attendance: Mr. Todd Greiss, Borough resident 
 
                                    
 PUBLIC COMMENTS ON NON-AGENDA ITEMS 
There were no comments from the public. 
 
 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES                    
Commissioners reviewed the July 13, 2026 meeting minutes.  A motion was made by Mr. Fogarty and seconded 
by Ms. Snyder to approve July 13, 2026 minutes, as written.   The motion passed by unanimous vote. 
 
 
REPORTS                  
Commissioners reviewed the July Community Development report.  Ms. Lopez mentioned that there was one 
business who officially opened at 49 S Maple Street, adding that it is staying a retail business.   
 
Ms. Lopez stated that no DCR’s have been issued, however, the office has received complaints of high weeds, 
noise, and dust. 
 
There were no additional questions or comments. 
 
 
ACTION ITEMS  
There were no action items. 
 
 
OLD BUSINESS/UPDATES 
•      Joint Comprehensive Plan      
Ms. Pat Snyder mentioned that the next meeting is this Thursday.   
 

Ms. Snyder stated that a request was made to Borough Council regarding how they would like to 
approve the chapters for the Joint Comprehensive Plan.  Ms. Lisa Ladd-Kidder reached out to Ms. 
Snyder and requested to see them when they come through as the second draft.   
 
Ms. Snyder reviewed with Planning Commissioners the topics for this Thursday’s meeting.   
 
Ms. Snyder stated that the survey is almost complete and it will be sent to each municipality. 
 
 
NEW BUSINESS 
•      Transient Retail Business License                          
Ms. Lopez mentioned that although there is a Transient Retail Business License in place, there is nothing in 
Zoning that pertains to food trucks.   
 
There was a lengthy discussion regarding food trucks, what should be included in a food truck ordinance as well 
as creating a fire inspection ordinance.   
  
•     Set agenda for the August 24, 2026 Planning Commission Workshop meeting, if necessary 
1.   Conditional Use Procedures and Standards Ordinance 
2.  Food Truck Ordinance 
3.  Joint Comprehensive Plan Housing section review 
 
 
OFF AGENDA                                                                                                                                                               
Ms. Lopez mentioned that Mr. Keith Mooney, Borough Solicitor, sent her the draft of the Conditional 
Use Procedures and Standards Ordinance that needs to be entered as an amendment to Zoning.    She 
added that she would like this to be a discussion at the August Planning Commission Workshop 
meeting. 
 
Ms. Lopez stated that Mr. Pete Smith is getting everything together to go to Zoning Hearing Board, 
adding that he is also working on Sketch Plan.   
 
Ms. Lopez mentioned that there is another plan coming and will have further discussion regarding that 
this coming Wednesday.   
 
Ms. Lopez questioned Planning Commissioners regarding a review of Chapter 225-33.3, which 
addresses alternative energy systems.  She added that when that section of the Ordinance was 
adopted, there was a section added stating that there is a mandatory 5 year review.   Following a brief 
discussion, Ms. Lopez stated that she will write a memo to Ms. Andrea Coaxum, Borough Manager, to 
have Kutztown Borough Council direct the Kutztown Planning Commission to conduct that review. 
 

Ms. Lopez stated that Dewy Meadow, the pie shell factory that took over the former Radius 
Toothbrush factory, would like to expand and add a freezer to their facility.  Ms. Lopez reviewed with 
Planning Commissioners the process they would need to go through before their request can come to 
Planning Commission.   
 
 
ADJOURNMENT  
With there being no further business, a motion was made by Ms. Snyder and seconded by Mr. Seidel to adjourn 
the meeting.  The motion passed by unanimous vote and the meeting adjourned at 8:26 p.m.
