 Section 225-33.3 review alongside Chapters 75, 168, and 225 
Planning Commissioners reviewed the proposed changes to Chapter 75.  Following a

Mooney for review.  Following a brief discussion, it was decided that this item would 
also be placed on the November Planning Commission agenda for further discussion.
