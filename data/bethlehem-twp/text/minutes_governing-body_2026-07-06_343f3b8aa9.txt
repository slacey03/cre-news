Mr. Merhottein called the meeting to order at 7:01 p.m. and led those assembled in 
the Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
Solicitor Broughal announced the Board of Commissioners met on July 6, 2026 at 
6:00 pm to approximately 6:58 pm to discuss potential litigation pursuant to Section 
708a of the Pennsylvania Sunshine Act. 
 
 
 
 
Mr. Schlegel introduced himself as the program coordinator for the Bethlehem 
Township Athletic Association and invited the board to their open house on 
Thursday, July 8th at 6 pm.  He also thanked the board for their annual support.  Mr. 
Merhottein thanked Mr. Schlegel for volunteering. 
 
Ms. Grube said the condition of Christian Spring Road is worsening.  Mr. 
Merhottein said he will send Public Works out to assess the road. He added that the 
board understands her concern and want to do a better job of paving which is 
expensive.  Mr. Merhottein said road paving will be discussed at the budget 
meetings.  Mr. Hudak said the cornfield near Housenick Park is not owned by the 
township. The repaving of Christian Spring Road was put on hold until it was 
developed.  Mr. Gawlik said he did some research and found that Christian Spring 
Road is a Cold-in-Place Recycling candidate. 
 
Mr. Kreier asked for a status of the retaining wall. Mr. Merhottein said they will 
have to move the wall in order to get the Certificate of Occupancy.   
 
Mr. Keefer asked the board if they were aware that a second retaining wall was 
being built.  Ms. Smith said they have a total of eight retaining walls included in 
their plan and are complying. Mr. Keefer also expressed concern about land being 
raised at the Miller tract.  Ms. Smith they were only stockpiling. 
 
Mr. Yoder said Community Days was a great event and “hats off” to everyone 
involved in making it a success.  Mr. Yoder asked if the township had a committee 
specifically for data centers. Ms. Smith said an ordinance was recently adopted on 
June 1st regarding standards for data centers.

Mr. Bucherer thanked Mr. Merhottein for trying to help him with his complaint 
about trash haulers collecting garbage before the time permitted in the ordinance. 
He said they were back out again at 5 am.  Mr. Hudak noted that the start time was 
changed to 6 am when Howard Kutzler was manager.  Mr. Deckman asked if this 
was a police matter.  Noise ordinances are enforced by the police department. Mr. 
Bucherer said contract time is approaching and suggested a blanket layoff notice.  
 
Mr. Link said the Pektor development took down trees on Church Road and now he 
has a view of the Park and Ride. He asked when the trees will be replaced.  Ms. 
Smith said they are unable to get a response and noted that they are also in violation 
with the Northampton County Conservation District.  Mr. Link asked if they had 
any deadlines.  Ms. Smith said not at this stage of the plan. 
 
Upon motion (Gallagher-Lawrence), the Board of Commissioners unanimously 
voted by voice vote to approve the June 15, 2026 regular public meeting minutes.  
 
 
 
 
 
A MOTION TO RELEASE PROJECT SECURITY, UPON SUCCESSFUL 
COMPLETION OF IMPROVEMENTS AND SATISFACTION OF THE 18-
MONTH MAINTENANCE PERIOD FOR THE BIRCHES AT 
BETHLEHEM PROJECT 
 
Upon motion (Hudak-Deckman) the Board of Commissioners voted unanimously 
by voice vote to release project security, upon successful completion of 
improvements and satisfaction of the 18-month maintenance period for the Birches 
at Bethlehem Project. 
 
A MOTION TO EXTEND THE DEVELOPER’S OBLIGATION TO 
COMPLETE IMPROVEMENTS, PURSUANT TO THE LAND 
DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT, TO NOVEMBER 15, 2026 FOR GREEN 
POND TRADITIONS OF AMERICA (TOA) SUBDIVISION PLAN 
 
Ms. Smith said the completion schedule required an extension request.  Mr. 
Deckman said he appreciated the schedule but was concerned that the items on the 
list might not be completed by the date. He asked what the original deadline was.  
Ms. Smith said there wasn’t one for this particular plan which is why she asked for 
a schedule.  Mr. Gallagher said without a deadline, there are no assurances.  He said 
many residents are disappointed in their performance.  Mr. Deckman said there 
should be a commitment to communicate the status.  Ms. Smith said with clear 
expectation set moving forward, the township is in a much better position. The 
developers must start when they say it starts. 
 
Mr. Puleo thanked the board and staff for putting this on the agenda and asked that 
keep them apprised.  He said the residents have felt they had no say until now. 
 
Mr. Roth said this should be made a priority.  
 
 
Mr. Bucherer asked how it got to this point.  Mr. Merhottein said it was still in the 
development process but township staff will keep a close watch on the work to 
ensure it is properly done.

Upon motion (Hudak-Lawrence), the Board of Commissioners voted unanimously 
by voice vote to extend the developer’s obligation to complete improvements, 
pursuant to the land development agreement, to November 15, 2026 for Green Pond 
Traditions of America (TOA) Subdivision Plan. 
 
 
 
 
 
A RESOLUTION AWARDING THE CONTRACT FOR THE CHURCH 
ROAD ROADWAY IMPROVEMENTS PROJECT 
 
The scope of work is the full depth reconstruction and associated profile 
adjustments to existing Church Road generally from Easton Avenue to Country 
Club Road.   
 
Mr. Puleo asked if additional drains were proposed.  Mr. Gawlik said no. 
 
Upon motion (Lawrence-Gallagher), the Board of Commissioners voted 
unanimously by voice vote to approve Resolution R057-26 awarding the contract 
for the Church Road Roadway Improvements Project to Northeast Site Contractors 
In the amount of $1,585,528.95. 
 
 
 
 
 
A MOTION APPROVING AN ACCESS AND HOLD HARMLESS 
INDEMNIFICATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN BETHLEHEM 
TOWNSHIP AND LEVEL A UNDERGROUND SOLUTIONS, INC. TO 
PERFORM TEST HOLES ON CHURCH ROAD TO CONFIRM DEPTH 
AND LOCATION OF CERTAIN HIGH PRESSURE GAS LINES 
 
Upon motion (Deckman-Lawrence) the Board of Commissioners voted 
unanimously by voice vote approving an Access and Hold Harmless 
Indemnification Agreement between Bethlehem Township and Level A 
Underground Solutions, Inc. to perform test holes on Church Road to confirm depth 
and location of certain high pressure gas lines. 
 
A MOTION APPROVING A PROPOSAL OF INSURANCE FOR 
TRAVELERS DESIGNATED BENEFIT PLAN FIDUCIARY LIABILITY 
 
Upon motion (Hudak-Gallagher) the Board of Commissioners voted unanimously 
by voice vote to table the motion approving a proposal of insurance for Travelers 
Designated Benefit Plan Fiduciary Liability. 
 
A MOTION APPROVING AND AUTHORIZING THE TRAFFIC 
MAINTENANCE CONTRACT RENEWAL BETWEEN BETHLEHEM 
TOWNSHIP AND SIGNAL SERVICE, INC. 
 
Mr. Hozza said this was a two-year contract for preventive services and 
maintenance of traffic lights. He said he was very impressed at how quickly they 
fixed the traffic light at the intersection of State Route 191 and Route 22.
