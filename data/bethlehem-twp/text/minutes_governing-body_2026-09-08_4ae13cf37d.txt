Mr. Merhottein called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led those assembled in 
the Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
Solicitor Broughal announced the Board of Commissioners met on September 8, 
2026 at 6:30 pm to approximately 7:00 pm to discuss personnel matters pursuant to 
Section 708a of the Pennsylvania Sunshine Act. 
 
 
 
 
Mr. Puleo reported that two sinkholes were remediated.  He also mentioned that Mr. 
Hozza came to meet with him and the residents regarding work that has yet to be 
accomplished by the developer. Mr. Deckman asked if there was leverage that can 
be used to get the developer to finish the projects quicker.  Ms. Smith said the 
bonding company has the ability to increase the security. Mr. Deckman asked Ms. 
Smith to keep the board updated.  Mr. Hudak asked Ms. Smith to get him the 
security balance. 
 
Mr. Healy said that one-tenth of a mile of Township Line Road is in disrepair and 
belongs to Bethlehem Township.  He asked if it could be repaired.  Mr. Merhottein 
said the board will address it.  
 
Mr. DeBlanco said parking in his neighborhood is difficult.  He said bike are non-
existent asked if the lanes could be removed to open roads for more parking.  Mr. 
Merhottein said the concept for the development included biking and walking. Mr. 
Hudak added that it was intentionally designed that way. 
 
Mr. Dearmas said a new cable company was digging holes in the right-of-way near 
his property and was concerned about how the work was being done and mentioned 
a ruling currently in federal court. Solicitor Broughal said the Federal 
Communications Commission permits and regulates the installation of wireline and 
cable infrastructure in public rights-of-way through federal guidelines.  
 
Mr. Roth asked for a status on work proposed at the intersection of Route 191 and 
Highfield Drive. Mr. Gawlik said he is working with public works and police on a 
plan.  A traffic control plan with PennDOT is currently under review. 
 
Mr. Knauss thanked the board for paving Danberry Drive and Kingsview.

A MOTION TO APPROVE PAYMENT-IN-LIEU OF INSTALLATION OF

A MOTION TO EXTEND THE DEVELOPER’S OBLIGATION TO 
COMPLY WITH THE CONDITIONS OF APPROVAL, PURSUANT TO 
RESOLUTION R080-22, TO JULY 31, 2027 FOR THE MOWRER ROAD 
LAND DEVELOPMENT PLAN 
 
Ms. Smith said this is the 5th such request for this project – the 4th request failed to 
due to lack of motion at the Board’s August 17th meeting. It would appear that 
recent updates to the Township Ordinances since submission of the plan would if 
anything, only result in minor modifications for compliance. Mr. Gallagher asked 
what the minor changes were. Solicitor Broughal said the Municipal Planning Code 
allows developers a five-year period to secure final plan approval for their projects. 
This timeframe begins from the date of preliminary plan approval, ensuring that 
developers have adequate time to complete necessary steps for project realization.  
Mr. Deckman asked what date marks five years.  Ms. Smith said September 2027 
will be five years. 
 
Mr. Roth said it’s just a parking lot and believes the developer should not be 
dragging their feet. 
 
Upon motion (Hudak-Merhottein), the Board of Commissioners voted by voice vote 
to extend the developer’s obligation to comply with the conditions of approval, 
pursuant to Resolution R080-22, to July 31, 2027 for the Mowrer Road Land
