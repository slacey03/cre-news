ADDING NEW DEFINITIONS TO SECTION 275-150 DEFINITIONS OF

VILLAGE OVERLAY DISTRICT, SECTIONS 275-247 CONDITIONS OF 
ELIGIBILITY, 275-250 AREA, BULK, HEIGHT AND YARD REGULATIONS, 
AND 275-251 DEVELOPMENT REQUIREMENTS, TO ADDRESS ANY 
INCONSISTENCIES WITH THE NEW PROVISIONS OF SECTION 275-165 
AND, REPEALING ALL ORDINANCES INCONSISTENT HEREWITH 
 
Ms. Smith Staff and solicitor have been working with St Luke’s representatives and 
their team to update sign regulations applicable to the Healthcare Village Overlay 
District. As currently adopted, the Anderson Campus follows sign regulations 
called ‘Design Guidelines’, which are adopted separately from the Zoning 
Ordinance. The regulations are difficult, cumbersome and perpetually outdated for 
both parties (staff and St Luke’s). This proposed amendment will move streamlined, 
simplified and functional sign regulations for the hospital campus into the Zoning 
Ordinance. The Planning Commission unanimously recommended approval.  
 
Mr. Roth asked if the ordinance mentions anything about digital signs.  Ms. Smith 
responded the township doesn’t allow them. 
 
Upon motion (Gallagher-Lawrence), the Board of Commissioners voted 
unanimously by voice vote to adopt Ordinance 07-26 amending Ordinance No. 8-97 
the Bethlehem Township Zoning Ordinance, Chapter 275 "Zoning", of the Township of 
Bethlehem Code of Ordinances, as amended, by further amending Article XVIII Signs to 
address signage in the Hospital Health Care Village Overlay District by adding new 
definitions to Section 275-150 Definitions of Signs; adding new provisions to Section 
275-165 Signs in Streetscape Overlay District; Article XXX Health Care Village 
Overlay District, Sections 275-247 Conditions of Eligibility, 275-250 Area, Bulk, Height 
and Yard Regulations, and 275-251 Development Requirements, to address any 
inconsistencies with the new provisions of Section 275-165 and, repealing all ordinances 
inconsistent herewith. Mr. Deckman and Mr. Hudak were absent.
