Mr. Merhottein called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and led those assembled in 
the Pledge of Allegiance. 
 
 
 
 
Mr. Puleo reported that no work was done since the prior Board of Commissioners’ 
meeting, two weeks ago, and not a word from the developer.  He noted the 
following in his development: 
• BTMA marked for laterals 
• Sinkhole on Victors Way was repaired. 
• Two smaller sinkholes developed but have not been repaired. 
• Fencing around detention ponds have not been done. 
• Curb repair still not finished 
 
He distributed photos to the board and asked them not to grant anymore time 
extensions. 
 
Ms. Grube asked if Christian Spring Road was on the schedule to be repaved.  She 
said she researched what the cost would be to pave the road at $3.10 a foot.  Mr. 
Hudak said it would cost a lot more than that.  He said the road has a dirt base 
which needs replacing.  The road also needs widening but are unsure who owns the 
field.  The township engineer looked at it and said it would cost $3 million for road 
reconstruction.  
 
Mr. Bucherer asked for the noise ordinance to be published in the newsletter. He 
also asked if the police officers were informed about it.  Mr. Merhottein said they 
are aware of the ordinance.    
 
Mr. Krier asked for a status on the tree plantings at River Hill Estates.  Ms. Smith 
said staff is working with them to determine where to place them.  Mr. Krier asked 
for a status on the retaining wall.  Mr. Merhottein said that as of now, they have to 
move the wall. 
 
Ms. Truscott inquired about the realignment of Farmersville Road and 
Freemansburg Avenue project.  Ms. Smith said it was supposed to start the end of 
this year.  Mr. Hudak said he would like to see more concrete numbers.

Mr. Conklin shared the same concerns as Mr. Puleo regarding the work in his 
development and asked if there is a list of work to complete. Ms. Smith said they

Upon motion (Gallagher-Lawrence), the Board of Commissioners unanimously 
voted by voice vote to approve the July 20, 2026 regular public meeting minutes.  
 
 
 
 
 
A MOTION TO EXTEND THE DEVELOPER’S OBLIGATION TO 
COMPLETE IMPROVEMENTS, PURSUANT TO THE 2022 RESOLUTION 
OF APPROVAL, TO SEPTEMBER 19, 2027 FOR THE MOWER ROAD 
LAND DEVELOPMENT PLAN 
 
Upon motion (Hudak-Gallagher) the Board of Commissioners voted unanimously 
by voice vote to table the motion to extend the developer’s obligation to complete 
improvements, pursuant to the 2022 resolution of approval, to September 19, 2027 
for the Mower Road Land Development Plan.  
 
A MOTION TO EXTEND THE DEVELOPER’S OBLIGATION TO

Mr. Roth asked if the money to repair anything damaged comes out of the security.  
Solicitor Broughal said the developer would still be liable. 
 
Upon motion (Hudak-Gallagher), the Board of Commissioners voted unanimously
