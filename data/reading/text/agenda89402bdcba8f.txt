HEARINGS WILL BE HELD IN THE FOLLOWING MATTERS: 
Appeal-2609-0025 – 1307 Good Street (UPI 16531622293350) 
Fairview Charitable Trust is seeking to appeal the Zoning Administrator’s determination to change the use 
to a medical office and a business office use. In the alternative the applicant is seeking a Variance for the 
same and any other relief required to support the project. The property is located in the R-3 Residential 
Zoning District and the Penn’s Common Historic District. The owner of record is the applicant. 
 
Appeal-2609-0026 – 13 S 4th Street (UPI 04530782703040) 
Mary L. Rodriguez is seeking Special Exception approval to use the property as a child care center and any 
other relief required to support the project. The property is located in the C-C Commercial Core Zoning 
District and the Callowhill Historic District. The owner of record is Jeffrey Burkhardt. 
 
Appeal-2609-0027 – 126 Church Street (UPI 07530783810056) 
Trinity Lutheran Church of the City of Reading is seeking a Variance from parking lot design and 
construction standards and required buffer strips and screening to establish a parking lot. The applicant is 
also seeking to appeal the Zoning Administrator’s determination. The property is located in the C-C 
Commercial Core Zoning District and the Callowhill Historic District. The owner of record is the applicant. 
 
Appeal-2609-0028 – 331 Calls Court and 334 Chestnut Street (UPI 05530626781901 and 
05530626780985) 
Inobin G. Guerrero is seeking Special Exception approval to adaptively reuse 331 Calls Court as three 
residential dwelling units. He is also seeking a Variance for habitable area for the residential dwelling units, 
the number of required off-street parking spaces, landscaping requirements for the adjacent parking lot, and 
any other relief required to support the project. The properties are located in the R-3 Residential Zoning 
District. The owners of record are the applicant and Santa Ines Guerrero Polanco. 
 
The properties referenced above are located in the City of Reading, Berks County, Pennsylvania. Complete 
applications and plans may be viewed by contacting the Zoning Office.
