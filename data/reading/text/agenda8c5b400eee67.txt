Admin Code 5-806 D.   Reserve. [Added 12-18-2017 by Ord. No. 108-2017] 
      (1)   The City's objective is to achieve and maintain a structurally balanced budget in all 
funds such that recurring revenues fund recurring expenditures. The City shall maintain a 
reserve containing a minimum of 20% of regular general fund operating expenditures based on 
the annual General Fund budget of City or a minimum of $22,000,000 whichever is higher to 
allow for: 
         (a)   Proper cash flow to fund operations and to allow for monthly contributions to the 
pension fund. 
         (b)   Worker Compensation Fund shall maintain the requirements established by the 
Pennsylvania Worker Compensation Fund plus $750,000 to manage catastrophic injuries. 
         (c)   Insurance Casualty Fund shall maintain a minimum reserve of $1,000,000. 
         (d)   City Healthcare Fund shall develop a reserve of $3,000,000 to allow for unexpectedly 
high claims during the budget year to allow a minimal drawdown on the General Fund. Each 
year any surpluses from the healthcare portion of the budget year shall be assigned to this fund.
