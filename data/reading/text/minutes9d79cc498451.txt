Consent Agenda 
The Consent Agenda is designed to provide efficient approval of non-controversial legislation that does not 
require discussion/debate by giving approval via acclimation when the meeting agenda is approved.  The 
President of Council will call Council’s attention to the list of Consent Agenda legislation at the meeting 
before action is taken, which allows Council to remove a piece of legislation for separate consideration.

I. Award of Contract – to Flyway Construction in the amount of $4,531,000, for the 
construction of the 5,500 sq ft ADA accessible Hillside Splashpad Project with 3 distinct areas 
of play which allow children from very young to experience small splashes and sprays to older 
children who can engage in water “wars” and dump buckets. The project encompasses 
renovation of all current buildings to include the restroom facility, pavilions, office, pump room 
and concession stand. Renovations to the basketball court are also included with the on-grade 
vehicle parking, comprising 37 off-street parking spaces. Families will be able to observe 
children playing from the tiered observation as well as 5 “Party” decks that can be reserved for 
Birthday parties or other gatherings. The facility is designed to be a “for fee” use similar to the 
Schlegel Pool. Working with the Recreation Commission, a nominal fee will be added to the 
Pool membership or purchased alone, will be the basis for utilization of the park. It is 
anticipated to be $10-$15 annually per household up to 4 people. Additional members can be 
added. Admission and Rental fees are solely intended to offset the operations and staffing of 
the park.  
 
J. Award of Contract – to Kistler O’Brien, a CoStars vendor, in the amount of $487,032.58, 
for the design and installation of a sprinkler system at 951 Morgantown Road. The City 
purchased 951 Morgantown Road in 2023 with the anticipation of creating the City’s Public 
Safety Building for the training and operations of the Police and Fire Departments. The full 
project is not expected to be completed in a timely manner; however, the building is in Cumru 
Township and the installation of a sprinkler system is required as per municipal codes. Cumru 
issued the City a temporary occupancy permit but will not allow full occupancy until the 
building is sprinkled for safety reasons. The deadline for the current permit is December 18, 
2026.
