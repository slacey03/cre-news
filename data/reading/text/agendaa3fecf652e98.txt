RECOMMENDATION 
Change the existing Ordinance § 77-103 Qualifications from:

pursuant to 53 Pa.C.S.S § 2161 et seq., out-of-state applicants must contact the Commonwealth of

To: 
 
A. The applicant must hold current certification as a police officer by the Commonwealth of 
Pennsylvania under the Municipal Police Officer Education and Training Commission (MPOETC) 
pursuant to 53 Pa.C.S.S § 2161 et seq., out-of-state applicants must contact the Commonwealth of 
Pennsylvania Municipal Police Officers’ Education and Training Commission and obtain the full 
waiver form, complete the assessment test, and obtain a full training waiver from MPOETC prior to 
appointment. 
 
B. The applicant must have been employed as a police officer or law enforcement agent for not less than 
a year. 
 
BACKGROUND  
An out-of-state applicant who receives a full training waiver can obtain MPOETC certification upon being hired by a 
Pennsylvania police department. However, prior to employment with a Pennsylvania police department, the out-of-
state applicant will not hold MPOETC certification, regardless of their waiver status. 
 
BUDGETARY IMPACT 
No budget impact.

EXHIBIT A 
§ 77-103. Qualifications. 
The Police Civil Service Board shall establish the qualifications for police officer positions to be filled 
without competition that shall include, at minimum: 
    
A. The applicant must hold current certification as a police officer by the Commonwealth of 
Pennsylvania under the Municipal Police Officer Education and Training Commission 
(MPOETC) pursuant to 53 Pa.C.S.S § 2161 et seq., out-of-state applicants must contact the 
Commonwealth of Pennsylvania Municipal Police Officers' Education and Training 
Commission and obtain the full waiver form, complete the assessment test, and obtain 
MPOETC certification prior to appointment, and obtain a full training waiver from MPOETC 
prior to appointment. 
 
B. The applicant must have been employed as a police officer or law enforcement agent for not less 
than a year.
