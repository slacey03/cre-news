Process for Review and Approval of Minutes of Meetings and Public Hearings. 
 
(a) The City Clerk’s Office shall prepare the initial draft of minutes of all committee of the whole 
(COW), committee(s) of Council, regular and special meetings of City Council and all public 
hearings.  The minutes must include the date, time, place, names of members present, 
substance of all official actions, votes cast, and a summary of comments, testimony and 
discussions, as required by § 706 of the Pennsylvania Sunshine Act. Minutes are not intended 
to be a verbatim transcript of the meeting. Minutes shall state actions, procedural events, and 
material summaries in a neutral manner and include a level of detail reasonably necessary to 
document compliance with applicable law, preserve the action taken, and allow the public to 
understand the business conducted, without converting the minutes into a transcript.  
 
(b) Audio or video recordings are supplemental and do not replace the written minutes required 
by law.   
 
(c) The draft minutes from the previous meeting(s) and/or hearing(s) shall be circulated by the 
City Clerk’s Office via email to the Council Members and Council Solicitor for their review prior 
to the next meeting. The minutes shall be reviewed for possible corrections, deletions or 
additions.  
 
(d) Neither Council, as a group, nor individual Council Members shall discuss or deliberate the 
contents of the draft meeting minutes via email or otherwise outside of the public meeting 
forum.   
 
(e) A request for corrections, deletions or additions shall be submitted via email back to the City 
Clerk’s office no later than 1:00 P.M. on the Wednesday prior to the next meeting when the 
subject minutes will be on the agenda for approval. 
 
(f) The City Clerk’s office shall have the discretion to make requested corrections, deletions or 
additions as timely requested by Council Member(s) that are of a clerical nature to address 
typographical errors, procedural inaccuracies or omissions, or similar mistakes but not 
corrections, deletions or additions of a substantive nature.  The City Clerk may consult with 
the Council Solicitor and the Council President in determining whether requested corrections, 
deletions or additions are of a clerical nature or of a substantive nature.   
 
 

 
8 
 
(g) In the event that the requested corrections, deletions or additions are of a substantive nature, 
the City Clerk shall respond in writing via email to inform the requesting Council Member(s) of 
the determination that the requested corrections, deletions or additions will not be included in 
the draft meeting minutes submitted for approval at the next meeting. 
 
(h) At the regular voting meeting of City Council, the draft meeting minutes will be a part of the 
Consent Agenda for approval and any requested corrections, deletions or additions of a 
substantive nature (or clerical if not identified prior to the meeting) may be made by a Council 
Member through a formal motion to amend the meeting minutes in accordance with applicable 
law and meeting procedure.  If corrections are approved, the minutes shall be approved “as 
corrected or amended.” 
 
(i) Corrections to minutes shall be limited to making the minutes accurately reflect what occurred. 
The correction process shall not be used to reconsider, revise, expand, or contradict an action 
previously taken.
