RULES FOR PUBLIC PARTICIPATION AT COUNCIL MEETINGS 
Public comment at all meetings of the City Council of the City of Reading shall be governed by the following: 
• Residents and/or taxpayers of the City who wish to make or submit public comment must pre-register 
utilizing one of three (3) following options: 
1. By calling (610-655-6205) or emailing (council@readingpa.gov) the City Clerk's Office by 4 
pm on the day of the meeting with your name, address and the subject matter or topic to be 
discussed. If you are not able to attend in-person, instructions to access the virtual meeting app 
or dial-in telephone number will be provided. 
2. By submitting written public comment to be read into the record at the meeting by submitting 
an email to council@readingpa.gov by 4pm on the day of the meeting. The email message must 
clearly be marked as Public Comment.  
3. By registering in-person at the Council Chambers on the sign-up sheet found on the podium in 
Council Chambers between 5:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. on the day of the scheduled meeting, with 
your name, address and the subject matter or topic to be discussed.  
• No person shall be permitted to speak who is not a resident or taxpayer of the City, except upon permission 
of the President of Council or a majority of City Council.  
• Public comment will occur only during the Public Comment period listed on the meeting agenda at which 
time the Presiding Officer will recognize speakers and written comment that properly registered in the order 
received.  
• All public comment shall be delivered from the podium and speakers may not approach the Council tables 
at any time. 
• Those individuals registered to speak on agenda matters will be allotted no more than 5 minutes and those 
individuals registered to speak on non-agenda matters will be allotted no more than 3 minutes.  The City 
Clerk will control a timer to monitor these time periods.  When the alarm rings, a speaker must immediately

Part 16:  Off-Street Parking and Loading 
§600-1601: General Application Criteria 
(A) Application:  Off-street parking and/or loading facilities shall be located, designed, constructed and 
maintained in accordance with the provisions of this Zoning Ordinance.  These facilities shall be 
provided whenever the following actions occur: 
(1) A new building has been constructed or a new use has been established in accordance with 
the provisions of this Zoning Ordnance. 
(2) The use of the building or property has been changed, altered or expanded, which requires 
additional off-street parking spaces to accommodate the use. 
(3) An off-street parking facility shall include the driveways, access drives, access isles, parking 
spaces and loading spaces. 
(B) Land Use Compliance:  Parking and loading areas shall be a permitted use as follows: 
(1) Principal Use:  The following off-street parking area and/or off-street loading area 
requirements shall apply if the off-street parking and/or off-street loading area is classified 
as the principal use on the property: 
(a) An off-street parking area containing ten (10) or more parking spaces, which are 
designated as the principal use shall only be permitted provided that such use is 
permitted within the underlying zoning district.  All such off-street parking areas shall 
comply with the lot area, dimensional and coverage requirements, as specified by 
the underlying zoning district. 
(b) An off-street parking area containing less than ten (10) parking spaces, which are 
designated as the principal use shall only be permitted provided that such use is 
permitted within the underlying zoning district.  All such off-street parking areas shall 
comply with: the minimum lot area requirement; the dimensional setback 
requirements may be reduced to three (3) feet from all property lines; and the 
maximum impervious coverage may be increased to 95 percent of the lot area. 
(c) An off-street loading area designated as the principal use shall only be permitted 
provided that such use is permitted within the underlying zoning district.  All such 
off-street loading areas shall comply with the lot area, dimensional and coverage 
requirements, as specified by the underlying zoning district. 
(2) Accessory Use:   An off-street parking area and/or off-street loading area may be permitted 
as an accessory use to a compatible principal use, subject to the provisions specified by 
this Zoning Ordinance. 
(3) Satellite Parking Spaces:  Off-street parking spaces may be established or credited at a 
conforming off-site or off-premises location, subject to the provision specified by this Zoning 
Ordinance. 
(4) On-Street Spaces:  Unless otherwise permitted by the City of Reading, all on-street parking 
spaces and/or on-street loading spaces shall not be counted or credited as a required 
parking or loading space.  All such spaces shall comply with the provisions established by 
the City of Reading Code.  

11 
 
(C) Location:  The following provisions shall apply to the location of off-street parking and/or loading 
areas: 
(1) Unless otherwise permitted, off-street parking and loading spaces shall be located on the 
same lot as the principal use.  
(2) All parking and loading facilities shall be arranged and marked to provide safe and orderly 
movement without disrupting traffic or moving other vehicles. 
(3) Unless otherwise permitted by the Public Works Department, the design of parking areas 
for all uses shall be such to prevent the back-up of vehicles onto a public street and that no 
portion of any parked vehicle extends over any property line. 
§600-1602: Design and Construction Standards 
(A) Accessibility:  All off-street parking and loading spaces shall have access to a public street by 
means of an access lane, driveway, alley or road that complies with the provisions specified by 
Chapters 515 and 600 of the Code. 
(B) Size:  Off-street parking and loading spaces shall comply with the following criteria: 
(1) Off-Street Parking Spaces:  The spaces shall have a rectangular shape that complies with 
following dimensional requirements: 
(a) Within an off-street parking lot, each parking space for motor vehicles (except those 
spaces dedicated for use by handicapped persons) shall have a minimum 
rectangular area of 162 square feet, which shall have a minimum width of 9 feet and 
a minimum length of 18 feet have a minimum area of 162 square feet with a 
minimum width of 9 feet and a minimum length of 18 feet.  Angled spaces will need 
to be longer in order to achieve the rectangular shape with the minimum dimensions. 
(b) Where parallel parking is permitted, the parking spaces shall be delineated by 
painted lines and shall have a minimum width of 8 feet, as measured from the curb 
or cartway edge and a minimum length of 20 feet, as measured along the curb or 
cartway edge. 
(2) Off-Street Loading Spaces:  The spaces shall have a rectangular shape that complies with 
following dimensional requirements: 
(a) Off-street loading spaces with a length of sixty (60) feet, width of fourteen (14) feet, 
and clearance height of fifteen (15) feet for commercial and industrial uses. 
(b) Off-street loading spaces with a length of forty-five (45) feet, width of twelve (12) 
feet, and clearance height of twelve (12) feet may be permitted for commercial, 
institutional and light industrial uses provided the landowner or applicant can 
demonstrate that the use will not require larger trucks to access the site for loading 
and unloading activities.  
(C) Grade:    The off-street parking and loading areas shall be properly graded to provide convenient 
vehicular access and proper drainage and shall be maintained in usable condition.  The minimum 
grade of areas used for parking shall be at least one (1) percent and the maximum grade shall not 
exceed five (5) percent.

comply with the minimum requirements specified in Table 1602-A.

(E) Composition:  All proposed driveways, access lanes, aisles, fire lanes, parking areas and loading 
areas shall be paved with an all-weather surface that is acceptable to City of Reading, considering 
the following requirements: 
(1) Parking lots with less than ten (10) spaces shall be paved with an all-weather surface within 
twelve (12) months from the date of an approved Zoning Permit. 
(2) Parking lots with ten (10) or more spaces shall be paved with an all-weather surface within 
twelve (12) months from the date of the approved Subdivision Plan or Land Development 
Plan, as further specified under Chapter 515 of the Code. 
(3) All loading spaces shall be paved with an all-weather surface within twelve (12) months 
from the date of an approved Zoning Permit. 
(4) Temporary parking or loading areas may be permitted for a period of time that does not 
exceed twelve (12) consecutive months provided that such temporary condition is approved 
by the Public Works Department and that a temporary permit is issued by the Zoning Office.  
The temporary status of the parking or loading area may be extended for an additional 
twelve (12) month period provided that a land development plan is submitted to the Planning 
Commission for review and consideration.   
(5) All temporary or permanent areas designated for parking and loading shall be maintained 
in a safe, accessible, stabilized and mud-free condition, as approved or permitted by the 
Public Works Department or Zoning Office. 
(F) Curbs, Guiderails and Wheel Stops:  The following provisions shall apply: 
(1) The perimeter of the off-street parking and/or loading area shall be curbed for all projects 
exceeding ten (10) or more parking spaces and/or five (5) or more off-street loading spaces. 
(2) The curb radius within the off-street parking area and/or loading area shall be a minimum 
of five (5) feet. 

13 
 
(3) Curbs or wheel stops shall be utilized to define the limits or depth of the off-street parking 
and/or loading area. 
(4) Guiderails and/or retaining walls may be required in order to compensate or protect 
designated areas with topographical relief exceeding 15 percent slope.  The guiderail and/or 
retaining wall shall be subject to the review and approval of the Public Works Department. 
(G) Pedestrian Accessibility and Ramps:    The off-street parking area and/or loading area shall include 
sidewalks, pedestrian paths, stairways and/or access ramps in order to accommodate internal and 
external pedestrian movements.  All such improvements shall be subject to the review and approval 
of the Public Works Department. 
(H) Pavement Markings:  All parking facilities shall be adequately marked and maintained for the 
purpose of defining parking spaces and interior drives.  All pavement markings shall be consistent 
with those utilized by the City of Reading and the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation. 
(I) Signs:  All traffic control signs within the off-street parking area shall be located, designed and 
installed as per the provisions specified by the City of Reading and the Pennsylvania Department 
of Transportation. 
(J) Plan and Permit Requirements:  The following planning and permitting requirements may apply for 
off-street parking and loading areas: 
(1) Zoning Permit:  A zoning permit issued by the Zoning Office shall be issued for all proposed 
off-street parking areas and off-street loading areas. 
(2) Land Development Plan:  A Land Development Plan shall be prepared and submitted for 
site improvements involving ten (10) or more parking spaces and/or five (5) or more off-
street loading spaces.  If the combination of site improvements exceed 2,500 square feet 
of impervious surface or 800 square feet of building surface, a Land Development Plan shall 
be required. 
(3) Stormwater Management Plan:  If the combination of site improvements exceeds 2,500 
square feet of impervious surface, a Stormwater Management Plan shall be prepared and 
submitted with the Land Development Plan. 
(4) Earth Disturbance Activities:  If the combination of site improvements exceeds 5,000 square 
feet of earth disturbance activities, an Erosion and Sedimentation Control Plan shall be 
prepared and submitted with the Land Development Plan. 
(5) Other Permits:  If the proposed site improvements encroach or traverse a street right-of-
way for use and occupancy, a Highway Occupancy Permit (HOP), Driveway Permit and/or 
Utility Permit may be required via the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation and/or 
City of Reading.  
(K) Landscaping and Buffer Yards:  The following provisions shall apply: 
(1) The following buffer yard requirements shall apply to an off-street parking area or off-street 
loading area: 
(a)  A six (6) foot wide buffer strip shall be provided along the perimeter of an off-street 
parking area containing ten (10) or more parking spaces or off-street loading area, 
which is adjacent to an existing residential use or residential zoning district. 

14 
 
(b) A three (3) foot wide buffer strip shall be provided along the perimeter of an off-street 
parking area containing less than ten (10) parking spaces, which is located adjacent 
to an existing residential use or residential zoning district. 
(c) In lieu of a landscaped buffer strip, a four (4) foot high privacy fence may be provided 
along the perimeter of an off-street parking area or an off-street loading area.  The 
privacy fence shall comply with the provisions established under Part 13 of the 
Zoning Ordinance. 
(d) A combination of a landscaped buffer strip and fencing may be considered in order 
to complement or enhance the design. 
(e) The landscaping and buffer enhancements shall not obstruct the required sight 
distance or within the clear sight triangle. 
(2) A 180 square foot curbed or raised landscaped island shall be incorporated into the off-
street parking and/or loading areas in order to create a defined green space at intervals of 
12 consecutive space or 108 foot intervals.  The landscaped island may be designed as a 
rain garden or stormwater management facility. 
(3) The use of existing healthy mature trees (12 inches or more in caliber) is encouraged and 
may be utilized as part of the required buffer yard. 
(4) The landscaping requirements and buffer yard shall be comprised of trees, shrubs and other 
plantings that will establish a complete visual barrier within two (2) years of the initial 
planting. 
(5) The landscaping design of the buffer yard and/or fence shall be subject to the review and 
approval of the Zoning Office. 
(L) Exterior Lighting:  The following provisions shall apply: 
(1) Exterior lighting shall be required for off-street parking areas involving twenty (20) or more 
parking spaces and/or off-street loading areas involving ten (10) or more spaces. 
(2) The illumination levels and spillage impacts shall comply with the exterior lighting provisions 
established by Section 600-912 of the Zoning Ordinance. 
(3) The exterior illumination levels shall be reduced by 50 percent between 10:00 pm and 7:00 
am

§600-1605: Emergency Management and Response Standards 
(A) External Access Points:  All means of ingress and/or egress shall be located, designed and 
constructed in order to accommodate traffic in a safe and efficient manner.  The landowner or 
developer shall be responsible for the purchase and installation of any traffic control devices as 
well as the design and construction of additional travel lanes or auxiliary lanes, which may be 
required by the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation or City of Reading. 
(B) Internal Access Drives and Lanes: The width of aisles within the off-street parking facilities shall 
comply with the minimum requirements established under Part 16 of this Zoning Ordinance.  All 
dead-end parking lots shall be designed to provide sufficient back-up area for all end spaces. 
(C) Emergency Access:  All off-street parking and loading areas shall be designed to consider the 
provisions established under Section 600-906 of this Zoning Ordinance. 
(D) Fire Lanes:  If required by the Fire and Rescue Department or by the Building and Trades 
Department, a fire lane shall be established within the property containing the off-street parking 
area and/or loading area. 
(E) Emergency Response and Accessibility:  The internal access lanes or aisles serving the off-street 
parking area and/or loading area shall be subject to the review and approval of the Fire and Rescue 
Department. 
(F) Sight Distance:  The provisions established under Section 600-1303 of the Zoning Ordinance and 
Section 515-502.F of the Subdivision and Land Development Ordinance shall apply to the 
specifications for sight distance, as required by the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation or 
by the City of Reading Code. 
(G) Clear Sight Triangles:  The provisions established under the provisions established under Section 
600-1303 of the Zoning Ordinance and Section 515-502.F of the Subdivision and Land 
Development Ordinance shall apply to the specification for clear sight triangles. 
§600-1606: Common or Shared Parking Facilities 
(A) Application:  Common or shared parking facilities or areas may be permitted for two (2) or more 
permitted uses, subject to the following requirements: 
(1) The permitted uses are adjacent or adjoining to one another. 
(2) The common off-street parking spaces are capable of accommodating the combined 
parking requirements that are specified by the permitted uses. 

22 
 
(3) The use of a common or shared parking area shall be permitted by the Zoning Office as 
part of a Zoning Permit Application and/or the Planning Commission as part of a Subdivision 
Plan or Land Development Plan. 
(B) Site Accessibility:  The following documents shall be established and recorded: 
(1) Access Easement: The record owners of the properties shall prepare and submit written 
documentation of the continued availability of the shared parking arrangement to the Zoning 
Office, Public Works Department and/or Planning Commission.  The limits of the access 
easement shall consider all pedestrian and vehicular traffic movements between the street 
right-of-way, the permitted uses and the internal common or shared off-street parking 
facility. 
(2) Driveway Easement:  If required, a shared or common driveway easement shall be 
established in order to permit access from the public street to the common or shared off-
street parking area.  
(3) Stormwater Easement:  If required, a stormwater management easement shall be 
established for the use, occupancy and maintenance responsibilities that may pertain to the 
common or shared parking area. 
(4) Utility Easement:  If required, a utilities easement shall be established for the use, 
occupancy and maintenance responsibilities that may pertain to the common or shared 
parking area. 
(5) Legal Description:  All easements shall be accurately described by bearings and distances, 
which are referenced within an agreement and depicted on a plan.  If permitted, a blanket 
easement may be considered as an alternative for the description. 
(C) Modifications or Termination:  The common or shared parking agreement may be modified or 
terminated at the direction of the Zoning Office and/or Public Works Department if the common or 
shared parking facilities should create the following conditions: traffic congestion; traffic hazards; 
overflow parking conditions; and/or threats pedestrian safety. 
(D) Car Pool Parking Facility:   All common parking areas classified as “car pool facilities” shall comply 
with the design requirements established under Part 16 of the Zoning Ordinance.  All such facilities 
shall be subject to the review and approval of the City of Reading. 
(E) Valet Parking Service:  All permitted uses may utilize valet service as a parking option at a 
designated on-site parking space on the property or a designated off-site parking space at a 
permitted public or private parking facility.  All such arrangements shall be subject to the review 
and approval of the Zoning Office.

Zoning Ordinance. 
(B) Drive-through service shall not result in adverse impacts that are associated with the hours of

§600-1608: Electric Vehicle Charging Stations (EVCS) 

23 
 
(A) Electric Vehicle Charging Stations (EVCS) shall be permitted as an accessory or subordinate use 
by right within all zoning districts, subject to the following provision: 
(1) The EVCS shall be located within a building (principal or accessory) occupied by the 
principal use or within the parking or loading area for the principal use. 
(2) The EVCS shall be located at least ten (10) feet from the street right-of-way line and all 
other property lines. 
(3) Parking spaces containing an EVCS shall count towards the minimum required number of 
parking spaces for the principal use(s) contained on the property. 
(4) The EVCS may be leased or utilized to other users during non-operational hours designated 
by the principal use. 
(5) The EVCS shall be clearly marked or identified with signage. 
(6) The EVCS shall be outfitted with a standard EVCS connection or plug-in. 
(7) The EVCS spaces shall not exceed 125 percent of the total number of off-street parking or 
loading spaces required for the proposed use. 
(B) A decommissioning plan and agreement shall be established and endorsed as part of the zoning 
permit for the EVCS. 
§600-1609: Non-Motorized Vehicle Stations (NMVS) 
(A) Non-Motorized Vehicle Stations (ENMVS) shall be permitted as an accessory or subordinate use 
by right within all zoning districts, subject to the following provision: 
(1) The NMVS may be located within a building (principal or accessory) occupied by the 
principal use or within the parking area for the principal use.  Alternative locations shall be 
subject to the review of the Zoning Office. 
(2) The NMVS shall be located at least ten (10) feet from the street right-of-way line and all 
other property lines. 
(3) The NMVS may be located on a space dedicated or reserved for an off-street parking 
provided that 90 percent of the remaining parking spaces remain available for the principal 
use(s) contained on the property. 
(4) The NMVS may be leased or utilized to other users during non-operational hours 
designated by the principal use. 
(5) The NMVS shall be clearly marked or identified with signage. 
(B) A decommissioning plan and agreement shall be established and endorsed as part of the zoning 
permit for the NMVS. 
§600-1610: Public or Private Parking Garages 
(A) Public or private parking garages shall be permitted as follows: 
(1) A public parking garage shall be permitted by right within the Commercial Core (C-C) Zoning 
District. 

24 
 
(2) A public or private parking garage shall be permitted by right within the Manufacturing 
Commercial (M-C), Heavy Manufacturing (H-M) and Municipal (MU) Zoning Districts and 
the Institutional (INS) Overlay District. 
(3) A public or private parking garage shall be permitted by special exception within the 
Commercial Residential (C-R), Commercial Neighborhood (C-N) and Commercial Highway 
(C-H) Zoning Districts. 
(B) Permitted Uses:  The following principal and accessory uses may be contained within the public or 
private parking garage: 
(1) The principal use shall be the public or private parking garage, which shall include the 
driveways, access lanes, parking spaces and all structural components of the garage. 
(2) Additional permitted principal uses may be attached to the public or private parking garage 
provided that such principal uses are permitted by right within the underlying zoning district 
and that the use can be independently operated as a functional principal use with access 
to the street level and parking garage. 
(3) The accessory uses of the public or private parking garage may include the following: 
administrative offices, maintenance facilities; storage facilities; security areas and facilities; 
work stations; toll booths, elevators; stairs; pedestrian crossing areas; vending machines, 
concession stands; snack bars; newsstands; government installations; and/or other similar 
areas.  The total gross floor area for all permitted accessory uses shall not exceed twenty 
(20) percent of the total gross floor area of the public or private garage. 
(C) External Dimensional Requirements:  The following provisions shall apply to a public or private 
parking garage: 
(1) The minimum lot area shall be 20,000 square feet. 
(2) The minimum lot width shall be two hundred (200) feet. 
(3) All uses, buildings and/or structures associated with the garage shall be located at least ten 
(10) feet from the street right-of-way line and all property lines. 
(4) No more than ninety (90) percent of a lot shall be covered by any combination of buildings, 
structures and impervious surfaces. 
(5) Unless otherwise permitted, the maximum height of the garage shall be sixty (60) feet, as 
measured from the average ground elevation to the highest point of the building or structure. 
(6) All other uses and site improvements shall comply with the bulk and height requirements, 
which are further specified by the appropriate zoning district to which the garage is located. 
(D) Internal Dimensional Requirements:  The following provisions shall apply to the aisle width, parking 
space and clearance levels within a public or private parking garage: 
(1) The vehicular aisle or access lanes located within the garage shall comply with the following 
criteria: 
(a) The minimum aisle width shall be at least twenty (20) feet in width in order to 
accommodate two (2) way travel conditions within the garage. 

25 
 
(b) The minimum aisle width shall be at least fourteen (14) feet in width in order to 
accommodate one (1) way travel conditions within the garage. 
(c) The minimum aisle width for entrance and exit lanes shall be a ten (10) feet in order 
to accommodate one (1) way travel, which shall be delineated by curbing or 
markers. 
(2) The individual parking spaces contained within the garage shall comply with the following 
criteria: 
(a) At least 80 percent of the parking spaces shall have a minimum rectangular area of 
162 square feet, which shall have a minimum width of 9 feet and a minimum length 
of 18 feet. 
(b) A maximum 20 percent of the parking spaces may be reduced in order to 
accommodate compact vehicles with a minimum rectangular area of 128 square 
feet, which shall have a minimum width of 8 feet and a minimum length of 16 feet. 
(c) Angled spaces will need to be longer in order to achieve the rectangular shape with 
the minimum dimensions. 
(d) Handicapped parking spaces shall comply with the provisions established under 
Section 600-1603.D of this Zoning Ordinance. 
(3) The clearance level within the garage area shall not be less than eight (8) feet, as 
measured from the parking deck floor to the bottom of any support structure or 
ceiling within all deck levels. 
(4) A public or private parking garage may include a section designated for EVCS 
and/or NMVS, as permitted under Part 16 of this Zoning Ordinance. 
(5) A ten (10) percent reduction may be permitted to the internal dimensional 
requirements specified under Section 600-1610(D) of this Zoning Ordinance 
provided that engineering studies can support the reduction without creating safety 
issues within the garage.  All such reductions may be authorized by the Zoning 
Hearing Board as part of a special exception application. 
(E) General Requirements:  The following general provisions shall also apply to a public or private 
parking garage: 
(1) The garage shall be serviced by public sanitary sewer facilities and public water supply 
facilities. 
(2) All exterior speaker, microphone or intercom systems shall be designed in a manner so that 
the messages, music or other sounds shall be reduced by seventy (70) percent at any street 
line or property line. 
(3) Any booths or other structures utilized for the collection of admission and/or parking fees 
shall be setback and arranged to prevent vehicle backups on adjoining roads during peak 
arrival periods.  Any other collection of fees, such as by roaming parking lot attendants, 
shall be conducted in such a manner as to prevent vehicle backups on adjoining roads. 

26 
 
(4) The landowner and operator of the garage shall be responsible to resolve, mitigate and/or 
correct any traffic congestion problems that occur on public roads, which are related to 
ingress and egress to the garage. 
(5) The use and related activities shall not emit noise, glare, vibration, electrical disturbance, 
electromagnetic interference, dust, smoke, fumes, toxic gas, radiation, heat and/or other 
perceptible or objectionable nuisances that would impact neighboring properties, or be 
noticeable at or beyond the property line. 
(6) The applicant shall furnish evidence that the proposed use will not be detrimental to the use 
of adjoining properties due to hours of operation, noise, light, dust, smoke, litter and 
pollution. 
(7) All points of vehicular ingress and egress shall comply with the provisions established by 
Chapter 515 and 600 of the City of Reading Code. 
(8) Internal circulation shall allow parking surfaces to be level (without any slope) along each 
parking garage facade adjacent to a public street or public space. All ramps between levels 
shall be located along building facades that are not adjacent to a public street or public 
space, or shall be located internally so that they are not visible from adjacent public streets 
or public spaces. 
(9) A pedestrian accessibility plan shall be developed and implemented to include linkages, 
connections and cross-walks within the neighborhood.  
(10) The location of elevators and stairs shall be highlighted through the use of architectural 
features or changes in façade colors, textures, or materials so that visitors can easily identify 
these entry points. 
(11) The interior parking garage lighting shall not produce glaring sources toward adjacent 
properties while providing safe and adequate lighting levels. The use of sensor dimmable 
LEDs and white stained ceilings are recommended to control light levels on-site while 
improving energy efficiency. 
(12) A streetscape enhancement plan shall be developed and implemented in order to consider 
street trees, landscaping and lighting along all public streets.  
(F) Plans and Permits:  All public and private parking garages shall be subject to the following criteria: 
(1) All required site plans, building plans and structural improvements shall be designed and 
sealed by a professional engineer licensed within the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. 
(2) A Land Development Plan shall be submitted to the Planning Commission. 
(3) All plans and permits shall be subject to the review and approval by the Zoning Office, 
Planning Office, Building and Trades Department, Fire and Rescue Department, Public 
Works Department, and Police Department.
