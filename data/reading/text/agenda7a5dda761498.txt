Reading Shade Tree Commission Facts 
The purposes of the Shade Tree Commission are to promote and advance the general 
welfare of the City by: 
• Guiding and regulating the orderly growth and development of vegetation on or 
near public areas in accordance with the Shade Tree Map and with long term 
objectives, principles and standards deemed beneficial to the interest and welfare 
of the people of the City. 
• Protecting the character and the social and economic value of both private and 
public property. 
• Preserving and enhancing the visual character and natural beauty of the City. 
• Restoring, insofar as is possible, a healthy natural environment including but not 
limited to photosynthesis, underground water stability, surface runoff stability, 
the reduction of noise, the filtration of pollutants, such as ozone, sulfur dioxide, 
particulate matter, etc., and the propagation of animal life. 
 
Shade Tree Commission Questions for New Applicants 
1. What is the purpose of the Shade Tree Commission? 
2. Are you aware that the Shade Tree Commission currently meets on the 2nd 
Monday of each month at 4 pm?  Will you be able to regularly attend those 
meetings? 
3. Why are you interested in serving on the Shade Tree Commission? 
4. How does a shade tree program benefit Reading? 
5. Do you have experience with trees and/or landscaping? 
Shade Tree Commission Questions for Reappointments 
1. Are you able to regularly attend meetings? 
2. What does the Shade Tree Commission do well? 
3. In what areas or ways does the Shade Tree Commission need to improve? 
4. Are there any issues that City Council could assist with?

Ethics Board Facts 
The Ethics Board was approved by referendum in the General Election as part of the 
Home Rule Charter. The purpose of the Ethics Board is to hear and decide all cases 
alleging violations of the Code of Ethics. Insofar as permitted by state law the Board 
shall issue advisory opinions, binding opinions, impose penalties and administrative 
fines, refer cases for prosecution, and conduct investigations on its own initiative and 
on referral or complaint. City Council shall appropriate sufficient funds to enable the 
Board to perform the duties assigned to it, including expenses for independent counsel 
and other necessary staff. The Ethics Board must also educate elected and appointed 
officials, employees and the public about the Code of Ethics. 
Ethics Board Questions for New Applicants 
1. What is the purpose of the Ethics Board? 
2. Why are you interested in serving on the Ethics Board? 
3. Why do you think the Ethics Board is needed? 
4. Are you aware of any of the issues the Ethics Board has handled in the past? 
5. Have you read the City’s Code of Ethics? If you could modify one section which 
would it be and why? 
Ethics Board Questions for Reappointments 
1. The Ethics Board currently meets as needed.  Are you able to regularly attend 
those meetings? 
2. What has the Ethics Board done well and what could be improved? 
3. Are there any issues that the City could assist with? 
4. What types of Ethics issues has the Board handled?  And how were they 
resolved? 
5. Explain how the Ethics Board works to educate City officials and the public. 
6. Do you believe the Ethics Board is a valuable tool?  Why or Why not?

Code and License Appeals Board Facts 
Over time the City enacted ordinances creating separate appeals boards for the Housing 
Ordinance, including the Disruptive Conduct Report (DCR), the Solid Waste and 
Recycling Ordinance, the Vending Machine Ordinance, and the Property Maintenance 
Code Ordinance.  The ordinances required three individual citizens and three alternates 
to serve on each individual appeals board. The City could not find and appoint 15 to 30 
individual citizens to serve on these individual appeals boards so the City in 
consultation with the Solicitor’s Office drafted legislation to merge these five (5) appeals 
boards into one Code and License Appeals Board. This omnibus board is now 
composed of the Finance Director, as Chair, a Council member and three individual 
citizens representing the business community, a community group and a citizen at large 
to hear appeals to the Housing Ordinance, the Solid Waste Ordinance, the Solid Waste 
and Recycling Ordinance, the Vending Machine Ordinance and the Property 
Maintenance Code Ordinance. The representative from the business community shall 
be appointed by the Mayor without Council approval.  The Board will meet with the 
City attorney prior to the start of any appeal hearing to explain the appeal process 
under consideration. There is no set meeting schedule for this Appeals Board. 
Code & License Appeals Board Questions for New Applicants 
1. Why are you interested in serving on Code and License Appeals Board? 
2. Have you ever had any violations of the Housing Ordinance, including the 
Disruptive Conduct Report (DCR), the Solid Waste and Recycling Ordinance, the 
Vending Machine Ordinance, and the Property Maintenance Code Ordinance? 
3. What is your opinion on the need for a City appeals board? 
4. Have you had any experience considering testimony and making decisions based 
on the testimony presented? 
Code and License Appeals Board Questions for Reappointments 
1. How many appeal hearings has the Board held over your term.  
2. Do you believe that the process used to conduct the hearing is productive and 
efficient?  What changes would you recommend? 
3. What changes to the board composition would you recommend?

Convention Center Authority Facts 
The Convention Center Authority was formed by Bill No. 16-1996 to build and then 
manage the Santander Convention Center and the SPAC.  This is a 7 member joint 
authority formed with the County Commissioners with 3 members appointed by the 
City, 3 members appointed by the County, and a 7th member who is appointed 
alternatively by City & County. The Authority is responsible for attracting, stimulating, 
developing and expanding business, industry, commerce and tourism in the county 
seat, the surrounding counties and the Commonwealth as a whole.  
 
The Authority oversees the management of the Santander Convention Center and the 
SPAC which includes repayment of debt service, capital maintenance and management, 
along with promoting the convention center to make sure that it serves the needs of the 
community and is financially stable. 
Convention Center Authority Questions for New Applicants 
1. What is the purpose of Convention Center Authority? 
2. The Convention Center Authority currently meets on the 3rd Thursday of each 
month at 7 am.  Will you be able to regularly attend the meetings? 
3. Why are you interested in serving on the Convention Center Authority? 
4. What issues would you like the Authority to address? 
5. Do you have any experience with the management of facilities, finances or in the 
entertainment business? 
Convention Center Authority Questions for Reappointments 
1. Are you able to regularly attend meetings? 
2. What does the Authority do well? 
3. In what areas or ways does the Authority need to improve? 
4. Are there any issues that the City could assist with? 
5. In what direction will the Authority go over the next five years?
