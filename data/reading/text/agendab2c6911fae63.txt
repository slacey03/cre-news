AN ORDINANCE OPENING THE STREETS KNOW AS “KINGS WAY” AND “QUEENS 
COURT” IN THE CITY OF READING,  PENNSYLVANIA, AS PER CITY CODE SECTION 
576-102, AND PA TITLE 11 “CITIES, THIRD CLASS CITY CODE” 
 
 
WHEREAS, the Home Owner’s Association representing the Castlewood Subdivision have 
requested, by petition, the dedication of the streets within that subdivision which is located in the 
Second Ward in the City of Reading, and 
 
WHEREAS, the two streets within the subdivision known as “Kings Way” and “Queens Court” 
(the Streets) are currently private streets, and are not included in the City’s Official 
Topographical Survey of Streets, and 
 
WHEREAS, Public Works and the Administration have coordinated with the Castlewood Home 
Owner’s Association (the HOA) to accept the request for dedication and to ensure that the 
Streets, together with the associated stormwater management system, are acceptable with respect 
to the City’s engineering standards, and 
 
WHEREAS, the HOA has submitted a “Revision to a Plan of Record” to the Planning 
Commission, for their approval, that revises the original subdivision plan to state that the Streets 
will be dedicated to the City, and  
 
WHEREAS, the HOA and Property Owners have agreed in writing to manage and maintain the 
associated stormwater management system consisting of the street side swales, driveway culverts 
and storm basins, and 
 
WHEREAS, the Planning Commission approval of that plan is contingent upon City Council’s 
approval of the opening of these two streets, and 
 
WHEREAS, the Planning Commission, Public Works and the Administration have agreed to 
accept the Streets with respect to City Code 505-502.G.(2), Cul-de-sac streets, based on the 
knowledge that the Streets have been in use without major problems for over 30 years and that 
the Streets otherwise meet the conditions for cul-de-sac streets, and 
 

WHEREAS, the Planning Commission, Public Works and the Administration are recommending 
this action since this will benefit the City, including the potential for increased allocation of 
PennDOT Liquid Fuel Tax revenue, and will help to ensure that the integrity of the streets and 
the associated stormwater management system continues to meet the City’s standards and the 
PA Department of Environmental Protection’s standards, and 
 
WHEREAS, the Planning Commission has recommended that the Streets be officially opened and 
placed on the Official Topographic Survey. 
 
NOW THEREFORE, be it ENACTED and ORDAINED by the City Council of the City of 
Reading, Berks County, Pennsylvania as follows: 
 
SECTION ONE:  The streets known as “Kings Way” and “Queens Court” located within the 
Castlewood subdivision, as shown on the attached drawing, “Exhibit A”, shall be opened as city 
streets. 
 
SECTION TWO:  the attached drawing D-4900416 sheets 1 through 4, noted as “Exhibit A”, 
prepared by Ludgate Associates, together with the digital file of the same (AutoCAD file), are all 
made part of this ordinance.    
 
SECTION THREE:  The Director of the Department of Public Works or the City Engineer shall be 
authorized and directed to enter and record the above-described changes in the official 
Topographical Survey of streets in the Department of Public Works. 
 
SECTION FOUR: This Ordinance shall become effective ten (10) days after its adoption in 
accordance with Sections 219 and 221 of the City of Reading Home Rule Charter.
