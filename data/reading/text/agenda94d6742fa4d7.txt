A N    O R D I N A N C E 
 
CONTINUING THE TEMPORARY MORATORIUM ON THE IMPOSITION OF VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES 
FOR A MOTOR VEHICLE OR CONVEYANCE IN VIOLATION OF PROHIBITED PARKING ZONES ON NORTH 
5th STREET AT THE DOUGLASS AND OLEY STREET INTERSECTIONS AND INTERSECTIONS ON HAMPDEN 
BOULEVARD, WITH THE EXCEPTION OF THE INTERSECTION IN THE SOUTHBOUND LAND AT HAMPDEN 
AND ROBESON WITH NO PARKING SIGNS IN THE CITY OF READING  
WHEREAS, pursuant to the recommendation of the former City Engineer and the Public Works traffic 
engineering consultant, parking restrictions at certain intersection along North 5th Street and Hampden 
Boulevard were expanded in an attempt to improve traffic safety threats by improving line of sight 
distances to provide drivers and pedestrians with better visibility of the oncoming traffic; and 
WHEREAS, new traffic studies were performed and the City’s Traffic Engineering consultants made 
traffic calming recommendations for North 5th Street and Hampden Boulevard to the new City Engineer 
and Public Works Director, and 
WHEREAS, City Council has determined that it is in the best interests of the local residents in the 
impacted areas to extend the moratorium on parking violations and penalties at these locations to 
provide Public Works and the Finance Department with sufficient time to define a financial plan to 
implement the traffic calming recommendations for North 5th Street and Hampden Boulevard. 
NOW THEREFORE THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF READING HEREBY ORDAINS AS FOLLOWS: 
 
SECTION 1. Incorporating the recitals herein. 
SECTION 2. The City of Reading agrees to extend the temporary moratorium on the issuance of 
violations and penalties by the City of Reading Police Department and/or the Reading 
Parking Authority under Section 576-401 et. seq. for vehicles or conveyances parked in 
violation of the “No-Parking” restrictions located at the following intersections: 
• North 5th Street and Oley Street and 
• North 5th Street and Douglass Street 
• Hampden Boulevard and Amity Street, 
• Hampden Boulevard and Perry Street, 
• Hampden Boulevard and College Avenue/Richmond Street, and 
• Hampden Boulevard and Robeson Street, with the exception of the 
southbound lane at Hampden Boulevard and Robeson Street 
 

6 
 
SECTION 3. This temporary moratorium shall remain in effect for a period of six (6) months from the 
effective date unless and until it is earlier terminated or extended by further legislative 
action of City Council. 
 
SECTION 4. This ordinance shall become effective ten (10) days after its adoption, in accordance 
with Sections 219 & 221 of the City of Reading Home Rule Charter.
