October 21, 2026 
5 pm 
Council Chambers / Hybrid Meeting 
 
COUNCIL MEMBERS PRESENT: 
D. Reed, C. Miller, V. Campos, J. Baez – in person and M. Ventura - virtually 
 
OTHERS PRESENT:  
J. Famiglietti, L. Kelleher, J. Gombach, I. Litvinov, M. Adams, J. Batz, M. Sninsky, J. Searfoss, 
M. Simpson, R. Leonard, H. Holthaus (Spotlight) – in person and M. Smith, A. Schreiner, 
BCTV - virtually 
 
The meeting was called to order at 5:04 pm by Ms. Reed.  She stated that Ms. Ventura is 
attending virtually this evening and she will not be chairing this meeting. 
 
I. Budget Review        5:00 – 6:00 pm 
 
• Library 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that revenue line items will remain level in 2027.  He stated that there 
are nine City positions – three management and six AFSCME. 
 
Mr. Gombach requested that Ms. Adams provide Council with an explanation of the Library’s 
structure.  Ms. Adams stated that the Library has a separate board which oversees operations.  
She stated that the library receives State, County, City, and private donations to fund 
operations totaling approximately $3.4 million.  She stated that 12% of revenue must be spent 
on the Library’s collection.  She stated that personnel is the Library’s primary expense. 
 
In response to a question from Mr. Miller regarding staffing at the branch libraries, Ms. Adams 
stated that there are a total of 46 staff and stated that, besides the nine positions listed above, 
these salaries are paid by the Library.   
 
In response to a question from Mr. Miller regarding the cost of repairs to the elevator at the 
main library, Mr. Famiglietti stated that ARPA funds were used for these repairs. 
 
COMMITTEE of the WHOLE 
 
CITY COUNCIL

Ms. Reed thanked Ms. Adams for her work.  Ms. Adams thanked Council for their support. 
 
Ms. Adams left the meeting at this time. 
 
• Fire Department 
o Fire Administration 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that fire prevention permit revenue has increased by $10,000 and that 
there has been an increase in heart and lung reimbursement due to increased activity in these 
line items.  He stated that the false fire alarm fees revenue line item has not been adjusted 
because increased activity due to the new fee amount cannot yet be determined. 
 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that the correct amounts for salaries are $650,469 and for civilian salaries 
is $137,084.  He stated that the 2026 overtime budget is insufficient and will be increased in 
2027 to $12,000.  He stated that the main item in fees is the Parking Authority.  He stated that 
some items contained in the contracted services line item have been relocated to dues and that 
patient care supplies have been moved into the EMS budget. 
 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that the physical fitness line item was not used in 2025 or 2026 and is not 
budgeted in 2027.  He stated that Janitorial Supplies is a new line item and will show supplies 
for fire stations.  He stated that these costs were formerly part of the Public Works budget and 
have been reassigned to Fire.  He explained that Minor Equipment is used for furniture needs 
at the fire stations. 
 
In response to a question from Mr. Miller regarding the Parking Authority fees, Fire Chief 
Leonard stated that the Department pays for parking in a nearby parking garage for fire 
fighters housed at the Plum & Franklin station. 
 
In response to a concern voiced by Mr. Miller regarding the non-use of the physical fitness line 
item, Ms. Simpson explained that in the past the Department had gym memberships at 
Albright College but that is no longer necessary because there is fitness equipment in the fire 
stations. 
 
Ms. Campos requested that Mr. Famiglietti repeat the correct salary line item amounts and he 
did so. 
 
o Preventative Education 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that this division is the Fire Marshal’s office.  He stated that there are 
four positions in the IAFF.  He stated that the overtime budget has been increased in 2027.  He 
explained that some of the items previously within contracted services have been relocated to 
dues and to miscellaneous to be properly categorized.  He stated that general plant supplies 
are budgeted for $12,000 and that there is an error for this line item in the budget binder.  He 

3 
explained that the decrease in minor capital equipment is due to recategorizations and stated 
that the cost for the K-9 is $8,600. 
 
In response to a question from Mr. Miller about the elimination of the disaster recovery 
services line item, Mr. Searfoss stated that this line item was used to purchase supplies during 
COVID but are currently an EMS expense. 
 
o Fire Suppression 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that the damages recovery revenue is directly tied to an expense line 
when insurance pays for damages.  He stated that this division includes 102 fire fighters with 
total wages of $8,674,801.  He stated that the fuel line item has increased to $125,000.  He stated 
that this cost is currently difficult to predict and will continue to be monitored. 
 
In response to a question from Ms. Reed regarding the price of diesel used for this estimate, 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that $6 per gallon was used.  He stated that Council will be asked to 
make 2026 budget adjustments for fuel costs at an upcoming meeting. 
 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that the contracted services line item is for Fire dispatches only at a cost 
of $239,000.  He stated that due to the lease program for apparatus the costs for 
maintenance/repair equipment should decrease.  He explained that uniforms is a new line item 
to differentiate between the clothing allowance, which is included in payroll, and the fund 
used to purchase uniforms.  He stated that minor capital equipment is a new line item for 
consistency. 
 
Ms. Reed stated that the current Department has 102 fire fighters.  She questioned the optimal 
number that Reading should have.  Fire Chief Leonard stated that due to Reading’s 
population, its density, and its high rises, there should be a minimum of 152 fire fighters. 
 
Ms. Reed questioned Ms. Kelleher if the Department has ever had this number of fire fighters.  
Ms. Kelleher stated that there were 144 positions (104 in suppression) in 2010 and 126 positions 
(87 in suppression) in 2011. 
 
In response to an additional question from Mr. Miller regarding the optimal number of fire 
fighters for Reading, Chief Leonard stated that there should be a minimum of 48 fire fighters 
as minimum manning due to the high rises but that Reading currently has only 21. 
 
Mr. Gombach stated that the Reading Fire Department has submitted information to the 
Administration noting that Reading should have four fire fighters per rig but currently has 
two.  He stated that this large increase in personnel was discussed with the IAFF during 
contract negotiations.  He stated that the IAFF acknowledged the increase would come at an 
extreme cost. 
 

4 
Ms. Reed noted the age of the City’s housing stock and that many properties have been 
converted from single family to several apartments.  She stated that Reading has a great Fire 
Department but stated that she would be more comfortable if additional staff could be added.  
She stated that she would consider supporting a tax increase, if necessary, for this funding.  
She noted the recent multiple alarm fires with many residents being displaced and several fire 
fighter injuries.   
 
Mr. Gombach stated that the Administration’s goal is to grow both the Fire and Police 
Departments.  He noted the need to do this responsibly and that he looks forward to future 
conversations. 
 
Mr. Baez thanked the Fire Department for their work.  He stated that as the City continues to 
grow, Council knows that all aspects of the City will also need to grow.  He stated that he finds 
it unfortunate that the City needs four people per apparatus but has only two.  He stated that 
he is not naive and noted the need for all departmental leadership to understand their 
restrictions.  He noted the need for all leadership to ensure that the City is delivering the best 
services that it can provide and considering the residents and the durability of the City’s 
finances.   
 
o EMS 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that the user fees revenue line item will remain at $4 million due to its 
volatility.  He explained that this revenue will be higher than anticipated in 2026 and stated 
that this revenue has been performing well.  He stated that Chief Leonard and Deputy Chief 
Sninsky have been helpful through the budget process in helping him understand how to 
provide the best service to residents. 
 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that EMS includes 36 positions with total wages of $2,859,971.  He 
explained that the collection expense line item is a 5% fee paid to the collection agency for user 
fees collected.   
 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that the EMS insurance reimbursement line item is budgeted at zero in 
2027.  He stated that the costs previously paid from this line item will be paid directly from the 
revenue line item.  He stated that this process is also used in other City departments.  He 
stated that this will also assist with showing the real revenue in that line as well.   
 
In response to a question from Ms. Reed about the patient privacy compliance line item,  
Deputy Chief Sninsky stated that this is related to HIPAA requirements.  He stated that he 
needs continuing education in order to enforce HIPAA regulations. 
 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that the dues line item has increased.  He explained that ESO Solutions 
is an EMS expense that had previously been budgeted as a Fire expense.  He stated that the 
fuel line item has been increased due to increased costs and that medical / surgical supplies 

5 
has also been increased based on trends.  He stated that the dispatching medical line item of 
$233,123 is for the County EMS dispatch fee. 
 
In response to a question from Mr. Miller regarding other EMS services that are no longer 
viable, Chief Leonard stated that the City is planning how to address this and that there have 
been discussions with Mr. Famiglietti and Mr. Gombach.  Mr. Famiglietti added that there is 
one ambulance included in the capital budget annually. 
 
In response to a question from Ms. Kelleher regarding the number of employees that would 
need to be added for an additional ambulance, Deputy Chief Sninsky stated that there are 
currently twelve EMS vacancies.  He stated that there would need to be an additional eight 
employees so that there could be two people on the unit for four platoons.  He stated that 
discussions to add ambulances are underway. 
 
Mr. Gombach reminded Council that contract negotiations with IAFF are underway and that 
some budget amounts may change based on the final agreement.  He stated that Council will 
be updated. 
 
Ms. Reed suggested reviewing the Fire budget again later in the process if needed. 
 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that he can provide a summary to Council. 
 
o Fire Training 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that this division has one employee.  He stated that in the past this 
division’s budget had only one line item and all expenses used that line.  He stated that a new 
budget has been created to better track expenses.  He explained that the advertising line item 
may be renamed to better reflect that this is outreach for CPR trainings for others.  He 
explained that recruit training is the cost of training new fire recruits.  He stated that this 
division’s total budget is $490,403. 
 
Ms. Reed noted Council’s appreciation of the quality of training of the Fire Department.  She 
stated that the training results can be seen and she thanked the Fire Department for their work. 
 
The meeting adjourned at 6:00 pm. 
Respectfully Submitted by 
Linda A. Kelleher, CMC, City Clerk
