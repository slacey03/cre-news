 Planning Commission Facts 
Originally established by the PA Municipalities Planning Code, the Planning 
Commission is charged with administering the Subdivision and Land Development 
Ordinance (SALDO) and making recommendations for approval or denial of projects, 
reviewing and commenting on all Zoning Ordinance amendments and Conditional 
Uses, and preparing the Comprehensive Plan.   
 
Planning Commission Questions for New Applicants 
1. What is the purpose of the Planning Commission? 
2. Why are you interested in serving on the Planning Commission? 
3. The Commission currently meets on the 4th Tuesday of each month at 6:00 pm.  
Will you be able to regularly attend those meetings? 
4. What plan review experience do you have? 
5. What would you like to see the Planning Commission accomplish? 
Planning Commission Questions for Reappointments 
1. Are you able to regularly attend monthly meetings? 
2. What has the Planning Commission done well and what could be improved? 
3. Are there any issues that the City could assist with? 
4. How is the Commission’s relationship with the City Planner and Zoning Offices?   
5. Are there issues that the City needs to address?

Electrician’s Examining Board Facts 
City Council adopted the International Electrical Code and Uniform Construction Code 
to apply to properties in the City.  This is to insure the public health, safety and welfare 
as they are affected by the installation.  These codes govern the maintenance of electrical 
systems and regulates the design, construction, material quality, erection, installation, 
alteration, repair, location, replacement, addition to, use or maintenance of electrical 
systems in the City and provides for the issuance of permits and the collection of fees.  
 
The Electrician’s Examining Board also determines the fitness of applicants for licenses 
and investigates and reports on all proposed suspensions or revocations of licenses, and 
administers tests and investigates complaints against license holders.  
 
New Applicant Questions – Electrician’s Examining Board 
1. What is the purpose of the Electrical Board? 
2. Why are you interested in serving on the Electrical Board? 
3. The Electrical Board meets quarterly at 4:30 pm.  Are you willing to dedicate time 
and effort to the duties of this Board? 
4. What would you like to see the Electrical Board accomplish? 
5. Are you aware of any issues/problems with Electrical installations that need to be 
addressed? 
Reappointment Questions – Electrician’s Examining Board 
1. Are you able to attend meetings consistently? 
2. What issues has the Board considered during your term? 
3. What has the Board done well and what could be improved? 
4. Are there any issues that the City could assist with? 
5. Are there any enforcement problems that you have become aware of?

Ethics Board Facts 
The Ethics Board was approved by referendum in the General Election as part of the 
Home Rule Charter. The purpose of the Ethics Board is to hear and decide all cases 
alleging violations of the Code of Ethics. Insofar as permitted by state law the Board 
shall issue advisory opinions, binding opinions, impose penalties and administrative 
fines, refer cases for prosecution, and conduct investigations on its own initiative and 
on referral or complaint. City Council shall appropriate sufficient funds to enable the 
Board to perform the duties assigned to it, including expenses for independent counsel 
and other necessary staff. The Ethics Board must also educate elected and appointed 
officials, employees and the public about the Code of Ethics. 
Ethics Board Questions for New Applicants 
1. What is the purpose of the Ethics Board? 
2. Why are you interested in serving on the Ethics Board? 
3. Why do you think the Ethics Board is needed? 
4. Are you aware of any of the issues the Ethics Board has handled in the past? 
5. Have you read the City’s Code of Ethics? If you could modify one section which 
would it be and why? 
Ethics Board Questions for Reappointments 
1. The Ethics Board currently meets as needed.  Are you able to regularly attend 
those meetings? 
2. What has the Ethics Board done well and what could be improved? 
3. Are there any issues that the City could assist with? 
4. What types of Ethics issues has the Board handled?  And how were they 
resolved? 
5. Explain how the Ethics Board works to educate City officials and the public. 
6. Do you believe the Ethics Board is a valuable tool?  Why or Why not?
