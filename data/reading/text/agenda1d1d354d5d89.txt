City Council 
Nominations and Appointments Committee 
Monday August 17, 2026 
Meeting Report 
 
 
Attendance: C. Miller, V. Campos, J. Baez (in person) 
 
Others Attending: M. Smith, B. Rivera (in person) and BCTV (virtually) 
 
The meeting was called to order at 4:00 pm by Mr. Miller. 
 
Mr. Miller introduced himself, Ms. Campos, and Mr. Baez.  He stated that there are two 
interviews for this meeting; one is for an appointment and the other for a reappointment.   
 
I. Interviews 
• Alex Roche – appointment – Reading Housing Authority 
Mr. Miller greeted Mr. Roche and briefly explained the interview process.  Mr. Miller questioned 
Mr. Roche’s interest in serving as a member of the Reading Housing Authority (RHA).  Mr. 
Roche stated that he had been a long-time resident of Reading and an involved citizen.  He stated 
that he knew former Council member and President, Jeff Waltman, as they were neighbors.  He 
stated that he had moved out of the area for a brief period and returned as he always expected he 
would.  
 
Mr. Roche expressed his belief that if you do not become a part of the process, you also do not 
become a part of the change.  He stated that he works with the Greater Reading Chamber 
Alliance (GRCA) making economic development changes.  He stated that at the onset of his 
career 10 years ago, he worked with the Penn Street Market.  He stated that he also worked with 
the RHA, in particular working with Mr. Knockstead as part of a community engagement team.  
He noted that all major issues such as food or clothing are directly related to housing.  He stated 
that when he saw that there was a vacancy, he saw the opportunity to help those in need and 
eagerly applied for the position.   
 
Mr. Miller questioned how Mr. Roche intends to balance this appointment with his existing work 
at GRCA.  Mr. Roche stated that his appointment to the RHA would allow him to continue his 
work, explaining that our nation is in a housing crisis and this directly relates to economic 
development.  He briefly described what a well-balanced urban area should look like and what 
steps should be taken to positively achieve that.  He stated that urban areas should possess public 
housing, market housing, and subsidized housing in balanced forms to function ideally.  He 
stated that there is a housing advocacy role that he plays in collaborating with others through his 
work at GRCA.    

2 
 
Mr. Baez commended Mr. Roche for his interest in serving on the Housing Authority and his 
passion for what he does.  He questioned what Mr. Roche believes are the pros and cons 
regarding the current functions of the RHA and through his experience of working at GRCA, 
what progress can be made to improve the cons.   
 
Mr. Roche stated that RHA has many effective programs in place such as Helping Harvest.  He 
stated that he can appreciate that along with being in public housing, there are a host of other 
needs that can be serviced such as access to food and resources that can be utilized to fit almost 
every need.  He mentioned that the senior community comprises a large portion of people in 
public housing.  He stated that RHA assists the elderly by informing them of potential scammers 
that prey on their vulnerability and so much more. He stated that if anything could be improved, 
it may be community engagement and awareness.  He stated that even if you are not a part of 
public housing, there are certain things that should be common knowledge or at the very least, 
some awareness of the resources available for the many needs of those in public housing. He also 
mentioned educating people who relate public housing to transitional housing as they are not the 
same. 
 
Ms. Campos greeted Mr. Roche and stated that she appreciates his interest.  She stated that she is  
very impressed with the background information mentioned in his application as there are many 
strengths he brings to the table with his extensive volunteerism.  She questioned how this list and 
his background will assist in his being a member of RHA. 
 
Mr. Roche stated that being a board member would require him to learn more about the City and 
he openly welcomes growth.  He stated that he has long been an active volunteer and really 
wants to work alongside other members of RHA who have the same vision of improving our 
communities. 
 
Mr. Roche stated that he is very excited about the opportunity to serve the City in this capacity 
and looks forward to the work if he is appointed.  
 
Mr. Miller stated that he appreciated Mr. Roche’s statement about needing to be part of the 
process to be a positive part of the change.  He stated that if everyone had that same attitude, we 
would be living in a much-improved world. 
 
Mr. Miller thanked Mr. Roche for appearing in person for his interview and explained that Ms. 
Smith would be in contact with him to inform him of his appointment.   
 
Mr. Roche left the meeting. 
 
Mr. Miller stated that Mr. Roche appears to be a fine candidate to move forward to Council.  He 
stated that he has had some experience with RHA which is a benefit and he is very enthusiastic; 
he recommends his appointment. 

3 
Mr. Baez agreed with Mr. Miller stating that he has an infectious energy to serve.  He  mentioned 
how Mr. Roche started  becoming active in the community at an early age and that may have 
something to do with Mr. Waltman planting the seed to begin picking up where others left off.  
He recommended his appointment.  
 
Ms. Campos agreed with Mr. Miller and Mr. Baez stating that Mr. Roche’s responses to the 
questions posed were very thorough and everything on his background that he listed impressed 
her.  She stated that she is looking forward to his participation as a board member.  She stated 
that she recommended his appointment.  
 
The Nominations and Appointments Committee recommends the appointment of Alex Roche 
to the Reading Housing Authority. 
 
• James Schlegel – reappointment – First Energy Stadium Commission  
Mr. Miller greeted Mr. Schlegel and questioned what the Stadium Commission has done well 
during his tenure and what improvements can be made moving forward.   
 
Mr. Schlegel stated that when he began serving on this Commission, Ms. Milch was the 
chairperson, and he assisted her, along with the help of Ms. Kelleher, in creating procedures and 
bylaws for the Commission’s functions to be more professional and productive.  He stated that 
they also reduced the number of members from nine to five in keeping the business of the 
Commission more efficient.   
 
Mr. Schlegel stated that during his tenure there was a time when there was a real threat of losing 
the Reading Phillies to another city.  The Commission got through it with the exceptional 
leadership of Mr. Hunzinger who never conceded to do what needed to be done for that not to 
happen.  He stated that beyond Mr. Hunzinger’s determination in keeping the Stadium 
flourishing, he was amazed and humbled by the amount of support received by so many, 
including the City, for the vision that required the much-needed upgrades.   He stated that it has 
taken some time to make the renovations necessary, but he is of the opinion that it has been well 
worth it.  He is proud of the Stadium and how it brings many spectators from near and far to 
enjoy what it has to offer.   
 
Mr. Schlegel stated that he cannot think of anything negative about the Commission.  He stated 
that there are very committed members who all have the same passion for the work of the 
Commission and rarely, if ever, is there a member absent at a meeting.   
 
Mr. Schlegel stressed the importance of having supportive entities such as the City of Reading to 
continue to provide funds for the many projects and initiatives the Stadium has and continues to 
offer the public.  He noted that he wanted to continue his work on the Commission because he 
feels passionate about the work it has been doing and he would like to see that continue.   
 

4 
Mr. Schlegel spoke briefly about the purchase of the former bank property and what possible 
plans that area could be utilized for.   
 
Ms. Campos greeted Mr. Schlegel and stated how appreciative she feels to have a Stadium 
member with such enthusiasm and passion.  She questioned what Mr. Schlegel would like to see 
a possible new chairman do in the next five years.   
 
Mr. Schlegel stated that there are many ideas he and the other board members have for the 
stadium, but for these ideas to come to fruition, funding is essential.  He briefly spoke about 
putting a parking lot in the area of the bank for handicapped patrons which would be shuttled to 
the park.  He stated that could cost somewhere in the vicinity of $1M to implement. He stated 
that much-needed renovations to both men’s and women’s restrooms in a portion of the stadium 
are a priority as that area has been placed on hold for far too long.  He stated that a new roof is 
needed on the grandstand.  He stated that a new sign on Centre Avenue is needed as the old one 
does not function effectively and is quite dated.  He mentioned how many people come from 
many different places to enjoy the Reading Stadium and that he is proud to offer so many fun 
activities for all to really enjoy while at the stadium. 
 
Mr. Baez wished Mr. Schlegel a happy belated birthday and thanked him for all the work he has 
put into being on the Commission over the years.  He stated that he was impressed with the 
information on his application and his extensive background in the transportation field.  
 
Mr. Baez questioned how Mr. Schlegel would protect the City’s interest in ensuring transparency 
under Diamond Baseball’s ownership.  Mr. Schlegel stated that the Commission has truly little 
power over Diamond Baseball’s ownership as the Commission is the landlord.  He stated that 
Mr. Hunzinger assured the Commission that their ownership was the correct thing to do and that 
nothing was to change on a drastic level.   
 
Mr. Schlegel stated that he appreciates all the support that the City offers the Stadium, including 
their presence at opening day.   
 
Mr. Miller commented on how impressed he is that the Stadium leadership makes such an effort 
to invite organizations, both nonprofit and for profit. 
 
Mr. Schlegel left the meeting at this time. 
 
Mr. Miller stated that Mr. Schlegel’s responses were very thorough.  He stated that he 
understands why Mr. Schlegel is so interested in being reappointed as he’s seen many things 
happen, both positive and negative, during his tenure and he wants the Commission to keep 
progressing.   He also noted his understanding of the statement that the Commission is a 
landlord which means keeping the team happy and the spectators happy is extremely important; 
hence, the maintenance of the stadium is of high priority.  He stated that he recommended Mr. 
Schlegel’s reappointment. 

5 
 
Ms. Campos stated that she is in favor of recommending Mr. Schlegel be reappointed noting that 
his commitment is obvious as he is so passionate about his work on the Commission.  She stated 
that those who have been reappointed to the Stadium Commission have all spoken very highly of 
Mr. Schlegel so it is clear that he is appreciated in his leadership role and the work he provides on 
the Commission.   
 
Mr. Baez stated that Mr. Schlegel was extremely thorough in all of his responses and providing 
more information and insight than was asked.  He stated that he is 100% in favor of his 
reappointment. 
 
The Nominations and Appointments Committee recommends the reappointment of James 
Schlegel to the First Energy Stadium Commission.
