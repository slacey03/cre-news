EXHIBIT A 
 
Chapter 431 
PROPERTY SALES 
Part 2 
Property Transfer Inspections 
 
§ 431-204.  Property Transfer inspection procedure. 
  A. Property Transfer inspection shall be performed prior to any settlement or transfer of property. 
(1) The seller of any property shall submit an application for inspection to the City of Reading Property 
Maintenance Division no later than 30 days prior to the anticipated settlement or transfer of the property. Applications 
received less than 15 days prior to settlement will include a “rush” fee pursuant to Chapter 212, Fees. 2  The Property 
Maintenance Division will, without unreasonable delay, inform the seller of the date, time and cost of the inspection as 
per the City of Reading Fee Schedule.2 Failure to comply with the Property Transfer Inspection requirement prior to 
settlement or transfer of a property may subject the seller to penalties, including but not limited to fines and additional 
fees.  

 
Page6 
(2) Upon successful completion of the Property Transfer Inspection, a Property Transfer Compliance Letter shall 
be issued which shall be valid for 12 months. If the property has not transferred to a new owner after 12 months of the 
date of the issuance of the Property Transfer Compliance Letter, any property that remains for sale while owned by the 
original applicant, shall undergo a new Property Transfer Inspection, the fee for which will be half the fee of the first 
inspection. 
 
   B. If the Property Transfer Inspection is not completed prior to settlement or transfer of a property in accordance 
with this Chapter, the Property Maintenance Division shall, within 15 business days of notification from the Recorder 
of Deeds, notify the new owner of the Property Transfer Inspection. The Property Maintenance Division will, without 
unreasonable delay, inform the Owner of the date, time and cost of the inspection, in addition to any penalties imposed 
for failure to comply with this Chapter may be provided in Chapter 212, Fees. 2   
C.  In addition to the Property Transfer Inspection that is required prior to the transfer or sale of any property, any 
owner may request a Property Transfer Inspection at any time by submitting an application for a Property Transfer 
Inspection to the City of Reading Property Maintenance Division. The Property Maintenance Division will, without 
unreasonable delay, inform the owner of the date, time and cost of the inspection as per the City of Reading Fee 
Schedule. 2 
D. An inspection of the property shall be performed and billed to determine compliance with minimum standards and 
requirements as per the current allowable use. The fee includes the costs of the original inspection and one re-
inspection within 12 months of the first inspection, but further re-inspections will be billed according to the City of 
Reading Fee Schedule.  
E. The standards to be applied in a Property Transfer inspection are as follows:  
      (1)   Each dwelling unit must have an operational (working) smoke detector installed in all common areas, 
including the basement, every designated bedroom and every finished attic space as required by the Property 
Maintenance Code. 
      (2)   Each dwelling unit must meet fire standards and have sufficient egress as per the Property Maintenance Code. 
      (3)   Each dwelling unit must have an existing acceptable 60 ampere service, or a minimum 100 ampere three-wire 
electric service must be installed for the dwelling as required by the Property Maintenance Code. 
      (4)   Each dwelling unit must have kitchen countertop receptacles, laundry and bathroom receptacles must be 
ground-fault-circuit-interrupter protected, as required by Property Maintenance Code. 
      (5)   No basement or attic may be used for habitable bedroom units except by authorization, approval or variance of 
the City Zoning Hearing Board and shall comply with the requirements of the Property Maintenance Code. 
      (6)   All properties must be supplied with clearly identifiable numbers (minimum of three inches tall) outside the 
property, in clear view of the street, designating the street number of the property as required by the Property 
Maintenance Code. 
      (7)   The property must have a sufficient number of storage containers for solid waste. 
      (8)   The exterior of the property shall be in compliance with the Property Maintenance Code, including but not 
limited to abutting sidewalks and alleyways.  
      (9)   All properties must be free from infestation of insects or vermin as required by the Property Maintenance 
Code. 
      (10)   All properties must have the proper permits, licenses and zoning permits if being used for anything other 
than single-family owner-occupied purposes. 
      (11)   All sidewalks, walkways, stairs, driveways, parking spaces and similar areas shall be kept in a proper state of 
repair, and maintained free from hazardous conditions as required by the Property Maintenance Code. 
      (12)   All foundation walls shall be maintained plumb and free from open cracks and breaks and shall be kept in 
such condition so as to prevent the entry of rodents and other pests as required by the Property Maintenance Code. 
      (13)   Every exterior stairway, deck, porch and balcony, and all appurtenances attached thereto, shall be maintained 
structurally sound, in good repair, with proper anchorage and capable of supporting the imposed loads as required by 
the Property Maintenance Code. 
      (14)   There shall be no illegal sewer/water connections, as defined under the applicable City policies. 
      (15)   Every handrail and guard shall be firmly fastened and capable of supporting normally imposed loads and 
shall be maintained in good condition as required by the Property Maintenance Code. 

 
Page7 
D. F  Compliance with these minimum standards shall not be construed as compliance with any other applicable 
standards, ordinances or laws, and shall not prohibit other appropriate inspections by the City or the issuance of 
citations for other issues with the property that may be identified by the inspector during the inspection. 
E. G. The Property Maintenance Inspector performing the inspection has the right to request assistance from another 
inspector or department of the City of Reading, including but not limited to Building and Trades, Fire Marshal, 
Zoning, or other City personnel authorized to enforce City codes. In evaluating the inspection, the inspector has the 
right to review records of other City departments or divisions. 
 F. H. At any time after the receipt of the Property Transfer Inspection Report, the Property Maintenance Division may 
undertake such further actions or inspections as it deems appropriate consistent with the applicable ordinances of the 
City of Reading, including, but not limited to, a full inspection for any applicable codes compliance and/or the 
issuance of a notice of violation based upon the Property Transfer Inspection report to repair or remediate or otherwise 
correct any deficiencies indicated in the Property Transfer inspection report within such time as may be set forth in the 
notice of violation. 
  G.  I.  Failure to appear for inspection. If the seller/owner or authorized agent cannot be available at the proposed 
time, said seller/owner or authorized agent shall provide no less than 24 hour written notice to the Property 
Maintenance Division. Upon failure to give such written notice or upon failure to gain entry, an administrative fee will 
be assessed against the owner failing to supply written notice to appear. If the seller/owner or authorized agent fails to 
appear for the second scheduled inspection, an administrative fee will be assessed against the property owner. If the 
seller/owner or authorized agent fails to appear for the third scheduled inspection, the Property Maintenance Division 
shall assess an administrative fee and may placard the subject property and/or promptly seek an administrative search 
or inspection warrant from a court of competent jurisdiction by supplying all necessary affidavits and testimony in 
support thereof. Refer to the City of Reading Fee Schedule for appropriate fees. 3 
2.   Editor's Note: See Ch. 212, Fees. 
3.   Editor's Note: See Ch. 212, Fees.
