PROCLAMATIONS AND COMMENDATIONS. 
 
Citizens, businesses, and organizations can be honored with (i) a Proclamation or Commendation 
at Council Meetings or by Councilors at events, or (ii) letter(s) of congratulations or recognition 
delivered directly to the recipient.   Staff from the City Clerk’s office can prepare 
Proclamations, Commendations, and Certificates of Recognition to assist Council to honor 
people, businesses, events, etc. in accordance with this Policy 
 
PROCLAMATIONS - Proclamations are formal public statements or announcements and are 
generally issued by the Mayor.  (I.E. Proclaiming the month of February to be Black History 
Month).  Proclamations can also be issued by Council, as a whole body, if they are adopted by 
resolution at a public meeting. 
 
COMMENDATIONS - Commendations are official awards given to show high approval or 
expressing favorable opinion and are generally given by individual Council member(s) to 
persons or entities within their respective Council district.  Proclamations  Commendations 
can also be issued by Council, as a whole body, if they are adopted by resolution at a public 
meeting. All commendations are signed by the Council President and can include the name of 
the councilor making the request - for very significant events all seven members of Council 
can be included.  This category of commendations also includes “certificates of commendation 
or recognition” which are less formal and can be signed by the individual councilor making 
the request provided that it otherwise complies with this policy.  A certificate of recognition 
is a tangible acknowledgment of someone’s effort, accomplishment, or positive behavior in a 
particular context vs a commendation or proclamation which is a formal recognition of 
someone’s lifetime or career achievements 
 
• Council staff keeps a schedule of Commendations and Proclamations that are regularly 
presented by Council or individual Council member(s). 
• Staff from the City Clerk’s office can easily prepare Proclamations and Commendations 
others to help you Council honor people, businesses, events, etc. in accordance with this 
Policy.   

 
 
LETTERS OF CONGRATULATIONS OR RECOGNITION –  
 Council staff from the City Clerk’s office will help a Council member or Council, as a 
whole body, to prepare letters that relate to City or Individual Council District issues that 
require intervention by the State, Federal or other governmental entities (I.E. letter to the State 
Attorney General to address potential criminal or civil matters outside the jurisdiction of the 
City).   If a district councilor reports or requests complaint a matter to be handled to Council 
Staff that is outside his/her district to address hereunder, he/she must copy the district 
councilor on the email and be subject to item #2 below. As a safety net, Council Staff shall 
include both Councilors on all responses. 
 Letters or notes that provide congratulations, express support, express sympathy, 
recognition, etc. shall be written by the Councilor individually on letterhead or on a City 
notecard.  Each Councilor shall be provided with a batch of notecards, refills are available, and 
individual letterhead for his/her own individual use in accordance with this policy.   
 Requested letters of support for grant funding or other financial support must be 
approved and signed by the Council President. 
REQUESTS FOR PROCLAMATIONS, COMMENDATIONS OR LETTERS HEREUNDER 
SHALL COMPLY WITH THE FOLLOWING: 
 
1. Submissions or requests from Council member(s) requiring assistance from 
Council Staff must be sent to the City Clerk first, who can then determine who 
the assignment will be directed to. 
 
2. If an individual Council member requests a proclamation, commendation or 
letter hereunder regarding a matter, issue, person or entity that is outside the 
requesting Councilor’s district, he/she must refer the issue to the proper 
Councilor first who can then join in the matter and direct the City Clerk staff, 
if appropriate, to issue the request in the individual or both Councilors’ 
names.   
 
3. City postage can be used for individual District-event related correspondence 
or correspondence that provides Council, as a whole body, support.  But, City 
postage cannot be used for letters or notes that provide congratulations, 
express support, express sympathy, etc. – shall be written by the Councilor 
individually on letterhead or on a City notecard 
 

 
4. Requests hereunder must be submitted via email to the City Clerk at least 7 
business calendar days in advance of the date needed for the proclamation, 
commendation or letter.  The City Clerk’s office will use its reasonable best 
efforts to address requests with lesser advance notice but there is no guarantee 
that it can be timely accommodated.
