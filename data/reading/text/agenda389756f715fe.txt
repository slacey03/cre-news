REQUESTED ACTION:     Council’s approval for a 2026 Agency Fund Budget amendment to recognize the award of a 
grant from the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania through the Office of Community and Economic Development.  
 
RECOMMENDATION: 
The City Finance Director recommends the above budget amendment to the 2026 Agency Fund Budget.

The City Of Reading received a grant from the Department of Community and Economic Development to support the 
final phase of construction for the Reading Skate Park.  The award amount is $183,600 with a cash match requirement of 
$26,400 which will be paid utilizing project savings from the Capital Improvements Fund.  A budget amendment is needed 
for the dept to recognize this award and utilize the funds.  
 
BUDGETARY IMPACT 
The authorization will amend the 2026 Agency Fund budget by increasing the expense account 310771-42160, Contracted 
Services, $183,600, also increasing the revenue account 310771-35540 Grants and Gifts, $183,600. 
 
PREVIOUS ACTIONS 
None 
 
SUBSEQUENT ACTION 
Council to take action to approve an ordinance for the authorization to amend the 2026 Agency Fund budget as described 
above.

Finance Director

Council to approve the Healthy Homes Program Specialist position    
                         
RECOMMENDATION 
The Community Development Department is recommending the creation of the Healthy Homes Program 
Specialist.  
 
BACKGROUND 
The City of Reading was awarded a $1,296,998 grant from the HUD Office of Lead Hazard Control and Healthy 
Homes.  This grant provides funding to help remediate current hazardous conditions in residencies throughout 
the City of Reading. The Healthy Homes Program Specialist position will be responsible for conducting 
assessments, environmental reviews, radon testing, site monitoring and compliance activities. 
BUDGETARY IMPACT 
This full-time position is funded primarily by the HUD Office of Lead Hazard Control and Healthy Homes 
grant, with a 10% match from the General Fund out of CD Property Maintenance.  The hourly rate for this 
position is $25.78.

None 
 
SUBSEQUENT ACTION 
Council to take action to approve the job posting.

The Managing Director recommends approval.

AMENDING THE 2026 POSITION ORDINANCE TO CREATE THE HEALTHY HOMES PROGRAM 
SPECIALIST IN THE COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT DEPARTMENT. 
 
Whereas, the City of Reading was awarded a $1,296,998 the HUD Office of Lead Hazard Control and 
Healthy Homes; and  
Whereas, said grant was awarded to help remediate current hazardous conditions in residences 
throughout the City of Reading.  The Healthy Homes Program Specialist position will be responsible for 
conducting assessments, environmental reviews, radon testing, site monitoring and compliance 
activities; and 
Whereas, said grant provided for the hiring a full-time Specialist to perform the above mentioned 
duties; and 
Whereas, this position will be a Union position, covered under the AFSCME 2763 Collective 
Bargaining Agreement, with an hourly rate of $25.78, and this position will be eliminated upon 
expiration of the grant funding; and 
Whereas, the Community Development Department and the City of Reading Administration Office are 
in support of this position; and, 
 
THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF READING HEREBY ORDAINS AS FOLLOWS:  
SECTION 1.  Creating a position of Healthy Homes Program Specialist in the Office of Community 
Development; 
Section 2.  The budgetary impact of the additional position will be minimal as there will be a 10% 
match from the General Fund out of CD Property Maintenance, and the position will be eliminated 
upon expiration of the grant funding. 
Section 3.  All Ordinances or parts of Ordinances that are inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed. 
 
Section 4.  This ordinance shall be effective ten (10) days after its adoption in accordance with 
Sections 219 and 221 of the City of Reading Home Rule Center. 
 
Enacted on_____________________, 2026
