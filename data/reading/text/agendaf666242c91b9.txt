D.   Unnecessary/needless fire and medical alarm responses. 

 
Page24 
      (1)   Whereas, the Department of Fire and Rescue Services reserves the right to bill for 
nonemergency and emergency calls. The determination of "Needless Alarms" is in accordance 
with the language below and the opinion of the Fire Code Official. 
      (2)   Needless alarms/false alarms: The City of Reading - Department of Fire & Rescue 
Services reserves the right to charge for response(s) to calls resulting from the repeated 
activation of fire and medical alarm systems due to causes other than smoke or fire or the 
intended use of the fire alarm or medical alarm system. Such cases shall include, but are not 
limited to the following: 
 
1. Needless alarms/false alarms: The City of Reading – Department of Fire & Rescue 
Services reserves the right to charge for response(s) to calls resulting from the activation of 
fire and medical alarm systems due to causes other than smoke or fire or the intended use 
of the fire alarm or medical alarm system.  
 
2. Any person determined of using an alarm system, which reports or causes to report more 
than five (5) false alarms within the calendar year shall be required to have the fire alarm 
system tested by a Nationally Recognized Testing Laboratory (UL, FM or Intertek) 
 
Such cases shall include, but are not limited to the following: 
         (a)   Alarm activation for no apparent cause. 
         (b)   Alarms resulting from failure to properly maintain or repair a fire alarm system. 
         (c)   Failure to protect devices from dust, dirt, and/or fumes as a result of construction or 
hot work, fumigation or similar conditions. 
         (d)   Failure to use proper exhaust or ventilation equipment to prevent activation of 
detection devices by cooking, smoke, steam, fumes, etc. 
         (e)   Intentional activation of alarm when no emergency exists. 
         (f)   Intentional (When no emergency exists) activation of the medical alarm system. 
         (g)   Defective medical alarm system activating without cause requiring responses by the 
Department. 
 
3.  The fee for a false alarm activation is $400 per occurrence. Each day, or portion of a day, 
violation of any provision of this ordinance shall constitute a separate offense.

REQUESTED ACTION: Authorize the Transfer of Funds within 2026 Budget 
 
RECOMMENDATION 
 
The Fire Chief requests the approval of the transfer of funds in the 2026 Budget totaling $30,200.00 
within the Suppression Division of the Fire Department budget. 
 
$30,200.00 from 10934-47550 - Repairs to Damaged Property to be transferred to 010934-44002 
Maintenance and Repair/Equipment 
 
 
BACKGROUND 
There is a need for the transfer of funds to cover costs for an emergency repair to Engine 9, 
a front-line apparatus.    
 
BUDGETARY IMPACT  
None to the overall Fire Department budget with respect to the transferred amounts from 
within the Department.  
 
PREVIOUS ACTIONS  
None 
 
SUBSEQUENT ACTION 
Council to take action to approve an ordinance authorizing the transfer of funds 
 
RECOMMENDED BY 
The Fire Chief recommends approval. 
 
RECOMMENDED MOTION 
Approval of the transfer of the funds as listed.

AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 2026 GENERAL FUND BUDGET  
TO REFLECT THE TRANSFER OF FUNDS WITHIN THE FIRE DEPARTMENT BUDGET PROVIDE 
THE NECESSARY FUNDING FOR APPARATUS REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE FOR THE FIRE 
DEPARTMENT.  
 
The Council of the City of Reading hereby ordains as follows: 
 
Section One: The 2026 General Fund Budget Ordinance is hereby amended by changing the 
ordinance to provide the funds needed to pay for an emergency repair to a 
frontline apparatus (Engine 9).  The funds will be paid by reallocation of funds 
within the Fire Department’s budgeted funds. 
 
Section Two: Repairs will be paid out of Maintenance/Repair Equipment (010934-44002) from 
the Suppression Division of the Fire Department’s Budget.  Funds being 
transferred into this line item will be transferred from:  
 
$30,200.00 (010934-47750) - Repairs to Damaged Property 
  
Section Three: This Ordinance shall be effective ten (10) days after adoption pursuant to Sections 
219 and 221 of the City of Reading Home Rule Charter.
