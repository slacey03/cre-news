RESOLUTION 2026-__48_  
A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY OF READING PLANNING COMMISSION  
RECOMMENDING COUNCIL FOR THE CITY OF READING ENACT AMENDMENTS TO THE 
CITY OF READING ZONING ORDINANCE PROVIDING FOR AMENDMENTS TO 
REGULATIONS AND USES IN THE  
COMMERCIAL CORE (C-C) AND MANUFACTURING COMMERCIAL (M-C) ZONING 
DISTRICTS  
WHEREAS, the Pennsylvania Municipalities Planning Code, act of July 31, 1968, as amended, 
53 P.S. §§10101 et seq. (“MPC”), enables a municipality through its zoning ordinance to regulate 
the uses of property; and  
WHEREAS, Council for the City of Reading enacted a Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance in 
2010; and  
WHEREAS, the Comprehensive Zoning Ordinance of the City of Reading is known as the City of 
Reading Zoning Ordinance of 2010; and  
WHEREAS, Council for the City of Reading has amended from time to time the Comprehensive 
Zoning Ordinance; and  
WHEREAS, the City of Reading Zoning Ordinance with amendments is codified as Chapter 600 
in the City of Reading Code of Ordinances; and  
WHEREAS, uses permitted by right, conditional uses and special exception uses are set forth in 
Part 8 of the Zoning Ordinance entitled “Districts”, Section 807 entitled “C-C Commercial Core 
District”: and  
WHEREAS, uses permitted by right, conditional uses and special exception uses are set forth in 
Part 8 of the Zoning Ordinance entitled “Districts”, Section 811 entitled “M-C Manufacturing - 
Commercial District”: and  
WHEREAS, Section 609 of the MPC 53 P.S. §10609 provides that the Planning Commission of a 
municipality may draft amendments to a Zoning Ordinance; and  
WHEREAS, upon review of the Zoning Ordinance for the City of Reading it was ascertained that 
additional amendments were necessitated; and  
WHEREAS, amendments to the Zoning Ordinance to provide for amendments to uses permitted 
by right, conditional use and special exception uses in the C-C Commercial Core and M-C 
Manufacturing Commercial Zoning Districts were found to be necessary; and  
WHEREAS, these amendments are referred to as C-C Commercial Core and M-C Manufacturing 
Commercial Zoning District Zoning Ordinance Amendments; and  
WHEREAS, the amendments have been reviewed by the City of Reading Planning Commission 
and the Berks County Planning Commission in accordance with the provisions established by the 
City of Reading Code and the Pennsylvania Municipalities Planning Code; and  
WHEREAS, the Berks County Planning Commission issued their review on December 31, 2025; 
and  
WHEREAS, the City of Reading Planning Commission reviewed and considered the  
C-C Commercial Core and M-C Manufacturing Commercial Zoning District Zoning Ordinance 
Amendments during several public meetings in 2026; and  
WHEREAS, at a public meeting held on April 28, 2026 the City Planning Commission reviewed 
the final draft of the C-C Commercial Core and M-C Manufacturing Commercial Zoning District 
Zoning Ordinance Amendments amending the Zoning Ordinance Part 8 entitled “Districts”, 
Sections 807 and 811 entitled respectively as C-C Commercial Core and M-C Manufacturing 
Commercial” and voted unanimously to recommend that the Amendments presented to it should 
be considered and approved by City Council.  
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Planning Commission for the City of Reading 
that the Commission recommends Council for the City of Reading approve the amendments to 

the Zoning Ordinance, Part 8 entitled “Districts”, Sections 807 entitled “C-C Commercial Core” 
and Section 811 entitled and “M-C Manufacturing Commercial” as attached hereto as Exhibit “A”  
FURTHER RESOLVED, the foregoing is a true and accurate copy of a Resolution of the City of 
Reading Planning Commission which was fully adopted at a meeting held on the 28th day of April 
2026.
