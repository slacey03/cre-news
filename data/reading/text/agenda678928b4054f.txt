II. Tax Exoneration for 139 S 5th St 2024 Property Taxes of $14,408.05 
Abbot Giac Chinh thanked Council for their time on this matter.  He stated that his Temple is 
glad to join the Reading community.  He stated that the Temple was unaware of the need for 
the exemption and exoneration process.  He stated that the property will be used for religious 
and educational purposes only.  He stated that Buddhists are committed to serving their 
communities and asked Council to consider approving this exoneration. 
 
Mr. Zaffuto provided a summary of the situation to Council.  He stated that the Temple has 
purchased the former St. James Lutheran Church at S 5th & Chestnut Sts.  He stated that this is 
a historic building and the Temple will continue the religious use.  He stated that no transfer 
tax was paid at settlement because of the tax exemption.   
 
Mr. Zaffuto stated that the Temple was not aware that they were not automatically exempt 
from property taxes and began receiving tax bills.  He stated that the County granted the tax 
exemption in 2025 and there is a one year lapse, hence the request for the exoneration of the 
2024 property taxes.  He stated that the School District has approved the exoneration of their 
property taxes and he will apply to the County after the City process is completed.   
 
Mr. Zaffuto stated that the building was constructed in the 1800’s and is difficult to maintain.  
He stated that the Temple is currently working to renovate the building to continue religious 
uses.  He stated that education and meditation programs will also be provided.  He stated that 

3 
if funds are needed for property taxes, there will be fewer funds available for maintenance and 
programming.   
 
Mr. Nunez questioned whether Mr. Zaffuto or the Abbot have spoken with the Administration 
about this request.  Mr. Zaffuto stated that the Abbot has spoken with the Administration.  
Abbott Giac Chinh stated that he had spoken with the Managing Director about homeless 
people living at the Temple.  He stated that they were removed with Police assistance.  He 
stated that this was his only contact with the City. 
 
Mr. Baker thanked Mr. Zaffuto and Abbot Giac Chinh for the presentation.  He noted the 
historical significance of the property and stated that the City values new opportunities.  He 
noted that the two religions may have differing styles and questioned if there would be 
changes to the architectural exterior of the building.  Mr. Zaffuto stated that there are no plans 
to change the architecture of the building’s street appearance.  He stated that if changes are 
considered they would comply with the City’s regulations. 
 
In response to a question from Mr. Baez regarding comments at this time, Mr. Gombar stated 
that comments and questions should be made while the applicants are present.  He stated that 
the resolution will appear on Council’s next agenda. 
 
Mr. Baez stated that there had also been another similar hearing recently.  He stated that he is 
in favor of granting this request for religious purposes.  He questioned if the Temple had filed 
for future exemption.  Mr. Zaffuto stated that this has been granted from 2025 forward and 
will remain in place until there is an additional change in ownership of the property. 
 
Ms. Reed thanked Mr. Zaffuto for the presentation. 
 
Abbot Giac Chinh thanked Council for their consideration. 
 
Mr. Baker suggested that Abbot Giac Chinh be invited to provide invocations at Council 
meetings. 
 
III. Other Matters 
Mr. Nunez noted the number of drivers that do not stop for children and school buses.  He 
questioned if there were ordinances to require schools to install cameras at bus stops.  Mr. 
Gombar stated that he is not aware of any ordinance with this requirement but that some 
municipalities use a program that will install cameras to monitor violations.  He stated that he 
will provide additional information about the program.   
 
Mr. Nunez stated that these cameras are used in Philadelphia and suggested that they be 
placed at all bus stops.  Mr. Gombar stated that stopping for school buses is already State law.  
He suggested looking into vendors that could assist Police in monitoring the issue.
