II. Tax Exoneration – S. Welz     5:30 pm – 6:00 pm 
Mr. Welz introduced himself and Pastor Santos.  He explained that Iglesia Pentecostal Un 
Nuevo Comienzo Inc is the new church property owner. 
 
Mr. Nunez connected to the meeting virtually at this time. 
 
Mr. Welz provided a summary of the church property and stated that Grace Lutheran Church 
was established in 1888 and acquired the other properties over time to enlarge the main 
building and to provide church parking.  He stated that over time, fewer Lutheran churches 
were needed and that Grace Lutheran had been in financial distress.  He stated that in 2024 
four Reading Lutheran Churches, including Grace Lutheran, merged into a new congregation 
and is using a different church property.  
 
Mr. Welz stated that Grace Lutheran planned to sell the properties and was aware of this 
growing Hispanic congregation.  He expressed the belief that the best continued use of the 
properties is for a church and that the new congregation has been successful.   
 
COMMITTEE of the WHOLE 
 
CITY COUNCIL 
 
 


2 
 
Mr. Welz stated that tax notices were sent to the new congregation but that they did not 
understand that these notices cannot be disregarded.  He stated that he has now helped them 
to file for tax exemption and exoneration.  He stated that the tax exemption was granted in 
January 2026 and stated that the exoneration request is for 2024 and 2025 tax years. 
 
Ms. Reed thanked Mr. Welz for this background information. 
 
Mr. Baez thanked Mr. Welz for his presentation.  In response to a question from Mr. Baez 
regarding the other taxing bodies approving the tax exoneration, Mr. Welz stated that the 
School District has provided the tax exoneration.  He stated that once the City has provided it, 
he will approach the County for their approval.   
 
In response to a question from Mr. Baez regarding the amount of the exoneration and the 
taxpayer burden, which he estimated at approximately $100,000, Mr. Welz agreed that the 
total amount is substantial.  He stated that the properties are assessed at approximately 
$800,000.  Mr. Gombar stated that the requested exoneration amounts are part of the 
application form which is part of this agenda packet.  He stated that the total amount is 
approximately $26,871 for 2024 and $66,680 for 2025. 
 
In response to a question from Mr. Baez regarding the use of the properties, Mr. Welz stated 
that all of the properties are used for the ministry and that the parking areas are for church 
staff and the congregation only.  There is no community parking available. 
 
Mr. Miller stated that he is glad to see that the building will be occupied and not create a 
hardship to the neighborhood.  He stated that this transition will allow the church to continue 
to be an anchor to the neighborhood.  He noted the high cost of maintaining large church 
buildings and the substantial tax exoneration request.  He noted that he is glad this church 
building will not become like the partially demolished abandoned church at Perkiomen Ave & 
Spruce St.  He stated that all congregations are tax exempt and he requested that the 
congregation provide a PILOT to the City in the future. 
 
In response to a question from Ms. Reed regarding whether the church would sell the parking 
area properties to a private owner in the future, Pastor Santos stated that there is no plan to do 
so.  He stated that his congregation hopes to honor Grace Lutheran and to continue their good 
works.  He stated that all of the parcels are used for parking only. 
 
In response to clarifying questions from Mr. Welz, Pastor Santos stated that there are no 
buildings on these properties, the parking is for staff and the congregation only, and that there 
are no third parties involved.   
 

3 
Mr. Gombar stated that the total amount of the tax exoneration may be nearly $100,000.  He 
stated that he will determine the correct amount before the voting meeting.  He noted the need 
to amend the agenda to include both the 2024 and 2025 exoneration requests. 
 
In response to additional questions from Councilor Baez, Pastor Santos stated that the 
congregation began using this building in 2022.  Mr. Welz explained that the properties were 
still owned by Grace Lutheran at that time.  He stated that Iglesia Pentecostal Un Nuevo 
Comienzo was leasing the property at that time.  He stated that the title was transferred in 
June 2024.  He stated that the new congregation has also completed some maintenance and 
renovation projects as well. 
 
Mr. Baez noted his agreement with Mr. Miller about the property not being vacant.  He 
questioned how to prevent this situation from happening again in the future.  Pastor Santos 
stated that the congregation has approximately 100 members and it has been his dream to 
acquire a church building and help the neighborhood.  He stated that the congregation 
recently held Vacation Bible School and provided meals to the children.  He stated that they 
feed the homeless on a monthly basis and that they also provide family services. 
 
Mr. Baez thanked Mr. Welz and Pastor Santos.  He questioned if the tax exemption status was 
received by the congregation.  Mr. Welz stated that tax exempt status was granted in 2026 and 
that this situation occurred because the congregation missed the filing deadline to address the 
prior taxes due. 
 
In response to a question from Mr. Baez regarding the progress of the process, Mr. Welz stated 
that the School District has already approved the exoneration.  He stated that the resolution on 
Council’s agenda this evening will exonerate the City taxes.  He stated that if this is granted, 
the congregation will approach the County for their exoneration as well.  He stated that this 
would then relieve the congregation of the past due taxes. 
 
In response to a question from Mr. Baez regarding how to prevent this situation again the 
future, Mr. Welz stated that the church is now tax exempt and will not need to pay taxes in the 
future.  He stated that they must continue their good works in order to keep that status, per 
State law. 
 
Ms. Campos thanked Mr. Welz and Pastor Santos for their presentation.  She questioned how 
often the City is requested to exonerate taxes.  Mr. Gombar stated that this happens frequently 
when titles are transferred.  He stated that the new property owner must apply to continue 
their exempt status.  He stated that if this is not done immediately, this situation occurs and 
explained that many non-profit organizations are unaware of this requirement.  He stated that 
exonerations were done several times but that the amount of the exoneration is usually not this 
significant.  He stated that the taxing bodies have determined that the School District, City, 
and County will consider the requests in that order.  He stated that generally the School 
District is the highest of the amounts owed so they consider the request first.   

4 
 
Ms. Campos noted that she is curious about how many other congregations experience the 
same issue.   
 
Mr. Baez stated that he has found information from 2022 and 2023 where the City did approve 
exonerations but the amounts were less than $1,000.  The 2023 amount was $3,708.  He stated 
that he is in favor of approving this exoneration since if done properly, there would have been 
no tax bill in the first place. 
 
Mr. Welz stated that this does not only happen in the City.  He expressed the belief that there 
is an informational gap as a title company matter.  He stated that the County Assessment 
Office automatically sends a tax bill when there is a title change.  He stated that the parties are 
not advised that the exemption does not stay and that they need to re-apply.  He stated that he 
plans to address this with the Bar Association. 
 
Mr. Miller questioned whether Mr. Welz was aware of the former church buildings from the 
merger and questioned if they plan to find congregations to purchase the buildings.  Mr. Welz 
stated that there is a congregation currently renting the former St. John’s Lutheran Church 
with the hope that they will acquire the building.  He noted the need to educate the new 
congregation about the building’s maintenance needs and expenses.  He stated that he has no 
knowledge of pending sales. 
 
Ms. Reed thanked Mr. Welz and Pastor Santos and stated that Council appreciates their 
presentation and explanation of the issue. 
 
This portion of the meeting concluded at 5:28 pm.
