o Patrol 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that Patrol and the Traffic Unit are components of the Patrol Division He 
stated that the revenue line item in patrol represents the Reading School District’s payment for 
50% of the Crossing Guard contract - $345,000. He stated that Patrol is composed of 132 FOP 
Police officers and a part-time civilian secretary in  the Traffic Unit.  A new line item was 
created to track expenses associated with the K9 program. - $38,500.  
 
In response to a question from Ms. Ventura, Mr. Famiglietti stated that the former Traffic 
Secretary transitioned to a similar position in Records.  The need for a $52,413 full-time 
position in this unit was re-evaluated, and the position was changed to $30,000 as part-time.  
The position is currently unfilled. He added that expenses were reclassified to provide 
improved tracking. 
 
In response to a question from Ms. Ventura, Mr. Famiglietti stated that six of the Police Officer 
Trainee positions are vacant and that there are two levels of pay for Officer Trainees – the 
reduced rate of $71,869 covers the Officers going through the Academy and the rate of $80,081 
is applied when the officer graduates and completes the FTO (Field Training Officer) period.   
 
• Admin Division 
Mr. Famiglietti explained that the revenues line item comes from the various services provided 
by the Department.  These fees have not been updated for multiple years and the work to 
update those fees will take place in 2027 to make sure all fees reimburse the City for their 
expenses and services. (Note: Fire Fees are usually updated annually) 
 
Mr. Famiglietti stated that the Admin division is composed of 5 uniformed officers and one 
civilian.  This area also includes the $40,000 expense for the Youth Police Academy. 
 
In response to questions from Mr. Miller, Mr. Famiglietti stated that across the Department, 
there are three Police positions unfunded for 2027 – the VSU Supervisor, a Telecommunicator 
in the Desk Sgt area and an Investigator in Criminal Investigation (CI).  He added that the 
Investigator position cannot be filled until all Patrol positions are filled.  He noted that the 
Crisis Intervention employees are included in the Special Services area.  
 
In response to a question from Mr. Miller, Mr. Famiglietti explained that in Police the need for 
overtime is mainly caused by unfilled positions so it is balanced on the unused salaries.
