5:00 – 6:00 pm Cento Hispano and Tec Centro presentation on grant funded projects – M. Toledo 
 
C. Resolution – authorizing the submission of a FY2027 Community Project Funding grant request 
to U.S. Representative Chrissy Houlihan for -Tec Centro’s Site Preparation, construction, and 
development of their property located at 433 Pearl Street in the amount of $1.150M for Tec Centro 
Berks’ skilled trades workforce programs, including Construction Pre-Apprenticeship, 
Commercial and Residential Plumbing, Facilities Maintenance, Welding, and Forklift Certification 
programs.  
 
D. Resolution – authorizing the submission of a FY2027 Community Project Funding Grant 
request to U.S. Representative Chrissy Houlihan in the amount of $1M to support Centro 
Hispano/Blueprint Communities with a Healthy Homes repair program which will help support 
homeowners with home repairs that present health hazards and prevent the homes from being 
designated as blighted homes, preventing potential homelessness and addressing quality of life 
concerns the constituents.
