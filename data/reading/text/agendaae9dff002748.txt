II. Review Amendment to Council Policy Handbook re Review and Approval of 
Minutes of Meetings and Public Hearings    5:30 pm – 5:50 pm 
Ms. Reed stated that Mr. Gombar and Ms. Kelleher prepared the draft policy.   
 
Mr. Gombar stated that he was tasked with drafting a policy to formalize the minute approval 
process.  He stated that the draft was distributed for Council review prior to today’s meeting.  
He summarized the draft and stated that this policy does not prevent the City Clerk from 
making corrections at her own discretion. 
 
Mr. Baez noted that the policy includes a deadline for Council to submit their revisions but 
that it does not include a deadline for the Clerks to provide the minutes to Council.  Mr.

Gombar agreed that there is no deadline included for the Clerks to provide the minutes to 
Council.  He requested that Ms. Kelleher respond. 
 
Ms. Kelleher stated that the minutes will be provided within a week of the meeting.   
 
Mr. Baez stated that given a one week deadline, Council should receive the minutes by the 
following Monday so that they have three days to review prior to submitting corrections to the 
Clerks, according to the draft policy.   
 
Ms. Kelleher stated that this may not work in all occasions and noted that if there is a special 
meeting or hearing on Thursday the Wednesday deadline would be missed.   
 
Mr. Gombar agreed and stated that for regular Monday meetings the policy will work but that 
accommodations can be made for meetings on other days as needed.  He stated that he will 
check the City Code to clarify the deadline and will amend the policy as needed. 
 
Mr. Baez noted the need to be on the record and to keep this perspective.  He noted the 
housing crisis and that many people are facing homelessness and that deserves attention.  He 
noted that he does not believe this policy discussion is a high priority when it’s been done the 
same way for many years and questioned why this policy has come forward.  He stated that 
there was a recent disagreement about requested changes to a meeting summary and 
questioned if that is the reason this has moved forward.  He noted the need for public 
transparency. 
 
Ms. Reed expressed the belief that it’s always good for a legislative body to understand its job.  
She stated that meetings are also recorded and noted that summaries of what was discussed 
are provided.  She stated that the summaries are not transcripts or verbatim accounts of every 
comment and expressed the belief that this is not appropriate for minutes.  She stated that 
Reading is fortunate to have recordings that a person can refer to after reading the summary if 
they would like to hear the discussion first hand.  She noted the staff workload working with 
City Council and their other BACs.  She noted the need for an open conversation. 
 
Mr. Baez agreed that there should be consistency.  He stated that he requested the inclusion of 
a question in a summary and that he is not asking for a transcript.  He stated that he is 
requesting accuracy of context and that he does not want his words to be used out of context.  
He stated that context matters.  He stated that he will be moving to amend the August 17 
meeting summary to include the question as requested in his email.   
 
Mr. Baez stated that the City has so many things it should be working on and suddenly 
because of one request a policy is required.  He stated that he looks forward to having 
consistency. 
 

3 
In response to questions from Ms. Ventura about the policy, Mr. Gombar stated that letter D is 
to prevent Sunshine violations.  He noted the need for a quorum of Council to not be 
discussing or deliberating via email.  He stated that under the Sunshine Act, one or two 
Councilors can hold discussions but noted the need for Councilors to be cognizant of the fact 
that emails are discoverable for Right to Know requests and that Council should avoid written 
communications, even with one individual.   
 
In response to a question from Ms. Ventura regarding why, in letter F, the City Clerk has final 
discretion to make requested corrections when Council is a body of seven.  Mr. Gombar stated 
that he drafted this language to create an efficient way to make corrections.  He stated that 
ultimately Council can propose an amendment to meeting summaries at meetings as they feel 
necessary.   
 
Ms. Ventura stated that all Council members are equal and speak with one voice.  She noted 
the need for public discussion of minute corrections as needed. 
 
Ms. Reed noted the need for efficiency and noted that public discussion of minutes may take 
time away from other matters for discussion. 
 
Ms. Ventura stated that this will not always occur.  She stated that decisions about meeting 
summary corrections should not be decided by Ms. Kelleher, Mr. Gombar, and Ms. Reed 
alone. 
 
Mr. Gombar stated that he will amend this language.   
 
Ms. Reed suggested not making typographical corrections during meetings and that 
discussion should center on substantive changes only.  Mr. Gombar agreed. 
 
Mr. Baker thanked Ms. Kelleher and Mr. Gombar and noted his appreciation for their work.  
He noted his disagreement with some comments made and stated that one of Council’s job is 
employee management.  He noted the need for fair treatment of staff and not to overburden 
them unnecessarily.  He stated that meetings are recorded and noted that Council should not 
get into the weeds.  He noted his agreement with Ms. Ventura regarding a quorum of Council 
in email discussions and agreed with the need to amend letter B. 
 
Mr. Gombar stated that he would continue to recommend not debating issues or deliberating 
in writing via email. 
 
Ms. Reed stated that two Councilors may be conversing but all seven members may be reading 
the discussion. 
 
Mr. Gombar agreed that if all seven Councilors are copied on the email that it is a Sunshine 
violation.  He opined that if Ms. Ventura wishes to converse with one other Council member

that does not violate Sunshine but that the emails must be turned over in the event of a Right 
to Know request.  He stated that this applies to texts and emails. 
 
Ms. Reed suggested that the policy be amended at the table this evening and be removed from 
the consent agenda. 
 
Mr. Gombar stated that he will make these policy changes. 
 
Ms. Campos stated that Council has responsible Clerks and that she does not understand the 
need for these policy amendments.  She requested that Mr. Gombar specify the requested 
amendments.  Mr. Gombar stated that he will add a deadline for the Clerks to provide the 
meeting summaries to Council, will note the quorum and Right to Know issues, and will 
remove discretion about requested changes from the Clerk, himself, and Ms. Reed. 
 
Mr. Nunez questioned why this policy is needed. 
 
Mr. Baez stated that the policy was drafted by Ms. Kelleher and Mr. Gombar. 
 
Ms. Reed stated that the policy was drafted to clarify the meeting summary approval process. 
 
Mr. Gombar stated that it is best practice to memorialize processes.  He stated that this will be 
part of the Council Handbook and can be amended in the future as needed. 
 
Ms. Campos stated that she agrees with the need for amendments #1 and #2 but not with #3.  
She stated that policy letter H still allows Council to discuss the summary as needed.  She 
expressed the belief that correcting minutes at every meeting will be time consuming and 
stated that it is the Clerk’s responsibility to determine if changes should be made. 
 
Mr. Baez stated that there does not seem to be consensus on amendment #3.  He stated that it 
is Council’s job to review the meeting summaries. 
 
Ms. Reed questioned if consensus is needed on all three amendments.  Mr. Gombar noted the 
need for majority.  He stated that a motion will need to be made at the table.  He stated that if 
all three amendments are proposed and there is no support, each amendment should be made 
separately.  He stated that the amendments will be voted on first and the policy, as amended, 
would then be voted on. 
 
Mr. Baez stated that he supports all three amendments.  Ms. Ventura stated that she also 
supports all three amendments.  Ms. Campos stated that she agrees with amendments #1 and 
#2 only.  Mr. Miller and Mr. Nunez noted the need for additional conversations.  Mr. Baker 
stated that he also agrees with only #1 and #2.

• Award of Contract – to Flyway Construction in the amount of $4,531,000, for the 
construction of the 5,500 sq ft ADA accessible Hillside Splashpad Project with 3 distinct 
areas of play which allow children from very young to experience small splashes and 
sprays to older children who can engage in water “wars” and dump buckets. The 
project encompasses renovation of all current buildings to include the restroom facility,

7 
spaces. Families will be able to observe children playing from the tiered observation as
