Dear Esteemed Members of the City of Reading City Council,  
I am writing to respectfully request that the City of Reading consider exoneration of real estate 
taxes on property owned by the Sakyamuni Buddhist Sangha of US Dharma Meditation 
Temple, known as 139 S. 5th Street, Reading, Pennsylvania, PIN No. 04530627799172. 
 As a non-profit religious educational organization with a 501(c)(3) designation, our temple 
provides various educational programs, community services, and cultural events that benefit 
the local community. Our mission is to promote Buddhist teachings, cultural exchange, and 
social welfare, and we strive to create a harmonious and inclusive environment for people of all 
backgrounds.  
However, we have recently become aware that due to our lack of familiarity with the nuances of 
Pennsylvania law regarding real estate tax exemptions, we are facing outstanding real estate 
taxes in amounts that seriously threaten the continued operations of our organization and its 
ability to pursue its mission. When we purchased the property in 2023 from another religious 
organization, we were aware that it was classified as tax exempt. We did not come to understand 
until after 2024 taxes had already been assessed that the exemption had been removed and that 
we would have to reapply to obtain a new tax exemption.  
Upon learning of these circumstances in 2024, we hired counsel and submitted an application 
for tax exemption to the Berks County Board of Assessment. After a hearing, the exemption was 
granted, but unfortunately the exempt designation did not become effective until January 1, 
2025. The taxes billed for 2024 were not invalidated or discharged by the exemption and are 
now considered delinquent, causing a significant hardship and potentially jeopardizing the 
ability of our temple to continue operating. The face value of these 2024 City taxes is $11,617.06, 
and penalty and interest to the present date amount to an additional $1,161.71 and $575.04, 
respectively.  
We have spent several months in discussions and negotiations with the Reading School District 
and the Berks County Tax Claim Bureau, which ultimately resulted in the exoneration of 
delinquent School District real estate taxes. We are seeking exoneration from the County of 
Berks concurrently with this request for exoneration from the City.  

 
Page3 
Our organization is funded entirely by private donations and does not have sufficient funds to 
pay these real estate tax amounts. Exoneration of the City taxes for 2024 would enable us to 
allocate more resources to our educational programs and community services and much-needed 
renovations to the physical space. This, in turn, would benefit the local community, particularly 
students, families, and individuals who participate in our programs. 
We have enclosed a completed Tax Exoneration Application Form, along with the following 
supporting documentation:  
A. Property Deed  
B. Exemption Application Filed at Board of Assessment  
C. Board of Assessment Notice of Decision Approving Exemption  
D. Delinquent Real Estate Tax Information from Berks County Tax Claim Bureau, as of October 
31, 2025  
We would be grateful if you could consider our request and provide us with information on any 
additional steps the City may require to approve our exoneration request. We are confident that 
our organization will provide vital resources to the community and look forward to the 
opportunity to discuss this matter further.  
Thank you for your time and consideration.  
Sincerely,
