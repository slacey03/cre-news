1 
 
City Council 
Nominations and Appointments Committee 
Monday September 8, 2026 
Meeting Report 
 
 
Attendance: C. Miller (in person) and V. Campos, J. Baez (virtually) 
 
Others Attending: M. Smith, B. Rivera (in person) and BCTV (virtually) 
 
The meeting was called to order at 4:00 pm by Mr. Miller. 
 
Mr. Miller introduced himself, Ms. Campos, and Mr. Baez.  He stated that there are three 
interviews for this meeting; one reappointment and two appointments.   
 
I. Interviews 
• Suzanne Cody – appointment – Planning Commission 
Mr. Miller greeted Ms. Cody who appeared in person and briefly explained the interview process 
to her and the viewing audience. 
 
Mr. Miller questioned what prompted Ms. Cody to apply for a position on the Planning 
Commission.  Ms. Cody stated she has been speaking with City team members regarding the 
Commission’s work for the last six months; also knowing that changes would be coming 
regarding the HARB.  She is of the opinion that with her skillset, this may be a good fit for her. 
 
Mr. Miller questioned what knowledge Ms. Cody has regarding the functions of the Planning 
Commission.  Ms. Cody stated that her understanding of the Planning Commission’s work is that 
it has a larger oversight role in the projects of our community.  She stated that it is like an 
overview of all the moving parts of development in the City. 
 
Ms. Campos greeted Ms. Cody and thanked her for her interest in serving on this Commission.  
She stated that it is refreshing to have a female candidate for the Planning Commission.  She 
stated that she has read all of Ms. Cody’s accomplishments in the application and highly 
commended her background.   
 
Ms. Campos questioned Ms. Cody’s experience in reviewing plans.  Ms. Cody thanked Ms. 
Campos for her generous compliments and stated that she has two degrees in Interior Design 
which come with knowledge of both hand drafting and how to read blueprints.  She stated that 
she has also interned and worked in many design firms and through that she has had the 
opportunity to learn how to read plans and have more extensive participation in that process. 
 

2 
Mr. Baez greeted Ms. Cody and apologized for not being able to attend the interview in person as 
he is feeling under the weather.  He stated that he is Council liaison to the Planning Commission 
and is very much looking forward to having her as a member.  He acknowledged her extensive 
background  and skillset that would benefit this position. 
 
Mr. Baez questioned if Ms. Cody has a specific goal or accomplishment that she would like to 
achieve if she is appointed.  Ms. Cody stated that as a creative person with creative projects, she 
loves them all equally, but to successfully make progress on projects, there are systemic 
requirements to be met effectively.  She stated that she hopes to assist the Commission with that 
component as she is of the opinion that with an orderly and deliberate focus, the process can be 
made more streamlined and therefore more operational.   
 
Mr. Baez thanked her for her response and noted that Ms. Cody has a history of not only 
developing properties but building partnerships as well.   
 
Mr. Miller questioned how Ms. Cody learned about the vacancy on the Planning Commission.  
Ms. Cody stated that she learned of the vacancy through her work on HARB and through her 
company’s internship program that brought her into City Hall to meet various staff members.  
She stated that she acquired her knowledge of the Planning Commission’s work and vacancy 
through networking. 
 
Ms. Cody was presented with the opportunity to ask questions.  She stated that on the occasions 
of her working late afternoons and into evening hours for the Commission, she respectfully 
requests that she be escorted to her car by either another Commission member or someone else. 
 
Mr. Miller stated that he understood her concern and is hopeful that can be worked out in some 
way.  Ms. Campos added that Ms. Diaz-Melendez also attends the meetings and could likely 
walk out at the same time after Planning meetings.  Mr. Baez also stated that as long as he is 
attending meetings, he can escort her to her car as well.   
 
Ms. Cody left the meeting at this time. 
 
Mr. Miller noted that Ms. Cody has been an active member of various groups in the City such as 
the Pagoda Foundation and HARB, giving her an advantage of being versed in the business of 
the City.  He stated that he is in favor of her appointment.   
 
Mr. Baez agreed with Mr. Miller, stating that beyond her background and education, she is a 
wonderful person to work with; he also recommended her appointment.   
 
Ms. Campos stated that she is in favor of recommending Ms. Cody adding that her background 
and knowledge is extensive and would benefit the Planning Commission. 
 

3 
The Nominations and Appointments Committee recommends the appointment of Suzanne 
Cody to the Planning Commission.                 
 
• Timothy Waldman – reappointment – Electrician’s Examining Board  
Mr. Miller greeted Mr. Waldman who appeared in person and reiterated his interest in serving 
another term on the Electrician’s Examining Board.  Mr. Waldman concurred by stating that he 
had worked as the City’s Electrical Inspector from 2005 to  2020 and during that time served as a 
liaison on that Board.  He stated that when he retired in 2020, he noticed a vacancy and applied to 
be appointed to the Electrician’s Examining Board.  He stated that he has been a liaison or a 
member, currently acting as secretary, since 2005 and is hoping to continue his service on the 
Board.  He stated that things are going quite well on the Board as there was a period when that 
was not the case. 
 
Mr. Miller questioned Mr. Waldman about the business, Waldman Electric, and there was a brief 
discussion regarding the history of the family business. 
 
Ms. Campos stated that she is very impressed with Mr. Waldman’s extensive background and  
experience and all that his family’s business amassed through three generations.  She thanked 
him for his service on the Board and his interest in continuing that service for another term.   
 
Ms. Campos questioned if there is any way that the City can assist the Board.  Mr. Waldman 
stated that after the many presentations to Council and all of the behind-the-scenes work done by 
the Board, all seems to be falling into place and restored to the state it was prior to the rough 
patch that existed.  He stated that it would be beneficial to have all the positions for inspectors in 
the Building & Trades Division of the City filled as that would create a better foundation for the 
Board moving forward. He stated that electrically, the Board is doing well with a bit of a snag 
regarding reciprocal licenses, namely as it relates to Philadelphia licenses which is referenced in 
the Board’s meeting summary. 
 
Mr. Baez stated that he did not have any questions for Mr. Waldman and thanked him for his 
continuing service on this Board.  He stated that his background is impressive and he encouraged 
Mr. Waldman to keep up the good work.   
 
Mr. Waldman stated that he appreciated the compliment by Mr. Baez.  He stated that this may be 
his last term, but he will give it his all while he can do so.  
 
Mr. Waldman left the meeting at this time. 
 
Mr. Miller stated that Mr. Waldman has broad knowledge of the trade and how it relates to the 
City’s Building & Trades Department as he has worked many years in the department.  He stated 
that he is in favor and recommended his reappointment.  
 
Mr. Baez agreed with Mr. Miller and recommended Mr. Waldman’s reappointment.   

4 
 
Ms. Campos agreed with both Mr. Miller and Mr. Baez, adding that Mr. Waldman’s responses 
were very well thought out and reflected his extensive knowledge and experience.   She stated 
that she is in favor of his reappointment. 
 
The Nominations and Appointments Committee recommends the reappointment of Timothy 
Waldman to the Electrician’s Examining Board.   
 
• Karen Glass – appointment – Board of Ethics  
Mr. Miller greeted Ms. Glass and thanked her for appearing in person for her interview.  He 
stated that he recalled her being interviewed for the Redevelopment Authority and thanked her, 
despite not being selected, for her continued interest to be involved in a different capacity; he 
reiterated his appreciation that she chose another manner in which to volunteer her time by 
applying for the Board of Ethics. 
 
Mr. Miller questioned how Ms. Glass came to choose her involvement on the Board of Ethics.  
Ms. Glass stated with full transparency that Ms. Smith suggested the Board of Ethics.  She stated 
that she looked at the City’s website regarding the Board of Ethics and then did some additional 
research on the work that the Board has done to date.  Mr. Miller was pleased to hear that Ms. 
Glass researched the Board of Ethics and has an idea of what the work of the Board involves.   
 
Mr. Miller questioned if Ms. Glass would have any issues attending the necessary meetings for 
the Board of Ethics.  Ms. Smith introduced herself as she and Ms. Glass have been in contact via 
email but never formally or personally met.  She stated that she is the liaison to the Board of 
Ethics and the Board only meets when there is a need.  She stated that when the Board must 
meet, she polls the members providing various dates to determine which date/time would be 
most suitable with the most members.  She stated that the meeting time will never be before 5pm.  
Ms. Glass stated that she would not have any issues with attendance. 
 
Mr. Baez thanked Ms. Glass for her interest in serving on the Ethics Board.  He questioned why 
Ms. Glass feels the Ethics Board is needed.  Ms. Glass stated that there always needs to be as 
much objectivity as possible in any process even though human error exists.  She stated that the 
Board bases their work on deciding factors when there are conflicts of interest.   
 
Mr. Baez concurred with Mr. Miller’s statement regarding Ms. Glass’s resolve to serve on another 
Board and doing the research involved in understanding the work of the Ethics Board.  He noted 
the range of experience Ms. Glass provided on her application, specifically being an End-of-Life 
Practitioner with Tower Health, which sounds very interesting.  He commended Ms. Glass on 
that type of work as it requires compassion, discretion, and a deep understanding of individuals’ 
needs.  Ms. Glass updated Mr. Baez stating that she is no longer affiliated with Tower Health as 
her private practice has flourished; although she continues to donate much of her personal time 
through her private practice.  
 

5 
Ms. Campos greeted and thanked Ms. Glass for her interest in serving on the Ethics Board. 
Ms. Campos questioned how Ms. Glass’s work as an End-of-Life Practitioner strengthens her 
candidacy for the Ethics Board.  Ms. Glass stated that ethics is foundational to any type of life and 
death practice or consideration.  She explained that when a person is dying, there are multiple 
family dynamics which sometimes come with a political component.  She stated that much of 
what she does is guiding family through a process of understanding their own personal 
influences and experiences.  She stated that she then reviews everyone’s information together to 
work on a viable resolution.  She opined that this has been quite an effective process for her and 
her private practice as it has yielded positive results.  She believes that this skillset of 
independent decision and a good sense of how people respond and react would benefit her 
contribution to the Ethics Board. 
 
Mr. Miller stated with full disclosure that Ms. Glass is a tenant in one of his rental properties and 
though that should not have any effect on Ms. Glass’s candidacy, he wanted to disclose that fact, 
nonetheless.   
 
Ms. Glass was given the opportunity to ask questions.  She questioned who usually is tasked 
with being an investigative officer.  Ms. Smith stated that it is a lawyer.   
 
Ms. Glass questioned whether they, too, volunteer their services to which Ms. Smith stated that 
the Board Solicitor and Board Investigative Officer are financially compensated.   
 
Ms. Glass stated that she prepared a statement for the Committee and was not sure if it had any 
value toward her candidacy.  Mr. Miller stated that it will be forwarded to the committee 
members who are attending virtually for their review.   
 
Ms. Glass left the meeting at this time.  
 
Mr. Miller stated that Ms. Glass would bring a different perspective to the Ethics Board and the 
fact that she wants to serve as a volunteer, even when she was not chosen for her primary 
interest, speaks to her commitment.  He stated that he is in favor of her appointment. 
 
Mr. Baez agreed and is in favor of Ms. Glass’s appointment. 
 
Ms. Campos stated that she was impressed with Ms. Glass’s responses as they were well thought 
out and she stated that she is excited to have her on the Ethics Board adding that she, too, is in 
favor of her appointment.  
 
All Committee members agreed that Ms. Glass’s prepared statement was a first for the 
Committee and very much appreciated.  
 
The Nominations and Appointments Committee recommends the appointment of Karen Glass 
to the Ethics Board.   

6 
 
 
The meeting was adjourned at 4:40 pm.
