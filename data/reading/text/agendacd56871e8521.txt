City Council 
Nominations and Appointments Committee 
Monday July 6, 2026 
Meeting Report 
 
 
Attendance: C. Miller (in person), V. Campos, J. Baez (virtually)  
 
Others Attending: M. Smith, B. Rivera (in person) and BCTV (virtually) 
 
The meeting was called to order at 4:00 pm by Mr. Miller. 
 
Mr. Miller introduced himself, Ms. Campos, and Mr. Baez.  He stated that there are two 
interviews for this meeting which are both reappointments.   
 
I. Interviews 
• Stephan Thompson – reappointment – Environmental Advisory Council (EAC) 
Mr. Miller greeted Mr. Thompson and briefly explained the interview process.  He questioned if 
Mr. Thompson feels there are improvements needed in how the EAC operates.  Mr. Thompson 
stated that the group of individuals currently on the EAC are very committed to the 
environmental betterment of the City which is the work that the EAC is tasked with.  He stated 
that there are several subcommittees that work well within the EAC.   
 
Mr. Thompson stated that as the Chairperson, he would like to see more assistance for tabling at 
events.  He stated that he understands how busy EAC members are and that manpower 
availability is affected by this.  He stated that aside from needing more assistance at events, the 
EAC works very well in its operations.   
 
Mr. Miller questioned Mr. Thompson about the general number of volunteers at each event to 
which Mr. Thompson stated that it all depends upon the type of event taking place.  Mr. 
Thompson stated that there are more volunteers at Earth Day events, which typically run all day, 
as opposed to Wacky Water Wednesday events, which are a couple of hours or so.  He stated that 
he understands that people work during daytime hours, which is generally when these events 
take place. 
 
Ms. Campos greeted Mr. Thompson and thanked him for his continued interest in the EAC.  She 
questioned what is most enjoyable for Mr. Thompson about being on the EAC.  Mr. Thompson 
stated that he was first approached by Ms. Curran-Myers who was, at the time, his professor at 
Alvernia, about joining the EAC.  He stated that he has always had an interest in issues related to 
the environment, but he has learned so much more since becoming a member of the EAC about 
how impactful the environment really is to quality of life.

Mr. Baez joined the meeting in person at this time. 
 
Mr. Thompson stated that he would really like to see more of the younger generations involved 
with the EAC as they are the future.   
 
Mr. Baez stated that before he questioned Mr. Thompson, he wanted to echo his belief that the 
younger generations are so very important and needed in these efforts.  To that end, he has been 
a great proponent of youth involvement in all City matters, including but not limited to 
environmental issues.  He stated that the youth afford us all with different perspectives and 
propel us forward in our ever-changing world.  
 
Mr. Baez questioned if the EAC has solicited assistance from non-profit organizations or schools 
regarding volunteer efforts for many of the events he has talked about.  Mr. Thompson stated 
that the EAC has a fellowship with Luisa Lara of Barrio Alegria who has done and continues to 
do a phenomenal job of getting information out to the community.  He stated that he has also 
directed Ms. Lara to the Reading Recreation Commission for more involvement.  He opined that 
the Wacky Water Wednesday events are fantastic in reaching younger generations as they are 
always well-attended.  He stated that he would really like to educate students of middle schools 
in the area so that they could in turn get their parents involved in how to protect our 
environment.   
 
Mr. Baez stated that Mr. Thomspon indirectly answered his next question which was to speak of 
the greater challenges facing the EAC.  He stated that through Mr. Thompson’s earlier response, 
it is clear to him that the greater challenge is finding volunteers.  He stated that he understands 
that the EAC is already on the path to addressing that challenge and is working toward a resolve.  
Mr. Thompson reiterated the great work that the fellowship with Ms. Lara of Barrio Alegria has 
aided in the EAC’s efforts.   
 
Mr. Thompson expressed how proud he is of Reading’s EAC work.  He stated that he has seen 
the work of other EACs in surrounding municipalities/boroughs and Reading’s is so much more 
advanced in achieving higher standards.  He noted that many other EACs do not have the 
subcommittees that Reading has nor do they have a mission statement that provides an end goal.  
He stated that he is grateful for the team he has and how well each member works toward the 
common mission of the EAC. 
 
Mr. Miller thanked Mr. Thompson for appearing virtually to his interview as did the other 
Committee members and stated that Ms. Smith would be contacting him.   
 
• Barbara Waller – reappointment – Code & License Board of Appeals 
Mr. Miller greeted Ms. Waller and questioned how many appeals Ms. Waller has been involved 
with during her tenure on the Board.  Ms. Waller stated that she has been on the Board for a very 
long time and the answer to his question would greatly depend on who was involved with the 
appeal process at the time it was brought to the Board.  She stated that in the early years of her 

3 
tenure, the meetings were packed with members of the police and several different departments.  
She stated that as the years went by, many positions were not filled in the same manner as they 
had been and changes began to occur.  She stated that there was a time when there were many 
appeals heard and then there were times when there were none.  She explained that when off-
road vehicles came into the picture there were more hearings held.  She stated that generally the 
Board would meet approximately 3 to 4 times per year and she stated that during her tenure, she 
may have been involved with hearing 55 to 60 appeals as a Board member.  She stated that in the 
last 6 months or so, business on the Board has begun to pick up and look like the earlier days 
when she first started on the Board.     
   
Mr. Baez greeted Ms. Waller and questioned what the greatest challenge is facing the Board.  Ms. 
Waller stated that selecting the right people in the positions needed will effectively carry out the 
work to the letter of the Code.  She stated that there have been approximately four appeals in the 
last year and a half that were brought to the Board which she stated were great work for the 
Board.  
 
Ms. Campos greeted Ms. Waller and thanked her for her commitment to the work of the Board 
for so many years.  She questioned if Ms. Waller believes the processes of the Board are efficient 
and productive to the Board’s work.  Ms. Waller stated that currently the processes are very 
efficient and productive, however, that has not always been the case during her time on the 
Board.  She stated that it is so important that the appellant understands the violation he/she is 
appealing to the Board and that starts with the process of being fined.  She stated that 
appropriate documentation is crucial in showing that the process was performed as it should be 
and there are no questions about following requirements set forth by the Code.  
 
II. Appointment Recommendations                            
 
Mr. Miller stated that he is in favor of recommending both Mr. Thompson and Ms. Waller adding 
that they each have a vested interest on their Boards and have a wealth of knowledge as they 
each have served years. 
 
Ms. Campos agreed with Mr. Miller stating that she too is in favor of recommending both 
reappointments.  She stated that their years of experience speaks volumes to their commitment in 
serving.  She stated that Ms. Waller has seen many changes in her tenure and has much to offer to 
the Board.   
 
Mr. Baez stated that he is in awe that Ms. Waller is 82 years old and still holds a full-time job 
while serving as a volunteer.  He expressed the belief that this is true commitment.  He stated 
that he is in favor of both reappointment recommendations as they each have extensive 
knowledge and experience in their respective Boards. 
 
The Nominations and Appointments Committee recommends the reappointment of Stephan 
Thompson to the Environmental Advisory Council.
