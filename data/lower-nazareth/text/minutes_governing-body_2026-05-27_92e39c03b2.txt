Subdivision and Land Development

Nazareth Nest — Attorney Asteak reported the Township Engineer has been notified by the developer
that the engineer is not permitted to make inspections of what the developer labels “private
improvements.” The Board discussed the situation, and some board members expressed concerns about
allowing construction to continue that is not inspected and waiting until he attempts to reduce his
security. The Board requested information to be provided at the next meeting in order to facilitate any
further discussion, if necessary.

Solicitor’s Report

Data Center Ordinance — The proposed ordinance was reviewed by the Township Planning Commission,
LVPC, and the Nazareth COG. Their comments have been received. The ordinance has been duly
advertised and is ready for Board consideration. The public hearing was opened. No public comment
was received. Supervisors noted this is a very important amendment to the zoning ordinance and while
improvements will be sure to follow, this is a necessary amendment. J. Pennington made a motion to
adopt Ordinance 261-05-26. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Borrowing Ordinance — The purpose of the borrowing is to provide the necessary funding for the
reconstruction of Township Line Road as the grant award requires Lower Nazareth Township to expend
the funds first and seek reimbursement of their expenditures after.

S. Brown made a motion to approve and accept the loan commitment proposal from People’s Security
Bank Trust and authorize the solicitor to advance the submission in accordance with the Local
Government Unit Debt Act. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

J. Pennington made a motion to adopt Ordinance 262-05-26, an Ordinance authorizing the borrowing of
$1,250,000 for the purpose of a construction loan for the reconstruction of Township Line Road. M.
Boucher seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Nazareth Area School District (NASD) — Curative Amendment — G. Asteak announced the Township is
in receipt of a curative amendment application from the Nazareth Area School District through their
legal counsel, Julie Burkart. The premise of the curative amendment application is that: (1) the school
district pre-empts local zoning regulations and (2) the public sewer requirements should not be
applicable to the school district if they can demonstrate that an on-lot system is appropriate.

The school district has provided correspondence waiving the 60-day requirement for the municipality to
commence the curative amendment hearing. The school district is requesting the hearing(s) to
commence on September 15". N. Teague made a motion to authorize the extension requested and to
commence the hearing on September 15". M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (4-0). S. Brown
abstained.

G. Asteak informed the Board that under the MPC the Board may retain special counsel to defend the
Township zoning ordinance. J. Pennington made a motion to appoint Matthew Deschler, Esq. to be
compensated at the solicitor’s litigation hourly rate. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (4-0). S.
Brown abstained.

G. Asteak further reported to the Board that the school district is recommending the hearing be moved to
a location that can hold a significant number of people as they are sure this hearing will be well
attended. J. Pennington made a motion to direct staff to prepare the garage area for the curative
amendment hearing if attendance exceeds the regular meeting room capacity. M. Boucher seconded.
Motion passed (4-0). S. Brown abstained.

G. Asteak announced S. Brown will be abstaining from all votes pertaining to the Nazareth Area School

Engineer’s Report

No report.

Manager’s Report

Time Extension Report — L. Stauffer presented a report of the time extensions accepted since the last
meeting.

2026 Paving Project Change Order — This matter will be tabled until the June 10" meeting.

Greenways, Trails, and Recreation Grant — The Board previously directed L. Stauffer to apply for a trail
and parking lot expansion at Hahn’s Meadow Park. As part of the grant application process, the Board
needs to adopt a resolution authorizing the manager to apply on the Township’s behalf. N. Teague made
a motion to adopt Resolution No. LNT-20-2026. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

2026 Grow NorCo Grant — L. Stauffer reported that she checked with Northampton County and
codification would not be an eligible project. After discussion, the Board determined to not apply this
round.

Nuisance Ordinance Amendment — L. Stauffer reported a draft ordinance is in front of the Board for
consideration that amends the Nuisance Ordinance adopted by the Board in October 2025. The
amending ordinance provides for regulations for junk vehicles, recreational vehicles, and infestations.
The Board determined some items pertaining to recreational vehicles should be changed. M. Boucher
made a motion to direct the manager to make the requested changes and to authorize the solicitor to
advertise the proposed ordinance. S. Brown seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Herman Security Release - In accordance with correspondence from Keystone Consulting in 2013,
confirmation was made that the arborvitaes have been installed; therefore, the release of the security
funds is appropriate. J. Pennington made a motion to release the security as recommended in the
amount of $900. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Township Line Road Contingent Approval — The Board previously authorized the Township engineer to
go out to bid for the complete reconstruction of Township Line Road from the Hanover Township line

border to the intersection of Silvercrest. The Township received a grant in the amount of $1,250,000 for
the project. Bids were received on May 19, 2026, with a total of seven contractors bidding. The lowest,

Grant Funding Awarded / Not Awarded — Lower Nazareth Township was awarded four grants for
equipment for Bethlehem Township Volunteer Fire Company, the Township’s ambulance provider. The
grants are as follows: (1) $30,000 through Northampton/Lehigh LSA for portable radios; (2) $63,556
for a cardiac monitor; (3) $21,659 for personal CO2 monitors; and (4) $32,699 for portable radios.

One of the grants is a duplicate, which overfunds the portable radios request. The Commonwealth
Financing Authority recommended two potential courses of action: (1) order additional radios or (2)
seek a change to the scope of the $30,000 grant. After brief discussion, M. Boucher made a motion to
authorize the Township Manager to submit a change request for the $30,000 Northampton/Lehigh LSA
grant for the purchase of a police vehicle for Colonial Regional Police. S. Brown seconded. Motion
passed (5-0).

Traffic Impact Committee Resolution Amendment — L. Stauffer reported the Board has received an
amended resolution for the Traffic Impact Committee because of review of the original resolution by the
solicitor. J. Pennington made a motion to adopt Resolution LNT-21-26. M. Boucher seconded. Motion
passed (5-0).

G. Asteak stated the update of the Traffic Impact Plan has to take a rewind to ensure we are in
compliance with all statutory and MPC requirements. He reviewed with the Board that a public hearing
must be held for the Land Use Assumption Report and the Roadway Sufficiency Report. He has
selected a date of 06/30/26. The public hearing must be advertised twice. The draft reports and the plan
must be sent to the school district and adjoining municipalities. S. Brown made a motion to authorize
the advertisement as outlined by the solicitor. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

G. Asteak reported that at this time all new submitted land development and subdivision plans will be
subject to a $1,000 traffic impact fee.

Gun Club Road — L. Stauffer reported she received communication from the Township Manager and
Director of Public Works at Upper Nazareth Township. Upper Nazareth Township intends to go out to
bid for the full-depth reclamation of Gun Club Road. They inquired if Lower Nazareth Township, who
owns approximately 75 to 100 feet of Gun Club Road, would be interested in the road work including
Lower Nazareth’s portion. The estimated financial contribution from Lower Nazareth would be
between $5,000 to $10,000. M. Boucher made a motion to authorize Upper Nazareth Township to
