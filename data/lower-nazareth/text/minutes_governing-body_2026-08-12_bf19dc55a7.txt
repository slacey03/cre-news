John Caffese, 634 Country Club Road, appeared to discuss the widening of the Country Club Road issue
and his desire for the preservation of the Catalpa tree. He presented photos and the report by arborist. J.
Caffese reviewed photos and again argued against the necessity of widening the road in front of his
property. He again requested the Board waive the requirements of the widening.

The Board individually provided comments on their decision on this matter:

N. Teague — Inquired to J. Coyle how much of tree is in the ROW; J. Coyle said it is entirely in
public ROW. She noted she has been back and forth but is in favor of the widening for three reasons:
#1 future traffic planning #2 safety, the tree is a hazard and #3 the liability of not widening and
future issues.

S. Brown - also back and forth but is in favor as the engineer recommended the widening for safety.
S. Brown is concerned about the result of not widening the road and is looking to promote as much
safety as possible.

M. Boucher — not in favor of widening; keep the tree.

J. Pennington — also back and forth but the road would be three different widths so for this stretch to
be widened, will it make a difference? He was not convinced that removing tree will make a
difference.

A. Templeton — while safety is a concern, she was also concerned that if we widen the road more
people may travel the road. She also noted that the preserved land won’t be developed.

M. Boucher made a motion not to widen the road. J. Pennington seconded. M. Boucher, J. Pennington,
and A. Templeton voted yay and S. Brown and N. Teague voted nay. Motion passed (3-2).

L. Stauffer noted that it is not going to get any cheaper to complete the road widening project, so the
Board can decide not to widen and release Mr. Herman’s security. S, Brown made a motion to release
the escrow to Mr. Herman. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (5-0). Solicitor Asteak noted that we
may want an agreement and recording of a document.

J. Caffese noted that the funds may be connected to land. Solicitor Asteak reported he will review prior
to the release of funds.

L. Stauffer said a condition of lot line adjustment was road widening; Solicitor Asteak noted he will
review resolution.

US Geological Field Survey at Newburg Park — L. Stauffer reviewed the June email request from Paige
Koenig. Questions from Board were forwarded to her and she provided responses. J. Pennington made a
