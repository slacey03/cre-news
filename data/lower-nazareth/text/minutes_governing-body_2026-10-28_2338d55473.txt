BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
MINUTES
October 28, 2026

The Lower Nazareth Township Board of Supervisors held a public meeting on Wednesday, October 28,
2026, Chairperson Templeton called the meeting to order at 7:00PM.

Present were:

Amy L. Templeton - Chairperson Gary Asteak - Solicitor

Martin J Boucher - Vice-Chairman Justin Coyle - Township Engineer

James S. Pennington - Supervisor Lori A. Stauffer - Manager

Stephen A. Brown - Supervisor Tammi Dravecz - Secretary/Treasurer

Nancy A. Teague - Supervisor Lori Seese - Planning/Zoning Administrator

*** Executive session was held prior to this evening’s meeting for pending litigation. No action was taken. ***

Agenda Amendment and Approval

L. Stauffer requested to add Bethlehem Township Sewer under Manager’s Report. N. Teague made a
motion to approve the amended agenda. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

M. Boucher made a motion to approve October 20" minutes. J. Pennington seconded. A. Templeton
abstained. Motion passed (4-0).

available on the website.

for recommendation for BOS to act on at the November 11" meeting. N. Teague made a motion to
forward on to the EAC for recommendation. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Parks and Recreation — S. Brown noted that this Saturday is Halloween with Trick-or- Treat from 6pm —

8pm. HVFC and CRPD will be handing out candy. Next event is the Tree Lighting on December 4".

anniversary and noted they will be selling bricks for the memorial.

Solicitor’s Report

SALDO Authorization for Advertisement — Solicitor Asteak discussed the new document that is ready to
be advertised for adoption at the December 9" meeting. J. Pennington made a motion to authorize
advertisement. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

No Truck Traffic Ordinance #265-10-26 — Solicitor Asteak discussed the advertised ordinance for
consideration for adoption. S. Brown made a motion to adopt the ordinance. M. Boucher seconded.

Public Comments: Resident inquired how the 9,000lbs limit was established. J. Coyle stated that is the
limit in the current ordinance. J. Coyle stated that the road is not posted for tonnage; signs are posted to
limit truck traffic. Resident also inquired what is considered “local deliveries?” J. Coyle noted deliveries
on that road only, not a pass through.

M. Boucher suggested an increased weight to 16,000-17,000lbs. L. Stauffer stated if it is the Board’s
desire to amend, they need to withdraw their motions and make motion for Solicitor Asteak to amend
the ordinance.

Board of Supervisors
October 28, 2026


The Board withdrew their motions. N. Teague made a motion to have J. Coyle amend the report for
Solicitor Asteak to amend the ordinance. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Engineer’s Report

HVAC — 623 Municipal Drive — J. Coyle reviewed his letter recommending the Board reject the bids
making adjustment and then rebid the project. J. Pennington made a motion to reject the bids and rebid
the project. S. Brown seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Butztown Road Change Order #4 — J. Coyle was happy to report that Butztown Road will be reopening
tomorrow afternoon. This change order is for the pavement restoration following the trenches needed for
the water main relocation. J. Pennington made a motion to approve change order #4 in the amount of
$15,878.56. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Butztown Road Payment #6 — J. Coyle reviewed his letter recommending the release of payment #6 in
the amount of $111.402.74. S. Brown made a motion to release payment #6. J. Pennington seconded.
Motion passed (5-0).

Township Line Road Change Order #3 — J. Coyle was also happy to report that Township Line Road
will be reopening tomorrow. He commented that H&K did a wonderful job. L. Stauffer noted that a
majority of this project will be covered by the multi-modal grant ($1,250,000) The balance will be split
between East Allen and Lower Nazareth Townships. M. Boucher made a motion to approve change
order #3. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Foxwood Farms Traffic Study Report — J. Coyle reviewed the requested traffic study for Rockbridge
Road. He noted that stop signs are not used to regulate speed and traffic volumes do not warrant a three-
way stop; therefore, we need to find ways to remedy speed; options include striping and speed feedback
sign placement.

Michael Metzger, Foxwood Farms resident, was present. He said he observed the township and school
meeting and the discussion of kids walking to school. Mr. Metzger noted it is low because of the traffic
in the neighborhood. He believes the stripping and the feedback sign may improve the situation.

A. Templeton inquired in Public Works could complete the line striping. L. Stauffer noted that we A-1is
hired to complete stripping; but that striping has already been completed for this year. L. Stauffer noted
that movement of the speed signs is at the Board’s discretion so we can place it in this location. N.
Teague asked to have Colonial Regional stage in the neighborhood to discourage speeding.

Gradwohl Switch Speed Limit Report — J. Coyle reviewed his report recommending speed be reduced
from 35 mph to 25 mph. S. Brown made a motion to have Solicitor Asteak draft an ordinance to reduce
the speed on Gradwohl between Route 191 and Newburg Road to 25 mph. N. Teague seconded. Motion
passed (5-0).

Township Line Road Payment #1 — J. Coyle reviewed his letter for payment #1 in the amount of
$23,253. This section was added to the project, and the cost will be split between Hanover and Lower

Manager’s Report

Newburg Intersection — PennDOT Offer — L. Stauffer reviewed the PennDOT offer. N. Teague made a
motion to authorize the Chair to execute agreements with PennDOT. S. Brown seconded. Motion passed
(5-0).

Georgetown Manor Sewer Hardship Application & Creation of Hardship Program — L. Stauffer noted
that we have a reviewed letter from resident requesting assistance in complying with the requirement to

connect by the end of the year. L. Stauffer noted that NBMA has a hardship program, but it has a
difficult application and unclear parameters. Solicitor Asteak suggested that we create our own hardship
program. J. Pennington made a motion to authorize L. Stauffer and Solicitor Asteak to create an
application and parameters for a hardship program. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

2026 Road Project — Georgetown Manor Authorization for Payment — L. Stauffer noted that the
Georgetown Manor Road work is complete and that H&K did an incredible job. Amount due for this

portion of the project is $354,161.48. N. Teague made a motion to release payment. M. Boucher
seconded. Motion passed (5-0). L Stauffer reported that work on Country Club Road has begun and will
be completed shortly.

Employee Health Insurance — L. Stauffer noted that the quote for our health insurance renewal had a
51% increase, so we looked at alternative options. The Board selected a plan with deductibles and will
supplement employees with an HRA account. L. Stauffer met with employees today. J. Pennington
made a motion to authorize the change from current plan to a new plan with deductibles. M. Boucher
seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

2027 Draft Budget — Authorization for Advertisement — L. Stauffer report that the $44,000 savings from
changing the health insurance plan was moved to other expense lines; $25,000 to police and $19,000 to
winter maintenance/salt. M/ Boucher made a motion to authorize the 2027 budget for advertisement. N.
Teague seconded. Motion passed (5-0). L. Stauffer noted the budget will be on the December 9" agenda
for adoption.

Time Extension Report — L. Stauffer represented the time extension report.

Computer Purchase — L. Stauffer reviewed the proposal to replace 2 computers in public works and one
for meeting room with a monitor at the desk. The total cost is $7,249.91. N. Teague made a motion to

Bethlehem Township Sewer — L. Stauffer discussed the prior purchase of capacity from the City of

and get security refunded this work must be completed. R. Moore stated he feels the work should have
been done by now and wants the Township to use the security to get the work done so it will be done
correctly.
