LOWER NAZARETH TOWNSHIP
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
MINUTES
July 22, 2026

The Lower Nazareth Township Board of Supervisors held a public meeting on Wednesday, July 22, 2026,
Chairperson Templeton called the meeting to order at 7:02PM.

Present were:

Amy L. Templeton - Chairperson Justin Coyle - Township Engineer
Martin J Boucher - Vice-Chairman Lori A. Stauffer - Manager

James S. Pennington - Supervisor Tammi Dravecz - Secretary/Treasurer
Nancy A. Teague - Supervisor

** Chairperson Templeton reported an executive session to discuss legal matters was held prior to tonight’s
meeting **

Agenda Amendment and Approval

L. Stauffer requested to add Commerce Park Drive and BTVFC under Manager’s Report. J. Pennington
made a motion to approve the amended agenda. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Approval of Minutes
Approval of the meeting minutes was tabled.

Reports - No reports

Supervisor’s Comments

Open Space Preservation — No report

Parks and Recreation — No report

Library News — No report

CRPC / HVFC - M. Boucher noted CRPC meets on August 3
COG — No report

Reconsideration of Installation of Flock ALPR on Route 248 — Detective Chris Templeton appeared to
answer concerns and inquiries from the Board of Supervisors and residents regarding the Flock cameras
that were installed by Northampton County within Lower Nazareth. N. Teague inquired on some of the
issues including use and policies surrounding the cameras as well as concerns regarding use of the data
collected. Following the discussion, the Board thanked Detective Templeton, and no action was taken.

Board of Supervisors Minutes
July 22, 2026


Subdivision and Land Development

Fence Waiver — 4437 Cottonwood Drive — The property owner present to discuss fence waiver request.
M. Boucher made a motion to grant the fence waiver. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Mikol Preliminary/Final Minor Subdivision — Resolution #LNT-25-26 — The applicant, Andrea
Pokowski, and engineer Brian Pysher were present. J. Coyle reviewed his letter and had recommended

approval. The requested waivers listed below are recommended for approval by the Planning
Commission and J. Coyle.

#1 Article 7, Section 741.6: Requirement for the right-of-way and cartway of Hollo Road to be widened
#2 Article 7, Section 741.10: Requirement for curbing to be installed along Hollo Road

M. Boucher made a motion to approve Resolution #LNT-25-26 and grant requested waivers. J.
Pennington seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Draft SALDO Update Review — L. Stauffer said the Board should have received a digital and paper
copy of draft SALDO. Please provide comments to L. Seese or me; all comments are due to AJ by to
end of the month.

The next steps L. Seese will present the final draft to the Planning Commission in August. In September,
the Board with authorize it be forwarded to LVPC for review.

Fehnel Minor Subdivision Waiver Request — Applicants Matthew Fehnel and Nicole Somers were
present to make request recreation fees be waived as they are developing on a property that has already
been developed. They are replacing an existing structure with a new home. L. Seese had no comments. J
Coyle no comments. N. Teague made a motion to grant the waiver request. M. Boucher seconded.
Motion passed (4-0).

Solicitor’s Report

Land Use Assumptions — Amended Resolution #LNT-24-26 — Solicitor Asteak reported that the Board
previously adopted Resolution LNT-22-24. There were minor calculation updates on July 10". Peter
O’Halloran from Carroll Engineering was present via Zoom for questions. J. Pennington made a motion
to approve Resolution #LNT-24-26. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Monocacy Creek Jaindl — Lower Nazareth Settlement Agreement Extension — Solicitor Asteak drafted
an Addendum to Monocacy Creek Settlement Agreement. Jaindl had a time limit to proceed or
withdraw. He has requested extension 180 days through January 14, 2027. The extension is necessary
due to pending litigation and issues involving sewer service accessibility.

Charlie Iszard, 363 Little Creek Drive, questioned whether this extension was legally permissible and
formally objected to the extension. He provided a written statement and questioned...

#1 Was the Extension Request Timely? Answer was yes

Board of Supervisors
July 22, 2026


#2 What is the Good Faith Showing? Solicitor Asteak answered the applicant filed prelim plan,
interested parties were for review, and a meeting was held with BT Sewer Authority.

#3 What is the Sewer Status? L. Stauffer noted that the Township’s role was to coordinate meetings; a
lot has gone on between the two parties, but we are not privy to their conversations.

Solicitor Asteak noted that the applicant has been moving forward with this complex situation, litigation
adding to the complexity. N. Teague noted that we are receiving regular reports on progress.

#4 What Happens at the End of the Extension? Can they provide another extension? Solicitor Asteak
said we will consider it at that time.

C. Iszard’s closing statement was that the Board said they would do better moving forward; the Board
should take this opportunity and not grant the extension. N. Teague clarified that she meant the Board
would do better keeping the public informed on meeting agenda items/actions.

J. Pennington made a motion to execute the addendum as presented. N. Teague seconded. Motion
passed (4-0).

Engineer’s Report

J. Coyle presented his June and July Report for the Board.

Easton Eastgate (Popeye’s) — Commence Maintenance & Letter of Credit Reduction — J. Coyle reviewed

his letter recommending a reduction in security and commence maintenance. M. Boucher made a
motion to reduce security and commence maintenance. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Bill Hunter Detention Basin — J. Coyle reviewed his letter regarding the failed discharge pipe. He
believes that given the failed pipe structure discharges stormwater leading from the public right-of-
way/roadways, the Township may wish to consider performing the necessary repair work with public
forces. J. Coyle noted that the basin is well maintained by property owners.

Solicitor Asteak reviewed the letter and consulted with J. Coyle and agrees that Lower Nazareth can
assume responsibility.

N. Teague made a motion to make the necessary repairs to the basin. M. Boucher seconded. Motion
passed (4-0).

Manager’s Report

Greenways, Trail and Recreation Grant — Amended Resolution #LNT-26-26 — L. Stauffer noted that she
received a call from DCED saying we could apply for more money. N. Teague made a motion to

approve Resolution #LNT-26-26. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Board of Supervisors
July 22, 2026


Township Line Road — L. Stauffer presented a quote for the additional in Hanover Township. Hanover
Engineering estimate is $23,253; our portion would be $11,621. L. Stauffer requested authorization up
to $12,000 which will be paid from the Highway Reconstruction account.

M. Boucher made a motion to authorize up to $12,000. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Hahn’s Meadow Farm Lease — L. Stauffer noted that at a previous meeting, the Board requested
Solicitor Asteak to prepare a bid to lease to a farmer. The bid did not get out this year due to timing. At
the last meeting grass and wildflower planting was suggestion. L. Stauffer requested a decision so we
know how to proceed. M. Boucher, plant wildflowers. He noted that if we lease that land, then we pay
taxes on that portion. J. Pennington noted that this land isn’t next to another farm. M. Boucher made a
motion to rescind the previous motion and to plant meadowgrass and wildflowers. J. Pennington
seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Time Extension Report — L. Stauffer represented the time extension report.

BTVFC -— L. Stauffer met with BTVFC EMS/Ambulance. They expressed interest in participating in our
community events. L. Stauffer stated that we have an agreement with BTVFC that we pay them $30,000
a year for service and they waive charges to residents. They stated that they want to abide but requested
an amendment to the agreement to deal with lift assist calls. Currently they have 3 residents that call for
home health aide services. The amendment would add language that lift assist calls after 5 times in a
year will be charged $105 per incident. J. Pennington made a motion authorizing Solicitor Asteak to
draft an amendment to agreement. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Commerce Park — Staff held a meeting today to discuss adopting this 20-year-old road. The developer
offered to make road “improvements” prior to accepting roads into our system. L. Stauffer feels this is
just a band-aid. She believes reconstruction of the road is necessary, prior to accepting the road;
otherwise, it should remain a private road. She wants to protect the township from a major repair costing
taxpayers. All Board agreed.

Payment of the Bills

M. Boucher made a motion to pay the bills as presented. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (4-0).
Courtesy of the Floor - No comments

Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 8:14 pm
