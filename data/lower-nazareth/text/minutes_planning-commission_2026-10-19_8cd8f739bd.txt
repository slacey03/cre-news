SUBDIVISION/LAND  DEVELOPMENT
McDonald's  Preliminary/Final  Land  Development  &  Subdivision  Plan  -859  Nar,areth  Pike
Present  for  the Application:  Justin  Klee,  P.E.,  Bohler  Engineering
Justin  Klee  provided  a summary  update  of  the project.  Zoning  relief  has been  obtained  for  the
drive-thru  lane  and  emergency  fire  lanes  since  their  last  Planning  Commission  appearance
Waivers  were discussed,  particularly  Section  400 for preliminary/final  submission;  Section
733.621  related  to distance  for  a driveway  centerline;  a deferral  from  Section  749.'l  for  installing
the sidewalk;  and Section  774.3'l  to permit  a detention  basin  to dewater  longer  than  24 hours.
Township  Engineer  Justin  Coyle  did  not  have  any  additional  comments  or concerns.
The  motion  to recommend  granting  waivers  related  to the McDonald's  Preliminary/Final  Land
Development  & Subdivision  Plan,  particularly  Section  400 for  preliminary/final  submission;
Section  733.621  related  to distance  for  a driveway  centerline;  a deferral  from  Section  749.1 for
installing  the sidewalk;  and  Section  774.3  1 to permit  a detention  basin  to dewater  longer  than  24
hours  was  moved  by Hugh  Harris  and seconded  Bert  Smalley.  The  motion  carried  unanimously.
The  motion  to recommend  approval  of  the McDonald's  Preliminary/Final  Land  Development  &
Subdivision  Plan,  provided  all conditions  of  the letters  from  Carroll  Engineering,  dated  October

15, 2026,  Zoning  Officer's  letter  dated  October  15, 2026,  and Hecktown  Fire  Company,  dated
October  16, 2026  letter  are addressed  was  moved  by Hugh  Harris  and seconded  by Bert  Smalley.
The  motion  carried  unanimously.
Justin  Klee  shared  this  development  is slated  for  construction  in 2028  due to expected  approval
lead  time  from  PADOT.
No  comments  under  COURTESY  OF  THE  FLOOR.
