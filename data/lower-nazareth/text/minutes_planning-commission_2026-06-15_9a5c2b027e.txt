SUBDIVISION/LAND  DEVELOPMENT
Minor  Subdivision  of  Land  for  Jeffrey  Mikol,  et al  -  Preliminary/Final  Plan
Present  for the Application:  Applicants  Andrea  Polkowski  and Jeffrey  Mikol,  and Engineer
Brian  Pysher
Pysher  introduced  the  project,  which  proposes  subdividing  a 1.789  acre  lot from  the
approximately  ninety  (90) acre agricultural  lot. The farm  has an agricultural  conservation
easement,  which  permits  subdivision  of  two  (2) acres  for  a single-family  dwelling  (SFD). The lot
and  new  SFD  will  be for  Polkowski  and  her  family  to reside  in.
Coyle  read  the June 12, 2026  Carroll  Engineering  review  letter  aloud.  The Applicants  have
already  obtained  the necessary  variances  from  the Zoning  Hearing  Board.  Waiver  requests  were
submitted  for curbing  and widening  the right-of-way  and cartway  on Hollo  Road. Coyle
referenced  the plan  as straightforward,  with  no technical  reviesv  items  regarding  stormwater,
traffic  or  utilities.
Pysher  stated  that  septic  testing  has been  completed  in coordination  with  the Township  Sewage
Enforcement  Officer  (SEO)  and  will  be reflected  on the  revised  plans.
Coyle  advised  that  discussion  will  need  to be held  on  the waiver  requests.

Capecci  questioned  why  the  new  lot was  not two  (2) acres.  Pysher  explained  that the
conservation  easement  reads to the centerline  of  Hollo  Road. The new  lot area had to be
calculated  from  centerline,  which  conflicts  with  the Zoning  Ordinance  requirement  of  calculating
the lot  area from  the right-of-way.
Harris  asked  if  there  were  any plans  to widen  Hollo  Road.  Coyle  responded  that  he was not
currently  aware  of  any.
Seese read  her June 10, 2026  review  letter  aloud.  Seese requested  that  the zoning  table  on the
plans  be updated  to still  show  the compliant  dimensions.  The proposed  SD will  contain  an
accessory  dwelling  unit  (ADU),  and the zoning  table  needs to reflect  a SFD  with  ADU.  Seese
also  noted  that  the Applicant  will  be responsible  for  payment  of  tlie  Open  Space  Fee.
Pysher  responded  that  he has already  drafted  the requested  zoning  table  updates  and they  will  be
shown  on the next  plan  submission.
Seese asked  if  residual  lot  testing  was  performed  and Pysher  confirmed  it had been. The septxc
testing  will  be shown  on the next  plan  submission.
Capecci  expressed  she had  no issue  with  waiving  the curbing  but  believed  that  they  needed  to be
consistent  with  widening  the  roadway.  Capecci  suggested  they  collect  an escrow  for the
widening  of  the roadway  if  there  were  no current  plans.
Coyle  clarified  that  the right-of-way  would  be widened  from  sixteen  (16)  feet  to thirty  (30)  feet.
Capecci  stated  she thought  it was  fair  to take  the additional  right-of-way.
Seese pointed  out  that  they  cannot  take  additional  right-of-way  on the preserved  portion  of  the
property.  Pysher  agreed.
Discussion  was held  on whether  the additional  right-of-way  can be taken  without  condemning
the  property,  as the property  is preserved  with  a conservation  easement.
Harris  asked  if  the issue of  requiring  additional  right-of-way  had arisen  with  other  preserved
properties  and Seese was  not  able  to provide  an example.
Dulac  commented  that  if  they  cannot  widen  all of  Hollo  Road,  it would  not  make  sense to require
it on this  property  alone.
Capecci  stated  that  if  the property  was  not  preserved,  she would  have  liked  to see it taken.
Harris  stated  that  since  there  was  no curbing  in the vicinity,  he did  not have  an issue  with  the
waiver  request.
Smalley  agreed  that curbing  was  not  necessary  and that  widening  the roadway  with  preserved
farmland  on  both  sides  did  not  make  sense.
Motion  to recommend  approval  of  the Minor  Subdivision  for  Jeffrey  Mikol,  et al, and approval
of  the waiver  requests  for  Section  741.6  and Section  741.2  requiring  widening  of  the right-of-
way  and cartway,  and Section  741.40  requiring  curbing,  with  the condition  that  the comments
contained  in  the Jiu'ie 12, 2026  review  letter  from  Carroll  Engineering  and June 10, 2026  review
letter  from  Lori  B. Seese are adequately  addressed  was  moved  by Harris  and  seconded  by Dulac.
The  motion  carried  unanimously.

Moyer  introduced  the project,  which  proposed  subdividing  two  (2) acres  for  a SFD  from  the
approximately  forty  (40)  acre  lot  and  keeping  the  remainder  of  the  property  in agriculture.  The
farm  is enrolled  in  Act  319  and  only  two  (2)  acres  of  land  can  be disturbed  without  breaking  the
covenant.  The  lot  and  new  SFD  will  be for  Fehnel  and  his  fianc6e  to reside  in.
Moyer  stated  that  they  will  do a grading  plan  at the  time  of  constructing  the  new  single-family
dwelling.  Fehnel  stated  that  the  existing  trailer  on the  property  will  not  be removed  until  after
they  have  received  approval  and  permits  for  the  new  lot  and  home.
Coyle  read  his June 11, 2026  review  letter  aloud.  The  Applicants  have  already  obtained  the
necessary  dimensional  variances  from  the  Zoning  Hearing  Board.  Coyle  stated  that  no waivers
had  been  requested,  and  a waiver  will  be required  for  the  plan  scale.  Coyle  noted  that  widening
of  the  right-of-way  and  cartway,  and  curbing  will  be items  of  discussion.
Coyle  noted  that  the property  does  not  have  a conservation  easement,  but  it is on the Official
Map  as property  to be under  development  right  acquisition.  Seese added  that  the  property  was
enrolled  in  the  Agricultural  Security  Area.
Harris  asked  on the status  of  the Township  Line  Road  paving  project.  Coyle  replied  that  bids
paving  should  take  place  sometime  in  July  2026.  The  width  of  the  roadway  will  not  be increased.
Seese  read  her  June  10, 2026  letter  aloud.  Seese  noted  that  removal  of  the existing  trailer  is a
condition  of  the Zoning  Hearing  Board's  decision.  Seese noted  that  the property  currently  has
four  (4) accessory  structures.  The  Zoning  Ordinance  only  permits  two  (2) accessory  structures.
Fehnel  replied  that  they  will  remove  the  necessary  structures  to address  the  cornrnent  and  reach
compliance.  The  structures  to be removed  will  be noted  on  the  next  submission.
Seese  stated  that  she had  spoken  with  the  Applicant  on  the  possibility  of  requesting  a waiver  for
septic  testing  on the residual  tract.  Seese confirmed  that  waivers  were  previously  granted  for
similar  applications.  Seese  also  noted  that  the  Applicant  will  be responsible  for  payment  of  the
Open  Space  Fee.
Coyle  requested  the  PC's  opinion  on  the  widening  of  the Township  right-of-way.  Moyer  stated
that  they  planned  on  seeking  waivers,  but  did  not  plan  to include  them  until  the  next  submission.
Crook  cornrnented  that  they  should  widen  the  right-of-way  on  Township  Line  Road,  but  not  the
cartway.
Harris  agreed,  especially  since  the  road  is being  paved  and  there  is no  widening  proposed.
Capecci  asked  for  clarification  that  the right-of-way  will  be on both  lots  and not  just  the  new
flag  lot.  Coyle  confirmed  it will  be for  both  lots.
Coyle  advised  that  no action  should  be taken  until  waivers  are formally  requested.  Crook  agreed
the  plans  should  be tabled.
Harris  expressed  that  curbing  is not  necessary,  and no escrow  should  be required  for  future
widening.
Motion  to table  the 564 Township  Road  Minor  Subdivision  plans  was moved  by Harris  and
Seconded  by  Dulac.  The  motion  carried  unanimously.
