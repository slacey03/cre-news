The Lower Nazareth Township Board of Supervisors held a public meeting on Wednesday, June 10, 2026,
Chairperson Templeton called the meeting to order at 7:15 PM.

Present were:

Martin Boucher - Chairperson Gary Asteak - Solicitor
Nancy Teague - Supervisor Justin Coyle - Engineer
James S. Pennington - Supervisor Lori A. Stauffer - Manager
Lori Seese - Planning/Zoning Administrator

Agenda Approval

L. Stauffer stated a request came in to be placed on the agenda by Mr. Huratiak; however, it was already
after the agenda had been posted on the website. Mr. Huratiak is requesting to be placed on the agenda
to request placement of his recreation fees in an escrow account. No motion was made.

J. Pennington made a motion to approve the agenda as originally submitted. N. Teague seconded.
Motion passed (3-0).

Approval of Minutes

The meeting minutes of the meeting of June 10" were tabled.

Reports

The Financial Report for May 2026 has been submitted and is available on the website.

Supervisor’s Comments

Library News — N. Teague provided an update on the summer events at the library. She also reported
that she attended an event at the library to celebrate the opening of their new technology center that was
made possible via a $50,000 grant from Astound.

CRPC / HVFC — M. Boucher reported he attended the police meeting and provided statistics on
violations and criminal activity.

COG - J. Pennington reported the COG discussed the curative amendment submitted to Lower Nazareth
Township.

Subdivision and Land Development

4454 Fieldstone — L. Seese stated it is the property owner’s desire to place a fence in the easement at the
rear of their property. N. Teague questioned the property owners if they understand that if a time comes
where the Township needs to access the easement, they may be required to remove the fence. The
owners acknowledged that they understood. N. Teague made a motion to grant the fence waiver for
4454 Fieldstone as requested. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (3-0).

591 Rockbridge — L. Seese stated it is the property owner’s desire to place a fence in the easement at the
side of the property. N. Teague questioned the property owners if they understand that if a time comes
where the Township needs to access the easement, they may be required to remove the fence. The
owners acknowledged that they understood. J. Pennington made a motion to grant the fence waiver for
591 Rockbridge as requested. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (3-0).

Solicitor’s Report

Nuisance Amendment Ordinance — G. Asteak stated the draft ordinance was advertised on June 4, 2026.
The proposed ordinance before the Board amends the existing Nuisance Ordinance that was adopted in
October 2025 by the Board of Supervisors to provide regulations for junk vehicles, regulations for
recreational vehicles, and regulations pertaining to exterior property infestations. There was no public
comment on the proposed ordinance. J. Pennington made a motion to adopt Ordinance 263-06-26.

Engineer’s Report

Newburg Park Concrete Project — J. Coyle reported he has received a payment application for the
concrete work at the splash pad. The payment application includes the original contract and change
orders 1 and 2 for a total of $93,782.86. The Township will hold a 5% retainage. It is his
recommendation to pay the amount requested. N. Teague made a motion to authorize the payment to
Delaware Environmental Construction Services in the amount of $93,782.86. J. Pennington seconded.
Motion passed (3-0).

Newburg Park Tree Planting — J. Coyle reported he has received a payment application for the
remaining balance of the Newburg Park Tree project in the amount of $8,913. The contractor planted a
total of 82 trees and 13 of the trees required replacement. It is his recommendation to pay the amount
requested. N. Teague made a motion to authorize the payment to Delaware Environmental Construction
Services in the amount of $8,913. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (3-0).

Shammy Shine Letter of Credit Reduction — J. Coyle reported pursuant to site inspections of the land
development site improvements, it is his recommendation to reduce the letter of credit by $106,090. J.
Pennington made a motion to authorize the letter of credit reduction in the amount of $106,090. N.
Teague seconded. Motion passed (3-0).

Faulkner Subaru — J. Coyle reported pursuant to site inspections of the land development site
improvements, it is his recommendation to reduce the letter of credit in the amount of $1,415,422.08 and
that the project be moved into the 18-month maintenance period. N. Teague made a motion to authorize

Appointment of SEO — L. Stauffer reported Hanover Township has hired a new member of their team;

Motion passed (3-0).

Courtesy of the Floor

Bill and Christi Hunter, 4631 Ash Drive, appeared to discuss a significant maintenance issue in the
detention basin located on their property. Mr. Hunter stated they have spent over $27,000 maintaining
and mowing the basin over the time they owned the property. They provided the board with photos of
the issues that need repair, including what appears to be a sinkhole opening under the pipe. The outlet
pipe is more than 14” below the rip rap. The cavity under the pipe is approximately 30” deep near the
edge. Additionally, there has been structural separation of the exit pipe. The detention basin is not
functioning as designed. They are requesting support and help financially. The detention basin is
approximately two acres in size. N. Teague explained the Township will investigate if there are issues
in the public road that we can repair to assist; however, public funds cannot be used on private property.
The Board requested J. Coyle to investigate and report back.

John Caffese, 634 Country Club Road, appeared to discuss the widening of the Country Club Road
issue. He reminded the Board that he initiated litigation against the Township over the preservation of
the Catalpa tree. He again requested the Board waive the requirements of the widening and restated his
reasoning from prior meetings. N. Teague stated since litigation is involved, she did not feel it was
appropriate for the Board to be engaging in this conversation.

Justin Huratiak, developer of Nazareth Nest, appeared before the Board hoping for clarification of the
Board’s motions at the June 10" meeting. He requested the Board authorize placing his recreation fees
in an escrow account while he seeks judicial review. He further stated he felt this was a reasonable
request. No motion was made.

Adjournment — The meeting adjourned at 7:51 pm
