LOWER NAZARETH TOWNSHIP
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
MINUTES
September 9, 2026

The Lower Nazareth Township Board of Supervisors held a public meeting on Wednesday, September 9,
2026, Chairperson Templeton called the meeting to order at 7:00PM.

Present were:

Amy L. Templeton - Chairperson Justin Coyle - Township Engineer

Martin J Boucher - Vice-Chairman Lori A. Stauffer - Manager

James S. Pennington - Supervisor Tammi Dravecz - Secretary/Treasurer

Stephen A. Brown - Supervisor Lori Seese - Planning/Zoning Administrator
Nancy A. Teague - Supervisor

*** Supervisor Brown arrived at 7:34pm during Engineer’s Report ***

Agenda Amendment and Approval

L. Stauffer requested to add Northampton/Lehigh County and Monroe County LSA Grant Resolutions
under her reports. J. Pennington made a motion to approve the amended agenda. N. Teague seconded.
Motion passed (4-0).

Proclamation — Chief James DePalma

A. Templeton called Chief DePalma to the front to present him with a Proclamation and Plaque to thank
him for his years of service.

Approval of Minutes

J. Pennington made a motion to approve August 12" minutes. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Reports
Hecktown Fire Incident Report for August 2026 was presented and is available on the website.
Supervisor’s Comments

Open Space Preservation — no report
Parks and Recreation — no report
Surrey Glen Little Library Request — discussion under Manager’s Report
Library News — N. Teague noted that at the latest meeting there was budget discussion. The budget
should be ready to present to Lower Nazareth for review in a few weeks.

Board of Supervisors Minutes
September 9, 2026


CRPC / HVFC — J. Pennington attended the CRPC meeting which held budget and police contract
discussions.

COG — J. Pennington noted that ordinance changes for Upper Nazareth and Tatamy were discussed at
the last COG meeting.

A. Templeton shared that recently Supervisor Boucher’s wife Kim unexpectedly passed away and asked
the Board to hold a moment of silence.

Subdivision and Land Development

Henry Yeska Minor Subdivision Plan Withdrawal — L. Stauffer presented a letter from H. Yeska
withdrawing his proposed subdivision plan. N. Teague made a motion to acknowledge and accept the
plan withdrawal. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Northwood Improvements Agreement — Authorization to Execute — L. Stauffer noted this agreement is
required prior to plan recording. J. Pennington made a motion to authorize execution upon receipt of
letter of credit. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

564 Township Line Road Minor Subdivision — Resolution #LNT-32-26 — Matt Fehnel and Nicole
Somers were in attendance to answer any questions. L. Stauffer noted that they are swapping one

residential unit for another; replacing the trailer with a house. Numerous waivers/deferrals were
requested, and L. Stauffer believes the curbing deferral that was requested should be granted as a
waiver. N. Teague made a approve Resolution #LNT-32-26 with section 1D being a waiver.

M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Cottages at Meadowstone Presentation — Chris Brown and Phil Malitsch presented an amended plan
changing the proposed dwellings from duplexes to townhouses. The proposed zoning amendment is
from TD-4 to TD-5.

C. Brown discussed the improvements with this proposal including increased open space, increased
parking, maintaining unit count, and avoiding the cul-de-sac issue. Townhouses also allow for the
dwellings in their entirety to remain outside the easement

J. Pennington inquired on the road width; J. Coyle said 32-foot cartway. J, Pennington was glad to see
increased parking as sufficient parking is an issue for townhouses. Streets become filled when there is
not enough parking.

J. Pennington wants that open space piece dedicated as open space, so it never gets developed. M.
Boucher agreed. P. Malitsch noted they would be willing to memorialize that the open space so it
remains open even though the zoning would allow for additional dwellings.

The developer noted they intend to dedicate the roads to Lower Nazareth. The HOA will have a
contractor to plow parking lots and sometimes they include sidewalks.

Board of Supervisors
September 9, 2026


N. Teague suggested the open space piece be a playground. The developer was open to discussion.

Solicitor Asteak noted that the typical path is for the zoning map amendment to run parallel with the
land development plan.

Solicitor’s Report

Traffic Impact Ordinance - #Ord #264-09-26 — Solicitor Asteak reviewed the Board’s actions leading to
this ordinance; including the land use assumption, roadway sufficiency, and capital improvement plan
resolutions. The fee is $1,950.31 per weekday afternoon peak hour trip

Solicitor Asteak noted that this hearing was advertised and opened the floor for public comment. There
was no public comment, and no developers were present to oppose this ordinance.

J. Pennington made a motion to approve Ordinance #264-09-26. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed
(5-0).

Solicitor Asteak noted that the Commonwealth Court will entertain argument for the Jaindl warehouse
appeals next week.

Engineer’s Report

J. Coyle presented and reviewed his August Engineering Report.

*** S_ Brown arrived at 7:34pm***

LVHN - Earth Disturbance Work — Security Release — J. Coyle reviewed his letter recommending the

release of the $164,358 being held in security. N. Teague made a motion to release security. M. Boucher
seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

LVHN — Commence Maintenance Period — J. Coyle reviewed his letter noting that based upon their site
inspection the following items shall be added to the as-built plans:

1. The concrete patio and landscaping area located between the Patient Wing and the Cancer
Institute

2. The walking path which traverses around the entire hospital property

3. The gravel parking area to the southeast of the Hospital along Hecktown Road, adjacent to the
Bus

Also required is verification of the Approval of the Notice of Termination (NOT) for the project’s
National Pollution Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) Permit from PA DEP.

No action was taken. Upon receipt of these items, they will review and provide the Township with a
recommendation to commencing the maintenance period.

Board of Supervisors
September 9, 2026


Butztown Road Change Order — J. Coyle reviewed his letter recommending approval of the change
order in the amount of $16,784.47. J. Pennington made a motion to approve the change order. N. Teague
seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Township Line Road Change Order — J. Coyle reviewed his letter recommending approval of the change
order in the amount of $137,897. L Stauffer noted that the grant received was $81,018.50 more than the
original bid amount. With this change order, the cost is now $56,878.50 over grant. Splitting that
amount with East Allen, Lower Nazareth will owe $28,439.25. This will go before East Allen’s Board
for approval at their September 10" meeting. M. Boucher made a motion to approve the change order. S.
Brown seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Manager’s Report

SALDO Update — Authorization to forward to LNT PC & LVPC - L. Stauffer reported that the final
draft is ready for review and comments. J. Pennington made a motion to forward the SALDO update for
review and comments. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Newburg Intersection Properties — L. Stauffer met/spoke an individual in partnership with PADOT for
the properties located at the intersection Newburg Intersection. Previously they discussed providing
compensation for the needed land; but now are asking the Board to donate.

M. Boucher said we had an arrangement that we were to acquire the properties to proceed.

J. Pennington asked how much money it was for the land. L. Stauffer noted that we won’t know until
appraisals are completed.

A. Templeton said an option would be to wait for appraisals and then decide.
S. Brown was concerned that if we don’t say yes, it might delay the project

N. Teague doesn’t see a problem donating the needed land but is interested in the price. S. Brown agreed
and wanted to wait to see the appraisals.

2027 Defined Benefit MMO — J. Pennington made a motion to acknowledge receipt of the 2027 DB
MMO. S. Brown seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

2027 Defined Contribution MMO — S. Brown made a motion to acknowledge receipt of the 2027 DC
MMO. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Northampton/Lehigh County LSA Grant — Resolution #LNT-33-26 — HVFC Garage Doors — L Stauffer
noted that this grant application in the amount of $113,360 is for the replacement of 8 garage doors. N.

Teague made a motion to approve Resolution #LNT-33-26. S. Brown seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Board of Supervisors
September 9, 2026


Monroe County LSA Grant — Resolution #LNT-34-26 — HVFC Garage Doors — L Stauffer noted that
this grant application in the amount of $113,360 is for the replacement of 8 garage doors. N. Teague
made a motion to approve Resolution #LNT-34-26. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Surrey Glen Little Library Request — S. Brown noted this request was discussed Recreation Committee
meeting. They have had no issues with the current little library at Newburg Park. The committee thinks
this is a good idea. N. Teague made a motion to approve the Little Library at Surrey Glen. M. Boucher
seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Payment of the Bills

M. Boucher made a motion to pay the bills as presented. J. Pennington seconded. Motion passed (5-0).

Courtesy of the Floor

Bob Hoyer noted that he has known the Chief since he was an officer with Nazareth. He spoke highly of
Chief DePalma and wished him well in retirement.

Andrew Miklos thanked the township for obtaining the appraisal and requested a private meeting with
staff to discuss. Solicitor Asteak said he would be in touch with Attorney Kaplan to schedule a meeting.

A resident of Trio Fields expressed frustration with Kay Builders, their quality of work, and the length
of time it is taking to complete the project. He asked what incentive Kay has to get the work completed

and get out of the neighborhood.

J. Coyle said the contractor, while currently there, tends to come and go. J. Coyle noted they are in
constant contact to get this resolved. The Township will hold the security until work is done.

L. Stauffer noted that each phase of the project has security attached. The Township cashed in the
letters of credit because the bank would not extend them due to the length of time this project is taking.

N. Teague suggested he review meeting minutes or submit a right to know request if he is looking for
the amounts being held in security.

Adjournment
The meeting adjourned at 8:01 pm
Respectfully submitted by,

Tammi Dravecz

Secretary/Treasurer
