SUBDIVISION  AND  LAND  DEVELOPMENT
Everson  Tesla  Sketch  Plan  -  631 Municipal  Drive
Present  for  the Application:  Applicant  Greg  Naumovich  and  Mark  Leuthe  of  Lehigh  Engineering
Leuthe  announced  that  he did not realize  that  Section  650 of  the Lower  Nazareth  Township
Zoning  Ordinance  requires  a 100-foot  setback  in the TD-9  district  when  bordering  the TD-5
district.  The  increased  setback  will  eliminate  an  entire  section  of parking  and building,
drastically  altering  the plans.
Seese confirmed  the setback  is only  on the one side of  the property  that  is abutting  the residential
properties.  Leuthe  stated  they  will  need  to redesign  the  plans  for  the increased  setbacks.
Naumovich  introduced  himself  as the President  and CEO  of  Everson  Tesla.
Smalley  asked  what  kind  of  trucks  are used.  Naumovich  replied  they  use full  size tractor  trailers
and flat  bed  trucks.  The  trucks  access  the existing  facility  on Gremar  Road  3-4 times  a month.
The  existing  facility  borders  the proposed  project.  An  easement  to allow  truck  traffic  between  the
two  facilities  will  not  be possible  due  to the 100-foot  setback.
Smalley  asked  what  the hours  of  operation  are. Naumovich  replied  they  operate  24 hours  a day,
with  employees  working  two  12-hour  shifts.  The  new  facility  will  have  the same  hours.

Dulac  asked  for  confirmation  that  the existing  facility's  operations  will  not  change.  Naumovich
confirmed  that  the new  facility  will  be for  additional  manufacturing  capacity.
Leuthe  described  the project,  which  proposes  44,000  s.f. of  light  manufacturing  with  access onto
Municipal  Drive.  The  property  is proposed  to connect  to the existing  site's  parking  lot.
The  existing  site does not  have  the 100-foot  setback,  as it pre-dates  the residential  development.
Smalley  asked  if  complaints  are ever  received  for  the existing  Everson  Tesla  facility.  Seese
responded  that  no complaints  have  been  received  by  the Township.
Crook  asked  if  combining  the lots  would  be help'Jul.  Seese stated  the 100-foot  setback  would  still
be applicable.  Leuthe  noted  that  a stormwater  swale  runs  between  the lots.
Naurnovich  provided  an  overview  of what  Everson  Tesla  does.  Everson  Tesla  is  an
electromagnetic  company  and supplies  the semiconductor  and defense  industry.  They  are a key
supplier  in equipment  and chips.  The  ends  pieces  range  from  being  "very  small"  to requiring  an
oversized  load.  The  process  starts with  raw  materials  of  copper,  steel and insulation  to create
large  coils.  Neither  the raw  materials  or products  are considered  hazardous.
Capecci  asked  if  the facility  generates  noise.  Naumovich  explained  that  some  noise  is generated,
specifically  during  hot  summer  nights  because  they  work  with  the garage  doors  open  due to a
lack  of  air  conditioning  in  the facility.  The  new  facility  will  not  produce  an increase  in  noise.
Naumovich  explained  that they  would  construct  the building  in two  phases,  constructtng  an
initial  20,000  s.f. and  completing  the remaining  20,000  s.f. at a later  date.
Capecci  asked  how  many  employees  Everson  Tesla  has. Naumovich  replied  that  the existing
facility  has roughly  25 people.  The  new  facility  would  employee  25-30  employees  once  built  and
functioning  at capacity.
Naumovich  confirmed  they  intend  to purchase  the property  from  Selvaggio.
Dulac  asked  if  the process  creates  emissions.  Naumovich  replied  that  they  do not.
Harris  expressed  he has no concenis  with  the proposal,  but  that  they  will  need  to obtain  relief  to
build  on  the side  adjacent  to the TD-5.  Smalley  agreed.
Horwath  briefly  discussed  the September  17, 2026  letter  from  Carroll  Engineering.  Leuthe  did
not have  any questions  on the Carroll  Engineering  letter  or the September  18, 2026  letter  from
Seese.
Capecci  would  like  to see the plan  reworked  to meet  the zoning  setback  as much  as possible,
noting  that  the setback  is there  for  a reason,  but  expressed  that  she is not  in opposition  of  the
pro3ect.
