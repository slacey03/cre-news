Manager’s Report

2027 Budget Workshop — L. Stauffer presented the proposed $6,161,100 budget with a 0% tax increase.
She wanted to be mindful of our residents as they are receiving tax increases from both the school and
county. L. Stauffer reviewed page by page and answered questions regarding revenue and expense account
line items including water, fire, sewer, winter maintenance, and equipment repairs.

L. Stauffer reviewed bank balance and loan obligations. Lower Nazareth has Open Space and Township
Line Road loans. Hecktown Fire has two truck loans.

L. Stauffer reported that we purchased Laserfische and Amy and Eric are busy scanning documents into the
system. The process will take time but will be cost savings to the Township by completing this work in-
house.

She noted a large increase in employee health insurance and provided an alternative option for the Board’s
review. The plan would remain with Capital Blue but there would a $2,000 deductible for single employee
plans and $4,000 for a family plan. The current HRA funding would be increased to cover this deductible.
