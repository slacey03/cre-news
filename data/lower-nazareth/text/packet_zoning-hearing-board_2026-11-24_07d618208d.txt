NOTICE OF ZONING HEARING BOARD MEETING 
 
Public Notice by the Lower Nazareth Township Zoning Hearing Board is hereby given for a 
Zoning Hearing meeting to be held on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, at 6:30 p.m., in the Lower 
Nazareth Township Municipal Building, 623 Municipal Drive, Nazareth, PA, 18064 regarding the 
following zoning appeal  from the Lower Nazareth Township Zoning Ordinance, adopted 
December 13, 2023, last revised November 13, 2024: 
Mitchell D. Mogilski, Jr.  (ZA2026-09) – The applicant is appealing a Cease and Desist Notice  
from the Zoning Officer date d September 23, 2026 relative to Article V, Section 503.3.B.(1) 
restricting the maximum height of accessory structures to 25 feet but not being higher than the 
principal structure. The property location is 4318 Christian Springs Rd, Nazareth, PA 18064, 
specifically Tax Map #K7-17-11A, and is zoned TD-4, Village Residential. 
The applicants must appear in person at this Zoning Hearing.  Members of the public are invited 
to attend this meeting in person or virtually via the connection below.  Anyone who wishes to testify 
must appear in person.  A copy of the agenda will be posted on the Township website, 
www.lowernazareth.com, 24 hours prior to the hearing.   
Copies of the above-noted appeal application and supporting documents are available upon request.  
The Lower Nazareth Township Municipal Building is accessible to the physically challenged.  Any 
party interested in the above matter has the right to appear in person, by counsel or by representative 
and be heard at the time and place indicated above.
