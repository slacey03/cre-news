CURATIVE  AMENDMENT
CA2026-01  -  Nazareth  Area  School  District  - PC  Comment  Only
Present  for  the Application:  Julie  Wagner  Burkhart  of  Stevens  and  Lee  and  Victor  Scomillio  of
Holzinger,  Harak  & Scomillio,  counsel  for  Nazareth  Area  School  District  (NASD);  Matthew
Deschler,  Special  Counsel  for  the  Board  of  Supervisors  (BOS)
Asteak  provided  an introduction  of  the  application.  CA2026-01  was  filed  in April  of  2026  and
proposes  an amendment  to the  Zoning  Ordinance  to permit  schools  by Conditional  Use  in all
zoning  districts  and  to permit  schools  to be served  by  public  sewer  only  where  available.  The
application  was forwarded  to the Township  Planning  Commission  (PC),  the Lehigh  Valley
Planning  Commission  and  the  Nazareth  COG  for  review  and  comment.  The  curative  amendment
hearing  is scheduled  for  September  15,  2026  at 7:00PM.
Wagner  Burkhart  provided  the  PC  with  background  on  the  application.  The  subject  property  was
purchased  in 2025.  NASD  appeared  in  front  of  the  Board  of  Supervisors  multiple  times  in  2025
to discuss  the school's  proposal  for  the  property  prior  to making  the  purchase.  NASD  believes
their  legal  argument  will  allow  them  to preempt  local  zoning.  The  Township's  current  zoning

permits  for schools  in three (3) zoning  districts.  No properties  within  the three (3) zoning
districts  are feasible  for  NASD.  The  Township's  Act  537  plan  does not  provide  for  public  sewer
at the subject  property,  and  Wagner  Burkhart  believes  the Act  537 needs  to be updated.
Harris  questioned  whether  no other  area or property  can meet  the needs  of  the NASD.  Wagner
Bmkhart  replied  that  a search  was conducted  and the subject  property  had the best  features.
Expansion  of  the current  elementary  school  is not  considered  feasible.
Deschler  introduced  himself  to the PC as special  counsel  appointed  by the BOS  to defend  the
validity  of  the Zoning  Ordinance.  Deschler  is confident  that  he will  be able  to uphold  the validity
of  the Zoning  Ordinance.
Schwartz  presented  a slideshow  titled  "Curative  Amendment  101".  Schwartz  discussed  what  a
curative  amendment  application  was  and  the process  the application  follows.  Schwartz  discussed
the chronology  of  planning  activities,  including  adoption  and creation  of the Township's
Comprehensive  Plan,  Zoning  Ordinance  and Official  Map.
Schwartz  corrected  the prior  statement  of  Wagner  Burkhart  that  schools  are actually  permitted
within  four  (4) zoning  districts,  not  three  (3).
Schwartz  showed  several  maps  representing  the subject  property,  which  is located  in the TD-1
Agriculture  zoning  district  and the Rural  Resource  overlay  district.  The property  is located
outside  of  the designated  growth  area, has prime  agricultural  soils  and is listed  on the Official
Map  as "lands  to be acquired  for  parks".
Schwartz  summarized  that  the Zoning  Ordinance  and Official  Map  are the products  of  a long
period  of  intentional  planning  with  motivation  for  open  space  preservation,  preservation  of  the
character  of  the community,  and desire  to keep development  in areas with  the resources  to
support  it.
Deschler  stated  that  the Township  provides  ample  provisions  for  schools  to be located  in districts
other  than  the TD-1.  Deschler  noted  that  other  municipalities  in NASD,  such  as Upper  Nazareth
Township,  permit  schools  in all  zoning  districts.
Deschler  asked  that  a recommendation  be made  to the BOS  affirming  the validity  of  the Zoning
Ordinance  and  Map.
Dulac  was  involved  in putting  together  the Zoning  Ordinance  and Map  with  the goal  to preserve
open  space  and  supports  the Zoning  Ordinance  and  Map.
Smalley  has no problem  supporting  the Zoning  Ordinance  and  Map,  which  was  completed  using
sound  principles,  expert  guidance  and  was  completed  over  a long  period  of  time.
Harris  asked  if  NASD  had  access  to the Township's  Comprehensive  Plan,  Zoning  Ordinance  or
Official  Map  when  they  were being  updated.  Schwartz  confirmed  that the Municipalities
Planning  Code requires  draft  Comprehensive  Plans be shared  with  the School  District  for
comment.  NASD  received  the plan  for  review  but  did  not  provide  comment.
Harris  sees no reason  for  the Zoning  Ordinance  and  Map  to be changed.
Seese read  an email  dated  August  16, 2026  from  Capecci  aloud.  Capecci  is not in favor  of  the
curative  amendment  and does  not  support  the  requested  amendments.

SUBDIVISION  AND  LAND  DEVELOPMENT  ORDINANCE  AMENDMENT
Schwartz  summarized  the revisions  that were  made  to the draft  following  the previous  PC
meeting.  Dulac  stated  that  the revisions  captured  his comments  and  he was  happy  with  them.
Capecci  emailed  questions  on August  16, 2026  regarding  the definitions  and widths  listed  for
"right  of  way"  and "cartway",  specifically  regarding  the mention  of  a minimum  width.  Schwartz
explained  that  the definition  lists  a minimum  width  to account  for  existing,  substandard  roads.
All  new  roadways  with  be dedicated  with  a fifty  (50)  food  right-of-way  and  paved  thirty-six  (36)
foot  cartway.
Crook  asked  if  an argument  can  be made  for  a new  road  to have  the substandard  minimum.  Input
will  be needed  from  the Township  Engineer  to answer.  Schwartz  suggested  they  will  need  to
define  two  different  types  of  right-of-way,  historic  and new.  Harris  and Smalley  agreed  with
updating  the definitions.
Schwartz  explained  that Appendix  F has been  fully  incorporated  into  the Ordinance  and no
longer  exists.
Crook  suggested  changes  under  definitions  for  "flood"  and "structure"  Schwartz  will  adjust  the
definitions.
Motion  to recommend  the current  draft  to the  Board  of  Supervisors  to begin  the adoption  process
by Dulac  and  seconded  by Smalley.  The  motion  carried  unanimously.
