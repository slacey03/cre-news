LOWER NAZARETH TOWNSHIP
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
MINUTES
September 23, 2026

The Lower Nazareth Township Board of Supervisors held a public meeting on Wednesday, September 23,
2026, Chairperson Templeton called the meeting to order at 7:00PM.

Present were:

Amy L. Templeton - Chairperson Justin Coyle - Township Engineer
Martin J Boucher - Vice-Chairman LoriA. Stauffer - Manager

Stephen A. Brown - Supervisor Tammi Dravecz - Secretary/Treasurer
Nancy A. Teague - Supervisor

Agenda Approval

S. Brown made a motion to approve the agenda. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Proclamation — Detective Templeton

A. Templeton called Detective Templeton to the front to present him with a Proclamation and Plaque to
thank him for his years of service.

Approval of Minutes

N. Teague made a motion to approve September 9" minutes. M. Boucher seconded. Motion
passed (4-0).

Reports

Financial Report for August 2026 was presented and is available on the website.
Supervisor’s Comments

Open Space Preservation — S. Brown noted that the EAC will meet in October.

Parks and Recreation — S. Brown said we had an excellent day for our Dogs Day Event and it was well
attended. Our next event will be the tree lighting on December 4".

Library News — N. Teague reminded everyone that Touch a Truck is October 3™ at Bushkill Township
Park. She listed numerous other events which can be found on the library’s website.

CRPC / HVFC — M. Boucher noted that Chief DePalma retired on September 11" and Lieutenant Darrin
Wendling was promoted to Chief. Officer Mike Enstrom was promoted to Lieutenant.

Board of Supervisors Minutes
September 23, 2026


COG — no report
A. Templeton, along with J. Pennington and L. Stauffer attended Chief DePalma’s retirement party.

She thanks L. Stauffer for her efforts in grant writing as we have been awarded three LSA grants;
$97,853 for a new police vehicle, $241,500 for recycling building improvements, and $500,000 for a
new fire truck. A. Templeton also thanked Lisa Boscola and Joe Emerick for their support.

Subdivision and Land Development

Caffese Lot Line Adjustment Amended Approval — Resolution #LNT-35-26 — L. Stauffer commented
that at a prior meeting the Board decided to not follow through with road widening; therefore, this
revised plan revised reflect the removal of the widening.

M. Boucher made a motion to approve Resolution #LNT-35-26. A. Templeton seconded. M. Boucher
and A. Templeton voted yay; S. Brown and N. Teague noted nay. Motion did not pass.

Country Club Road Escrow — Acknowledge Receipt of Agreement & Authorize Disbursement — L.

Stauffer noted the release of escrow was approved by the Board. Mr. Herman has assigned the escrow
be released to Mr. Caffese. The money will be released upon recording of the amended plan.

Solicitor Asteak inquired on the timeline and that we need reason(s) for the denial. While the October
14 meeting is for budget discussion, if necessary, this matter can be placed on the agenda.

Solicitor’s Report

No Truck Traffic Signage — Authorize Solicitor to Advertise Ordinance — N. Teague made a motion to
authorize advertisement. S. Brown seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Engineer’s Report

Chick-fil-a — Letter of Credit Reduction and Commence Maintenance — J. Coyle reviewed his
September 16" letter recommending the security be reduced by $23,493.24 and to commence the 18-
month maintenance period. N, Teague made a motion to reduce security and commence the
maintenance period. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Nazareth Nest — Letter of Credit Reduction — J. Coyle reviewed his September 18" letter recommending
the security be reduced by $264,909.98 based upon inspections completed on September 16th. Mr.
Huratiak appeared to dispute the amount of security being recommended for release. He stated that in
his opinion, he has completed more work and the amount of security to be released should be

higher. He stated he needs this money to pay his contractors. J. Coyle stated the request for security
reduction was received on September 9th. The site was inspected on September 16" as it takes a few
days to generate a recommendation letter. Additional work was performed after the date of inspection;
however, that work has not been certified as part of the security release because it occurred after the
September 16" inspection date. J. Coyle stated he stands behind his recommendation letter. He stated
that the MPC allows a municipality up to 45 days to act on a request for security reduction. He stated he

attempted to get the security reduction request processed in a timely fashion so it could be addressed at
this meeting. Otherwise, Mr. Huratiak would have to wait.

Mr. Huratiak further stated he had offered Mr. Miklos a sum of $4 million dollars for his apartment
project. He stated by the Township taking the development rights as it has robbed him of his right to
develop the property. He threatened to sue the Township for taking his rights.

Mr. Huratiak became enraged and began threatening the Board members. He stated he was going to
come after each one of the Board members personally and then began talking about their individual
homes. He stated he was going to sue each one personally. He began screaming that he was going to
get government clearances for HUD / low-income housing and see how much the Board likes that. Mr.
Huratiak was highly enraged and eventually stormed out of the meeting room.

N. Teague made a motion to authorize a release of security in the amount of $ 264,909.98 per the
recommendation of the Township engineer for the Nazareth Nest project. M. Boucher
seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Newburg Park Concrete Project — Authorization to Release Final Payment — J. Coyle reviewed his
September 18" letter recommending release of the final payment in the amount of $4,935.94. S. Brown

made a motion to release final payment. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Manager’s Report

Update Fee Schedule — L. Stauffer reviewed the updated fee schedule with amended traffic impact fees.
M. Boucher made a motion to approve the amended fee schedule. N. Teague seconded. Motion passed
(4-0).

Grinder Pump Agreement — 4355 King George Drive — L. Stauffer requested authorization for

Chairperson Templeton to execute the agreement. S. Brown made a motion to authorize Chairperson
Templeton to execute. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

Sewage Enforcement Officer Appointment — Steven Hawk — L. Stauffer reviewed Jake Schray’s letter,
requesting the Township appoint additional sewage enforcement officer, Steven Hawk. S. Brown made a

motion to appoint Steven Hawk as an additional sewage enforcement officer. N. Teague seconded.
Motion passed (4-0).

L. Stauffer noted that October 14" will be a budget meeting. The Board will have their binder by
October 9",

Payment of the Bills

N. Teague made a motion to pay the bills as presented. M. Boucher seconded. Motion passed (4-0).

The meeting adjourned at 7:38 pm
