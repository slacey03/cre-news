Nazareth Area School District - Substantive Challenge and Proposed Curative Amendment

A. Templeton opened the meeting and advised the public that the hearing would conclude at 9:30pm. She
stated if additional testimony was required, the hearing would be continued to another night, which would
be announced at the end of the hearing. This protocol will be observed for all subsequent hearing

nights. She then turned the hearing over to Attorney Asteak. Attorney Asteak proceeded to state the nature
of the application, the date of the advertisement of the public hearing as well as the conduct of the public
hearing.

Julie Burkhart, Esq., attorney for the Nazareth Area School District provided the Board with an opening
statement. At this time, Matthew Deschler, Esq., special counsel for Lower Nazareth Township,
provided his opening statement.

The hearing commenced with Isabel Resende, superintendent of the Nazareth Area School District
providing testimony. The hearing continued with Zachary Worthen, PowerSchool representative
providing testimony.

The full substance of testimony can be read in the hearing transcript.
