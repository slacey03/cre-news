ZA2026-05  Setzer  Properties  NAT,  LLC
The  advertisement  for  the  hearing  was  read  aloud  and  all  parties  who  would  testify  in  the  hearing
were  sworn  in.  Present  for  the Application:  Attorney  Matthew  Deschler,  Project  Manager
Jeffery  Baughrnan  and  FedEx  Service  Manager  Stan  Long.
Exhibit  Z-1 -  Township  folder  containing  the original  application  and supporting  materials,
affidavit  of  publishing,  proof  of  posting  and  certification  of  mailing  was  requested  to be entered
into  the  record  by  Seese.
Deschler  introduced  application.  The  Applicant  is requesting  an expansion  of  the existing
building,  which  horises  the  non-confirming  use of  a truck  terminal.  The  use  predates  the  current
zoning.  A  truck  terminal  is not  currently  permitted  in  the  TD-9  zoning  district.
Baughrnan  provided  testimony.
Exhibit  A-1  -  Property  Deed.  Setzer  Properties  NAT,  LLC  purchased  the  property  on June  9,
2021.

Exhibit  A-7  -  Property  information  printout  from  NC  Pub.  The  property  is sized  at 8.27 acres.
The existing  truck  terminal  is 11,788  s.f, with  7,591 s.f. for loading  docks,  2,002  s.f. for  the
office  and 2,195  s.f. for a maintenance  bay. The  surrounding  properties  are commercial  and
agricultural  in nature.
Exhibit  A-2  -  Google  Earth  aerial  imagery  from  March  29, 1992,  Aprill5,  2005,  May  20, 2012,
October  15, 2022 and August  15, 2026 showing  the subject  property.  The footprint  of  the
existing  building  has not  changed.
Baughman  stated  the current  use is a trucking  terminal  operated  by  FedEx.  The  property  owner  is
looking  to expand  the existing  building  to add a breakroom  and upgrade  the  restroom  facilities.
Exhibit  A-3  -  Site  development  plan  of  the subject  property.  The  existing  building  is depicted  in
light  gray  shading.  The  dark  gray  shading  depicts  the proposed  expansion  of  a break  room  and
restrooms.
Baughman  testified  that  the restrooms  will  be upgraded  to be ADA  compliant.  There  will  be no
change  in the number  or size of  tnick  bays. The  current  area for  the proposed  expansion  is
pavement.  No additional  impervious  will  be added to the lot. The addition  is 564 s.f. of
additional  floor  space  and equals  4.8%  of  the existing  building  size.
An  external  man  door  will  be eliminated  with  the expansion  to allow  for  access between  the
loading  docks  and addition.
Acceptance  of  the exhibits  presented  was moved  by Gaul  and seconded  by  Reyes.  The  motion
carried  unanimously.
Reyes  asked  if  the breakroom  will  be accessed  from  outside  or inside  the facility.  Long  replied
that  he believed  it would  be from  both  the external  loading  dock  and from  inside  the building.
The  access  points  would  be ADA  accessible.
Nordahl  asked  if  modifications  would  be needed  for  water  and sewer.  Baughman  replied  they
worild  use  the current  facilities,  on-site  septic  and public  water,  for  the expansion.  Nordahl  asked
Seese if  the Township  Sewage  Enforcement  Officer  (SEO)  needed  to review  the expansion.
Seese replied  that  they  are only  adding  batl'u:ooms,  not  changing  the employee  count,  so it would
not  need  to be evaluated  by  the SEO.
Closure  of  the hearing  testimony  was moved  by Gaul  and seconded  by Nordahl.  The  motion
carried  unanimously.
Motion  by  the Board
Motion  by  Gaul  to approve  the special  exception  to expand  the non-confirming  use of  a truck
terminal  with  a 564 s.f. addition,  subject  to compliance  with  the application  presented  and
testimony  provided.  Seconded  by  Reyes.  The  motion  carried  unanimously.
LaBar  advised  that  she lost  her connection  to Zoom  and did  not  record  the motion.  The  motion
by  the Board  was  repeated.
Motion  by  Gaul  to approve  the special  exception  to expand  the non-confirming  use of  a truck

The  advertisement  for  the  hearing  was  read  aloud  and  all  parties  who  would  testify  in  the  hearing
were  sworn.  Present  for  the Application:  Justin  Klee  of  Bohler  Engineering  and Applicant
Alexander  Ortiz
Exhibit  Z-I  -  Township  folder  containing  the original  application  and supporting  materials,
affidavit  of  publishing,  proof  of  posting  and  certification  of  mailing  was  requested  to be entered
into  the  record  by  Seese.
Klee  introduced  himself  and  provided  an overview  of  his  professional  resume.  Klee  requested  he
be accepted  as an expert  in  civil  engineering  and  zoning.  Garil  stated  he was  fine  with  accepting
Klee  as an expert.  The  Board  unanimously  agreed.
Exhibit  A - Zoning  Appeal  application  and supporting  material  packet  as submitted  to the
Township.
Exhibit  B -  Professional  resume  of  Justin  Klee.
Klee  provided  background  on the site. The  vacant  parcel  is located  at 859 Nazareth  Pike  and
totals  6.22  acres.  McDonald's  will  subdivide  1.24  acres.  The  property  is located  in the TD-7
zoning  district.  The  proposed  McDonald's  will  be a 3,780  s.f. restaurant  with  side-by-side  drive-
thru  lanes  that  converge  into  one  lane.
Exhibit  D -  Site  plan  of  the  proposed  McDonald's.  Access  to the  site  will  be from  an existing
driveway  in  the  shopping  center.  Traffic  will  be one-way  circulation  around  the  building.
Klee  is requesting  relief  to allow  the  drive-thru  window  lanes  to be separated  from  the  parking
lot by  striping  instead  of curbing.  Striping  is being  requested  because  of  the operational
challenges  that  come  from  installing  curbing.  The  curbing  creates  an obstruction  around  the
drive-t  which  can restrict  customers  from  leaving  the  queue  in an emergency.  McDonald's
offers  pull  forward  windows  and reserved  parking  spaces  for  customers  with  large  take-out
orders.  Curbing  would  restrict  customers  from  leaving  the  queue  to access  tliese  waiting  spots.
Exhibit  E -  Photograph  showing  an example  of  an existing  McDonald's  drive-thru  lane.
Nordahl  asked  if  the  configuration  proposed  is used  at the  existing  McDonald's  in Northampton
Crossings.  Klee  replied  that  he will  show  photographs  of  that  specific  McDonald's  in a later
exhibit,
Crook  expressed  concern  about  the  stacking  of  traffic  at the  entrance/exit  point  to the  facility  and
asked  if  they  had plans  for  a second  exit  point.  Klee  responded  that  it was considered  but
PennDOT  preferred  the use of  only  one access  point.  Discussions  are still  being  held  with
PennDOT  regarding  improvements  at the  intersection  with  Route  248,  which  may  help  alleviate
the  traffic  concerns.
Exhibit  F- Site  plan  of  the  McDonald's  at 3708  Easton-Nazareth  Highway  in the  Northampton
Crossings  shopping  center.  The  side-by-side  drive-thru  lanes  were  approved  in 2023  without
curb  separation.
Exhibit  G  -  Photographs  of  quick-serve  restaurants  within  L,ovver  Nazareth  Township  that  do not
have  curbing  in  the  drive-thru  lanes,  including  McDonald's,  Arby's  and  Panera  at Northampton
Crossings  and  the  Dunkin'  on  Route  191.

Klee  described  the need  for  curbing  as a hardship  because  it creates  a physical  obstruction  that
restricts  McDonald's  operations.
Klee  requested  relief  from  providing  a designated  fire  lane. A fire lane would  increase  the
amount  of  impervious  on site and create  a deficiency  in  parking  spaces.
Exhibit  H - Review  letter  from  Lori  Seese, Planning  & Zoning  Administrator,  dated  July  17,
2026.  Comment  3d references  the Zoning  Ordinance  reqriirements  for  a fire  lane.
Exhibit  I -  Letter  from  Jeffrey  Seip,  Fire  Marshall,  dated  July  23, 2026.  The  letter  states  that  a
fire  lane  is not  required  and an emergency  access  lane  will  be adequate.
Gaul  asked  where  the emergency  access lanes are located.  Klee  replied  the emergency  access
would  be the parking  lot that circles  the building.  Because  the structure  is one-story  and
relatively  small,  it does not  require  a separate  lane.
Klee  requested  relief  to allow  tlie  proposed  free-standing  sign  to be twenty  (20)  feet  in height
instead  of  the permitted  ten (10)  feet.
Exl'iibit  J -  Signage  plan  sliowing  the location  and design  of  all signs  proposed  for  the site. The
free-standing  sign  would  be a 5' x 5' red box  with  the "M"  logo.  Additional  panels  would  be
below  to show  additional  information,  such  as "WiFi"  or "24Hours".  The  sign  complies  with  the
square  footage  requirements.
Crook  mentioned  how  McDonald's  are often  located  in other  municipalities  with  historic
districts  or  specially  regulated  areas, and  he does not  see why  they  can't  comply  with  the  Zoning
Ordinance.
Exhibit  C -  Aerial  image  of  tlie  subject  property.  Klee  described  the main  commercial  corridor
as being  located  along  Route  191 and Jandy  Blvd.  The proposed  McDonald's  will  not be
centralized  to that  corridor.  The  presence  of  trees  and lack  of  frontage  along  Route  191 will
make  a 10-foot  sign  "get  lost".  Klee  hopes  the 20-foot  sign  will  be visible  from  Route  191 and
Jandy  Blvd  so customers  know  where  they  are located.
Nordahl  asked  if  the sign  worild  be static.  Klee  responded  they  originally  proposed  a digital  sign,
but  that  request  was  removed  and it is now  proposed  as static.
Nordahl  asked  if  this  McDonald's  location  will  be open  24/7 or have  limited  hours,  and if  the
sign  will  be illuminated  at all hours  or shut down  when  the building  is closed.  Klee  responded
that  the  sign  will  be illuminated  at all hours.
Ortiz  described  the ideal  scenario  for  this location  as being  open  with  a 24/7  drive-thru.  They
would  request  the sign  be illuminated  if  the restaurant  is open.
Reyes  asked  if  they  considered  placing  a sign  at a different  location  on the property  to improve
visibility.  Klee  replied  that  the sign  would  still  be set a far distance  away  from  Route  191 and
Jandy  Blvd.
Exhibit  K  -  Existing  free-standing  sign  for  tlie  shopping  center.  Klee  believes  the sign  is around
20-feet  in  height.
Nordahl  asked  if  the free-standing  sign  is the only  indicator  of  the  McDonald's.  Klee  referenced
other  signage  that  will  be placed  on the site  to provide  directions  to the access  drive.
