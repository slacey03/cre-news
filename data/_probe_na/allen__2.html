Allen Township News

Road Work: Atlas Road - Temporary Daytime Detour

(October 27th through October 30th, 2026)
Motorists are advised a temporary daytime traffic detour will be in effect on Atlas Road (in the vicinity of the intersection with Savage Road and John Drive) starting on October 27th, 2026 due to median construction. The road is planned to be opened at the end of each workday, upon installation of traffic safety measures.

The construction is expected to last until October 30th, 2026. Drivers should expect delays and are encouraged to use alternate routes.

Please slow down when traveling through the work zone. Follow all posted traffic signs and obey the signage and instructions of flaggers directing traffic.

Road Work Notice (Towpath Estates/River Side Point Development)

Asphalt preparation work is scheduled to begin on all roads starting Tuesday, September 22, 2026, and continue through Monday, September 28, 2026.

Asphalt placement is currently scheduled as follows: - River Run: Friday, September 25
- Lock Drive and Levee Run: Monday, September 28
- Delta Road: Tuesday, September 29

Please remove all vehicles from the roadway by 7:00 a.m. on your scheduled work date. Work is expected to begin at 7:00 a.m.

Vehicles left on the roadway may interfere with paving operations and could delay the project. Please use caution and drive slowly when traveling through active work areas.

Important reminder: Preparation work may occur before your scheduled asphalt placement date.

Thank you for your cooperation and patience while this work is completed.

Real Estate Tax

The Allen Township's Real Estate Tax Collector, starting in 2025, will be Berkheimer. Please contact Berkheimer by phone at 800-360-8989 or through the following link: https://www.hab-inc.com/contact-customer-care-form-individual/ for more information or questions regarding your Allen Township Real Estate Bill. Bills will be mailed out during the first week of March.

Local Services Tax Changes

The Allen Township's LST collector, starting in 2025, will be the Keystone Collections Group. Please see the following link for LST payment and registration information: Finance / Taxes - Allen Township, or contact Keystone Collections directly at (866) 539-1100. Allen Township will only accept LST payments from 2024 and prior. For quarters starting in 2025, please send payment to Keystone Collections following the instructions in the above link.

Welcome To Allen Township

Allen Township is a community of 5,456 residents (pursuant to the 2020 published census) and is a wonderful place to live, work, and visit. Family friendly, vibrant, and ideally situated, Allen Township residents enjoy a high quality of life. We pride ourselves on quality municipal services, attractive and safe tree-lined neighborhoods, first-rate schools, exceptional parks and recreation opportunities, engaging community events, and caring citizens who generously volunteer their time and energies to countless civic organizations and endeavors.

Your local government is dedicated to effectively and cost-efficiently serve all citizens. We encourage you to explore our site to learn more about your community. Use the menu above or our search feature to navigate this site.

If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us or call our office at 610-262-7012.

Thank you for visiting our website.
