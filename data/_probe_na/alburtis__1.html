Council Agenda / Minutes

Borough Council Meetings are held the 2nd and last Wednesday of each month at 7:00pm in the Borough Hall, 260 Franklin St., Alburtis, PA., except on legal holidays when the meeting is held the Monday prior. Residents are encouraged to attend those meetings
