  The September 10, 2026, meeting of the Shillington Borough Council 
was called to order at 7:30 p.m. by President John W. Hoffert.  Following the 
invocation given by Mayor Hivner, and Oath of Allegiance, roll call was taken.  
Nicholas M. Bentz was absent.  Council members in attendance were Elizabeth M. 
Bentz, Mark C. Grounard, John W. Hoffert, Michael A. Hoffert, Jared A. Hopgood and 
Mr. Ronald R. Klee, Jr. Present in addition to Council members, were Borough 
Manager Scott D. Brossman, Fire Chief David M. Bentz, Mayor Andrew R. Hivner, 
Solicitor Thomas L. Klonis, and Secretary Kristen M. Schlottman.  Terrence Naugle 
of Great Valley Consultants was also in attendance. 
 
  Following the correction of two typographical errors in the 
August 19, 2026, minutes, Mr. Klee made a motion to approve the minutes as 
amended. Mr. Hopgood seconded the motion, which was duly carried. 
  
PUBLIC COMMENT: None 
 
  Committee Reports 
 
Mayor: Mayor Hivner presented the Police Department’s report for the month of 
August.  During the month, the Department handled 355 incidents, including 42 
arrests and officers traveled 2,781 patrol miles. 
 Mayor Hivner also reported that at the last Civil Service Commission 
meeting, there was a proposed change to the Rules & Regulations to be addressed 
by Council and that the Commission has ended the current hiring process due to 
lack of participants.  The Commission is ready to proceed with a new eligibility list 
upon activation by Council. 
 Council was also advised of several incidents that occurred during the last 
month including a shots ﬁred call in the 200 block of South Wyomissing Avenue, 
following which a suspect was located and charged, as well as an incident that 
occurred at the Governor Mifflin High School football game on August 28,2026. 
During the game, it was communicated that an individual was witnessed in 
possession of a ﬁrearm.  The game was subsequently stopped and the stadium was 
evacuated. Through the investigation it was determined that the initial reports were 
not accurate.  Due to the community concern that was expressed, a meeting was 
held with School Officials, Law Enforcement and residents to discuss the incident 
as well as planning for similar incidents that may occur in the future.   
 In closing, Mayor Hivner requested Council authorize the preparation and 
advertising of an ordinance for the 2026 Halloween curfew.  Following a motion by 
Mr. Klee, seconded by Mr. Grounard, and duly approved, authorization was granted.

Fire: Mr. Michael A. Hoffert, Chairman, presented the Fire Chief’s report for the 
month of August. During the month, the Department responded to thirty-nine (39) 
dispatches, including seven (7) within the Borough and thirty-two (32) mutual aid 
responses to neighboring municipalities.  Daytime staff successfully covered all 168 
available staffing hours, and Department members participated in ﬁve training 
sessions. 
 Mr. Hoffert then requested Council’s permission to purchase and install a  
20x20x9 carport at 217 Catherine Street. Following a motion made by Mr. Hopgood, 
which was seconded by Dr. Bentz, and duly carried, Council granted permission to 
purchase and install the carport, provided that it ﬁts within the property boundaries.  
Council also waived any applicable setback requirements from the property lines.

Zoning and Codes: Mr. Mark C. Grounard, Chairman, presented the report of the 
Borough’s Building Code Official, Terrence Naugle, for the month of August.  
Mr. Naugle has issued six (6) new permits and thirteen (13) certiﬁcates of 
use/occupancy during the month. 
 After Mr. Grounard concluded his remarks, Mr. Naugle, of Great Valley 
Consultants addressed Council regarding the status of the ongoing appeal 
concerning rental inspections and refusals of such inspections. Mr. Naugle 
indicated that the appeal process will likely be lengthy.  In the interim, he 
recommended that the Borough continue with its normal rental inspection 
procedures and appropriately document any instances in which an inspection is 
refused.
