Public Comment Protocol: 
1. Residents may address Borough Council at the times allotted for Public Comment 
2. Both residents and non-residents shall provide their names and addresses to the Borough Secretary 
to record in the minutes.  Anyone who refuses to provide their name and address may not be 
recognized to speak. 
3. All comments shall be addressed to the President of Council. 
4. The President of Council may place time limits on comments. 
5. Anyone who conducts themselves in an inappropriate manner (as determined by the President of 
Borough Council or Chief of Police) may be asked to leave the meeting.   
6. Borough Council meetings are recorded electronically.
