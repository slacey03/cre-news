MANAGER’S REPORT 
Crosswalk Signaling 
Motion: Approve the purchase of two (2) crosswalk signal signs for the intersection of N. Mill Street and E. 
Main Street for a cost not to exceed $2,700.

signal signs for the intersection of N. Mill Street and E. Main Street for a cost not to exceed $3,500 utilizing 
Harms Benefit funds.  Mary Ann Cotter concurred with the amendment. 
 
Moved by: Ryan Acevedo Seconded by: Mary Ann Cotter Vote: Motion carried 7-0 
 
Grass/Weeds Violation Enforcement 
Motion: Authorize Borough Staff to assume the initial handling of grass and weed complaints in accordance 
with the terms set forth in Chapter 187 of the codification, titled, “Grass, Weeds, and Other Vegetation, 
furthermore; designating Seth Riegel, Bonnie Frisco, and Kelly Yanos to give notice and enforce the provisions 
therein. 
Moved by: Mary Ann Cotter Seconded by: Nancy Kauffman Vote: Motion carried 7-0 
 
Street Light Request (200 Block of S. Water Street) 
A request was received to add a street on the 200 block of S. Water Street.  Council noted there is sufficient 
street lighting in that area. 
 
Fire Company Accounting 
Manager Yanos noted that following the resignation of the Fire Company’s Treasurer; last week, the Borough 
Secretary/Treasurer assumed those responsibilities and recommended compensation for such to be paid by 
the Fire Company.  This matter will be discussed further in Executive Session. 
 
Announcements 
The Borough is aware that a number of intersection curbs throughout the Borough are in need of repainting. 
Beginning June 24 and continuing for approximately four to six weeks, the Public Works Department will be 
operating with a reduced workforce. Until that time, the department will focus on projects that require a 
three-person team. Once staffing is reduced, they will shift their attention to projects that can be completed 
with a two-person crew, including curb repainting and other maintenance tasks. 
 
A Zoning Hearing is scheduled for Wednesday, June 24, at 6:00 PM for consideration of a variance application 
submitted by First Street Storage, LLC, located at 331 West 1st Street.
