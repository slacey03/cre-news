The Bushkill Township Board of Supervisors held their regular meeting on Thursday, July 2, 2026, in the 
Bushkill Township Municipal Meeting Room. The following Supervisors were present: Mrs. Bender, Mrs. 
Hill, Mr. Knauss, Mr. Kocher, and Mr. McKeon . Also present at the meeting were the Manager, Belinda 
A. Roberts, Attorney Gary Asteak, Township Engineer Robert Collura, Chief Michael McLouth , Zoning 
Officer Todd Weidman, and Open Space Coordinator Kerry Reider. There were 11 people in the audience.

Flag.

SUBDIVISIONS:  2025-08 Douglasville Road Subdivision (148 W. Douglasville Road) 
Preliminary/Final Plan - Major Subdivision - creating 3 lots in the (RR) Rural Residential 
Zoning District. During the June 22, 2026, Planning Commission Meeting, the plan received 
conditional approval with the following conditions per the Engineers Review: 
 
1. Plans must be signed by the Owner(s) and notarized. (Bushkill Township SALDO 
§503.C.C.5)  
2. Monumentation (or securities for placement) must be in place prior to Plan recording.  
3. Comments by the Northampton County Conservation District must be considered. 
 
The following waivers are also granted: 
• SALDO §502.A. A preliminary plan submission is required for a major subdivision or 
land development. 
• SALDO §1004.C.2. Where a subdivision or land development abuts or contains an 
existing street of inadequate cartway or right-of-way width, additional right-of-way and/or 
cartway width shall be required conforming with Table 10.1, "Design Standards for 
Streets." 
  
    Resolution 2026-12 - Approves the Preliminary/Final plan for the Douglasville Road Subdivision with 
the conditions as listed above provided by the Township Engineer. Motion by Randy Knauss was 
seconded by Julie Bender and approved unanimously to approve Resolution 2026-12  
 
Chairman Kocher inquired as to who would be responsible to hold the securities for the water line  
installation for the Douglasville Road project. Solicitor Asteak stated that the Municipality should 
hold the securities, unless the contract with PA American Water would require the securities be held
