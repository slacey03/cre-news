The Bushkill Township Board of Supervisors held their re gular meeting on Thursday, May 7, 2026, in the

1.  Police - Chief McLouth provided the Police Report.  
a. Community Canine Program - Chief McLouth explained that the Community Canine 
is a specially trained hypoallergenic Labradoodle that can operate in both law 
enforcement and public settings. The Community Canine will be used to support 
officer wellness, be part of Critical Incident Stress Management debriefings, support 
community wellness, and attend community events. The Community Canine Program 
is the first of its kind in Northampton County. Motion by Keith McKeon was seconded 
by Randy Knauss and approved unanimously to approve the Community Canine
