WAIVER REQUEST: 1295 Seifert Road – Property Owner Larry Parsons is seeking relief from Zoning 
Ordinance Section 905; Riparian and Wetland Buffers prohibiting land 
disturbance within a 50ft. Riparian Buffer from the edge of a wetland. The relief 
will allow for 30ft. of encroachment into the front edge of the wetland area along 
Seifert Road for the construction of a house and garage as shown on the plan 
drawn by KCE dated 6/5/26, and contingent on DEP’s approval for wetland 
encroachment. Chairman Kocher asked if there were any questions from the 
Board, Supervisor McKeon asked for clarification on the process. Mr. Parsons 
explained that the relief request is part of the requirement for DEP to review the 
encroachment request along with approval from Northampton County 
Conservation District. Chairman Kocher confirmed with Mr. Parsons that there 
weren’t any concerns from neighboring property owners regarding the wetland 
encroachment. Motion by Randy Knauss was seconded by Keith McKeon and 
approved unanimously to grant relief in accordance with the Zoning Ordinance 
Section 905 and condition of the granted relief is compliance with the EAC letter 
dated August 24, 2026.
