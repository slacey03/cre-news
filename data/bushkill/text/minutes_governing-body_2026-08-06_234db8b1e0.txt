The Bushkill Township Board of Supervisors held their regular meeting on Thursday, August 6, 2026, in 
the Bushkill Township Municipal Meeting Room. The following Supervisors were present:  Mrs. Bender, 
Mrs. Hill,  Mr. Knauss,  Mr. Kocher, and Mr. McKeon . Also present at the meeting were  the Manager, 
Belinda A. Roberts, Attorney Gary Asteak, Township Engineer Robert Collura, Chief Michael McLouth, 
Zoning Officer Todd Weidman, and Open Space Coordinator Kerry Reider . There were 11 people in the 
audience. 
 
Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag 
 
The Chairman, Brien Kocher called the meeting to order at 6:00 PM with the Pledge of Allegiance to the 
Flag.  
 
Sign Ins: None 
 
Motion to Approve the Agenda: Chairman Kocher asked if there was any need to amend the agenda. 
Belinda Roberts stated there were no changes to the agenda. Motion by Randy Knauss was seconded by 
Carolyn Hill and approved unanimously to approve the agenda.  
 
Approval of Minutes 
 
 July 2, 2026 - Motion by Julie Bender was seconded by Randy Knauss and approved 
unanimously to approve the minutes of July 2, 2026. 
 
SUBDIVISIONS: 2026-02 1033 Jacobsburg Road - Minor Subdivision Preliminary/Final - 2 lots, 
located in the  (RR) Rural Residential Zoning District. During the July 27, 2026, 
Planning Commission Meeting, the plan received conditional approval with the 
following conditions per the Engineers Review: 
 
1. Plans must be signed by the Owner(s) and notarized. (BT SALDO §703.C.4)  
2. Plan preparer's seal and signature shall be required. (§703.C.5) 
3. Monumentation (or  securities for placement) must be in place prior to Plan 
recording. (§703.E.2)   
4. Percolation test and soil probes must be noted on the plan. (§703.G.4) 
5. The comments from the EAC must be considered and resolved.  
6.  DEP approval of the Sewage Facilities Planning Module shall be required.  
 
      Resolution 2026-19 - Approves the Preliminary/Final plan for 1033 Jacobsburg Road 
Minor Subdivision with the conditions as listed above provided by the Township 
Engineer. Motion by Keith McKeon was seconded by Randy Knauss and approved 
unanimously to approve Resolution 2026-19

 2022-08 Greystone Capital, Inc. - 454 Male Road - Land Development - 
    3 Industrial/Manufacturing buildings, located in the (GCI) General Commercial/Industrial 
Zoning District. During the July 27, 2026, Planning Commission Meeting, the plan 
received conditional approval with the following conditions per the Engineers Review: 
 
1. The Owners Statement must be signed by the Owner(s) and notarized. 
(§503.C.C.5)  
2. Fire hydrants, at locations approved by the Fire Company Chief, must be 
provided. 
3. All facilities must be fully dimensioned (e.g. basin, antiseep collar)  
4. Any waivers or variances granted must be listed with the date of action taken by 
the Board of Supervisors and Zoning Hearing Board. 
5. DEP approval of the Sewage Facilities Planning Module shall be required. 
6. Recording of the Greystone Capital, Inc. Subdivision Plan approved on December 
7, 2023, shall be required 
 
 
The following waivers are also granted: 
• SALDO §1008.F.17. The maximum depth permitted for any detention 
basin shall be 48" measured from the top of the berm to the lowest point 
in the basin. 
• SALDO §1008.F.6. Maximum requirement inside and outside slopes of 
the basin is 5 horizontal to 1 vertical. 
• SALDO §1004.K.3.d. In areas of excavation of earth fill such as grading 
shall be done to a maximum slope of 4 horizontal to 1 vertical. 
• ZO §1500.G. Required number of parking spaces is 1 space for every 500 
square feet of gross floor area or 2 spaces for every 3 employees on 
largest shift, whichever is greater. 
 
 
  Resolution 2026-21 - Approves the Land Development for Greystone Capital, Inc. with the 
conditions as listed above provided by the Township Engineer. Motion by Julie Bender 
was seconded by Keith McKeon and approved unanimously to approve Resolution
