2017 Bernville Road – Isiah Speaks from Coldwell Banker representing a buyer, whom is in the    
early stages of evaluating the property at 2017 Bernville Road for a potential redevelopment 
project.  The concept is an indoor sports facility that can accommodate basketball, volleyball, 
indoor turf sports and athletic training.  It is our understanding this property is in the Light 
Commercial District, and the proposed recreational use is not specifically listed among the 
permitted uses. He wanted to introduce this concept and see what the Commission’s initial 
thoughts are and see if this type of redevelopment would be something the Board would be 
receptive to. Atty. Mooney noted the developer has two options moving forward, either request a 
variance or come to the Board of Supervisors meeting and request a change to the Zoning 
Ordinance to allow the use in some form of either Conditional Use, Special Exception or 
Permitted Use.  A question was raised about whether the property was formerly known as The 
Riveredge/Silo, hotel and old coffee shop.  Mr. Speaks confirmed that it was and stated that the 
existing structures would be demolished and replaced with new construction.  There was positive 
feedback from the Planning Commission.   
 
111 Air Museum Drive, waiver from Land Development Process- Doug Bodder member of 
the Civil Air Patrol at Reading Airport looking for a waiver of the Township’s Land 
Development process for a proposed 950 sq. ft. addition to their existing building at 111 Air 
Museum Drive.  Mr. Morey asked for confirmation between the email received which stated 950 
sq. ft. but the drawing provided showed dimensions of 26 x 66 feet, which is about 1,700 sq. ft. 
Mr. Bodder confirmed the addition is 26 x 26 which is 950 sq. ft. The Civil Air Patrol has 
outgrown its current space and is considering expanding the existing facility or relocating to a 
larger space. Reading Airport has granted Civil Air Patrol a lease contract for 30 years.  The Civil 
Air Patrol has been in this location for many years and would like to remain here.  Mr. Morey 
stated the Subdivision Land Development Ordinance requires a Land Development Plan to be 
submitted for any non-residential building greater than 180 sq. ft. which this exceeds.  The Civil 
Air Patrol is asking the Planning Commission to make a recommendation this evening to the 
Board of Supervisors to either waive or not waive Land Development for the proposed project.  
Mr. Becker made a motion to deny waiver from Land Development process, due to the size of 
proposed addition, seconded by Mr. Tricarico.  Motion carried.

Data Center Ordinance Discussion 
 
Ms. Jean Engle from the Reading-Berks Chapter of Citizens’ Climate Lobby is working with 
Penn State Berks in a program called Environmental Awareness and Community Action Project 
(EACAP).  Students participate in this program voluntarily as part of their curriculum.  There are 
two students from that program here this evening Marlena Marducci and Sopia Cassidy who are 
here to listen and do research on how data centers are being addressed in our area.   
 
Mr. Tricarico explained to the students that Bern Township has been working for the last several 
months to create an ordinance addressing data centers. Municipalities have had a window of 
opportunity to get an ordinance approved before the January 6, 2027, deadline.   
 
Mr. Tricarico stated the most up to date draft of the ordinance is about 23 pages long, so he 
provided everyone with a summary of the 6 topics that were focused on over the last several 
months.  There are two ordinances that are involved, one is called The Data Center Ordinance, 
and the other is called the Technology Industrial District Ordinance. Mr. Becker noted that the 
decommissioning definition is lengthy but is extremely important and needs to remain a part of 
this ordinance.  Atty. Mooney commended the Planning Commission for the time in constructing 
this Ordinance and feels there will be tons of case laws on this and this will be adjusted and 
amended as time goes on.   
 
Mr. Becker questioned under decommissioning timeline; what is the proper way to begin 
decommissioning.  Atty. Mooney commented that the Township should initiate this 
communication with the owner/developer regarding decommissioning.  Mr. Colflesh made a 
motion to submit the Data Center Ordinance to the Board of Supervisors, seconded by Mr. 
Tricarico.  Motion carried.
