Bern Township Planning Commission Meeting of August 11, 2026 
Present: Planning Commission: Michael Werley, Christopher Becker, James Colflesh, Steve 
Tricarico and Lou Bunnick.

Michael Werley called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m. 
Acceptance of Minutes 
The minutes of July 14, 2026, meeting were approved.  Motion by Mr. Becker, seconded by Mr. 
Colflesh.  Motion carried.  
Public Comment 
None 
 
Subdivision and Land Development Plans 
Berks Park 183 Lots A, F & G, Final Land Development Plan 
 
Mr. Morey reviewed several items from the SSM letter dated August 3, 2026.  Item # 1 under 
Compliance with Zoning Ordinance, drawings are identified as “Condo Plans” and “Lot 
Consolidation Plans”.  The “Lot Consolidation Plans” should be part of the land development 
and “Condo Plans” are a private issue. Atty. Mooney interjected he needs to review the Condo 
Declaration and make sure this meets the law of the Ordinance for the Township.  Item #4 
comments were received, and a re-submission to Reading Area Water Authority (RAWA) will 
take place.  Item #5 Mr. Zaborowski from BCIDA is asking for relief from the 
Guarantee/Fidelity Bond in the Improvement Agreement.  Atty. Mooney responded this request 
would need to be presented to the Board of Supervisors. Item #17 was reviewed and noted that  
once there is full occupancy in the buildings the Township could require a traffic signal analysis 
at the intersection of Aviation Road and Route 183.  Mr. Becker made a motion to recommend 
Conditional Plan approval to the Board of Supervisors subject to the SSM review letter dated 
August 3, 2026, for final land development, seconded by Mr. Bunnick.  Motion carried. 
 
Victory Baptist Church, Sketch Plan

Data Center Ordinance Discussion 
 
Designating LI and TI zones along with getting a revised Zoning map were discussed.  Mr. 
Colflesh provided a link from Montgomery & Chester Counties which provides a guide for 
writing Data Center Ordinances.  The commissioners focused on the water and power portions of 
the ordinance.  Definitions were added, testing, inspections were expanded on, discharge water 
was discussed and some follow up is still necessary. Energy language was reviewed and found to 
be captured adequately in the ordinance.  September’s meeting will focus on noise and 
emergency management/traffic issues.   
 
Alternative Energy Discussion 
Mr. Tricarico asked where this ordinance was in the process.  Atty. Mooney stated the ordinance 
was re-submitted back to the Berks County Planning Commission with the changes requested by 
the Township Supervisors.
