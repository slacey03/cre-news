ADMINISTRATION                   
 
Discussion/Approval to Offer the Samaritan Fund Program to Police and Administration 
Departments - Mr. Potts noted this is a fund that is sourced with funds from 3rd party sponsors, 
foundations and groups that provide donations.  This program is for people with chronic illness 
with costly health bills.  Mr. Potts explained the Samaritan Fund Program: if an employee or one 
of their family members has a chronic illness, they would apply to the Samaritan Fund Program, 
if accepted they would stay on this program for one year, any significant health charges or claims 
are moved out of our account and moved into the Samaritan Fund account.  The Township pays 
$60,000.00 to the Samaritan Fund and then Benecon (our health insurance carrier) would credit 
us the $60,000.00.  There is a debit card that is provided to the employee that is pre-coded which 
can only be used for medical expenses.  An applicant must re-apply each year for this program.  
Mr. Thompson made a motion to approve the Samaritan Fund Program to Police and 
Administration Departments, seconded by Mrs. Stork.  YES: Mr. Eveland, Mrs. Stork, Mr. 
Thompson and Mrs. Reed. Motion carried.
