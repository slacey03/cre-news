Bern Township Planning Commission Meeting of July 14, 2026 
Present: Planning Commission: Michael Werley, Christopher Becker, James Colflesh, Steve 
Tricarico and Thomas Egan.   
Also Attending: Kent Morey, Spotts, Stevens & McCoy; Atty. Keith Mooney, Barley Snyder. 
Michael Werley called the meeting to order at 7:30 p.m. 
Acceptance of Minutes 
The minutes of June 9, 2026, meeting were approved.  Motion by Mr. Tricarico, seconded by Mr. 
Colflesh.  Motion carried.  
 
Subdivision and Land Development Plans 
Riverfront FCU Subdivision and Land Development Plan  
 
Chuck Frantz, from C2C Design, noted receiving the necessary variances needed for this project 
after being in front of the Zoning Hearing Board.  Mr. Frantz is in receipt of the SSM review 
letter and has no issues with the listed comments.  C2C will revise the plans and submit them to 
the Bern Township Planning Commission, also the application was submitted to the Berks 
County Conservation District.  Mr. Frantz asked if there were any additional comments, 
questions or concerns to be incorporated into the revised plans.  Mr. Morey reviewed Items# 
3,4,7,8,9,10,11,12, & 13 from the SSM letter pertaining to Landscaping.  There was a lengthy 
discussion regarding the type of landscaping that should be planted and placement. Mr. Morey 
also reviewed Item# 20 regarding access easements.    
 
Revised Alternative Energy Ordinance  
 
Mr. Werley noted the Ordinance was presented to the Supervisors at last Tuesdays meeting and 
now has come back to the Planning Commission.  Atty. Mooney added that if the Planning 
Commission agrees with the noted changes from the Supervisors, then it is within the rights of 
the Planning Commission to make a recommendation for the Board of Supervisors to adopt this 
ordinance and then submit the Berks County Planning Commission for re-review.  Mr. Tricarico 
made a motion to recommend the Board of Supervisors proceed with the Ordinance with the 
changes that have been made, except the Planning Commission does not recommend that 
Commercial Solar be excluded from AB and RR districts, seconded by Mr. Colflesh. Motion 
carried. 
 
Data Center Ordinance discussion      
 
Mr. Becker has done a lot of research regarding regulations for data centers in Pennsylvania.  Mr. 
Potts also has gathered information from several different Pennsylvania counties regarding their 
ordinances for data centers.  It was discussed that the target date to present this ordinance to the 
Board of Supervisors should be for their November meeting.  There were several discussions 
regarding the information, calculations, water supply, requirements, continuous

monitoring/inspections to be included in the ordinance.  Atty. Mooney noted the five topics for 
review are: water, power, noise, emergency management and decommissioning.  Mr. Becker 
added facade as another topic for review.  Atty. Mooney suggested working on two or three 
topics each month, which was then agreed upon that the first topics to be reviewed would be 
water and power for August’s meeting.  Noise and emergency management/traffic for 
September’s meeting and decommissioning and facade for October’s meeting.  Mr. Becker, along 
with Mr. Tricarico, will work on these topics for each month’s meetings. Also, there was a 
lengthy discussion regarding the LI and TI districts.
