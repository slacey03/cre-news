ORDINANCES & RESOLUTIONS 
Review and Discussion of Alternative Energy Ordinance – Mr. Potts sent the ordinance to the 
Supervisors with noted changes made by the Planning Commission and Township staff.  Mr. 
Potts is asking for some decisions to be made this evening, so the ordinance can be submitted to 
Atty. Mooney and resubmitted to Berks County Planning Commission.  Dr. Wagner stated he 
believes these districts should be limited to GI & LI areas, not AB & RR and the ordinance 
should not be by Conditional Use. Mrs. Reed explained that alternative energy is allowed in all 
residential areas.  Currently GI and LI are the two districts where commercial solar is permitted. 
Mrs. Reed noted acreage size for each district.  AB is 3,121, RR is 3,567, and IR 2,788.  Mrs. 
Stork asked if the concern is that too many solar fields might be constructed in these districts?  
Mrs. Stork also questioned whether the shortage of electricity is still an issue.  Mr. Potts 
responded that the electricity shortage situation has been resolved with the substation. Set back 
regulations were reviewed and agreed upon: 100’ from any road, 150’ from any property line and 
300’ from any residential property.  A question was raised, who deems the decommissioning to 
be acceptable when returning the land to its former state? Mr. Potts responded that approval 
would be given by the Zoning Officer.  Also, concern was expressed regarding the 
decommissioning process and possible hazardous waste and the disposal of the panels.  Mr. 
Werley of the Planning Commission asked when decommissioning does happen what is the 
process for going back into Clean and Green?  Atty. Mooney responded that the property owner 
would reapply for Clean and Green with Berks County.  Mrs. Reed inquired about the next steps 
in the process for this ordinance.  Atty. Mooney replied it will be 30 days from the date he 
submits the revised ordinance to the Berks County Planning Commission; it will come back to 
our Planning Commission.   
 
Discussion/Approval Curative Amendment of Data Center Uses Resolution – Atty. Mooney 
explained this resolution is declaring our ordinance deficient as it relates to data centers.  By 
having the curative amendment, it gives protection to our ordinance, and it cannot be challenged 
if someone proposes a data center and declares our ordinance exclusionary.  Mr. Potts and Atty. 
Mooney noted if a developer states that the ordinance is exclusionary it gives the developer the 
right to put a data center anywhere.  Atty Mooney added that you cannot utilize another curative 
amendment for three years once this is amended. Mr. Potts highly recommended having this 
approved.  Mr. Eveland made a motion to approve the curative amendment of data center uses 
resolution, seconded by Dr. Wagner.  YES: Dr. Wagner, Mr. Eveland, Mrs. Stork, Mr. Thompson 
and Mrs. Reed.  Motion carried.

Discussion / Approval of Federal Highlands Boundary Inclusion Resolution- Mr. Potts stated 
the Township received a letter from the Game Commission.  This letter states that if we join the 
Highlands Conservation Act Grant Program it allows us to apply to the Federal Government and 
ask for 50% reimbursement of the cost we paid for the land.  Also, we can apply to DCNR for 
the other 50% if land is used for conservation or recreation purposes. Dr. Wagner made a motion 
for the approval of the Federal Highlands Boundary inclusion resolution, seconded by Mr. 
Thompson. YES: Dr. Wagner, Mr. Eveland, Mrs. Stork, Mr. Thompson and Mrs. Reed.  Motion 
carried.

Discussion of the Bernville Business Center Access Points – Brian Johnson from Kinsley 
Properties, Steve Walsh; Civil Engineer, Kevin Savage; Traffic Engineer both with Dynamic 
Engineering and Matt Close partner on this project from C&B Development provided information 
to develop a 169,000 square foot warehouse. We received a request to evaluate access to Route 
183 instead of using Leiscz’s Bridge Road.  They attended last month’s Planning Commission 
meeting and during this discussion it was determined that using Leiscz’s Bridge Road was the 
more appropriate access, but the topic was how to mitigate negative impacts for this access.  It was 
suggested to restrict left turn movement and were asked to present this concept to the Supervisors.  
Mr. Walsh from Dynamic provided a plan and explained this concept.  Mr. Savage also added 
details of the traffic study that was done for access to Route 183 versus Leiscz’s Bridge Road. 
There were lengthy discussions and questions regarding this potential warehouse and access 
points.
