Present: Planning Commission: Michael Werley, Christopher Becker, James Colflesh, and Beth 
Burkovich.

Public Comment 
Mr. Potts introduced Jessica Seiders, who will be the new Township Manager, when Mr. Potts 
retires at the end of the year.   Mr. Potts informed the Board the Judge for the Conditional Use 
appeal, ruled in favor of the developer (Reading One Three, LLC).  The Township will have to 
deliberate whether they want to make an appeal to the Commonwealth Court.  What the Judge’s 
ruling means is that the developer can build a 45’ tall building with no conditions.  Mr. Potts also 
stated he heard there was a rumor that the Township was looking to shut down the Planning 
Commission, and he wanted to make sure that everyone is aware there is no truth to this rumor.  
Mrs. Reed seconded this statement and commended the Planning Commission for always being 
prepared with their details and facts.   
Subdivision and Land Development Plans 
Victory Baptist Church, Sketch Plan  
Charles Nifenecker from Victory Baptist Church was present.  Mr. Morey stated there is only one 
main item of concern from the SSM letter dated September 1, 2026.  The building addition is

Eagle Metals, Preliminary Plan  
 
The engineer from McCarthy Engineering, along with the owner, Charlie Bernard, and a co-
worker Brian from Eagle Metals were present.  This project is a land development and lot 
consolidation.  The plan shows the lot consolidation for parcel 2 & 3 and in addition to this 
consolidation a portion of parcel 3 will be given to parcel 1 and the portion of parcel 1 will be 
given to the newly consolidated lot.  Mr. Bernard has purchased parcel 3 from Mr. Walter Kreitz.   
Eagle Metals currently operates as a manufacturing business at 1243 Old Bernville Road.  This 
project proposes a building addition to one of the existing manufacturing buildings with 
additional parking spaces and a new stormwater facility that will replace three of the existing 
stormwater facilities on the site.  The McCarthy engineer noted upon reviewing the SSM letter 
dated September 4, 2026, they feel they are able to comply with all the Township and Township 
Engineer noted comments.  Mr. Morey did note that Item # 11 should have been deleted from the 
letter.  Mr. Morey reviewed the letter for some clarifications.  Under Waiver Request, Item #1 
Section 154-24.A.(12) the last time Eagle Metals came in for an expansion on the Western side 
of the property they did some improvements along Old Bernville Road to the extent that they 
could, there is no room for them to do any more widening. Mr. Becker made a motion to the 
SSM letter dated September 4, 2026, under Waiver Request, Item #1 Section 154-24.A.(12) be 
granted, seconded by Mr. Colflesh.  Motion carried.   Mr. Morey read Item #2, Section 154-32.N. 
Mr. Becker made a motion to the SSM letter dated September 4, 2026, under Waiver Request, 
Item #2 Section 154-32.N. be approved, seconded by Ms. Burkovich.  Motion carried.  Mr. 
Morey read Item #3 Section 184-12.1.A, and Item #4 Section 184-12.1.(A)(4) under Compliance 
with Zoning Ordinance, the McCarthy engineer responded that the proposed building height will 
be 35’ not 60’.  Mr. Morey’s next major concern was Item # 26 Section 154-19.D.(20).  Mr. 
Bernard and the McCarthy engineer clarified this concern.  Mr. Bernard has spoken with the Fire 
Chief, and they currently access the building from the sides.  There is no concern with having to 
get to the back of the building.  Mr. Morey noted that Item # 29 should be noted as 
“Preliminary”.  Mr. Morey wanted to review two issues that were not listed on the SSM letter, 
the word “should” be replaced with “shall” and the lighting standard should have a minimum 5’ 
behind the curb or tire stops or the pedestal should be minimum of 30” high from the pavement.  
The McCarthy engineer asked for confirmation on Item # 5 regarding the percentage of the lot 
shall be maintained as open area, it is stated as 85%, but he thought it was 35%.  Atty. Mooney 
confirmed after looking at the Ordinance, the minimum open space is 35%, but the maximum 
building coverage area is 50%.  Mr. Morey will amend this section in the letter. Mr. Morey 
summarized, McCarthy Engineering is requesting this Board to take action on two additional 
wavier requests; 1) is for the fence and 2) for plan scale and to grant Preliminary Plan approval.  
Mr. Morey recommended that because McCarthy has not provided us with clear evidence 
regarding the open space coverage and the maximum building coverage and due to the 
complexity of the stormwater comments, it is his recommendation not to grant Preliminary Plan 
approval.

Data Center Ordinance Discussion 
 
Mr. Becker and Mr. Tricarico focused this month on noise issues, emergency response/traffic 
issues.  A question was asked about the different standards listed for weekend noise levels verse 
the weekday noise levels.  It was recommended to set the decibel level and stay consistent no 
matter the day of the week.  Atty. Mooney searched in the Zoning Ordinance and decibel level 
only comes up for recreational zoning district to not exceed 55 DBA.  Mr. Becker will 
incorporate the noise level of 55 DBA for this section.  Emergency response section was 
presented and Mr. Becker and Mr. Potts concluded that emergency management service should 
be included for their input in this section.  Mr. Potts will forward this section to Mr. Hinkle for 
review.  October’s meeting will focus on decommissioning and facade.
