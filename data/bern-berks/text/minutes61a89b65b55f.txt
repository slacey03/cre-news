PUBLIC COMMENT   
 
Steve Tricarico, Thomas Egan, & Mike Werley from Bern Township Planning Commission 
– Wanted to thank the Board for their continued hard work to keep Bern Township a great place 
to live.  Mr. Tricarico is here to summarize the review of the Alternative Solar Energy Ordinance.  
Over the past two (2) months the Planning Commission Committee has reviewed several other 
Berks County Municipalities that already have a solar ordinance along with Berks County 
Planning Commission’s recommendations.  On May 22nd a second version was submitted, along 
with Mr. Potts’s comments, some of which differ from the Planning Commission’s.  Mr. 
Tricarico discussed these differences and would like to come to a resolution with the Boards 
help.    Mr. Egan and Mr. Werley also provided noted differences and additional comments 
regarding commercial solar in some of the designated districts.

ordinance to allow this type of use in this location but ultimately looking to the Board for their 
feedback.  There were several questions asked by board members and residents.
