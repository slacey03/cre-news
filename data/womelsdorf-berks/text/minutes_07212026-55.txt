Council President Bruce A. Edwards called the meeting to order at 6:59 pm. The meeting opened with the Pledge of 
Allegiance. 
ATTENDEES: Council Members- Mr. Bruce Edwards, Mr. James Mellon Sr., Ms. Leanna Patches, Ms. Connie Keller, 
Borough Manager- Michael Williams, Borough Secretary – Jenna Thomason, Glen Bertolet- Kraft Municipal Group

Motion by Mr. Mellon, seconded by Ms. Keller to pay payroll between meetings. Motion carried unanimously.
COMMUNICATIONS: Tulpehocken Township request fire police, Sat. September 12, 2026. Motion by Ms. Patches, 
seconded by Ms. Keller, to provide 4 fire police as requested for Tulpehocken Township. Motion carried unanimously.
CITIZENS TO BE HEARD:
OLD BUSINESS: Traffic Ordinance (no action).  Vehicle ordinance 2026-03 and grass and weeds ordinance 
modifications, are scheduled to be published in Reading Eagle on Thursday, July 23, 2026, informing the public.
NEW BUSINESS: McDonald’s is requesting to place sidewalk/ADA ramps out of the right of way. Motion by Ms. 
Keller, seconded by Ms. Patches, to approve request to move sidewalk/ramps out of the right of way. Motion approved 
unanimously. 
MAINTENANCE: Working on getting signs for two staff only parking spots at library.  Discussion regarding backhoe 
radiator repair.  Received estimate of $3,300; for the repair. Motion by Ms. Keller, seconded by Ms. Patches, to approve 
repairs up to $3,400. Motion approved unanimously.
KRAFT MUNICIPAL GROUP – The Codes report was reviewed.  There was discussion regarding the code violation 
patrols.  Kraft requested and received confirmation that the 4 hours every other week is to include both the patrolling and 
associated administrative time. There was discussion regarding a pending court case involving Pottstown Borough and 
rental property program administrative warrants.  Pottstown legal counsel is looking for support from other municipalities 
with rental programs and are asking municipalities to contact the Pennsylvania State Association of Boroughs (PSAB) on 
behalf of Pottstown to support their appeal. Drafted letter to be sent to borough manager. Motion by Ms. Keller, seconded
by Mr. Mellon, to send letter supporting Pottstown’s appeal not to change the way inspections are done for rental 
properties currently. Motion carried unanimously. 
BOROUGH MANAGER – Borough Manager report was reviewed. Dollar General property assessment has been 
updated in county records, but Burk Ct. property has not been updated yet. Previous part-time employee has applied for 
unemployment.
MRS. WINDLEY – Absent
