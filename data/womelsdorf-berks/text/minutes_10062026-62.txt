Womelsdorf Borough Council Meeting
Held at 101 W. High St.
October 6, 2026
Council President Bruce Edwards called the meeting to order at 7:00 P.M.  The meeting opened
with the Pledge of Allegiance.
PRESENT:  Council Members – Mr. Bruce Edwards, Mr. David Craft, Mrs. Cindy Hopple, Ms. 
Leanna Patches, Mr. James Mellon Sr., Ms. Connie Keller, Mayor - Jennifer Gettle, Borough 
Manager- Michael Williams, Secretary- Jenna Thomason, Solicitor- Andrew George, 
Maintenance - Mike Smimmo, Engineer- James McCarthy
ABSENT: Mrs. Shannon Windley, Police Chief – John Pontician
MINUTES: Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Mr. Craft to approve the minutes from 
September 1, 2026. Motion carried unanimously. Motion by Ms. Patches, seconded by Mrs. 
Hopple, to approve the minutes from September 15, 2026. Motion carried unanimously.
BILLS TO BE PAID: Council members reviewed the list of bills to be paid. Motion by Mr. 
Mellon Sr., seconded by Mr. Craft to approve the list of bills to be paid in the amount of 
$123,334.92. Motion carried unanimously. Motion by Mr. Mellon Sr., seconded by Ms. Patches,
to approve ratification of bills in the amount of $150 to Della Bell for handicap parking 
application denial. Motion carried unanimously.
PAYROLL: Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Mr. Mellon Sr., to pay the current payroll.  
Motion carried unanimously. Motion by Ms. Patches, seconded by Ms. Keller, to pay payroll 
between meetings. Motion carried unanimously.
COMMUNICATIONS:  Fire police requested in Robesonia, Sunday November 1, 2026, for the
annual Jack Frost parade. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Ms. Keller, for fire police help 
in Robesonia for the annual Jack Frost parade. Motion carried unanimously. Discussion 
regarding utilizing the Berks ARL stray dog service, no action required.
CITIZENS TO BE HEARD:  Bob Martin – 501 W. High St., representing the Womelsdorf Fire
Company; the fire company attended the Berks County Radio meeting. On October 15, at 6pm, 
at the fire company they will be having a meeting; the purpose of the meeting, to see the 
different Motorola models being offered by the county. 
Phillip Henry - 37 Tulpy View Rd., representing the youth soccer association. Discussed on 
seeing if a financial agreement can be worked out to use the soccer fields at a discounted rate if 
they would maintain the fields.
SOLICITOR: Discussion of drafting an agreement for McDonald’s sidewalk project, no new 
action at this time. Drafted an agreement for PPL, awaiting the response to know how much the 
bond should be for. 
OLD BUSINESS: Traffic ordinance completed, to be reviewed at the next meeting.

NEW BUSINESS: LAO Co. at 410 N 3rd St., is looking to get a deadline extension on their 
sidewalk project. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Mr. Mellon Sr., for the deadline 
extension until May of 2027, for the sidewalk project with LAO Co. Motion carried 
unanimously. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Ms. Patches, for the same deadline 
extension to be given to McDonald’s, if an extension is requested. Motion carried unanimously. 
Resident residing at 21 Locust Ln., are looking to be given a stormwater exemption for installing
a shed, reviewed letters from resident and recommendations from McCarthy. Motion by Mr. 
Mellon Sr., seconded by Ms. Patches, for 21 Locust Ln., to be granted the stormwater exemption,
resident must still obtain required permits from Kraft for installing shed. Motion carried 
unanimously. Discussion regarding the fire company LSA grants. 
BOROUGH MANAGER: Discussed attending a PSAB training course on municipal 
employment law, costing $87.50. Motion by Mr. Craft, seconded by Ms. Paches, to attend PSAB
training course in municipal employment law. Motion carried unanimously. Discussed placing 
half of our PLGIT money into a term investment vehicle for about a year, at a 4.7% rate vs. a 
3.7-3.9% rate. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Mr. Mellon Sr., to place up to $594,000 of 
PLGIT money into a term investment vehicle. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Ms. 
Patches, to cancel one of the two HP ink monitoring accounts. Motion carried unanimously. 
McCARTHY ENGINEERING: Reviewed report. Discussion regarding the MTF grant for 
High St., the final bid tabulation. Reviewed the four different contractor bids for project. Motion
by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Mr. Mellon Sr., to award the contractor bid for the High St. project
to Forino Co. LP. Motion carried unanimously.
MR. SMIMMO-MAINTENANCE: The MS4 forum in Lancaster, was attended by the 
maintenance department this week. Leaf clean up has officially started. Discussion on the roof 
condition of the borough hall., should start looking into estimates for repair.
MRS. WINDLEY: Absent
MR. MELLON: Reviewed the upcoming curb and sidewalk project.  Discussion of applying for
2027-2028 MTF grant. 
MS. KELLER:  Library is looking into doing a bus trip to Longwood Gardens for Christmas, 
more information to come. 
MS. PATCHES: Rec board meeting tomorrow night.
