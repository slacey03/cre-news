WOMELSDORF BOROUGH WORKSHOP MEETING
Held at 101 W. High Street
 June 16, 2026
1
Council President Bruce A. Edwards called the meeting to order at 7:00 pm. The meeting opened with the Pledge of 
Allegiance. 
ATTENDEES: Council President Bruce Edwards, Council Members: Cindy Hopple, David Craft, James Mellon Sr, 
Leanna Patches, Connie Keller, Shannon Windley, Mayor Jennifer Gettle, Borough Manager- Michael Williams, Glen 
Bertolet- Kraft Municipal Group
ABSENT: Police Chief- John Pontician, Secretary- Jenna Thomason
BILLS TO BE PAID: Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Mr. Craft to pay bills in the amount of $29,233.78. Motion 
carried unanimously.
PAYROLL: Motion by Mr. Craft, seconded by Ms. Patches to pay the current payroll. Motion carried unanimously. 
Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Mr. Craft to pay payroll between meetings. Motion carried unanimously.
COMMUNICATIONS: A thank you card was received from recently retired borough secretary Charmaine Beck.
CITIZENS TO BE HEARD: Resident Shannon Windley spoke about receiving the letter regarding the upcoming curb 
project and she reiterated previously voiced concerns regarding inconsistencies in the sidewalk review process that she 
experienced during the transfer inspections when she bought and sold a home in the borough.  She expressed frustration 
about the potential financial burden of having to repair sidewalk on the new home she purchased and concern regarding 
other residents that may be impacted similarly. There was discussion about the property, upcoming project, and the 
parameters of sidewalk requirements.
OLD BUSINESS: Traffic Ordinance (no action).  Bravo network production has concluded.
NEW BUSINESS: No new business
MAINTENANCE: Reported ongoing maintenance of the pool, and high volumes of brush pickup as well as continuing 
standard ongoing maintenance of borough facilities such as mowing and weed removal.  Reported that the new mower is 
working great and they are waiting on delivery of the replacement motor for the other mower, expected to be delivered the
end of the month.
KRAFT MUNICIPAL GROUP – The Codes report was reviewed.  There was discussion regarding property 
maintenance and grass and weed maintenance in the borough as well as enforcement.  Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded 
by Ms. Patches to have Kraft Municipal Group patrol the borough for grass and weeds violations pending solicitor 
approval, not to exceed four hours every other week.  Motion passed 5-2.
BOROUGH MANAGER – Borough Manager report was reviewed.  The DEP 902 Grant application was submitted.  
The commercial insurance package was renewed. The borough website migration to the upgraded CMS platform was 
completed with some minor issues still needing worked out.  TNR program partnership with NNN is almost ready to be 
announced.
MRS. WINDLEY – Nothing to report

MR. MELLON – Reviewed the upcoming curb project and associated curb and sidewalk inspections. Reviewed the 
parameters of what work the MTF grant covers and does not cover.  There was discussion regarding the criteria for 
sidewalk inspections and the sidewalk specifications, and the scope of work covered by the grant.  There was discussion 
regarding the sidewalk inspection program as a whole moving forward.
MS. KELLER – Nothing to report
MR. CRAFT – Nothing to report
MRS. HOPPLE – Grant work on war memorial will start tomorrow.  Discussion regarding community events for the 
250th celebration.  Pool memberships are increasing.  There was discussion regarding eliminating pool walk-in entry for 
next year.  
MS. PATCHES – Nothing to report
MAYOR GETTLE – There are donations coming in for national night out the first Tuesday in August at Bridge of Hope 
Church.  Presented the report from the Police Department. Discussion regarding fireworks on New Year’s Eve.  There 
was discussion regarding the timeline of when the new police car will receive lettering and when the old police car will be
sold.  There was additional discussion on when the information for updating the traffic ordinance will be ready.
MR. EDWARDS – Discussed upcoming pension plan review meeting and expressed disappointment in performance of 
the plans from the preceding year and interest in shopping around plan administration.
Motion by Mrs. Keller, seconded by Mr. Mellon Sr. to adjourn the meeting at 8:53 pm. Motion carried unanimously.
Meeting adjourned.
Respectfully Submitted, 
Michael Williams, Borough Manager
