work needs to be completed by a licensed contractor.

welfare concerns for streets within the brough. Ordinance drafted for restricting commercial

the ordinance, such as emergency vehicles, local deliveries, and school buses.  Motion by 
Shannon Windley, seconded by Mrs. Hopple, made to advertise the new traffic restriction 
ordinance. Motion carried unanimously.
