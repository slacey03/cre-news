NEW BUSINESS: Application for a handicap parking spot at 335 W. High St. Motion by Mrs. 
Windley, seconded by Ms. Patches, for a handicap parking spot at 335 W. High St. Motion 
carried unanimously. Fire company is applying for two LSA grants for repair of 
driveway/parking lot and AEDs. Motion by Mr. Mellon Sr., seconded by Mrs. Windley, for 
Resolution 2026-05 LSAP Statewide Authorized Official to repair and replace the driveway and 
parking lot at the fire station. Motion carried unanimously. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by
Mrs. Windley, for Resolution 2026-06 LSAP Category 4 Authorized Official to be used to 
purchase AEDs. Motion carried unanimously. Discussed police officer contributions to the 
pension plan. Motion by Mr. Mellon Sr., seconded by Ms. Patches for Resolution 2026-07, 
reducing employee contributions to police pension plan. Motion carried unanimously.
BOROUGH MANAGER:  Discussion of 2027 minimum municipal obligations for uniform and
non-uniform pension plans. Per Berks Conservation District, there is no specific requirements for
intermittent stream maintenance.
McCARTHY ENGINEERING: Reviewed report. MTF grant is now out for bid, should be able
to vote on at the October meeting.
MR. SMIMMO-MAINTENANCE: The AAON (rooftop) unit at the library needs repaired, the
VFD failed and needs replacement. MIH Systems Group, is the only vendor for this model unit 
in the area. Motion by Mr. Mellon Sr., seconded by Mrs. Windley, to replace the VFD fan at the 
library for up to $2,400. Motion carried unanimously. Maintenance department to insulate the 
roof. Quotes received to repair the library HVAC unit and an additional head unit in conference 
room and children’s room. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Ms. Patches, to reject the quote
from Comfort Pro, due to not meeting the standards. Motion carried unanimously.  Motion by 
Mr. Mellon Sr., seconded by Mrs. Hopple to accept the bid from KB Heating, Cooling, and 
Plumbing, and that the library will also cover $2,500 towards the project. Motion carried 
unanimously.
MRS. WINDLEY: Nothing new to add.
MR. MELLON: Reviewed the upcoming curb and sidewalk project.  Finishing up sidewalk 
inspections throughout the borough.
MS. KELLER:  Absent
MS. PATCHES: Rec board meeting tomorrow night. Will be going to discuss with them how to
create a meeting agenda, take proper minutes, and report where the money is being distributed. 
Nothing else new to add.
MR CRAFT:  Meeting with UGI regarding the upcoming project and the detours in town. 
PennDOT has until September 14, to approve the detour plans. Until then, flagger force will be 
restricting to just one lane. Staging area for equipment is at the east end of town. Flaggers are to 
allow buses through as a priority.
