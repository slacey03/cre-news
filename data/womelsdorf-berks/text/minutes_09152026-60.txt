Womelsdorf Borough Workshop Meeting
Held at 101 W. High St
September 15, 2026
Council President Bruce A. Edwards called the meeting to order at 7:00 pm. The meeting opened
with the Pledge of Allegiance. 
ATTENDEES: Council Members- Mr. Bruce Edwards, Mrs. Shannon Windley, Ms. Leanna 
Patches, Mrs. Cindy Hopple, Ms. Connie Keller, Police Chief- John Pontician, Mayor- Mrs. 
Jennifer Gettle, Borough Manager- Michael Williams, Borough Secretary– Jenna Thomason, 
Maintenance - Mike Smimmo, Kraft Municipal Group- Glenn Bertolet
ABSENT: Mr. James Mellon Sr., Mr. David Craft
BILLS TO BE PAID: Motion by Ms. Keller, seconded by Mrs. Hopple to pay bills in the 
amount of $37,456.48. Motion carried unanimously. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Mrs. 
Windley, to approve ratification of bills in the amount of $300 for DJ Dave Kaley for Labor Day 
Party at the pool. Motion carried unanimously.
PAYROLL: Motion by Ms. Keller, seconded by Mrs. Hopple to pay the current payroll. Motion
carried unanimously. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, seconded by Ms. Patches to pay payroll between 
meetings. Motion carried unanimously. 
COMMUNICATIONS: Fire police requested in Myerstown, Saturday November 28, 2026. 
Rain date Sunday November 29, 2026, for the annual holiday parade. Motion by Mrs. Hopple, 
seconded by Mrs. Windley, for fire police help in Myerstown for the annual holiday parade. 
Motion carried unanimously. Discussion on utilizing South Heidelberg Police Department 
services, no action at this time.
CITIZENS TO BE HEARD:
OLD BUSINESS: Traffic Ordinance, McDonald’s sidewalk agreement, and PPL master bond 
agreements for street cuts, are in process, no new action.
NEW BUSINESS: Reviewed FastBridge Fiber ROW license agreement. Motion by Mrs. 
Hopple, seconded by Ms. Patches, for FastBridge Fiber to place their fiberoptic internet transfer 
box in the right of way (ROW). Motion carried unanimously. Handicap parking application 
reviewed for 33 N. 2nd St., the request has been declined, due to having two spots already in this 
block of N. 2nd St.
MAINTENANCE: Library repairs are being scheduled to be done at the end of this week and 
beginning of next week. Repairs will fix the concerns for temperature regulation throughout the 
library. Fall clean up was this past weekend, was a big success.
KRAFT MUNICIPAL GROUP: The Codes report was reviewed. Kraft has been patrolling the 
borough for any code violations; things have been going well with the response from the 
properties to get back in code.
