Womelsdorf Borough Workshop Meeting
Held at 101 W. High St
October 20, 2026
Council President Bruce A. Edwards called the meeting to order at 7:00 pm. The meeting opened
with the Pledge of Allegiance. 
ATTENDEES: Council Members- Mr. Bruce Edwards, Mr. James Mellon Sr., Ms. Leanna 
Patches, Mr. David Craft, Ms. Connie Keller, Borough Manager- Michael Williams, Borough 
Secretary– Jenna Thomason, Maintenance - Mike Smimmo, Kraft Municipal Group- Glenn 
Bertolet
ABSENT: Mrs. Shannon Windley, Mrs. Cindy Hopple, Police Chief- John Pontician, Mayor- 
Mrs. Jennifer Gettle
BILLS TO BE PAID: Motion by Mr. Craft, seconded by Ms. Patches to pay bills in the amount
of $118,848.52. Motion carried unanimously.
PAYROLL: Motion by Ms. Keller, seconded by Mr. Mellon Sr., to pay the current payroll. 
Motion carried unanimously. Motion by Ms. Patches, seconded by Mr. Craft to pay payroll 
between meetings. Motion carried unanimously. 
COMMUNICATIONS:
CITIZENS TO BE HEARD:
OLD BUSINESS: Traffic Ordinance, McDonald’s sidewalk agreement, and PPL master bond 
agreements for street cuts, are in process, no new action. Berks ARL stray dog service, found to 
have no financial commitment, unless the borough would utilize the services. Motion by Mr. 
Craft, seconded by Ms. Patches, to use the Berks ARL stray dog service, for a one-year
agreement. Motion carried unanimously.
NEW BUSINESS: Reviewed letter requesting the release of the escrow account for 128 E. High
St., as well as the letter of recommendation from McCarthy. Motion by Mr. Craft, seconded by 
Mr. Mellon Sr., for the escrow to be released for project at 128 E. High Street, contingent upon 
payment of all borough fees and invoices and receipt of the 18-month maintenance bond of 
$13,573.05. Motion carried unanimously.
MAINTENANCE: Contractors visited the borough to inspect the roof in need of repair. Bids 
are currently under review by council; a vote is scheduled to occur at the next meeting.
KRAFT MUNICIPAL GROUP: Codes report was reviewed. 2027 fee schedule was sent out to
borough.
BOROUGH MANAGER: Borough Manager report was reviewed.
MRS. WINDLEY: Absent
