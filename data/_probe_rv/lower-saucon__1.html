Planning Commission

  • Home
  • Government
  • Planning Commission

A Planning Commission is a governmental advisory body responsible for guiding land use and development policies. Its main role is to make recommendations to Council on matters related to urban planning, zoning, and development.

Key Functions of a Planning Commission

Review Development Proposals:
-Evaluates plans for new buildings, subdivisions, or commercial projects.
-Ensures proposals comply with zoning laws and comprehensive plans.

Zoning Recommendations:
-Advises on zoning changes or special exceptions to zoning rules.
-Helps maintain orderly growth and protect community character.

Comprehensive Planning:
-Helps draft and update a comprehensive (or general) plan, a long-term blueprint for the community’s physical development.
-Considers factors like housing, transportation, infrastructure, and environmental sustainability.

Public Hearings and Community Input:
-Holds meetings where the public can comment on planning issues.
-Balances private development interests with community needs.

Advisory Role:
-Makes non-binding recommendations; the final decisions are made by Council.
