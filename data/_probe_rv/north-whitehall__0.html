Parks & Recreation
A RICH HERITAGE.

A STRONG FUTURE.

Located in the Northwestern area of the Lehigh Valley, North Whitehall offers beautiful parks and trails, quality schools, open space, and convenient regional access, making it a great place to live, play, and grow.

Home to 16,000 residents, the community blends residential and agricultural charm, features some of the Valley’s best farmland, and welcomes thoughtful business and development to support continued growth.

3256 Levans Rd. Coplay, PA 18037
Emergency: Call 911
