Zoning Hearing Board

  • Home
  • Government
  • Zoning Hearing Board

A Zoning Hearing Board (ZHB) is a local, quasi-judicial body that makes decisions on zoning-related matters within a municipality. Its primary functions include:

  1. Hearing Variance Requests – Property owners can request relief from strict zoning regulations if they face unnecessary hardship. The ZHB determines whether a variance is justified based on legal criteria.
  2. Reviewing Special Exceptions – Some zoning districts allow certain uses only with special approval. The ZHB determines whether these exceptions meet zoning ordinance requirements.
  3. Handling Appeals – If a resident or business disagrees with a decision made by the zoning officer (such as a denied permit), they can appeal to the ZHB.
  4. Interpreting Zoning Ordinances – The board may be asked to clarify specific zoning rules or determine how they apply to a property.

The ZHB operates independently of Township Council to ensure impartiality in its decisions. Meetings are usually open to the public, and decisions can often be appealed to a higher court.

Please check the Municipal Planning Code for the Pennsylvania laws that govern the functions of Zoning Hearing Boards.  Members are appointed by Lower Saucon Council for terms not to exceed five years unless re-appointed for another term.
