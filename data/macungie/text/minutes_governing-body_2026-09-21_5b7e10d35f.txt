VII. New Business
A. Authorizing Chief of Police Recruitment Profile
Vice President Landis stated that he would like to see a wider range used for the salary to attract a
larger number of candidates and recommends $115,000 to $130,000. Ms. Megan Sell stated that
she would like to know the economic cost of what the fringe benefits would be. Mr. Ashelman
stated that is something that would be worked out in negotiations with the candidate.
Michael Mullen, Borough police officer, commented on the significant jump in salary from the
current Officer in Charge. Mr. Ashelman stated that the OIC is an hourly position with opportunities
for overtime among other things, and the Chief would be a salaried position.
Mr. Romig stated the Borough has not had a Chief of Police for ten years and the Council did not
listen to its own officers stating they needed one. Now that the Borough has paid for a study it
decided it was time for a Chief.
Ms. Buss stated that the Borough does not need a Chief of Police, because we made it this far
without one.
Jeff Hartman, resident, asked if internal candidates will be considered as well. Mr. Ashelman stated
that the current officers are welcome to apply. Mr. Hartman commented on the other assignments
that the consulting group has, other than finding a new Chief.
Motion by Vice President Landis to approve the Chief of Police Recruitment Profile, with
the starting salary updated to $115,000 to $130,000, seconded by Mr. Hutchison. Motion
passed unanimously.
