MACUNGIE BOROUGH COUNCIL
MEETING MINUTES
Special Meeting
Monday, September 28, 2026 — 1:00 P.M.

I. Call to Order — Pledge of Allegiance
The Macungie Borough Council meeting was called to order on September 28, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. at the
Macungie Institute, 510 E. Main Street, Macungie, PA 18062. In attendance were Carl Sell, Jr.,
President; Stan Landis, Vice President; Greg Hutchison, Member; Megan Sell, Member; Greg
Reinbold, Member; Lynette Sell, Member; Tom Ashelman, Member; Bob Sell, Jr., Mayor; Patrick
Armstrong, Esq., Solicitor; and Kalman Sostarecz, Jr., Borough Manager.
President Sell opened the meeting and asked all present to join him in reciting the Pledge of Allegiance
to the Flag of the United States of America.

I. Announcement of Executive Sessions
Mr. Sostarecz announced that an executive session was held on September 28, 2026 at 11:30 a.m.
regarding the Collective Bargaining Agreement with the Macungie Police Officer’s Association.

III. Old Business

A. Lumber Street Development — Discussion on Potential Paper Street

Solicitor Armstrong stated that based on the previous Council meeting, the Council wished to hold
this special meeting to discuss the potential paper street, Green Street, located on the Young
Development’s property. He has had the opportunity to review the deeds that have been provided
by adjacent property owners, the existing maps at Borough Hall, and recorded land development
plans. Although the deeds provided by residents do reference an alley, it is not depicted on any
maps that the Borough has. His opinion is that there is nothing actionable to be considered by
Council at this time. However, he did issue a supplement to the conditional approval letter to the
applicant, highlighting the information provided by the adjacent property owners.

Wendy Horn, resident, read from another deed she has located; Solicitor Armstrong requested she
provide a copy to the Borough, and also suggested she speak with the applicant’s attorney as is a
private property issue.

Brenda Decker, resident, requested that it be investigated if there is a title to the alley. She asked
why she was denied access to look at the plans. Mr. Sostarecz stated that she did review the plans
earlier this year and is welcome to do so again.

Paul Licata, business owner, stated that if the project moves forward, that the Borough changes
North Church Street to be one way going north to Lumber Street.

Tim Romig, business owner, stated that county records should be researched, and would like to
know what year the applicant’s attorney went back to. He commented that there are pins twenty
feet behind their properties in the grass. He stated that he had attended a Council meeting years ago
when Don Young had been at a Council meeting and asked Council about the alley. He also
commented on the previous vacation of Oak Alley. He believes the Council has not been presented
with truthful information by the applicant. He believes copies of the plans should be released under
the Right to Know Law. Solicitor Armstrong stated that copyrights need to be considered.

Terry Buss, resident, stated that Council should defend and support the residents She also
commented on the traffic study that was completed.


Jack McGrath, resident, requested to view the plans and commented on the stormwater runoff,

Bob Bogert, resident, stated that he feels the plan was approved without the full information on the
paper street. He feels that the Council had a chance to do something about the development and
chose not to.

Virginia Voros, resident, asked what the conditions are for the plan approval. Solicitor Armstrong
stated that there are several conditions, most of which are related to the Borough ordinances, and
that they are followed up on by Borough Staff and Consultants. She also commented on the paper
alley and in her experience the land is split among the adjacent land owners.

IV. New Business

A. Ratification of Police Collective Bargaining Agreement - TENTATIVE

Solicitor Armstrong stated that Council met in executive session to discuss the status of the
negotiations with the Macungie Police Officers Association, and that there is still one outstanding
item that needs to be determined prior to taking action. He believes a motion to table is appropriate
until the final item is settled. Mr. Ashelman stated that there have been very good discussions
occurring but they are waiting for one more piece of information.
Motion by President Sell to table the item based on the Solicitor’s advice, seconded by
Mr. Reinbold. Motion passed unanimously.

V. Public Comment on Non-Agenda Items

VI.

Mr. Romig commented on the curb and sidewalk installation occurring at the Mavis Tire, as well as the
traffic safety during construction. Mr. Sostarecz stated that BIA is well aware of the issues with the
curb and sidewalk and the contractor is going to be redoing their work to meet standards. Mr. Romig
also commented on the height of the grass at the Young property.

Mr. Licata commented on the agenda and supporting documents being available on the Borough’s
website after meetings. Mr. Sostarecz stated that historically those items are removed after the meeting
occurs, and the topic would be on the next Council agenda.

Adjournment
President Sell made a motion to adjourn the meeting at 1:39 p.m., seconded by Mr. Hutchison.
Motion passed unanimously.
