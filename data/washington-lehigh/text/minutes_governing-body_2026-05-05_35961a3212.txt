PUBLIC COMMENT 
Doug Mankos, 9239 North Loop Road – He said he came to vent because the township came down Loop 
Road and in his words, butchered a lot of trees, cut branches off 8-10 foot up in the air and never cleaned 
up the road or their property. He and his neighbor had to go out and clean up and then finish cutting 
branches that were just left hanging. He said he has enough to clean up his property and doesn’t need to 
clean up after the township. He thinks they need more supervision or more training. He was told this will 
be addressed with Wade. 
Jeremy Frable, 7618 Williamstown Lane – 5 years ago he lost his son, he was an athlete, played baseball 
at Slatedale Park and then became a coach when he was 17. He would like the board to take into 
consideration to rename the Slatedale Park in memory of his son Chase. He said he would make a yearly 
donation to the Township for grass cutting or whatever is needed.
