Board of Supervisor Budget Workshop Meeting Minutes 
Washington Township, Lehigh County 
Emerald, PA 18080 
October 12th, 2026 
 
 
 
Chairman Shawn Wanamaker called the budget workshop meeting for Washington Township, Lehigh 
County Board of Supervisors to order at 5:15 PM at the Washington Township Building, 7951 Center 
Street, Emerald, PA 18080.   
Members Present- Shawn Wanamaker, Tom Dengler, Robert Schramel 
Roll Call: Shawn Wanamaker - Yes, Tom Dengler - Yes, Robert Schramel - Yes 
Also present were Manager Wade Marlatt - Yes and Secretary/Treasurer Brenda Park - Yes 
NEW BUSINESS: Budget discussion for 2027 - Shawn explained the budget process for anyone that has 
not been at these meetings before. Wade Marlatt, Manager gave a high level overview with regards to 
the 2027 Budget.  He went over key points within the General Fund.  He briefly went over the Sewer 
Account, Recreation Fund, State Highway Fund and the Streetlight/Fire Fund which he explained will not 
be balanced next year due to the price of electric and water prices going up; however, due to the 
balance that is currently available we do not have to increase the prices this year.   
SUPERVISORS COMMENT: Shawn noted that there will be an Executive Session after the meeting to 
discuss personnel.  
Shawn asked if what we charge to rent Eagles Nest is still appropriate and in line with the other rentals 
and it was determined it is in line with Slatedale and Emerald. 
Tommy asked about increasing the Moving Permits to $5.00 to keep things more in line with the 
surrounding areas. Shawn wasn’t sure if this was something that needed to be voted on, Wade said he 
would check to see what it would take to increase the cost. 
Shawn asked about the Slatington Borough compost reimbursement money. Wade explained that is the 
money we receive from Slatington and Walnutport for the grinding and we pay the other third.  
Shawn asked if the legal fees were increased this year but Wade said no, he will have to keep a close eye 
on that as well as the Engineer fees. 
Tommy asked if it was necessary to have the Zoning Officer full-time instead of part-time. Wade said he 
thinks we do since he is currently fixing all the filing issues we had and he is busy all the time he is here. 
Shawn thinks it is worth it to keep everyone in line with new people coming into the township and make 
sure everything is in line. Shawn said he would need more information before cutting to part-time. 
Tommy said there is a government time study that could be done to see if it would be economical. 
Shawn told him to check on the cost for the time study. Tommy said he knows it really falls off in the fall 

and winter compared to spring and summer. Shawn said he is not opposed to it but needs more 
information on how he would want it structured.  
Tommy asked if we don’t get any of the grants, will we be purchasing that equipment instead. Wade 
said no, there is no money budgeted for that. Shawn asked when we would find about the grants, Wade 
said he would check on that.  
Shawn asked if they would like to look into the study and also how they would want to structure the 
zoning and meet again prior to posting the budget. Tommy said he could call and see what they get.  
Tommy asked also about an electronic door for the Slatedale bathrooms instead of paying someone. 
Bob asked if that money could come out of the Rec Fund. 
PUBLIC COMMENT:  Joel Merkel asked how much money the Township actually gets out of the 
inspection money. Wade said it is a pass through so we only receive the permit fees. 
MEETING ADJOURNED: 10/12/26 @ 6:02 PM  
A motion was made by Tom Dengler for adjournment and seconded by Robert Schramel. 
Roll Call: Shawn Wanamaker - Yes, Tom Dengler - Yes, Robert Schramel - Yes
