SPECIAL PRESENTATION:
Brian Preski, President of the National Water Specialties (NWS) Company, spoke about backflow
prevention.  This company provides backflow and cross-connection control programs in 11 states and it
does it for approximately 150 municipalities in Pennsylvania.  Pennsylvania's main public water rule is 25
Pa. Code § 109.709, titled cross-connection control program. It prohibits customers from introducing
contaminants into a public water supply through a service connection. It also makes the customer
responsible for eliminating cross-connections or providing backflow devices that protect the distribution
system from back siphonage and backpressure.  Mr. Preski cited the laws that require backflow and cross-
connection controls.  
As the law is written, NWS would initially manage the backflow for commercial and industrial businesses
and three (3) categories of residential locations:
1) Any residential with fire suppression;
2) Any resident with real irrigation; and
3) Any inground pool that has automatic fill for evaporation.

2
This is done annually for only commercial businesses and the three (3) types of residentials, listed above.
There is no cost to the Borough. NWS tracks all backflow devices via database.  The fire hydrants would
also be checked.  This program is mandated under Federal and State Law and the Environmental Protection
Agency (EPA) is beginning to enforce these laws to prevent contaminants from being introduced into the
water system.
