                                                                                                             Borough Manager Roy Hambrecht
Manager Hambrecht informed the Council that we have a notification system on the Borough website and urging
residents to sign up to be notified of emergency and non-emergency issues.
Manager Hambrecht reported that the Borough will be transitioning over to a new financial software system
during the first week of August, and a new utility billing system in October.  Both systems will be supported by
Edmunds GovTech.
Manager Hambrecht stated that the antenna for the water meter system was recently installed.
Manager Hambrecht stated that the Borough was awarded two (2) grants via the Local Share Account (LSA)
grant for the following:
 a new police vehicle; and
 a new roof for the Slatington Library (the Borough sponsored the Slatington Library).
The grant monies are being withheld as the Borough is not up-to-date with our audits.  The Department of
Community and Economic Development (DCED) amended its policy to now require completed audits rather
than accepting a commitment letter from an auditor.  After speaking with the Borough’s contracted auditor,
Manager Hambrecht received a commitment that the 2024 audit will be completed by the first week of August.
It is expected that funding will be released once the audit is submitted to DCED.
Manager Hambrecht briefly noted the budget.  We are currently under the 50% spend for all departments. There
are no expected large purchases at this time. Manager Hambrecht and Karen Leach will be meeting with
department heads along with the respective councilor, to discuss spending for the first half of the year and budget
prep for 2027.

2 Main Street Zoning overlay - Discussion of zoning classification (Residential vs Commercial). Jim
Macort (Keystone Consulting Engineers (KCE) and the Borough’s contracted zoning official) to attend
and provide details.
Mr. Macort stated that a lot of the properties on Main St., between Church St and Kern St., were
commercial properties at one time.  However, due to an ordinance change years ago, those properties
reverted back to residential.  Mr. Macort received many inquiries as to whether these properties can be
converted back into commercial status.  Rather than attempting to convert each property individually,
and to create a business-friendly environment within the Borough, Mr. Macort presented to Council to
broadly change the status of this section of the Borough to a commercial overlay district and open the
door to potential business growth in this section of the Borough.  Mr. Macort added he has noticed
approximately nine (9) properties currently that have old store fronts, which could easily be converted
back to a business.  
Ms. Burek asked if this would change the tax structure.  Mr. Macort stated it possibly could.
Councilor Schnaars made a motion, duly seconded by Councilor Hassold, to authorize Solicitor Young
to begin the necessary draft to make the zoning change along Main St., between Church St. and Kern St.
Councilor Schnaars would like this placed on the next committee agenda.  President Mayberry declared
the motion passed following a Voice Vote.
