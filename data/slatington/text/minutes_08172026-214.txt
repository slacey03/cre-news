PUBLIC COMMENT:
 
Gene Stalnecker, 136 Cherry Street. Signed in and was recognized to speak.  Mr. Stalnecker
informed the Committee that the police can run a report to determine if an animal has been deemed
dangerous.   After Mr. Stalnecker’s incident with a “dangerous dog” he sent that link to Officer
Hutchinson in an effort to share this information.  Officer Hutchinson replied he had received
it.  Mr. Stalnecker provided a copy of the laws to the Committee and the history of the incidents
he had with his neighbor’s dogs.  
 
Jim Layland, 221 Union Street, signed in and was recognized to speak. Jim Layland is the treasurer
for the Northern Lehigh Historical Society (NLHS).  Mr. Layland stated the Venture Group is a
project committee for the NLHS and operates under it.  The NLHS has been in existence since
2002 and has completed several projects, including the covered bridge and the restrooms at the
Trailhead.  The Venture Group now operates the D&L Trailhead in Slatington.
The Venture Group is a legitimate non-profit group.  Mr. Layland indicated that the Employee
Identification Number (EIN) is EIN 23-3061790 (DLN 17053225020041).  The initial letter
granting non-profit status was issued in January of 2002, the final determinant letter was issued in
November of 2002.  Its filings of the 990-Ns with the IRS are up to date as of 2025.
There are claims stating that the Venture Group is not a legitimate non-profit.  That is false.  In
fact, the Venture Group operates under the NLHS.
The Venture Group is questioning how they will get reimbursed for the electricity the new vendor
will be using.  The current vendor pays the NLHS to cover expenses only.    The NLHS pays all
PPL bills at the Trailhead and the covered bridge.  There are also other expenses that are required
including cleaning supplies, bathroom supplies, and maintenance costs. The current vendor will
do pro bono plowing of the parking lot in the winter and regularly makes donations to the
NLHS.   Mr. Layland added that the Lions Club pays the NLHS for its share of electricity during
the winter months (Christmas trees).    
Manager Hambrecht stated the Borough needs documentation that the Venture Group can legally
operate on behalf of the Borough.  Manager Hambrecht has contacted the Borough solicitor
regarding this situation.  The solicitor indicated that the Borough would have to step in and move
forward with operating the Trailhead if the Venture Group is unable to produce the necessary
documentation.
Council will have to make a determination as to how it wants to move forward.
