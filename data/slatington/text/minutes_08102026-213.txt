Crystal Bilby, 7257 PA Route 873, Washington Township, signed in and was recognized to speak.  Ms.
Bilby is trying to open a coffee business.  Ms. Bilby has been approved by the Borough to locate her coffee
van at the Trailhead; however, she is not willing to work with the Venture Group.  Ms. Bilby stated the
Venture Group has not provided documents stating it is acting on behalf of the Borough.   The Borough is
leasing the land from the County and there is no agreement that permits the Venture Group to do business
on behalf of the Borough.  Ms. Bilby doesn’t understand how the Venture Group can require rent since
there is no agreement with the Borough.  Ms. Bilby has submitted seven (7) Right-To-Knows (RTK)
requests from the Venture Group and, to date, Ms. Bilby has not received a response.  Ms. Bilby has
informed the Auditor General and the County District Attorney about this issue.  Ms. Bilby informed the
Council that the County may have the right to pull the lease agreement from the Borough.  Ms. Bilby has no
problem paying rent to the Borough, but not to the Venture Group.  The Venture Group is not currently
registered as a non-profit organization.  Ms. Bilby is requesting permission to give the rent directly to the
Borough.  President Mayberry informed Ms. Bilby the Council will discuss this at executive session with
the solicitor.  Ms. Bilby added the Venture Group has deterred vendors from working at the Trailhead.

Mayor Schoch received a call from Mr. Dale Stigerwalt, from the Masons, in reference to the back parking
lot on Church Street.  With the recent torrential rainstorm, the stone parking lot at the Masons building,
which cost $1,500, was washed out and a lot of debris washed downstream and collected there. Manager
Hambrecht provided photos.   Mayor Schoch spoke with Justin from Lehigh Engineering because in 2024
we spoke in depth about how to prevent this problem with the water coming down.  It was discussed to
install a line to redirect the water away from that property.  Mayor Schoch feels we should revisit this and
talk about how to address this problem.
Community Center (Mason Building) Background: Mr. Steigerwalt indicated that about 14 years ago when
the community center project started, the concrete headwall was placed there.   The plan was that the
headwall was to connect to an underground storm system, which would divert storm water away from the
parking lot.  This headwall was designed to carry just the surface water for the parking lot.  With recent
storm water improvements on Franklin Street, however, that water is being diverted and impacting the
community center parking lot.  The Masons invested a significant amount of money to excavate and make
sure the headwall is clear to prevent this problem, all of which was approved by the Lehigh Valley Planning
Commission. With the recent storm, a significant amount of debris washed into the headwall, clogging it
up, and washing away much of the recently placed stone from the parking lot. To replace the stone, the
Masons ordered two (2) loads of stone.  They also tried to build up the berm again. The concern is when
more money is invested to pave the parking lot, there is a risk of the water eroding the pavement.  The cost
of the paving is expected to be around $100,000.00.  Mr. Steigerwalt indicated that it isn’t the Masons’
responsibility to continuously repair a lot that is being damaged by water as a result of additional drainage

4
work the Borough needs to complete.  He is asking the Council for its help.  Mayor Schoch stated this will
be discussed in a committee meeting later.  President Mayberry requested Abigail Wieder to review the
information from Lehigh Engineering and have some suggestions for the Highway Committee meeting.
