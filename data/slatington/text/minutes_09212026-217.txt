B.  PUBLIC SAFETY
William Schippers -Chairman; Members: Kevin Steckel, David Schnaars
1. Committee Member discussion on listed topics:
a. Consideration of an Animal Control Office.  Police Chief Borst is waiting to hear back from the
Humane Society.  Chief Borst spoke with the CEO of the Lehigh Valley Humane Society on
September 16th.  He and Councilor Schippers will set a date for a tour/meeting at their facility.
b. Follow-up discussion regarding the development of ordinances for e-bikes.  Councilor Schippers
has various municipal ordinances and would like to do a review.  Councilor Schippers to provide
sample ordinances. Councilor Schippers added he feels the Borough does not have the funds to
implement some of the things other municipalities have.
Chief Borst will be attending a meeting on October 9th with the Lehigh County District
Attorney’s office and the PA Highway Safety Network to discuss micromobility issues that are
affecting the region.  
Councilor Schippers informed the committee people are charging their e-bikes and/or
phones at the Trailhead using the ATM electric power.  Councilor Schippers asked if the
highway crew could lock the power to prevent this.  Councilor Schnaars suggested a
sign stating not for public use for charging phones or e-bikes.

2
c. Visioning – Police Department (PD).  Chief Borst stated there have been a lot of upgrades at the
training center and by October they should be operational. They have received all their
equipment, mats, tables and chairs to host classes.
The PD will be holding its Civil Service Examination on October 10th, 2026, to create an
eligibility list for hiring.  The Police Department is anticipating a vacancy as soon as November
1, 2026, because of an officer retirement.  In addition, the PD hopes to be able to resolve the
medical leave issue with another officer and fill that position.
Chief Borst strongly suggests that the Brough Council consider hiring two officers after our
eligibility list is created.  This would address both vacancies and give us the opportunity to hire
qualified applicants before they are hired at other agencies.  
d. Visioning – Fire.  Councilor Schippers stated the Fire Department (FD) has been doing well with
its fundraising.  The FD has their Bingo scheduled for October 3rd and there are other things
pending for the coming months.
Chief Frable informed the Committee Fire Prevention Month is coming in October.  They have
scheduled it on October 9th, 2026, at Lehigh Elementary.  
There were no questions from the committee members; the Public Safety Committee meeting was
adjourned.
