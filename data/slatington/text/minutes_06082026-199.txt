2. Obtaining more information to hire an Animal Control Officer. President Mayberry read the report 
from Police Chief Borst:
Animal Control Officer Information
Chief Borst reached out to surrounding agencies to find out what other municipalities are doing for 
Animal Control within their municipalities.
o Chief Borst spoke with Chief Jason Nothstein of Walnutport Police Department
 Pick up stray dogs and take them to the Borough kennel.
 If not claimed, they contact surrounding shelters for placement.
 For dangerous dogs, they refer the issue to the PA Dog Warden.
 They only deal with domesticated dogs; no other animals.
 Walnutport has not established a contract with the Humane Society.
o Chief Borst spoke with the Pennsylvania State Police, Bethlehem Barracks
 Advised that every municipality is handled differently.
 Heidelberg Township contracts with Northampton Borough.
 Animals must be brought there from a Pennsylvania State Police (PSP) 
member.
 North Whitehall Township contracts with Mighty Paws.
 Washington Township does not contract with any agency, to their knowledge.
Councilor Schippers spoke with Nazareth and surrounding communities, and they all contact Blue 
Mountain Regional (BMR) for aggressive dogs.  BMR responds relatively quickly when called.
