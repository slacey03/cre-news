PUBLIC COMMENT:
Gene Stalnecker, 136 Cherry Street, signed in and was recognized to speak.  Mr. Stalnecker informed the
Council that it should draft an ordinance for an Animal Control Officer (ACO).  Mr. Stalnecker suggested
looking at Nesquehoning’s ordinance as a guide.  He stated that Nesquehoning and Slatington are similar,

the revenues, not the State.

welcome to put in a Right-To-Know request for that information.  Ms. Vreeland alleges that police have used
inappropriate language when responding to incidents.

number of calls. Ms.

Councilor Schnaars would like to move forward in hiring a full-time code enforcement
officer.Manager Hambrecht has a job description which will be put on the next Council agenda.
c. Discussion of Pavement Condition Study (PCS) (Acela provided a proposal).  Mike Waldron
from Acela provided an estimate with three (3) different approaches (options) of pursuing a PCS
as follows:
a. Option 1: PASER-Style Windshield Pavement Survey.  Acela evaluates the
condition of the roads on a scale of 1 to 10.  With this scoring, a decision can be
made as to which road you wish to prioritize. This cost would be $15,000.00.
b. Option 2: Paving Condition Index (PCI) study.  This study measures lengths of
cracks and how many there are in a statistical representative sample.   This type of
scoring can tell you how the roads are deteriorating over time and can assist in the
prioritization of those roads years from now. The cost would be between $50,000.00
and $75,000.00.
c. Option 3: Scanner System.  The equipment (scanner) is similar to the PCI (Option 2)
but provides additional details.  In addition to determining the long-term
prioritization of the road repairs, it does an inventory of street signs, water valves,
fire hydrants, etc. The cost would be between $90,000.00 and $135,000.00.
Councilor Schnaars would like this to be tabled until the August council meeting
when the budget for next year can be discussed.  The Committee agrees on the
second option.
d. Discussion of Shadow Oaks Lane (Acela provided a proposal).  This proposal is similar to the
Maple Springs Road paving project last year.  The cost for the engineering services will be
approximately $20,000.00.  The Council is projecting that this project will go out to bid by the
end of this year, so work can start next spring.  Councilor Schnaars would like this placed on the
next committee agenda to discuss the financing.  
e. Discussion on Tourism Development Grant possibilities – Deadline for the application to be
submitted is July 6 (attachment was provided).  President Mayberry informed the Committee
that he had discussed this with the Economic Development and Beautification Committee
(EDBC) and they are scheduled to meet with Vito Gallo, Borough Grant Writer, on Wednesday,
June 17, 2026, about the grant possibilities.  The EDBC has identified at least one (1) option at
this time.  This grant can be used to improve facilities related to tourisms as well as tourism
events. President Mayberry suggested contacting the Moose Lodge and asking if the Borough

frequenting the downtown area. Per President Mayberry, there will be a Special Meeting of
Council held on Monday, June 29, 2026, to take action on this issue and the grant for the Quality
of Life.   Manager Hambrecht will advertise the special meeting.
