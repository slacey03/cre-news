PUBLIC HEARING ON VACATION OF PERKIOMEN STREET
Mr. DeFrain stated a Public Hearing regarding the vacation of Perkiomen Street will take 
place prior to the Council Meeting and referred to Attorney Ulrich to run the hearing.
SOLICITOR COMMENTS
Attorney Ulrich stated that this is a public hearing with respect to the street vacation 
petition of CSW, 300 Furnace Street Emmaus LP also known as Iron Furnace Foundry Flats. He 
explained that the petition is to vacate a portion of Perkiomen Street and the application was 
received on May 4, 2026. Attorney Ulrich stated that the property address was 300 Furnace 
Street with property ID 549474474892 1, which is abutted by three property owners. He 
explained that CSW Furnace Street is one owner, David and Katherine Williams are the second 
owners at 480 S. 2nd Street, and Chris Romig is the third owner at 450 S 2nd Street. Attorney 
Ulrich stated the notice for the hearing was advertised on July 1 and 2 and August 8 and 9 in the 
East Penn Press and mailed to the owners on June 29, 2026. He marked the following exhibits as 
such:
Petition for street vacation by the applicant as #1. Public notice of the hearing as #2. The 
newspaper advertisement as #3. Letter to the owners from Borough Manager Shane Pepe dated 
June 29, 2026, as #4.
Attorney Ulrich inquired about a representative in attendance for the applicant.
Attorney Chris McLean, Fitzpatrick, Lentz, and Bubba, distributed handouts to Council. 
He gave a brief history of the Iron Furnace Foundry Flats. Attorney McLean presented the 
Lehigh County tax map showing the location of Perkiomen Street and the portion to be vacated.

He described the street and presented an aerial map showing the street and the three properties 
that border it. Attorney McLean presented a plan drawn by the applicant’s engineer showing 
only the area of Perkiomen Street to be vacated. He explained that the applicant is requesting the 
vacation to build a fifth apartment building. 
Attorney Ulrich asked Attorney McLean if this property touches any other Municipality. 
Attorney McLean responded that the property does not touch another municipality. 
PUBLIC COMMENT
Chris Romig, 450 S 2nd Street, asked for clarification of 8 PACSA 1732§A. He stated that
owners are allowed 15 days for personal appeal notice. Attorney Ulrich explained that a notice 
was sent to the owners on June 29, 2026. Mr. Romig stated that his letter is dated July 9, 2026. 
COUNCIL COMMENT
Attorney Ulrich stated that in order to complete the record, the public hearing will be 
rescheduled for August 17, 2026. He explained to Mr. Romig that new notices will be sent to the 
owners to be in compliance.  Attorney Ulrich reminded Mr. Romig that his statements and 
addresses with respect to the petition should be discussed at the public hearing in August. 
Attorney Ulrich asked Attorney McLean if rescheduling the public hearing and sending 
new notices to the owners is acceptable. Attorney McLean responded that rescheduling is 
acceptable.
ADJOURNMENT
Attorney Ulrich stated that, for the purposes of the public hearing, the testimony is closed and 
will be reopened on the continued date of August 17, 2026, at 6:00PM.
