4. UNFINISHED BUSINESS  
 
a.) House & Barn, 1449 Chestnut Street – Mini Golf Course 
 
Jason Buchta - ARRO Consulting Inc noted that in Hanover Engineering’s review letter dated 
October 1, there was an additional waiver suggested to Page 12, Section G2, b & c – Testing for 
the infiltration system.  Mr. Buchta explained that testing for infiltration has been completed by 
GTA and meets current BMP guidelines. Mr. Buchta stated that ARRO Consulting Inc. has 
prepared a waiver letter.  Mr. Buchta noted that Page 13, item I – Request for Stormwater System 
hydraulic capacity calculations.  Mr. Buchta explained that the calculations have been performed, 
and the report has been provided to Hanover Engineering. Mr. Youst confirmed that the office 
has received the report and believes the report covers the item. Mr. Buchta referenced Page 10, 
Section D, 1 – Curb & Sidewalks.  Chairman Gibson responded that a field survey of the site was 
conducted on September 15, 2026 and the item has been resolved.  He explained that it was 
decided to place a native pathway for pedestrians which will provide a continuous walkway.  Mr. 
Youst noted that the drawing incorrectly notes that the deferral was approved by Borough 
Council at their September 10. 2026 meeting. Commissioner Grice voiced concerns about 
accessibility for wheelchairs.  Commission Gibson responded that there are 3 ADA accessible 
accesses.  Commissioner Gibson also reminded that applicant that he will need to maintain the 
trail and have a maintenance agreement with the Borough.

7. CHAIRMAN’S BUSINESS  
 
 Westrum, 300 Furnace Street – Additional Building  
 
 Chairman Gibson stated that he is unsure when the plans for the 5th building at Iron 
Works will be submitted for Planning Commission review. He voiced concern about whether the 
new plan should be reviewed under the new SALDO & Zoning Ordinance or the old one. Mr. 
Reed stated that the Codes/Zoning office has not seen any plans yet, that the original plan showed 
a potential 5th building and the detention basin can handle the additional flow from the 5th 
building.  He explained that the original walking trail will be moved closer to the tree and brush 
will be removed.  Mr. Youst explained that the Solicitor’s advice is needed as to whether the 5th 
building will be considered a Major or Minor Land Development.  
 
Chairman Gibson thanked all parties involved for resolving the Trainer issue concerning 417 
S Mountain Street.  Chairman Gibson also reminded everyone that the Planning Commission is 
down 2 members and stated that there will be an article in the winter newsletter concerning the 
Planning Commission and vacancies.
