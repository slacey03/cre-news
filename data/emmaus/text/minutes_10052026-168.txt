PERSONAL APPEALS, PART I  
 
 Jonathan Harada, 224 S 12th Street, distributed a packet to Council. Solicitor Ulrich asked 
Mr. Harada if his personal appeal was in regard to the South 12th Street paving project. Mr. 
Harada stated that he is not in attendance to discuss the paving project.  
 
 Mr. Harada thanked Shane Pepe and Megan Brehm for treating his family with respect 
and professionalism when concerns about the paving project on South 12th Street were brought to 
attention.  
 
 Mr. Harada stated his reason for appeal was the information he received from his Open 
Records Request. He stated that his request was for records for S 12th Street, not just the records 
for his property that there are “systemic measurement discrepancies” across South 12th Street 
affecting multiple property owners. Mr. Harada stated that he received an attestation from the 
Borough Manager stating that all records provided were the only physical records on file. Mr. 
Harada stated that he is aware of records that had been given to a neighbor but were not included 
in the information he received. He stated that prior to the Borough requiring a resident to spend 
thousands of dollars, he believes there should be supporting records with applicable standards 
correctly applied. Mr. Harada claimed that measurements used in the notices sent to S. 12th Street 
residents were not done in inches. He stated that they were not measured in an uniform standard. 
 
 Mr. Labenberg stated that Mr. Harada’s 5-minute time limit had expired and asked 
Council for a motion to extend Mr. Harada’s time. No motion to extend was made. Mr. Harada 
requested an additional 30 seconds to conclude his appeal. Mr. Labenberg granted Mr. Harada a 
30 second extension.

PERSONAL APPEALS, PART II  
 
 Mr. Harada claimed that in the records he received from the Borough, it states there is 71 
linear feet of curb and sidewalk to be replaced at a residence on S. 12th Street. He stated that 
Lehigh County records show only 40 linear feet for the residence. Mr. Harada explained the cost 
increase makes replacing curbs and sidewalks impossible for some residents. He stated that the 
Borough should have supervision and oversight over departments.
