b. Health, Sanitation, and Codes Committee
Mr. Brown stated that Ordinance 1288 would be readvertised for the August 17, 2026, 
Council Meeting. 
Mr. Brown explained the utilization of the Compliance Engine software for third party 
fire inspections.  He reported that the Committee had a discussion with the fire inspector, Ryan 
Crawford, about the number of properties in the Borough that needed a fire inspection. Mr. 
Brown stated that per NPA72 and Life Safety 101 Code, the owner is responsible, at any time, to
provide inspection reports to the inspector or authority of jurisdiction. He further stated that there
is no cost to the Borough to use the software and that it would assist the fire inspector to become 
more compliant with property owners.

e. General Administration Committee
Motion by Mr. Labenberg, seconded by Mr. Brown to hire Joseph Sokolofski as the 
Chief of Police with the following conditions:  a prorated starting annual salary of 
$142,000, successful background check from the Pennsylvania Police Chiefs Association, 
and all benefits and conditions as outlined in the preliminary offer of engagement with a 
start date of September 7, 2026.
Mayor Gilbert questioned whether Mr. Sokolofski is required to be tested by the Civil 
Service Commission. Mayor Gilbert stated that Mr. Sokolofski is not a member of the 
department and in order for him to be appointed he must complete a non-competitive exam by 
the Civil Service Commission. Mr. Pepe suggested Mr. Labenberg amend his motion to include 
the option of the non-competitive examination by the Civil Service Commission to include 
taking the exam for hiring as well as for civil service protection, as Mr. Sokolofski requested the 
opportunity for Civil Service protection.
Motion amended by Mr. Labenberg and seconded Mr. Brown to include the non-
competitive Civil Service examination as a condition of hiring and for Civil Service 
protection. There were 7 ayes. Motion carried.
Motion by Mr. Labenberg seconded Mr. Brown to approve ongoing additional duty 
compensation for Acting Chief Bryan Hamscher in the amount of $12,000 to be paid on 
October 1, 2026. There were 7 ayes. Motion carried.
Mr. Labenberg reported that the next General Administration Committee will be on 
Wednesday, August 5th, at 9:00AM.
