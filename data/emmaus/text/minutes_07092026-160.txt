b.) 37 N 2nd Street – 12 Unit Apartment building 
 
The applicant, William Galiatsatos, explained that he is proposing to tear down the existing 
structure located at 37 N 2nd Street and build a 3 story, 12-Unit Apartment building.  He stated 
that his engineer, Maher Abdelaal, has prepared a Stormwater Management Plan.  He also stated 
that he purchased 180 Ridge Street to meet the parking requirements for 3 parking spaces.  He 
explained that the plan for the apartment building has remained the same and his impervious 
coverage will be 79%.  Chairman Gibson explained that he believes a covenant of perpetuity is 
necessary so that 180 Ridge Street remains with 37 N 2nd Street. Mr. Galiatsatos responded that
