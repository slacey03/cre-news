APPEAL #10654 – BAM Management, LLC, 3008 Macungie Road, Macungie, PA for 
property 559 Chestnut Street, Emmaus, PA 18049.  Applicant is requesting a Special Exception 
to permit conversion of existing vacant first-floor office space into a one-bedroom residential 
apartment.  Special Exception is required under Z.O., Table 27-4A and any other relief deemed 
necessary at the time of the hearing.  Property is located in the Downtown Core District (DC). 
Brent Reed stated that he is the Zoning Officer for the Borough of Emmaus, the appeal was 
properly advertised, the property was posted, and the adjoining neighbors were notified.  He 
explained that a Special Exception is required for the conversion of the 1st floor into residential 
space.  He explained that the 2nd floor is an apartment and it was a prior non-conforming use 
with no parking requirements.  The applicant Marissa Burkholder stated that she is a partner of 
BAM Management, LLC.  She stated that the first floor is currently vacant office space.  She 
explained that she previously used the first floor for her business but there were parking issues.  
She stated that she had 5 employees and only had 2 parking spaces at the back of the building.  
She stated that she also owns 557 Chestnut Street and has expanded the parking between the two
properties and now has 8 parking spaces. She stated that she is proposing to convert the 
commercial space into a one-bedroom apartment.  Chairman Iobst explained that you have to 
drive through the parking for 559 Chestnut Street to access 557 Chestnut Street parking and 
asked if she has designated parking spaces for her current tenants.  Mrs. Burkholder responded 
that she does not.  Chairman Iobst asked if there are any easements between the two properties.  
Mrs. Burkholder responded that there are none.  Mrs. Burkholder stated that she believes the 
conversion of the commercial space to residential is more conforming to the neighborhood.  
Comments:
1. James Rieder, 546 Chestnut Street – stated that he was concerned about parking, but 
the parking has been explained, and he is in favor of the conversion.
The Zoning Hearing Board voted 3-0 to grant the Special Exception.
