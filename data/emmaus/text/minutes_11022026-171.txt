BOROUGH MANAGER’S REPORT
Mr. Pepe stated that Chief Miller and Chief Priest, as well as himself, and the Public 
Safety Committee have interviewed Alexandria Kern-Hickey and have recommended hiring 
Alex as a full-time Paramedic. Mr. Pepe stated that Mrs. Kern-Hickey currently works part time 
with the department and will begin as a full-time Paramedic in December. He reported that Alex 
is well-liked and has great quality of care. Mr. Pepe stated that Dr. Williams was very impressed 
with Mrs. Kern-Hickey during her assessment.
Motion by Mr. Brown, seconded by Mr. Roland to hire Alexandria Kern-Hickey as 
a full-time Paramedic. There were 7 ayes. Motion carried.
