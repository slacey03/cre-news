b.) House and Barn, 1449 Chestnut Street – Mini Golf Course  
 
Jeffrey Ott, ARRO Consulting and Carl Billera, owner and developer of 1449 Chestnut 
Street, were in attendance to discuss the project.  
 
Mr. Ott stated that the concept for the mini golf course began in 2008 and has been in 
planning stages on and off since then.  He explained that it has been many years of effort and hard 
work to be able to present the plan to the Planning Commission.  He stated that the Land 
Development plans were presented to Upper Milford and they differed to the Borough.  Mr. Ott 
stated that the parking, including ADA parking, is located in Upper Milford.  He explained that 
the LOMA Determination document took 10-12 months for approval and it was determined that 
neither side of the proposed golf course is located in the 100-year flood plain.  He stated there 
were soil tests performed and the soil is good.  He explained that the Hanover Engineering review 
letter dated 9/4/2026 addresses the 100-year flood plain.  Mr. Ott stated that there are many 
waiver requests attached to this project and explained that an updated waiver request letter was 
issued on September 4, 2026.  
 
Commissioner Grice noted that the plans indicate that the path is not part of the project. Mr. 
Ott responded that the path is the Fields at Indian Creek’s responsibility and additional permitting 
will not be required. He also explained that the pedestrian bridge is part of the Fields at Indian 
Creek project and is expected to be completed by the end of the month.  He noted that the 
easements should be completed soon. Commissioner Grice asked if, under the proposed use, 
additional restrooms would be required.  Code Official Brent Reed responded that no additional 
restrooms would be required.  Mr. Billera explained that the shed is not the cashier’s office and 
stated that the cashier’s office will be located at the Barn.  He explained that the shed will be used 
for storage and the SILO will be removed.
