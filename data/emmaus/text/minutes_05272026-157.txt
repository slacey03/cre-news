May 27, 2026                  Borough of Emmaus Page 8
      Zoning Hearing Board
APPEAL #10652 – Glen and Chris Gregory, 29 Harrison Street, Emmaus, PA 18049.  
Applicant is proposing to build a 15’ x 40’ addition on property and is requesting a 6-foot 
Variance to Z.O. Chapter 27, Table 27-314.A., Secondary Street to Build, 15-feet min., and any 
other relief deemed necessary at the time of the hearing.  Property is located in the Central 
Residential District (RC). 
Brent Reed stated that he is the Zoning Officer for the Borough of Emmaus, the appeal was 
properly advertised, the property was posted, and the adjoining neighbors were notified.  He 
explained that the applicant is proposing to build a 15’ x 40’ addition to their property.  He 
stated that they are proposing to demolish the current addition for new space.  He stated that the 
property is located on a corner lot, and the Zoning Ordinance requires that the structure be 15’ 
off the property line.  He explained that the applicant is proposing to be 9’ off the property line 
and is requesting a 6’ variance. The applicant Chris Gregory stated that she and her husband 
have owned the property since November 1994.  She explained that there is currently a small 
dining room located where they are proposing to build the addition.  She stated that the proposed
addition will be one level.  She stated that the roof on the proposed addition will be higher than 
the roof that is currently on the dining room but will not be as high as the roof on the rest of the 
house.  She explained that the appearance of the home will be consistent with the rest of the 
neighborhood.  Mr. and Mrs. Gregory stated that they spoke with the neighbors and there were 
no objections to the proposed addition. Mrs. Gregory explained that the proposed addition will 
be vinyl siding with a cedar shaker look and brick corners to be consistent with the appearance 
of the rest of the house. She stated that the house is 1,162 sq. ft. with 3 bedrooms and 1 ½ 
bathrooms.  Mr. Gregory stated that they are proposing to remove the current addition and that 
they are unable to put the addition off the back of the house because of removing the brick and 
the structural wall.  Mr. Reed explained that if this was not a corner lot, a variance would not be 
required.  
The Zoning Hearing Board voted 3-0 to grant the 6’Variance.
