PERSONAL APPEALS, PART I
Michael Scheiry, 648 Ridge Street, discussed his concerns about parking on Ridge Street 
between 6th Street and 7th Street.  He stated that he was at a previous Council meeting and is 
requesting permit parking to prevent students from filling up the street.  He stated that he has 
called the Police Department 3 times for individuals parking in front of the hydrant and, even 
though they get a $25 ticket, it is not sending a message.  Mr. DeFrain responded that Council 
has decided to have our 1st responders address safety issues and the streets that are essential for 
their access.  We are going to address this all at once, rather than continuing to try to move them 
one block at a time.  He stated that we are currently just shifting the problem from one 
neighborhood to the next.  He also stated that we will discuss the cost for the fine at the Public 
Safety Committee.  
COMMUNITY MINUTE

sponsors, vendors, the schools, the bands, and all of our partners for their hard work and making 
this very large event a success.

Ordinance No. 1288: An Ordinance of the Borough of Emmaus, Lehigh County,

the taxpayers of the municipality.  Mr. Brown questioned how the process would work moving 
forward.  Attorney Ulrich explained that he disagreed with Attorney Sebia’s interpretation of the 
process and the law, however, he stated that the Borough could initiate the vacation of the alley

his interpretation for petition eligibility is based on the percentage of property owned 
surrounding the area, not based on the number of property owners.  However, he stated that if the
Borough initiates the vacation, the argument is moot, although anyone could sue for any reason.
