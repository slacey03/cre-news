PERSONAL APPEALS, PART I  
 
 Dave Biever – 415 S. 12th Street – was in attendance at the Health, Sanitation and Codes 
Committee.  He was following up on the status of the road project.  He stated that DeLong 
Avenue and one street in the Stryer section were not required to replace curbs.  The road is a 
mess.  He stated that they want to be treated the same.  There is massive water going down the 
street and is peeling the street away.  He asked for patching to be done and the curb boxes to be 
repaired, as previously promised.  Mr. Labenberg responded that patch work will be completed 
by the Public Works Department. 
 
 Christine Fennessy – 216 S. 12th Street – stated that the cost to replace her curbs and 
sidewalks is like death by 1,000 financial cuts. 
 
 Janice Kacsur – 337 S. 12th Street – stated that she is a widow.  She stated that obtaining 
a loan will not be easy, as she is ready for retirement.  She understands it is important, especially 
with all of the water.  However, she would like more time. 
 
 Jon Harada – 224 S. 12th Street – stated that he attended the committee meeting.  He 
reiterated that he would like to receive fair and equal treatment and supervisory oversight is 
needed.  He stated that he wants this to be investigated and addressed.

BOROUGH MANAGER’S REPORT  
 
 Mr. Pepe reported that the Borough received a $90,000 LSA Grant for the Arts Pavilion 
roof replacement, improvements to the women’s changing room in the pool bathhouse, and to 
replace the Rotary Bridge at Community Park.   
 
 Mr. Pepe stated that the Well # 2 is up and running.  It is in the testing phase, as we will 
not send water into the system until the PFAS level reads “0”.  Mr. Pepe confirmed that we will 
do press releases once the water is released into the system.  Well # 1 should be live in a few 
weeks.   
 
 Mr. Pepe also informed Council that the Borough received a Safety Committee grant for 
rescue rope for the Fire Department and a trench box for the Public Works Department.   
 
 Progress.
