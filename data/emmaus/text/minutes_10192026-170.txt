DECISION ON BIDS  
  
 Mr. Labenberg reported that Zgura’s Concrete Services submitted 2 bid proposals for the 
Lions Park concrete work. One proposal included the Borough supplying necessary materials 
and the other proposal stated they would furnish the materials. 
 
 Motion by Mr. Brown, seconded by Mrs. Buchman, to award Zigura’s Concrete 
Services the bid for the Lions Park concrete work to include furnishing the necessary 
materials and labor to install the sidewalk and curbing. There were 6 ayes. Motion carried.

to the Public Safety Committee. 
 
 Pedestrian safety concern on S 4th Street. Mr. Labenberg stated the concern is for the

 Bruce Bastian resignation as a part-time police officer effective November 1, 2026. Mr.
