Tulpehocken Township 
Board of Supervisors 
 Meeting of June 10, 2026 
 
The Tulpehocken Township Board of Supervisors met on June 10, 2026, in the Township meeting 
room at 7:00 P.M.  In attendance and voting were Supervisors: Chairman Gary Deck, Vice 
Chairman Lester Feick and Harold Zimmerman.  Also present were Bryan Dronick, Police Chief; 
Kathy Judy, Township Secretary / Treasurer and Atty. Christopher Hartman, Township Solicitor. 
Members of the audience included Kris Martin, Bill Dewart and Susan Wolfe. 
 
CALL TO ORDER:  Chairman Deck called the meeting to order at 7:00 P.M.  The meeting 
continued with the pledge to the American Flag.   
    
PUBLIC COMMENTS:   
   Susan Wolfe spoke about the Bethel-Tulpehocken Library, sharing that numbers are on the rise 
and they deserve the Township’s support and inquired about displaying Library information on 
the Township sign. 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES: Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to approve minutes from the 
May 13, 2026 Board of Supervisors Meeting - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick and passed 
unanimously (3-0). 
 
ADMINISTRATIVE ACTIONS: 
   Personnel:  None 
Planning Commission Recommendations:  None. 
Solicitor Legal Discussion:   
- Update Comcast Franchise Agreement – The Solicitor advised that revisions have been 
submitted to Comcast and we are waiting for a response.  No action at this time. 
- Omnibus Traffic and Road Ordinance – Since updates are needed, the Solicitor recommended 
incorporating previously adopted amendments to traffic regulations including other up to date 
speed limit, stop sign and snow emergency route regulations that may have been in separate 
documents previously, so that all of the traffic and road related regulations are in a single 
document going forward.  The Board asked that fine penalty amounts be researched and that 
the Chief review the Ordinance and confirm that the regulations are supported by the vehicle 
code allowing enforcement by traffic citation. Tabled for a future meeting. 
Escrow Release:  None 
Zoning:  No correspondence from Zoning Officer was received; the Board reviewed a log of 
Permits issued.   
Land Development:  None 
Recreation:   
- Lions Park: Maintenance & operation repairs and purchases:  None

SEWER OPERATIONS: 
- Judgements and Delinquent Sewer Accounts – The Board reviewed the report 
- Additional Tapping Fee for 19 West Market Street, Mt. Aetna – this was discussed with Kris 
Martin, the builder for a proposed duplex at the property.  The Board reviewed the records and 
agreed that only one tapping fee was paid for this property.  Supervisor Zimmerman made a 
motion that an Application to Connect, payment of a $4,421.55 tapping fee and a $150 
connection fee are required in order for the proposed duplex, or anything more than a single 
family dwelling, to be permitted at 19 West Market Street - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick 
and passed unanimously (3-0). 
- Equipment Repairs and purchases, including: 
o Update regarding dialers – one has been serviced and is operating again. 
o Vice Chairman Feick said that he priced, and would like to order, a magnet that can be used 
on the service truck crane to lift manhole lids – Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to 
approve the purchase of the manhole magnet from L.B. Waters for $1,052.80 - - seconded by 
Vice Chairman Feick and passed unanimously (3-0). 
 
UNFINISHED BUSINESS:  None 
 
NEW BUSINESS:   
-  Contract with Cintas for Uniform Service – Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to approve 
the contract on the conditions that under item 7 of the contract terms, the Secretary/Treasurer is 
authorized to reject any price increases and that the days payment is due within is changed 
from 10 days to 45 days instead - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick and passed unanimously 
(3-0).  
- Berks County Association of Township Officials asked if the Board would like to see PSATS 
seek any legislation.  Deadline to submit Resolutions is August 31, 2026.  This was discussed 
and no action taken. 
- 33 Deck Road - Approve a partial refund of taxes paid due to a change in assessed value – 
approved under payment of bills. 
 
CORRESPONDENCE:     
- Berks County Redevelopment Authority letter regarding Community Development Block Grant 
(CDBG) Program 2027-2029 - Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to remain in the County’s 
CDBG Program - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick and passed unanimously (3-0). 
- Complaint from resident about trucks idling overnight at Rehrerbsurg Sunoco Station.  It was 
discussed that: - it can’t be ruled out that sometimes trucks may be parked and running a reefer 
unit versus idling, - a letter was sent to the property owner requesting them to put up no idling 
signs, but no response was received, - the trucks are not parking in the right-of-way or along the 
road so it cannot be enforced by the Police under vehicle the vehicle code, - the property owner 
does have a sign that says tractor trailer parking only and that inviting truck parking may not 
comply with the Zoning for the property.  Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to refer this to 
the Zoning Officer to review for potential zoning violation - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick 
and passed unanimously (3-0)
