Tulpehocken Township Planning Commission Meeting 
June 4, 2026 
 
Present: Planning Commission Members: Scott Hetrick, Gary Deck, and John Sheaffer. Also, 
Township Zoning Officer, Jackie Hollenbach; Township Solicitor, Atty Chris Hartman; Planning 
Commission Secretary, Lisa Care. 
Absent: Laverne Frey, Robert Sattazahn 
Members of the Audience: None 
The Planning Commission meeting began at 7:45 p.m. and continued with the pledge to the 
American Flag.   
Gary Deck made a motion to approve the minutes from the May 7, 2026 Planning Commission 
meeting, seconded by John Sheaffer and approved unanimously. (3-0) 
     
New Plans 
 
 
a. Accept the plans for Rushtown Foods, LLC, West Side of Route 419 
Nye Consulting Services submission of plans for review for a manufacturing facility for 
the processing of eggs (grading and breaking). 
 
 
 Gary Deck made a motion to accept the plans submitted for review, seconded by John Sheaffer and 
approved unanimously. (3-0) 
 
 
b. Gantea Subdivision, 4425 Route 419 – Preliminary/Final 
              Carta Engineering submission of plans for review to subdivide the property into 
               three separate residential lots and a proposed garage expansion.   
 
  
John Sheaffer made a motion to accept the plans submitted for review, seconded by Gary Deck and 
approved unanimously. (3-0) 
 
 
Gary Deck made a motion to adjourn the meeting at 7:55 p.m., seconded by John Sheaffer and 
approved unanimously. (3-0) The meeting was adjourned. 
 
Respectfully submitted,  
Lisa Care, Planning Commission Secretary
