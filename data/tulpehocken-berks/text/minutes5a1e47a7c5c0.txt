Active Plans: 
 
 
a. Gantea Subdivision, 4425 Route 419 – Preliminary/Final 
              Carta Engineering submission of plans for review to subdivide the property into 
             three separate residential lots and a proposed garage expansion. The Engineer for this  
             project is currently working through the Township Engineer’s review letter. 
 
 Time currently expires September 4, 2025. Brynn Schaffer, the applicant’s engineer, submitted  
 a time extension letter to the Township for a continuance until November 12, 2026. The delay 
 being due to allow additional time to address items from the Township engineer’s review letter.   
 
                   Laverne Frey made a motion to recommend to the Board of Supervisors to  
                   accept the time extension request until November 12, 2026, seconded by 
                  Robert Sattazahn and approved unanimously. (5-0) 
 
 
 
b. Rushtown Foods, LLC, West Side of Route 419 – Update 
 
Per our Township Solicitor, the Zoning Hearing Board voted to reject the application. But the 
written decision has not yet been issued and there will be an appeal period that will be available 
to Rushtown Foods that will not expire until 30 days after the issuance of the written decision. 
Therefore, no action was taken on the plan and no review was done on the Township Engineer’s 
review letter and to first wait and see what happens with the zoning case.

Walk-In Discussions:  None 
 
 
New Plans: 
 
 
a. Dollar General Retail Store, Lancaster Avenue, Mt. Aetna –  
 
 
DeAnna Hyche with the Broadway Group discussed a proposed 9100 sq. ft. commercial retail 
facility. This will consist of a subdivision of property on Lancaster Avenue next to First 
Citizens Community Bank, owners of the land. The retail store will consist of 3.69 acres of the 
approximate tract of 9 acres. Hours of operation will be 8 am to 10 pm with 10-12 employees 
generally. This was a review and discussion only by the Planning Commission members and 
the Township Engineer made the suggestion to have the Broadway Group share the plan with 
Marion Township due to the close proximity to the township line.  
 
 
 
b. Earl Ray Martin Ag Operation, 20 Deck Road –  
 
 
Nye Consulting Services submitted an application for a waiver of land development and a drainage 
plan for a proposed 60 ft. X 210 ft. steer barn. This plan will be re-visited for the zoning and 
stormwater requirements and will need to confirm the number of animal units.   
 
     John Sheaffer made a motion to recommend to the Board of Supervisors to accept the  
                    land development waiver portion of the submission only based upon this plan went through  
                    land development in 2018 and that it is an accessory building that is being added,  
                    seconded by Robert Sattazahn and approved unanimously. (5-0)

Other Business 
 
a. Review Zoning Appeal Application for: 106 Wintersville Road 
Application submitted for a dimensional variance under section 844.022 to construct a 
residential garage/storage building. This structure is proposed to be 28 ft. X 35 ft. with a 
height listed as one and one-half stories high to be located approximately 5 ft. from the 
boundary line. The Township ordinance states the side yard setback should be 30 ft. 
 
 
Laverne Frey made a motion to recommend to the Zoning Hearing Board, should they 
grant this application, to consider the following recommendations; to require a survey 
of the side yard boundary to assure they meet whatever the approved setback is from the 
actual side yard boundary; to confirm the stormwater shall be directed away from the 
neighboring property; and no living quarters shall be permitted in the accessory 
structure, seconded by John Sheaffer and approved unanimously. (5-0) 
 
 
 
 
Robert Sattazahn made a motion to adjourn the meeting at 9:05 p.m., seconded by Gary Deck 
and approved unanimously. (5-0) The meeting was adjourned.
