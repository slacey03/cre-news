Tulpehocken Township Board of Supervisors 
 Meeting of September 16, 2026  
Preliminary Budget Meeting and Other Business 
 
The Tulpehocken Township Board of Supervisors met on September 9, 2026 at 6:00 P.M., in the 
Township meeting room, to discuss the Preliminary 2027 Budget and any other business.  In 
attendance and voting were Supervisors: Chairman Gary Deck, Vice Chairman Lester Feick and 
Harold Zimmerman.  Also present were Bryan Dronick, Police Chief; Kathy Judy, Township 
Secretary/Treasurer and Lisa Care, Sewer Services Secretary. 
 
Members of the audience included Susan Wolfe. 
 
CALL TO ORDER:  Chairman Deck called the meeting to order at 6:13 P.M.  The meeting 
continued with the pledge to the American Flag. 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT:  Susan Wolfe spoke to advocate for the Library, she explained what their 
public funding is based on, that it doesn’t provide all of the needed revenues, fundraising is often 
needed in order to offer programs and activities, and asked the Board to consider increasing the 
donation amount for 2027.  
NEW BUSINESS:  Review 2026 Preliminary Budgets 
Township Funds – The line-item breakdown for each accounts’ 2026 year to date and projected 
year end was reviewed and discussed by the Board.  
Changes for the 2027 General Fund budget that were discussed included: 
• Increasing the Library donation, Police radio, Fire Company radio, Ambulance radio, electric, 
fuel and insurance costs 
• Increasing the rate to charge for Police Services  
The Building Maintenance, Police PTO, Emergency Services and Recreation Funds were also 
reviewed. 
The Street Light Fund was reviewed.  
• It was noted that the revenue is equal to the utility bills coming in; there are no surplus funds 
remaining in the account. 
The Liquid Fuels Budget was reviewed.  
• The Secretary is to reconcile the Snow and Ice Removal expenses for 2026.  The Liquid Fuel 
account will reimburse the General Fund for the $20,000 that it was budgeted to spend in 2026 
for this line item. 
 
Sewer Funds – The line-item breakdown for each account was reviewed and discussed by the 
Board.  Changes to the 2027 Sewer Budget that were discussed included: 
• Increasing the Sewer treatment, plant repairs, maintenance services and electric and insurance 
costs.  
• Increasing the number of new tap-in and connects from (3) to (4) for 2027. 
 
Transferring $30,000.00 from the Sewer Service Prime Account to the to help pay 2026 invoices 
was discussed. 
 
The Township and Sewer Service projected revenues and expenses for 2026 and 2027 will be 
reviewed again in November.

OTHER BUSINESS:   
- A request for Fire Police Assistance was received from Upper Bern Township.  Supervisor 
Zimmerman made a motion to approve the request for Fire Police Assistance at Shartlesville 
Borough’s Parade & Block Party on September 29, 2026 - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick 
and passed unanimously (3-0). 
 
 - Bridge on Little Swatara Church Road – the board discussed the condition of the roadway and 
bridge at this site. Vice Chairman Feick stated that he would like to seek grant funding for a box 
culvert and concrete sidewall and advised that a report from a Structural Engineer will be 
required.  Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to authorize the Township Engineer to obtain 
the necessary reports and to proceed with an application for funding this project - - seconded by 
Chairman Deck and passed unanimously (3-0). 
 
 -  County Municipal Radio System Replacement – The Board discussed the latest information from 
the County on the proposed radio refresh.  No action was taken. 
 
ADJOURNMENT OF MEETING:  Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to adjourn the meeting 
at 8:30 P.M. - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick and passed unanimously (3-0).
