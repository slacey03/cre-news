Tulpehocken Township 
Board of Supervisors 
 Meeting of September 9, 2026 
 
The Tulpehocken Township Board of Supervisors met on September 9, 2026, in the Township meeting 
room at 7:00 P.M.  In attendance and voting were Supervisors: Chairman Gary Deck, Vice Chairman 
Lester Feick and Harold Zimmerman.  Also present were Bryan Dronick, Police Chief; and Kathy Judy, 
Township Secretary / Treasurer. 
Members of the audience included Susan Wolfe, Jeff Hahn and Roger Yiengst. 
CALL TO ORDER:  Chairman Deck called the meeting to order at 7:03 P.M.  The meeting continued 
with the pledge to the American Flag.   
PUBLIC COMMENTS:  Susan Wolfe spoke about the Library, the Legacy Council and asked the 
Board to consider increasing the amount it donates to the Library in 2027.  Also discussed was the 
traffic and speeding on Wintersville Road and an overall increase in traffic on many other roads right 
now due to the Route 419 bridge project and temporary road closure were discussed. 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES: Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to approve minutes from the 
August 12, 2026 Board of Supervisors Meeting - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick and passed 
unanimously (3-0). 
ADMINISTRATIVE ACTIONS: 
   Personnel, Planning Commission Recommendations, Land Development & Recreation: None 
Solicitor Legal Discussion:   
- Update Comcast Franchise Agreement – No action at this time. 
- Mountain Meadows Sidewalk – A question of who is responsible for repairs to the sidewalk and 
curbing at one of the mailbox clusters in the development was aksed.  Based on research done and 
in accordance with the Solicitor’s recommendation, Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion 
authorizing a letter be sent to the homeowner advising that under the plan and the property 
maintenance code, they are responsible for the maintenance and repair of the sidewalk and 
curbing - - seconded by Chairman Deck and passed unanimously (3-0). 
Escrow Release:  Met-Ed requested release of the Bond for the 2017 Substation project.  As built 
plans have been requested for the Township Engineer’s review – No action at this time. 
Zoning:   
- 4992 Rehrersburg Road Sunoco – There’s been no response to the letter sent on the allowed 
use and idling trucks.  To follow up on that, and also address a new complaint about lighting, 
a Notice of Violation has been sent. Deadline to respond is October 8, 2026. 
- 7656 Lancaster Avenue  – A letter had been sent, but no significant improvement was made.  
A Notice of Violation has now been sent.  The owner has until September 17th to cut the 
weeds/grass and until October 8th to remove the trash/rubbish.

Chief Dronick reported that during the month August there were 142 calls received through Berks 
County 911; noting that forty-four (44) were for Teen Challenge.   
- Equipment Repairs and purchases, including: 
• The Chief reported that the 2022 cruiser needs tires that will cost $913.96 from Custom 
Exhaust.  Vice Chairman Feick made a motion to approve the purchase and installation 
of the new tires - - seconded by Supervisor Zimmerman and passed unanimously (3-0). 
Chief Dronick reported that the Enradd system was calibrated and is working.  It first time out was 
about two hours on Wintersville Road.  
 
Road Master’s Report: Vice Chairman Feick read the August, 2026 report.  The work consisted of  
sewer maintenance, meter reading, highway mowing, cleaning grates, lawn mowing, working in the 
shop, getting the paint sprayer out and ready, cleaning gutters, and doing some work at Cherry Hill 
Road and Deck Road that is not complete yet. 
The Roadmaster reported that the Road Crew had to put up some barricades for poles that came 
down in the storms.  They also had seven trees that came down. 
- Equipment Repairs and purchases:  None. 
The Board discussed a guide rail on Teen Challenge Road that was damaged in a motor vehicle 
accident on 9/1/26.  Wm. Orr & Sons, Inc. gave an estimate of $7,150 to repair the guide rail.   
Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to amend the agenda to include “Damaged guide rail on 
Teen Challenge Road” - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick and passed unanimously (3-0).    
Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to move ahead and schedule the repair now; and to 
notify and seek reimbursement from the driver or the driver’s insurance company - - seconded 
by Vice Chairman Feick and passed unanimously (3-0).   
Fire Chiefs’ Report – Vice Chairman Feick reviewed activity for the Keystone Fire Company and 
read the report for the Rescue Fire Company for August, 2026.

UNFINISHED BUSINESS:  
- Update on complaint regarding 7646 Lancaster Avenue – It’s been alleged the property is not vacant 
and Sewer fees are currently being waived.  The Police Chief reported that he has not been able to 
prove someone is residing in the upstairs apartment.  Supervisor Zimmerman made a motion to 
authorize the Sewer Service to send a letter notifying the property owner that if proof of vacancy is 
not provided, monthly billing will resume, the waiver revoked and they will be back-billed for any 
waived fees to date - - seconded by Vice Chairman Feick and passed unanimously (3-0).  
- 44 Rehrersburg Road – The property owners were billed for abatement costs.  Payment was due 
on August 22nd; no payment has been received.  Per the court order, Supervisor Zimmerman 
made a motion authorizing the Solicitor to file a lien on the property for the unpaid invoice - - 
seconded by Vice Chairman Feick and passed unanimously (3-0). 
- County/Municipal Radio System Replacement – no new information received from the County 
NEW BUSINESS:  None.
