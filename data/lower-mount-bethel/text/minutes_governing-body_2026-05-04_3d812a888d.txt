2.  Presentation Re: Zoning - P. McInerney, PC Chair.  S. Newman, PC prior member. P. McInerney 
and S. Newman submitted a written outline of pertinent excerpts to protect LMBT P. McInerney 
gave the history of the Zoning Ordinance.  The previous zoning ordinance was 40 years old, and 
the new ordinance was adopted in 2021 after working on  it for 7 years, in 2014 the Township 
hired a consultant to finalize the ordinance.  Questions were sent out to residents to see what 
they value the most; 75% of the residents indicated Agriculture and Open Space was most 
important. A lot of work went into  the zoning ordinance to protect the township S. Newman 
discussed her outline and read the Governors Executive Order and reviewed the 2022 Comp. 
Plan and permanent zoning ordinance excerpts.  The zoning remains intact, and resources are 
very valuable.  J. Keifer thanked them for a very nice presentation.   
 
3.  J. Mauser Presentation regarding use of The Meadows – J. Mauser.  John presented his power 
point on The Meadows property.  This property was acquired with Grants and is a nice quiet 
place for birdwatching fishing and archery hunting.  The Township must follow the stipulations 
and caveats in the grant and follow DCNR & Northampton County requirements included in the 
deed, we don’t want to jeopardize future funding.   
 

 
 
 
John mentioned farm preservation in LMBT not paying as much as other townships for farmland. 
In no way did they want to jeopardize the farmers when passing the CAPZO ordinance. The Open 
Space Committee is discussing with Ryan Shaw to see if we can make up the difference using EIT 
money to compensate the farmers. J. Kiefer would like to see more farmland preserved and help 
the farmers with the EIT money. J. Mauser asked he be considered for the open  seat on the 
Open Space Committee.  
 
John suggested the 250 tons of stone (lot 5) to be used to protect all parameters of the trails.     
J. Keifer mentioned $490,000 EIT funds were used to purchase parks, not farmland.  J. Keifer

manager was looking into it and were waiting for Northampton County to get back to us to see 
if we can farm at the Meadows.  J. Mauser suggested we cut down the weeds 3-4 times per year 
and keep the control paths in place.  D. Ascani said he would like to give back to the farmer by

approximately $2.00 per household)  and the Township help with operational costs.  They need 
all support to move forward.  D. Ascani mentioned if we don’t have our volunteer Fire company 
and had to pay for Fire Services, it would be more than our budget. This will be on the agenda
