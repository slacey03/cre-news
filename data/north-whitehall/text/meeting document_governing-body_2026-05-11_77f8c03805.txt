RESOLUTION 05-11-26    AUTHORIZED CHECK SIGNERS 
Upon motion by Supervisor Semmel, seconded by Supervisor Klusaritz, the Board approved 
Resolution 05-13-26 amending the signer requirements for all accounts held in North Whitehall 
Township’s name at First Northern Bank and Trust and Neffs Bank. All checks require two 
signatures, one of which shall be the Director of Finance. In the event the Director of Finance is 
unable to perform their duties for a prolonged period of time, the Township Manager shall serve as 
the required signatory in place of the Director of Finance, and the second signature shall be 
provided by one of the three members of the Board of Supervisors.  Roll call: Supervisor Klusaritz - 
yes; Supervisor Geosits - yes; Supervisor Semmel - yes. There were no public comments on this 
item.

MANAGER’S REPORT: 
• Important Dates: 
Recreation Board Meeting - Monday May 18th at 6 PM; 
Night at the Drive In – Wednesday, May 20th at 6:45 PM; 
Zoning Hearing Board – Wednesday, May 20th, at 7:00 PM; 
Office Closed – Memorial Day, Monday May 25th; 
Planning Commission – Tuesday, May 26th at 7 PM; 
Food Truck Festival – Saturday, May 30th at 12 PM (Laurys Firehouse Park); 
Board of Supervisors – Monday, June 8th at 7 PM 
• Playground Program – Ironton Elementary is full with 125 spots filled and Schnecksville 
Elementary only has 16 spots remaining. In total, there are already 234 participants 
enrolled. Playground managers are starting this week and will begin putting curriculum and 
daily activities together.  
• 90’ Baseball Field at Laury’s Park – The renovated 90’ field at Laury’s Park is now open 
and getting played on.  
• Earth Day Clean Up – Public Works and approximately a dozen volunteers from the Ironton 
Rail Trail Commission completed beautification efforts at Portland Street trailhead on 
Wednesday, April 22nd for Earth Day.  
• EIT Educational Video – Staff has been working with a film crew, resident farmers and 
individuals from the Open Space and Farmland Preservation Board to create an 
educational video on the EIT initiative. The video will explain the process of the EIT and 
how it will assist the Township with preserving more land in NWT.  
• Grist Mill Road Bridge – We did experience a bridge failure on Grist Mill Road late last 
month. Staff are currently working with our engineers to determine the best replacement 
method. We are also considering grants for the projects. If we take the grant approach, we 
would be looking at a 2027 replacement. These grants would also kick us into some stream 
bank restoration efforts on private property and likely inflate the cost of the project. Staff are 
currently weighing all options to see what the most cost-effective approach is. The bridge 
will remain closed until further notice.
