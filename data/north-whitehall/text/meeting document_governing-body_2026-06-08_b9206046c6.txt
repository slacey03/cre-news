MANAGER’S REPORT: 
 
• Important Dates:  
Zoning Hearing Board – Wednesday, June 17th, at 7 PM; 
Planning Commission - Tuesday, June 23rd, at 7 PM; 
Farmland/Open Space Preservation Board – Thursday, July 9, at 7 PM; 
Board of Supervisors – Monday, July 13th, at 7 PM 
• Playground Program – Ironton Elementary is full with 125 and Schnecksville Elementary 
only has 4 spots remaining open. In total, there are 246 participants enrolled. Playground 
managers are currently in the office, wrapping up planning for the 8-week program which 
begins Monday, June 15th.  
• Junior 1st Responder Camp – This program will take place the week of July 6th at Tri-
Clover Fire Company. 35 children ages 10-15, will learn firefighting and prevention habits 
through games, lectures, and activities. The program is currently sold out.  
• Night at the Drive-In – In partnership with Shankweiler’s Drive-In, this program took place 
on Wednesday, May 20th and was free for North Whitehall Township residents. 120 
residents took advantage of this great program and residents will have another chance to 
participate on August 5th when The Lorax will be on the big screen.  
• 2nd Annual Fishing Derby – In partnership with Copeechan Fish & Game Club, 130 
children participated in a trout fishing derby. Winners in different age groups received prizes  
for the biggest fish 
• Drone Show – Th Township will be sponsoring a drone show in celebration of America’s 
250th. This is being done in partnership with Schnecksville Fair Committee to hopefully draw 
more people to the fair. We did receive a Lehigh County Tourism Grant for $2,500 to help 
support this effort. The show will be on Thursday, June 18 at 9:30 PM.  
• Hometown Hero Banners – Public Works recently installed 30 hometown hero banners 
along Route 309 in the Schnecksville area. We are receiving a lot positive feedback and 
anticipate this program will continue to grow.
