VISTAS AT PARK VALLEY     WATER LINE WAIVER 
Upon motion by Supervisor Klusaritz, seconded by Supervisor Semmel the Board denied the 
requested waiver of SALDO Section 375-74-1.E(s). Roll call: Supervisor Klusaritz - yes; Supervisor 
Geosits - no; Supervisor Semmel - yes.  
• Lew Rauch, Lehigh Engineering – Presented 
• Brian Horwith – Stated that there is a rule or law in place and that stands whether we agree 
with it or not. When this topic was presented to the Planning Commission, it was clear that 
a connection is required via SALDO which is why the Planning Commission recommended 
to deny waiver request. 
• Dennis Atiah – There are roads to nowhere like Scott Road. This project should be 
considered to improve the roads and create a threw road making traffic flow smoother and 
safer. 
• Mark Leuthe – The water company are the ones who benefit the most from this. There 
should be a mandatory water hookup for any homes along future water line extensions. 
• Heather Skorinko – A neighbor of ours had to pay for a water line and they were just a 
private resident, not a developer. 
 
 
TIMBERIDGE SEWER SYSTEM     
Upon motion by Supervisor Semmel, seconded by Supervisor Klusaritz, the Board authorized 
Township staff, Township solicitor, and Township consultant, PFM Financial Advisors, LLC, to 
commence the due diligence process, and, as appropriate, begin consideration and preparation of 
a draft asset purchase agreement in connection with the Request for Qualifications, released on 
June 22, 2026, relating to the possible sale of a Township-owned sanitary sewer collection system 
consisting of underground lines and appurtenances thereto and serving generally, the Timberidge 
and Ormrod communities. Roll call: Supervisor Klusaritz - yes; Supervisor Geosits - yes; 
Supervisor Semmel - yes. There were no public comments on this item.
