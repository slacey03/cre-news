MANAGER’S REPORT: 
• Important Dates 
Board of Supervisors Special Meeting – Tuesday, October 20th, at 5 PM; 
    Zoning Hearing Board – Wednesday, October 21st, at 7 PM (CANCELLED) 
    Planning Commission – Tuesday, October 27th, at 7 PM; 
    Election Day – Tuesday, November 3rd 
    Board of Supervisors – Monday, November 9th, at 7 PM 
• Neffs Valley Park - Phase 1 of the project has been completed and the park is now open to 
public access.  The project included relocating the parking area to Park Valley Road, 
installation of a 10’ wide paved pedestrian walkway and landscaping throughout the park.  
Phase 2 will begin sometime in the spring or early summer and will include additional 
paving of trails, streambank restoration and a new pedestrian footbridge across the Coplay 
Creek. 
• Independence Park - The township recently received a $250,000 grant from the 
Department of Conservation and Natural Resources (DCNR) to support park renovations.  
Renovations include a new playground and a pump tract for cyclists.  We are still awaiting 
the announcement of an additional $250,000 grant from DCED which we hope to hear 
about prior to the end of the year.  These improvements will likely occur in 2028/2029. 
• Farmland/Open Space Preservation Tour - On Thursday, October 8, individuals from the 
Farmland/Open Space Preservation Board, Planning Commission and Recreation Board 
got a tour of the township to specifically look at existing open space and landowners 
interested in preservation.
