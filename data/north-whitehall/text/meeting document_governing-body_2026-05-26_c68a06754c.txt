MAY 26, 2026 
 
Chairman Dennis Klusaritz called the meeting to order at 6:30 PM followed by the Pledge of 
Allegiance and a moment of silence.  In attendance, were Supervisor Al Geosits, Supervisor 
Richard Semmel, Township Solicitor Rocco Beltrami, Township Engineer Dave Alban, Township 
Manager Randy Cope, Township Planner Kevin Murphy and Secretary/Deputy Treasurer Jessica 
Koenig.  
 
ANNOUNCEMENTS – No Announcements 
 
OLD BUSINESS – No old business 
 
MOTIONS:  
 
AMENDING THE AUTHORIZATION TO ADVERTISE PROPOSED ORDINANCE 2026-9 ZONING 
AMENDMENT  
Upon motion by Richard Semmel, seconded by Dennis Klusaritz, the Board amended the May 11, 
2026, authorization to advertise proposed Ordinance 2026-9 Zoning Amendment as presented, 
subject to removing all provisions for Landscape Service Establishments. Roll call: Supervisor 
Klusaritz - yes; Supervisor Geosits - yes; Supervisor Semmel - yes. There were no public 
comments on this item.  
 
 
COMMENTS FROM THE FLOOR (Non-agenda items): No comments from the floor 
 
 
ADJOURNMENT: Meeting adjourned at 6:45 PM. 
 
 
Respectfully submitted,  
 
 
Jessica Koenig, Secretary/Deputy Treasurer
