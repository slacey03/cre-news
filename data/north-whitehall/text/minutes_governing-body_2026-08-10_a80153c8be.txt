MANAGER’S REPORT: 
• Important Dates –  
Zoning Hearing Board – Wednesday, August 19 at 7 PM CANCELLED; 
Back to School Party – Friday, August 21, at 5 PM (Kolapechka Park); 
Planning Commission – Monday, August 24, at 7 PM; 
Planning Commission – Tuesday, August 25, at 7 PM; 
BOS Budget Workshop - Wednesday, September 9 at 5 PM; 
Board of Supervisors – Monday, September 14, at 7 PM 
• New Intern – Starting on August 24, Kane Zerbe, from Kutztown University, will be joining 
us for an unpaid internship. As an intern, his primary responsibilities will include assisting 
with the general overview of permits and land development plans, researching ordinances, 
and drafting written comments for review by our Planning staff. Kane will also participate in 
relevant staff meetings and attend monthly meetings for the Board of Supervisors, Zoning 
Hearing Board, and Planning Commission.  
• Grist Road Bridge – The advertising of this bid has been slightly delayed because of utility 
coordination with UGI for the gas line. UGI will be relocating their gas line starting in early 
September and hopes to be completed by end of September. Our goal is to get this bid 
advertised later this month or early September and have a bid recommendation for the 
Board at the October meeting.  
• Neffs Valley Park – The contractor still has not shown up to the park for construction. They 
were supposed to be on site last month. Our contract with them states that substantial 
completion must occur by August 31. We are closely monitoring the situation and hounding 
the contractor to get started. They insist they will meet the deadline.  
• EIT – Staff has begun sharing videos and FAQs through social media to help residents 
make an informed decision when it comes time to head to the polls in early November. We 
will also be putting a lot of information in the upcoming newsletter and sending a postcard 
out to residents as we get closer to voting day. Yard signs will also start to appear in parks. 
• Website – The new website should be up and running by the end of the month. Staff will 
also start utilizing the new agenda management software for the September meeting. The 
new website and agenda software will also help residents navigate our meeting documents 
more efficiently on the website.
