 
 
  
 
 
 
 
NORTH WHITEHALL TOWNSHIP BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NORTH WHITEHALL TOWNSHIP MUNICIPAL BUILDING  
NOVEMBER 2, 2026 
 
Chairman Dennis Klusaritz called the meeting to order at 5:00 PM followed by the Pledge of 
Allegiance and a moment of silence.  In attendance, were Supervisor Al Geosits, Supervisor 
Richard Semmel, Township Manager Randy Cope, Director of Operations Jeff Mouer, and 
Secretary/Deputy Treasurer Jessica Oswald.  
 
This meeting is being recorded. Upon approval of the minutes by the Board of Supervisors the 
electronic recording file will be destroyed. 
 
PRESENTATIONS: 
 
A. Act 537 Plan Update – Presentation by Andrea Martin, Barry Isett and Associates  
• Following the presentation, the Board of Supervisors engaged in discussion with the 
presenter, requesting additional clarification regarding various aspects of the 
information provided. The Board also sought further explanation of the anticipated next 
steps in the process of updating the Township’s Act 537 Sewage Facilities Plan, 
including the procedures, requirements, and considerations involved in moving forward 
with the proposed update. 
• Jeanene Bauer, Jordan Rd., expressed the importance of extending extra opportunity to 
the taxpayers to have this plan discussed in front of them and be able to give their input. 
She also inquired if Barry Isett received input on the plan from the Township’s Sewage 
Enforcement Officer and if the plan would include that input.  
 
COMMENTS FROM THE FLOOR (Non-agenda items): There were no comments from the floor.  
 
 
ADJOURNMENT: Meeting adjourned at 6:01 P.M. 
 
 
Respectfully Submitted,  
 
 
Jessica Oswald, Secretary/Deputy Treasurer
