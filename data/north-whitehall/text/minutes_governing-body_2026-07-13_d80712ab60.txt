litigation and potential litigation matters, including the Trammell Crow Warehouse,

Board Appeals, the Bodamer/Haynes Zoning Hearing Board appeal regarding a dog

MANAGER’S REPORT: 
• Important Dates –  
Zoning Hearing Board – Wednesday, July 15 at 7 PM; 
Planning Commission – Tuesday, July 28 at 7 PM; 
BOS Budget Workshop – Tuesday, August 4 at 5 PM; 
Board of Supervisors – Monday, August 10th at 7 PM; 
• 3917 Buchanan Street – A few years ago, the township was able to get a court order to 
remove this blighted property. Over the course of the process, the property incurred a lien 
amount of $34,491.78. The Township was able to recently collect that lien in full. This 
project is now complete after nearly 7 years.  
• Neffs valley Park – Neffs Valley Park will be closed to the public starting on Monday, July 
20 for Phase 1 of the long-awaited improvements. Phase 1 will include removal of the 
existing parking area, a new parking area off Park Valley Road, additional ADA compliant 
walking trails, evergreen screening, native tree plantings and landscaping throughout. The 
park is anticipated to be closed for 2-3 months until construction is complete.  
• Summer Concert – Steel Creek will be performing at the Schnecksville Fair band stage 
this Friday, July 17 at 6:00 PM. Food and beverages will be available for purchase.  
• Goat on the Loose! – On Sunday, July 12, I received a phone call for Lt. Troutman of PSP 
with what may be the most unusual law enforcement update of the year. Troopers in the 
area successfully apprehended a goat that was running at large on Old Post Roa and 
causing a bit of excitement. After allegedly refusing to identify itself, the goat was read its 
rights and taken into protective custody. The goat quickly became a local celebrity on the 
Township Facebook page and now holds the all-time record for our most-liked social media 
post, generating more than 1,300 interactions. We can only assume the goat is currently 
seeking representation and negotiating a settlement. No bail was required.
