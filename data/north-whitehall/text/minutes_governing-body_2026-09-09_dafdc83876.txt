Chairman Dennis Klusaritz called the meeting to order at 5:00 PM followed by the Pledge of 
Allegiance and a moment of silence.  In attendance were Supervisor Al Geosits, Supervisor 
Richard Semmel, Township Manager Randy Cope, Director of Operations Jeff Mouer, Director of 
Public Works Rick Holtzman, Director of Finance/Treasurer Seth O’Neill, Director of Grants 
Preservation and Trails Mike Kukitz, Fire Code Administrator Jim Steward, and Secretary/Deputy 
Treasurer Jessica Oswald.  
 
 
NEW BUSINESS:  
 
2027 BUDGET DISCUSSION  
 
Discussion of the Proposed 2027 budget, Township Manager Randy Cope gave a synopsis the of 
the following funds: Street Light Fund, Recreation Fund, Farmland Fund, Traffic Fund, Sewer 
Fund, Fire Fund, Equipment Fund, ESLT Fund, Liquid Fuels Fund, and Capital Fund.  
 
Township Manager Randy Cope, and Director of Finance Seth O’Neill presented the option of 
subsidizing the Street Light Fund into the General Fund and discontinuing the Street Light Tax.  
• Brian Horwith, Springfield Drive, Coplay, inquired about the number of households in the 
Township and expressed his support for using General Fund revenues to subsidize the 
Street Light Fund. 
• Donna Kumernitsky, Township Tax Collector, stated that the Street Light Tax is a matter 
she often receives complaints about from residents.  
 
In discussion of the Farmland Fund, Township Manager Randy Cope, asked for the Boards opinion 
on how the Township move forward with the current .1% Farmland Tax if the EIT Referendum 
passes.  
• Donna Kumernitsky, Township Tax Collector, stated that residents have expressed 
concerns that imposing the proposed Earned Income Tax in addition to the existing 0.1% 
Farmland Tax could be perceived as the Township “double dipping.” 
• Brian Horwith, Springfield Drive, Coplay, inquired about the location of the Loomis property 
during the review of budgeted expenses for 2027. He also asked whether payments for the 
Steward property are currently being made in installments. Additionally, Mr. Horwith stated 
that the public may perceive the Township’s 0.1% Farmland Tax and the newly added 
Earned Income Tax as “double dipping.” Although he acknowledged that this perception 
may be difficult to change, he encouraged the Township to clearly communicate this if this 
is not its intent. 
 
In discussion of the ESLT Fund there was a public comment.  
• Brian Horwith, asked what drives LST and why the property owners are not responsible for 
this.  
 
During discussion of the Capital Fund, multiple options for the completion of and the financing and 
payment options of the new Yard Waste Site project were discussed.  
• Donna Kumernitsky, Township Tax Collector, expressed the opinion that if we build a pole 
barn at the new yard waste site, she didn’t believe it would be practical to keep all of our 
equipment there, because in the event of something like a fire, all of the Township’s 
equipment would be lost at one time.
