NORTH WHITEHALL TOWNSHIP BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
NORTH WHITEHALL TOWNSHIP MUNICIPAL BUILDING  
OCTOBER 20, 2026 
 
Chairman Dennis Klusaritz called the meeting to order at 5:00 PM followed by the Pledge of 
Allegiance and a moment of silence.  In attendance were Supervisor Al Geosits, Supervisor 
Richard Semmel, Township Solicitor Tom Dinkelacker, Planning Consultant Judy Goldstein, 
Township Manager Randy Cope, Director of Operations Jeff Mouer, and Secretary/Deputy 
Treasurer Jessica Oswald.  
 
 
NEW BUSINESS:  
 
CONSIDERATION OF A SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT BETWEEN NORTH WHITEHALL 
TOWNSHIP AND LANDSTON EQUITIES, LLC 
Upon motion by Supervisor Klusaritz, seconded by Supervisor Geosits, the Board approved the 
settlement agreement between North Whitehall Township and Landston Equities, LLC, resolving 
litigation between the Township and TCNE North Whitehall 2, subject to any non-material 
modifications as accepted by the Township Manager and the Township Solicitor; and further to 
authorized execution thereof by the Chairman of the Board of Supervisors. Roll call: Supervisors 
Klusaritz - yes; Supervisor Geosits - yes; Supervisor Semmel - yes.  
• Supervisor Klusaritz asked if there were any representatives present from Parkland School 
District. No members were present. He asked if there were any representatives from North 
Whitehall Taxpayers for Smart Growth Inc. present. Karen Komlos, Sam Claudio, Janine 
Bauer, Mark Kaintz, and Karen Kaintz were noted as present.  
• Sam Claudio, Orefield Rd, Orefield, asked for clarification on what the plan was depicting 
and if public water and sewer would be coming to the area. He also inquired if the applicant 
had any idea as to how many job opportunities the proposed plan would bring to the area.  
• Jenny Krumrine, Coplay Creek Rd., Schnecksville, stated that this plan fully addresses all 
previous concerns that she had, and she believes it will be a great commercial success.  
• Mark Kaintz, Route 309, Orefield, asked if PennDot is going to be involved with 
improvements at the intersection at Route 309 and Orefield Road. He also asked what the 
mixed uses of the plan will be and if the wellness center will be associated with any of the 
larger medical groups in the area. He also inquired if the applicant was looking at a certain 
design style for the single-family homes depicted in the proposed plan.  
 
 
 
COMMENTS FROM THE FLOOR: No comments from the floor.  
 
ADJOURNMENT Meeting adjourned at 5:26 PM. 
 
 
Respectfully Submitted,  
 
 
Jessica Oswald, Secretary/Deputy Treasurer
