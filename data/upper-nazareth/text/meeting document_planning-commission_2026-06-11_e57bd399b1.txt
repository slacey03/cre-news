NEW BUSINESS 
 
Zoning Ordinance Amendment- parking and heat mitigation 
 
Angela Strohl stated that she was reviewing the parking requirements in preparation of the NASD’s plan for the 
Calandra property. Currently the ordinance reads for indoor assembly. Nothing for fields. She is suggesting one 
space per four of capacity for public recreation. This is what we had in the old ordinance. It would amend table 
6.1. Robert Williams stated seems to be more for rural areas. Angela reviewed what we have currently and 
what she would like to be put in place prior to a submittal. She feels the school district would put in what would 
be needed but wants to make sure there is appropriate parking. A brief discussion ensued. Scott Sylvainus 
suggested removing “major driveways in gravel”. Angela stated that Palmer Township requires one space per 
1,000 square feet of outdoor fields or 500 square foot court plus one space per three seats for spectators. Brief 
discussion ensued of Palmer Township’s fields, the Chrin Center, and parking. Briefly discussed shared usage of 
parking. Scott suggested adding outdoor recreation- one per 1000 sq. ft. field, one for every 500 sq. ft. court, 
plus one for every three spectator seating. This would be added to table 6.1 with the indoor recreation. The 
Planning Commission was happy with this change. 
Heat mitigation- Angela referenced Lancaster and West Norriton. It is not really referenced by many. The 
Planning Commission had several suggestions from Angela. Robert asked about the data on this. Is there any 
information regarding heat mitigation in the Lehigh Valley? Angela replied not that she is aware of. The Board 
of Supervisors asked for this. Supervisor William Bartlett explained how the heat is exchanged into the

Karst Report final draft 
 
Planning Commission had draft #2 of the Karst Report. Scott stated that figures 8, 9, and 11 should be full page 
with clarity and in color. We need electronic versions of them, CAD versions. Justin Coyle stated they can get 
this GIS data. William Bartlett stated that Mr. Gillespie said he would provide a map with known sinkholes. One 
that is up to date. Justin stated the exhibits need to be bigger, in color, up to date, etc. Robert asked if there 
were more current data of sinkholes. Justin replied yes. There has to be newer maps since 1987. Robert stated 
that has to be updated. Figure #9 needs to be updated to current survey, in color of the entire Township, 
include known sinkholes, and be full page. The Township overlay needs to be correct. Angela asked who should 
reach out to Gilmore? Lisa Klem has been the one reaching out. Lisa and/or Justin will reach out regarding the 
updates. Julie Burkhardt requested that figure #8 be to scale for anyone who may be required to do that. 
Angela replied that all references to the Karst Report were removed from SALDO. The Planning Commission 
want an electronic version of the Karst distribution overlay map. The Planning Commission is asking for the 
following updates- Figures 8, 9, and 11 should be full page with clarity, in color, and have electronic versions. 
Figure 9 needs to be updated to current survey, be full page, be in color, cover the entire Township, and include 
known sinkholes. Figure 9 overlay needs to be correct. Karst distribution overlay map needs to also be in 
electronic version.  
 
Motion was made by Scott Sylvainus, seconded by Robert Williams, to recommend the Board of Supervisors 
accept the Karst Report once the requested changes are made. Motion approved unanimously.
