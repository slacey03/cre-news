Jay asked if there is a blind spot at the proposed entrance. Andy replied this location is per Penn DOT. Jay

Data Centers Ordinance 
 
Gary Asteak’s email regarding cooling system changes per LVPC recommendations was reviewed. Justin Coyle 
stated that he thinks the current version is sufficient as is. Scott asked if LVPC reviewed since their letter isn’t 
properly dated and it states it will be before the full commission on May 28, 2026. Craig Beaver, LVPC
