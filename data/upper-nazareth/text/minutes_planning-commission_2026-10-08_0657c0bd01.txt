Zoning Ordinance Amendment 
 
Angela stated the zoning amendments ordinance was before the Planning Commission for review. She will go 
through each section. Section one amends timber harvesting regarding conflicts with other agencies 
regulations. Heidelburg is working on several properties regarding timber harvesting. It was brought to her 
attention by the forester who is doing the work. They are thinning out various areas. 
Section two amends additional requirements for specific uses, requiring an erosion and Sedimentation plan 
approved by CCD if more than 25 acres is disturbed. Increased from one acre. 
Section three adds definition for single family attached dwelling. 
Section four amends 803 1 D, removing front yard reference from items within right of way. 
Section five replaces table 803.1 for new lots. 
Section six amends 402 36 C, fences, per the policy adopted at last night’s Board of Supervisors meeting. This 
makes the requirements (for fences in easements) very clear. Pamela asked what if it is not our easement. 
Angela replied our engineer would go out and review where they are and for what. They can make specific 
recommendations.  
 
Motion was made by Robert Williams, seconded by Pamela Berlew, to recommend the Board of Supervisors 
approve the Zoning Ordinance Amendment. Motion approved unanimously.
