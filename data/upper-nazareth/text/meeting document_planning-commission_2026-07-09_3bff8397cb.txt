NEW BUSINESS 
 
Zoning Ordinance Amendment- parking and heat mitigation 
 
Angela Strohl stated that the parking requirements and thermal heat mitigation amendments were reviewed at 
our last meeting. The Planning Commissions recommendations went to the Board of Supervisors. They are 
sending it back to the Planning Commission for final review and comments. We are still waiting for LVPC’s 
review comments.  
Public comment- Victor Scomillio, representing NASD, introduced himself, NASD Board members- Linda Stubits, 
Chair, Elmo Frey, Wendy Whiteash; Julie Burkhardt, and NASD engineer Arif Fazil. He thanked the Planning 
Commission for allowing them time to speak. NASD and Upper Nazareth staff met to go over NASD ideas for the 
space. Concerns were raised about parking as well as timing of field usage. A concept plan was handed out to 
the Planning Commission. There are no lights being planned for evening usage. We started to try to calculate 
for the parking (with proposed amendment calculations). It overburdens field space with parking. We are asking 
for adjustments to the calculations. What is a field? Is it just the playing area? It is not defined. We reviewed 
what would be needed for players and bleacher seating. It would require elimination of fields or excessive 
parking We are trying to keep parking off the streets. You don’t see fields being built except for those for profit. 
We have a methodology we wish to share. 
Arif Fazil stated that the concept plan feels like a good use of the area. It is a good use of the property, 
highlights the area, the administrative offices would repurpose the building, improves the Clipper field, and 
safety for the other schools, especially the Shafer baseball field. We have area for parking, bus drop off, and
