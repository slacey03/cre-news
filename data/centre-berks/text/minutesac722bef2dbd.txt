CENTRE TOWNSHIP BOARD OF SUPERVISORS 
MINUTES OF JULY 28, 2026 
     
 
The regular monthly meeting of the Centre Township Board of Supervisors was held on 
July 28, 2026 at the Centre Township Municipal Building, 449 Bucks Hill Road, 
Mohrsville, PA.   Chairman James Fitterling called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and 
led the Pledge of Allegiance, followed by a Moment of Silence.  The following were 
present:  James Fitterling, Daniel Unterkofler, member; Solicitor Liz Magovern, Ryan 
Rhode, Jeff Fiant, Constable Kevin Noecker. Melissa Krishock and Secretary/Treasurer 
Deborah Scull were absent. 
 
HEARING OF VISITORS ON AGENDA ITEMS 
None at this time. 
 
APPROVAL OF MINUTES  
A motion was made by Daniel Unterkofler to approve the June 23, 2026 Board of 
Supervisors Meeting Minutes.  Seconded by James Fitterling.  Motion passed. 
 
PAYMENT OF BILLS  
A motion was made by James Fitterling and seconded by Daniel Unterkofler to 
approve the payment of bills thru July 23, 2026.  Motion passed. 
 
SOLICITORS REPORT 
None 
 
ROAD DEPARTMENT 
The Oil and Chip and Paving Projects have been completed.  Mowing, equipment 
maintenance and other cleanup duties have been performed. 
 
James Fitterling made motion to approve the purchase of a Gingway Direct Fire 
Kettle at the cost of $1,150.00.  Seconded by Daniel Unterkofler.  Motion passed. 
 
SEO REPORT 
No Report 
 
ENGINEER’S REPORT – RYAN RHODE 
The 2026 street work payment application was presented which included a change 
order.  James Fitterling moved to approve the change order in the amount of 
$5,988.27, second by Daniel Unterkofler.  Motion Passed.  This change order 
increases the project cost to $124,760.57, from the originally quoted price of 
$118,772.30.  James Fitterling moved to approve the 2026 street work payment 
application in the amount of $124,760.57, second by Daniel Unterkofler.  Motion 
passed. 
 
Next, Ryan gave an update on the Fuel Station plans.  He has met with the road crew 
to review some ideas.  The next step will be to meet with an architectural firm to work 
towards getting a quote for the project. 
 
Escrow Release #8 in the amount of $109,048.48 was presented for Board approval.  It 
was noted that there are basically some punch list type items remaining to wrap up 
phase 1 of this project.  Resident Oliver Blatt was present and conveyed some concerns 
with exterior lighting encroaching into his yard.  Kevin Pyle noted the concern and will

get his taken care of with Denny’s Electric.  Mr. Blatt also questioned about tree planting 
and it was noted that the trees are still part of the items remaining to be completed.  
James Fitterling moved to approve Tiny Pear Escrow release #8 in the amount of 
$109,048.48, second by Daniel Unterkofler.  Motion passed. 
 
The retaining wall at 101 Railroad Road was discussed.  Ryan concluded that there is 
definitely some concern about the integrity of the wall and he noted that the township 
stands to lose the most should the wall fail.  Ryan will be coordinating with Solicitor Liz 
Magovern and looking to meet with some contractors to come up wit some cost estimates 
for the project. 
 
Lastly, there was some concern about semi-trucks on Easter Street not being able to 
make the turn onto Main Street in Mohrsville.  This is causing damage to road signs 
and traffic tie ups when a truck gets stuck in the intersection.  It is believed that most 
of the trucks having problems are coming from Alpha Packaging.  After discussion, Ryan 
will reach out to Alpha to remind truckers of the difficulty on that road.  Future signage 
may be needed. 
 
ZONING OFFICER REPORT 
Jeff Fiant of Kraft Municipal Services reported there were 7 permits issued totaling 
$162,663.75 in construction value.  20 inspections were performed.  Additionally, 
zoning issues were addressed on Our Alley, 129 Garfield (work without a permit), 556 
Grove (driveway washing out), Irish Creed (dog barking), Lower Loop (Pool without a 
permit) and 236 Shannaleigh ($1,600 property maintenance fine and appeal).
