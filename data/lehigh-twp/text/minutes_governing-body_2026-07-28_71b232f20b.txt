II POLICE DEPARTMENT 
A. Swearing in of Patrol Officer.  Magistrate Robert Hawke was present to administer 
the Oath of Office to the newest member of the Police Department, Brett Bindics.  
Officer Bindics started going through the hiring process in January and was given a 
conditional offer of employment in June.  He is a veteran of the United States Air 
Force and a retired officer from the City of Easton.   
 
B. Promotion of Sergeant.  Chief Henry announced the promotion of Officer Craig Ball 
to the position of Sergeant.  Sergeant Ball successfully completed the promotional 
process which started in January which included studying for a written test and 
completing two interviews.  He is a graduate of East Stroudsburg University and the 
Allentown Police Academy.  He has been with the Department since June 22, 2015.  
During his time with the Department, he has been in a leadership role by being a field 
training officer, firearms instructor, in charge of the property room and assisted or 
been lead investigator in many criminal investigations.

 Cindy Miller commented the Pennsylvania law states that a local government unit, 
 excluding counties and county authorities, may, by ordinance, impose in addition to their 
 statutory rate limits on real estate taxes set forth in the municipal code set for by that   
 local government unit, an open state tax on real property not exceeding the millage 
 authorized by the referendum under Section A3.  It also states a local government unit, 
 excluding counties and county authorities, may by ordinance, impose in addition to the 
 earned  income tax rate limit set forth in the Act of December 31, 1965, known as the

 local government unit, not exceeding a rate authorized by referendum under subsection

 decide  that they want to move it forward,  it has to go on referendum vote to the voters to 
 set the  millage rate, which at this point it was suggested to be .25 percent.  That is what
