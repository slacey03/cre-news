I. CALL TO ORDER.  The Lehigh Township Board of Supervisors held their second 
 monthly meeting on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 7:00 p.m.  The meeting was held at

II. 2027 BUDGET.  Alice Rehrig provided the Board with an updated budget for the General 
 Fund, State Fund, and Fire Tax Fund.  All the budgets have been balanced.  She noted 
 that the Liquid Fuels estimate is a similar amount to 2026. 
 
 Jerry Pritchard questioned what the total legal fees were in the budget.  Alice Rehrig 
 commented regular legal fees are $28,000; labor related fees are $7,500 and Zoning 
 Hearing Board fees are $18,000. 
 
 Cindy Miller questioned if the insurance company would be giving us a discount since 
 we will be having the cameras on the vehicles.  Alice Rehrig commented there isn’t a 
 specific discount; the workers comp carrier provided a grant towards the purchase of 
 them.  The rate for workers comp did not increase.  Any cost increases are driven by 
 payroll and claims.  For 2027, our experience rating has decreased. 
  
 Cindy Miller questioned if the on line payments would be pursued in 2027.  Alice Rehrig 
 commented she can continue with that.  In speaking with vendors, there would be a 
 3% fee which would get charged to the users.  She questioned if there would be an 
 interest in also allowing ACH payment so individuals can use a debit card.  The costs to 
 the Township would be minimal; it would just be for a machine if we were to accept a 
 card at  the window.  The Board was in agreement to looking into both options.  
 
 Jerry Pritchard questioned the contribution to the library.  It is almost like our residents 
 are paying twice.  They pay for it through the Township and they are also paying it 
 through the School Tax.  It was agreed that the contribution would be dropped to $2,000. 
 
 Cindy Miller commented she saw in the notes there was a question about how many dog 
 bag dispensers were needed for Indiantrail Park.  The Board agreed one should be enough 
 and have it placed in the area of the pavilions.

VI. NEW BUSINESS 
A. Pennsville Playground Equipment.  The Recreation Board had been looking at getting 
new playground equipment for Indiantrail Park.  The equipment they chose would 
include swings, a play area for children 2 to 5 years of age, and a play area for 
children 5 through 12 years of age.  The total cost of the equipment is $143,130.  The 
prices that were quoted are sales prices good through the beginning of December.  
The Jaindl Corporation owes the Township $92,000 for the land at the park and an 
additional $25,400 when they record their plan.  They have agreed to advance the 
money that is due for the plan so that the final cost of the equipment would be 
$25,760 which would be paid by Recreation Fees. The equipment could be installed 
and ready for use for the spring rental. 
 
Mike Jones questioned if the equipment will be going in the same place as the 
equipment that is currently at the park.  Alice Rehrig commented it would be in that 
general area.  The existing equipment would be removed by the Township.  Mike 
Jones also questioned if they would be using the mulch or fiber.  Alice Rehrig 
commented it would be playground mulch.  The fiber is more costly and tends to 
cause issues with the mower when it gets kicked out into the grass. 
 
Jerry Pritchard made a motion to move forward with the purchase of the equipment as 
proposed utilizing the fees from Jaindl Corporation and $25,760 coming from the Rec 
Land Fee Account.  Janet Sheats seconded the motion.  Linda Roman commented

B. Manager’s Report.  Alice Rehrig reported she checked into adding a light at the 
intersection of Route 145 and Washington Drive.  PPL typically does not install area

the street light is once one is installed, how many additional requests will be received.  
When she and Frank Zamadics discussed this, he suggested adding white reflectors to 
the roadway.  The Board agreed that the reflectors would be the better solution. 
 
Alice Rehrig had also been at a meeting with the School District where they discussed

C. Solicitor’s Report.  Attorney Backenstoe commented there are a lot of zoning issues 
taking place.  He has been working with the Zoning Officer on the more complicated 
matters.  Jerry Pritchard questioned if these matters are all going to the Zoning 
Hearing Board.  Attorney Backenstoe commented the Zoning Officer will send a 
general letter saying they are out of compliance and try to work with them to resolve 
the matter.  If they don’t cooperate, she sends an enforcement letter and if there is still 
not cooperation, zoning matters will go to the Zoning Hearing Board and code 
violations will go to the magistrate.
