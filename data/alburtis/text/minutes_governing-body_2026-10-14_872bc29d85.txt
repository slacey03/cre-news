 
Borough of Alburtis 
Meeting Minutes 
October 14th, 2026 
 
The October 14th, 2026, meeting of Borough Council was called to order by Council President Ron DeIaco at 7:00 p.m. 
in the Borough Hall, 260 Franklin Street, Alburtis, PA. The meeting was also streamed on Zoom. 
 
Attendance: 
Council: Ron DeIaco, Steve Hill, Chad Atkins, Mehmet Birtek(zoom), Michael Gerhart, Ashlynn Rivera  
Associates: Hector Moss- Mayor, Stephen Nemeth- Borough Manager, Dave Knerr-Solicitor 
   
PUBLIC COMMENT: 
No Public Comment. 
 
MEETING MINUTES of September 30th, 2026:  
Steve Hill made a motion to approve the minutes of September 30th, 2026. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 6-0.

Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to pay the bills, and Mehmet Birtek seconded the motion. The motion passed 6-0.

MACUNGIE AMBULANCE CORPS 
Ron DeIaco mentioned in the report 409 calls for Macungie Ambulance in September, with 14 being in the borough. 
Chad Atkins made a motion to accept the Macungie Ambulance report, Ashlynn Rivera 2nd, and a unanimous vote 
followed. Motion passed 6-0.  
 
Steve Hill mentioned that people should really start considering donating to the Macungie Ambulance. 
 
LMFD Quarterly Report:  

 
Chad Atkins made a motion to accept the LMFD Quarterly Report. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion. The motion 
passed 6-0.  
 
New Business: 
 
AMENDMENT TO PREVIOUS PARADE ROUTE APPROVAL – CHANGE IN RESPONSIBLE 
ORGANIZATION 
Steve Hill made a motion to approve an amendment to the previously approved parade route to reflect the change in 
responsible organization. Chad Atkins seconded the motion. The motion passed 6-0.  
 
Recommendation to Pay Allstate Septic Systems LLP   
Michael Gerhart made a motion to approve payment to Allstate Septic Systems LLP. Chad Atkins seconded the motion. 
The motion passed 6-0.  
 
Recommendation to Pay Kappe Associates Inc.   
Chad Atkins made a motion to approve payment to Kappe Associates Inc. Michael Gerhart seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 6-0.  
 
Request to apply for DCED Local Share Account Statewide 
Steve Nemeth mentioned that he would like to apply for this grant again like last year. We attempted but were not 
selected. Steve Hill made a motion to approve applying for the Local Share Account Statewide grant. Mehmet Birtek 
seconded the motion, and it passed, 6-0.
