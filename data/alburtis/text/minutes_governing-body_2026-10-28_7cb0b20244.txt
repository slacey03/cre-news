 
Borough of Alburtis 
Meeting Minutes 
October 28th, 2026 
 
The October 28th, 2026, meeting of Borough Council was called to order by Council President Ron DeIaco at 7:00 p.m. 
in the Borough Hall, 260 Franklin Street, Alburtis, PA. The meeting was also streamed on Zoom. 
 
Attendance: 
Council: Ron DeIaco, Steve Hill, John Aleszczyk, Chad Atkins, Michael Gerhart, Ashlynn Rivera, Mehmet 
Birtek(zoom) 
Associates: Hector Moss- Mayor, Stephen Nemeth- Borough Manager, Dave Knerr-Solicitor 
   
PUBLIC COMMENT: 
No Public Comment. 
 
MEETING MINUTES of October 14th, 2026:  
Steve Hill made a motion to approve the minutes of October 14th, 2026. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and it 
passed, 7-0.  
 
BILLS FOR PAYMENT:  
John Aleszczyk made a request for a copy of the Integra contract; he stated that he thinks that when this contract is up, we 
should go out for an RFP for IT services. Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to approve the bills for payment, and Michael 
Gerhart seconded the motion. The motion passed, 7-0.  
 
Reports: 
 
Mayor’s REPORT: 
No report at this time. Hector mentioned that he wanted to send sincere thanks to the Alburtis Social Club for helping with 
the Parade. Also made mention of thanking our first responders, since it is National First Responders Day 
 
MANAGER’S REPORT:  
Chad Atkins made a motion to accept Oct 14th- Oct 27th. Manager’s Report, and Mike Gerhart seconded the motion. The

New Business: 
 
Lower Macungie Library Presentation  
Debbie Jack presented an update on the library, noting higher in-person use, program attendance, and borrowing; 
highlights included 701 summer reading registrants, 206 programs, expanded digital resources, museum passes, “Library 
of Things,” tutoring/job resources, and the library’s 25th anniversary.

Request to repair Lockridge Church Concrete sidewalk and driveway 
Ron DeIaco, asked if we received the insurance check for this repair. Steve Nemeth stated that we did. Ashlynn Rivera

the motion, and a unanimous vote followed. The motion passed 7-0.

Steve Hill made a motion to repair the sewer generator; Michael Gerhart seconded the motion, and a unanimous vote 

 
followed. The motion passed 7-0. 
 
Request to modernize camera server storage area 
Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to approve the request to modernize the camera server storage area, Michael Gerhart 
seconded the motion, and a unanimous vote followed. The motion passed 7-0. 
 
Request to Outsource Payroll  
Steve Nemeth provided a summary of this request: As previously mentioned during the budget workshop process, we 
would like to outsource payroll. Currently, our QuickBooks payroll is not set up properly and has not been for some time. 
Our previous Auditor and current auditor, along with a QuickBooks specialist, are unable to fix the issue. Our auditor has 
recommended that since QuickBooks will be no longer supporting the desktop version going forward and we will need to 
migrate to the online version that now would be the best time to start looking at outsourcing our payroll. Attached, you 
will find the Letter from our auditor with his recommendations on this process, as well as four quotes. Several of the 
quotes have promotions that we would be interested in taking advantage of.  Mike Gerhart made a motion to approve 
outsourcing payroll, and Chad Atkins seconded the motion, and a unanimous vote followed. The motion passed 7-0. 
 
Municipal Pension Program; Request to Advertise RFP for Pension Consulting Services 
Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to approve the advertisement for the RFP for Pension Services; Michael Gerhart seconded 
the motion, and a unanimous vote followed. The motion passed 7-0. 
 
Request to advertise a public hearing for SALDO application 2026-01  
Mike Gerhart made a motion to approve the advertisement of a public hearing for SALDO application 2026-01, Ashlynn 
Rivera seconded the motion, and a unanimous vote followed. The motion passed 7-0. 
 
Adjournment 
Ron DeIaco mentioned that we would be going into an executive session for personnel. No vote will be needed upon the 
conclusion of the executive session. A motion to adjourn at 7:25 p.m. was made by Michael Gerhart, 2nd by Ashlynn 
Rivera, and a unanimous vote followed.
