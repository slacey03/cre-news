Reports: 
 
Mayor’s REPORT: 
Hector Moss commented on the recent storm event, recognizing the significant behind-the-scenes efforts of Borough staff 
and emergency responders, whose work often goes unnoticed. He noted that the storm developed and passed quickly, 
creating challenges for the community and response personnel. 
Hector Moss commended residents for coming together during the emergency, observing neighbors helping one another 
by sharing generators, extension cords, water, and assisting with tree and debris removal. He expressed appreciation for 
the community's willingness to support one another and was grateful that, despite the severity of the storm, everyone 
remained safe and conditions were not worse. 
Hector also acknowledged that power restoration was challenging for some residents, with a few outages lasting until 
approximately 2:00 a.m., and noted that recovery efforts are still a work in progress.

Request to purchase generator/ options for Maintenance shop: 
Currently, the maintenance shop does not have a generator, and when the power is out. The maintenance crew must climb 
the backhoe to open the garage doors. We are requesting to purchase a generator for the maintenance shop so that when 
the power goes out, they can work in the maintenance shop and access vehicles. It was mentioned to see about getting 
additional quotes and see what needs to be placed on the generator. Also, it was mentioned to look at getting a possible 
smaller one. That could be utilized if another generator is not working properly. Tabled until the next meeting.
