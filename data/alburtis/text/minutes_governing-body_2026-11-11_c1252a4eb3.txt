 
Borough of Alburtis 
Meeting Minutes 
November 11th, 2026 
 
The November 11th, 2026, meeting of Borough Council was called to order by Council President Ron DeIaco at 7:00 p.m. 
in the Borough Hall, 260 Franklin Street, Alburtis, PA. The meeting was also streamed on Zoom. 
 
Attendance: 
Council: Ron DeIaco, John Aleszczyk, Chad Atkins, Mehmet Birtek, Michael Gerhart, Ashlynn Rivera  
Associates: Stephen Nemeth- Borough Manager, Dave Knerr-Solicitor 
   
PUBLIC COMMENT: 
No Public Comment. 
 
MEETING MINUTES of October 28th, 2026:  
Chad Atkins made a motion to approve the minutes of October 28th, 2026. Mehmet Birtek seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 6-0.   
 
BILLS FOR PAYMENT:  
John Aleszczyk questioned the Eddinger bill and whether that was part of the original quote for the generator cost. Steve 
said it was not, and that this was for the initial fill. Mehmet Birtek made a motion to pay the bills, and Chad Atkins 
seconded the motion. The motion passed 6-0.   
 
Reports: 
 
Mayor’s REPORT: 
Mayor Moss provided a request to council to make a monetary contribution of $150.00 to the Alburtis Social Club for 
their food drive. Chad Atkins made a motion to approve the request, and Mehmet Birtek seconded the motion. The motion 
passed 6-0.   
 
MANAGER’S REPORT:  
Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to accept the October 28th- November 10th Manager’s Report, and Mehmet Birtek 
seconded the motion. The motion passed 6-0. 
 
TREASURER REPORT:  
Mehmet Birtek made a motion to accept the October Treasurer’s Report, and Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 6-0.  
 
ENGINEERS REPORT:  
Mehmet Birtek made a motion to accept the November Engineer’s Report, and Michael Gerhart seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 6-0. 
 
POLICE REPORT:  
Chad Atkins made a motion to accept the October Police Report. Michael Gerhart seconded the motion, The motion 
passed 6-0.  
 
MACUNGIE AMBULANCE CORPS 
Ron DeIaco mentioned in the report 450 calls for Macungie Ambulance in October, with 8 being in the borough. 
Mehmet Birtek made a motion to accept the Macungie Ambulance report, Ashlynn Rivera 2nd, and a unanimous vote 
followed. Motion passed 6-0.  
 
New Business: 
 
2026 All-Hazards EOP update 

 
Chad Atkins made a motion to approve the 2026 All-Hazards EOP update. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 6-0.  
 
Resolution No. 2026-21 A RESOLUTION OF THE BOROUGH OF ALBURTIS, LEHIGH COUNTY,

Chad Atkins made a motion to approve Resolution No. 2026-21. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion. The motion passed 
6-0.

Mehmet Birtek made a motion to approve the PPL LED Streetlight Conversion Agreement. Michael Gerhart seconded the

Resolution No. 2026-22 A RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR THE FURNISHING OF ELECTRIC STREET 
LIGHTING SERVICE TO THE BOROUGH OF ALBURTIS, LEHIGH COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA 
Chad Atkins made a motion to approve Resolution No. 2026-22. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and it passed, 6-0. 
 
Borough Solicitor and Engineer RFP update 
Steve Nemeth mentioned that RFP Submission Deadline for Solicitor and Engineering Services was on Friday, November 
6th. We received 4 RFP responses for soliciting services and 7 RFP responses for engineering services. Would like to get 
an idea of how Council would wish to proceed? Would interviews be wanted? If so, who would you wish to participate in 
this process? How many of the applicants should we offer interview time slots to? Ron DeIaco mentioned that we will let 
the personnel committee take the lead on this. Can set up a meeting to review the RFP’s and grade on a rubric, perhaps cut 
the applicant pool down, and interview about 2-3 from each of the two applicant pools. John recommended that his only 
major recommendation would be that we take the travel distance from the borough into account when reviewing the 
applicant pool. 
 
Adjournment 
A motion to adjourn at 7:26 p.m. was made by Ashlynn Rivera, 2nd by Chad Atkins, and a unanimous vote followed. 
Council then proceeded into an Executive Session with no votes to follow for potential legal.
