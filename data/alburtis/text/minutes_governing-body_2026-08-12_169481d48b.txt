 
Borough of Alburtis 
Meeting Minutes 
August 12th, 2026 
 
The August 12th, 2026, meeting of Borough Council was called to order by Council President Ron DeIaco at 7:00 p.m. in 
the Borough Hall, 260 Franklin Street, Alburtis, PA. The meeting was also streamed on Zoom. 
 
Attendance: 
Council: Ron DeIaco, John Aleszczyk, Chad Atkins, Mehmet Birtek, Michael Gerhart, Ashlynn Rivera  
Associates: Hector Moss- Mayor (zoom), Stephen Nemeth- Borough Manager, Dave Knerr-Solicitor, Tony Alsleben- 
Police Chief (zoom) 
   
PUBLIC COMMENT: 
No Public Comment. 
 
MEETING MINUTES of July 29th, 2026:  
John Aleszczyk made a motion to approve the minutes of July 29th, 2026. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and it 
passed, 6-0.  
 
BILLS FOR PAYMENT:  
Chad Atkins made a motion to pay the bills, and Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and approved unanimously, 6-0.  
 
Reports: 
 
Mayor’s REPORT: 
No Report 
 
POLICE REPORT:  
Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to accept the July Police Report. Chad Atkins seconded the motion, and it passed 
unanimously, 6-0. 
 
MANAGER’S REPORT:  
John Aleszczyk made a motion to accept the July Manager’s Report, and Chad Atkins seconded the motion. The motion 
passed 6-0.  
 
TREASURER REPORT:  
John Aleszczyk asked if we could look into the water fund and claimed that it has decreased since the previous year. 
Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to accept the July Treasurer’s Report, and Chad Atkins seconded the motion. The motion 
passed 6-0.  
 
ENGINEERS REPORT:  
John Aleszczyk asked when some of the items will come off of the report. Steve Nemeth mentioned that during the 
engineering meeting we mentioned we could remove some of them since they are completely closed out. They will be 
removed by the next meeting. John also asked why we were looking at stormwater concerns on School Street, when it 
floods more on his street on South Oak. Steve Nemeth mentioned that we are looking at most of the flooding areas, 
especially in the older parts of town, where the infrastructure was geared towards gravity, instead of physically installing 
inlets. Newer developments now are developed with inlets and stormwater infrastructure, whereas in the past that was not 
the case. Chad Atkins made a motion to accept the July Engineer’s report; Ashlynn Rivera 2nd, and a unanimous vote 
followed. The motion passed 6-0. 
 
MACUNGIE AMBULANCE CORPS 
No Report Submitted. 
 
New Business: 
 

 
Ordinance No. 607: AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 17 OF THE ALBURTIS CODIFIED 
ORDINANCES (RELATING TO POLICE PENSION PLAN) TO PROVIDE THE 2026 SPECIAL AD HOC 
MUNICIPAL POLICE AND FIREFIGHTER POST-RETIREMENT ADJUSTMENT MANDATED BY ACT 21 
OF 2026. 
Dave Knerr gave a summary of the ordinance. Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to approve Ordinance 607. Mehmet Birtek 
seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously, 6-0. 
 
Request to apply for a local share account grant for a new backhoe  
Steve Nemeth mentioned that he would like to apply for this grant again, like last year. We attempted but were not 
selected. 
Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to approve applying for the local share account grant. Michael Gerhart seconded the 
motion, and it passed, 6-0. 
 
Resolution No. 2026-17: A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE BOROUGH MANAGER TO EXECUTE ALL 
DOCUMENTS AND AGREEMENTS BETWEEN THE BOROUGH OF ALBURTIS AND THE 
PENNSYLVANIA COMMONWEALTH FINANCING AUTHORITY TO FACILITATE AND ASSIST IN 
OBTAINING THE REQUESTED NORTHAMPTON/ LEHIGH COUNTY LOCAL SHARE ASSESSMENT 
GRANT. 
Mehmet Birtek made a motion to approve Resolution No. 2026-17. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and it passed, 6-
0. 
 
Public Comment: 
Ron DeIaco asked if anybody else had anything for the group. Dave Knerr mentioned how we had a successful day in the 
matter of 108 S Main Street. The clerk's office will send out the judge's order as to when they must be out by, or the 
sheriff's will remove them. 
 
Adjournment 
A motion to adjourn at 7:30 p.m. was made by Ashlynn Rivera, 2nd by Chad Atkins, and a unanimous vote followed.
