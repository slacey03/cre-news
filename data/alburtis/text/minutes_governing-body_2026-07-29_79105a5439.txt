Dave Knerr gave a summary of all the resolutions. The resolutions authorize the Borough to enter into agreements with 
Berkheimer, the Borough's current Earned Income Tax collector, to also administer the collection of the Per Capita Tax 
and the Local Services Tax. The resolutions appoint Berkheimer as the Borough's tax collector for these taxes, authorize 
the firm to collect delinquent taxes and retain the authorized collection costs assessed to delinquent taxpayers, designate 
Berkheimer as the hearing officer for matters related to these taxes, and permit the sharing of confidential tax information 
with the Borough Manager as necessary for tax administration. Mike Gerhart made a motion to approve all eight 
resolutions; Resolutions 2026-9- Resolution 2026-16. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously, 
6-0. 
 
Request to purchase generator/ options for Maintenance shop: 
Steve Hill asked Steve Nemeth where the money would come from for this. Steve Nemeth mentioned that it would come 
out of the Highway Reserve Fund, which currently has $153,000 in the fund. John Aleszczyk mentioned that he knows 
this is a vital piece of equipment, but his concern is that we are spending more than we have. Steve Nemeth mentioned 
that this is a vital piece of equipment for our entire maintenance department to operate the borough, and that is all that we 
are requesting. When the power was out for four days, Donnie had to take equipment home just to charge it up because we 
had no power at the maintenance shop. We lost power there in the winter; when they get called in for storm duty, 
normally it’s the on-call person that comes in first to start the process. If one person slips, attempting to open garage doors 
by climbing due to not having a proper power source, we are all liable because we all knew about this issue. 
Mike Gerhart made a motion to approve the purchase of a generator, accepting the quote from Wetzel Enterprises. 
Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and it passed, 5-1.
