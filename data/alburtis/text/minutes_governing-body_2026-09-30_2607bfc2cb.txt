 
Borough of Alburtis 
Meeting Minutes 
September 30th, 2026 
 
The September 30th, 2026, meeting of Borough Council was called to order by Council President Ron DeIaco at 7:00 
p.m. in the Borough Hall, 260 Franklin Street, Alburtis, PA. The meeting was also streamed on Zoom. 
 
Attendance: 
Council: Ron DeIaco, Steve Hill, John Aleszczyk, Chad Atkins, Michael Gerhart, Ashlynn Rivera  
Associates: Hector Moss- Mayor, Stephen Nemeth- Borough Manager, Dave Knerr-Solicitor 
   
PUBLIC COMMENT: 
No Public Comment. 
 
MEETING MINUTES of September 9th, 2026:  
Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to approve the minutes of September 9th, 2026. Mike Gerhart seconded the motion, and it 
passed, 6-0.  
 
BILLS FOR PAYMENT:  
Ashlynn Rivera made a motion to approve the bills for payment, and Chad Atkins seconded the motion. The motion 
passed, 6-0.  
 
Reports: 
 
Mayor’s REPORT: 
No report at this time. 
 
MANAGER’S REPORT:  
Ron mentioned that the maintenance crew did a great job on cleaning up the church lot and was curious about the status of 
the cement repair. Steve Nemeth mentioned that he received a quote for the work and submitted it to insurance and is 
currently waiting to receive the check before requesting to proceed. John Alesczyk asked if we could reach out to the 
county for an update on the Lockridge furnace project. Steve Nemeth mentioned that he will follow up with the county. 
Steve Nemeth provided a detailed report involving a sewer maintenance update. Mike Gerhart made a motion to accept 
Sept 9th- Sept 30th. Manager’s Report, and Chad Atkins seconded the motion. The motion passed 6-0.  
 
New Business: 
 
Alburtis Parade Route- Alburtis Area Community Center:  
Beth Kuhns from the Alburtis Area Community Center stated that the route is the same as last year. Nothing has changed 
from last year's submission. She stated that all documents have been submitted to PennDOT; all they need is the letter of 
approval from the borough. Steve Hill asked Beth about the latest on nonprofit status; Beth said everything was fine and 
that the documents had been submitted. They were just waiting on the borough’s letter. Mike stated that as of this 
morning, it was showing that the status was still revoked. Mike Gerhart made a motion to approve the parade route 
contingent on Alburtis Area Community Center providing proof that 501 (c)(3) nonprofit status was reinstated in person at 
the October 14th council meeting, and Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion. The motion passed 6-0.  
 
Authorizing the advertisement for Request for Proposals of Municipal Solicitor:

Authorizing the 2027 Minimum Municipal Obligation for Pension Plans 
Dave Knerr mentioned that for next year we budget a little more conservatively. Mike Gerhart made a motion to 
authorize the 2027 Minimum Municipal Obligation for Pension Plans; Ashlynn Rivera 2nd, and a unanimous vote 
followed. Motion passes 6-0.
