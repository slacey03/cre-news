PUBLIC COMMENT  
No Public Comment 
 
Approval of Meeting Minutes of June 10th, 2026 
John Aleszczyk made a motion to approve the Minutes of June 10th, 2026. Chad Atkins 2nd the motion, and a unanimous 
vote followed. The motion passed with a 7-0 vote. 
 
BILLS FOR PAYMENT – June 24th, 2026  
Steve Hill asked how much of the ARRO invoice is reimbursable. Steve Nemeth stated that most of it is. John Aleszczyk 
inquired if we could total up how much the road projects came up to in comparison to what we budgeted. Steve Nemeth 
stated that he will put that information together. Chad Atkins made a motion to pay the bills of June 24th,2026. Ashlynn 
Rivera 2nd the motion, and a unanimous vote followed; the motion passed with a 7-0 vote.  
 
MAYOR’S REPORT   
Hector Moss mentioned that he wanted to congratulate the chief and his team for the successful ice cream event; prior to 
that, Hector mentioned that he had the opportunity to tour the elementary school and read to the students.  Hector stated 
that his next event that he is attempting to set up is a painting with the fire department event.
