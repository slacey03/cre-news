MEETING MINUTES of April 29th, 2026:  
Chad Atkins made a motion to approve the minutes of April 29th, 2026. Michael Gerhart seconded the motion, and it 
passed, 7-0.  
 
BILLS FOR PAYMENT:  
Mehmet Birtek questioned what exactly ABE Labs is testing for. Stephen Nemeth mentioned that they test our water for 
items like PFAs and other DEP-mandated tests. Test results get posted yearly in our CCR report. Mehmet Birtek made a 
motion to pay the bills, and Chad Atkins seconded the motion and approved unanimously, 7-0.  
 
Reports: 
 
Mayor’s REPORT: 
No Mayor reports. 
 
POLICE REPORT:  
Steve Hill asked about what makes up the increase in traffic stops. Tony mentioned that having the full complement of 
officers now, we can dedicate more to traffic. Stop signs and speeding are the two top citations in the category. John 
mentioned that if there was one thing a person running for state office could do, it would be getting radar for 
municipalities. Since Pa is the only state without radar. Mehmet Birtek made a motion to accept the April Police Report. 
Chad Atkins seconded the motion, and it passed unanimously, 7-0. 
 
TREASURERS REPORT:  
Michael Gerhart made a motion to accept the April Treasurer’s Report, and Mehmet Birtek seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 7-0.  
 
ENGINEERS REPORT:  
Mehmet Birtek made a motion to accept the April Engineer’s report, Michael Gerhart 2nd, and a unanimous vote 
followed. The motion passed 7-0. 
 
MACUNGIE AMBULANCE CORPS 
Ron DeIaco mentioned in the report stated 403 calls for Macungie Ambulance in April, with 3 being in the borough. 
Michael Gerhart made a motion to accept the April Ambulance report, Mehmet Birtek 2nd, and a unanimous vote 
followed. The motion passed 7-0.

uest to Hire Mark Rothrock as a full-time maintenance employee.

Re
quest to Apply for DCED Multimodal Grant for Ridgeview Paving Project 
Stephen Nemeth mentioned that he would like to apply for this grant again since we weren’t awarded last year. This

cover the rise in costs since last year’s submission. Michael Gerhart made a motion to apply for DCED Multimodal and 
PennDOT Multimodal grants. Mehmet Birtek seconded the motion, and the motion passed 7-0.
