 
Borough of Alburtis 
Meeting Minutes 
June 10th, 2026 
 
The June 10th, 2026, meeting of Borough Council was called to order by Council President Ron DeIaco at 7:00 p.m. in 
the Borough Hall, 260 Franklin Street, Alburtis, PA. The meeting was also streamed on Zoom. 
 
Attendance: 
Council: Ron DeIaco, Steve Hill, Michael Gerhart, Ashlynn Rivera, John Aleszczyk(zoom), Mehmet Birtek,  
Associates: Stephen Nemeth- Borough Manager, Dave Knerr-Solicitor, Tony Alsleben- Police Chief (zoom), East Penn 
Press (zoom)  
Visitors: Gary DeRoner, Ray Rickets, Carl Rottenberger 
   
PUBLIC COMMENT: 
No Public Comment 
 
MEETING MINUTES of May 24th, 2026:  
Steve Hill made a motion to approve the minutes of May 24th, 2026. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and it passed, 
6-0.  
 
BILLS FOR PAYMENT:  
Mehmet Birtek made a motion to pay the bills, and Michael Gerhart seconded the motion and approved unanimously, 6-0.  
 
Reports: 
 
Mayor’s REPORT: 
No Mayor reports. 
 
POLICE REPORT:  
Chief Tony Alsleben mentioned that on Monday June 15th from 6pm- 8pm the police department will be hosting scoops 
with the cops at Drizzle, a play on of coffee with the police. Steve Nemeth mentioned that he wanted to commend Tony 
and his officers for their PR campaign on spreading the word on the no phone while driving law going into effect. 
Mehmet Birtek made a motion to accept the May Police Report. Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion, and it passed 
unanimously, 6-0. 
 
MANAGER’S REPORT:  
Michael Gerhart made a motion to accept the May Manager’s Report, and Mehmet Birtek seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 6-0.  
 
TREASURERS REPORT:  
Mehmet Birtek made a motion to accept the May Treasurer’s Report, and Ashlynn Rivera seconded the motion. The 
motion passed 6-0.  
 
ENGINEERS REPORT:  
Michael Gerhart made a motion to accept the May Engineer’s report, Mehmet Birtek 2nd, and a unanimous vote followed. 
The motion passed 6-0. 
 
MACUNGIE AMBULANCE CORPS 
Ron DeIaco mentioned in the report stated 414 calls for Macungie Ambulance in May, with 9 being in the borough. Steve 
Hill made a motion to accept the May Ambulance report, Ashlynn Rivera 2nd, and a unanimous vote followed. The 
motion passed 6-0. 
 
New Business: 
 

 
Ordinance No. 605- AN ORDINANCE PROVIDING ENTRY-LEVEL DRIVER TRAINING TO FULL-TIME 
MAINTENANCE EMPLOYEES REQUIRING SUCH TRAINING TO ACQUIRE A CLASS B COMMERCIAL 
DRIVER’S LICENSE, AND PROVIDING FOR THE EMPLOYEES TO REIMBURSE THE BOROUGH FOR 
THE COST OF THE TRAINING IF THEY FAIL TO OBTAIN THE LICENSE WITHIN ONE YEAR OR WHOSE 
EMPLOYMENT TERMINATES WITHIN TWO YEARS (EXCEPT IN LIMITED CIRCUMSTANCES); AND 
REVISING ALBURTIS CODIFIED ORDINANCES § 36-401 (RELATING TO FEES UNDER THE 
RESIDENTIAL RENTAL PROPERTY ORDINANCE) TO ADD A ONE HUNDRED FORTY DOLLAR ($140.00) 
FEE FOR AN INSPECTION FOLLOWING A FAILURE TO PROVIDE ACCESS TO THE INSPECTOR 
AT THE TIME THE INSPECTION WAS PREVIOUSLY SCHEDULED, AND TO MAKE CLARIFICATIONS. 
Steve Hill made a motion to adopt Ordinance No. 605. Michael Gerhart seconded the motion, and the motion passed 6-0.

Informational.

A motion to adjourn at 7:13 p.m. was made by Mehmet Birtek, 2nd by Michael Gerhart and a unanimous vote followed.
