New Business 
Eagle Scout Project Presentations 
• C
hurch Eagle Scout Project: fixing brick walkway, planting flowers, fixing bench at the memorial stone-
Sarah Lysinger. Scout from Scout Troop 519 in Allentown and a member of Venturing Crew 77 in
Alburtis, requested approval to complete an Eagle service project benefiting the local community. They
explained that their units meet at the church on Church Road and Ballock Ridge Church. For the
proposed project, the Scout plans to improve accessibility and enhance the appearance of the area beside
Lockridge Church by repaving the path with pavers to better accommodate individuals using canes or
wheelchairs, installing a bench, and creating flower beds for a pollinator garden. The addition of flowers
is intended to support local pollinators while beautifying the space. The Scout expressed enthusiasm for
making a positive impact and thanked the group for their time and consideration. Steve Hill made the
motion to approve the eagle scout project. motion was 2nd by Mehmet Birtek, and a unanimous vote
followed. The motion passed with a 6-0 vote.

 
 
• Welcome to Alburtis Signs: flower planting with retaining wall- Christian Wiesner- a Scout from 
Troop 72 and Crew 77 working toward the rank of Eagle, presented a proposed improvement project in 
Alburtis, which they described as a beautiful community. They explained that, in addition to an existing 
Eagle Project sign, there are two other signs in the area that could benefit from sprucing up. Their plan is 
to create a flower bed around one of these signs, incorporating a brick wall border, a modified H-stone 
base, layers of sand and dirt, and perennial flowers that will return each year. The Scout noted that they 
have already prepared a workbook and a simple sketch of the concept and can produce a more detailed 
version with dimensions, material quantities, and other specifics as needed. Mehmet Birtek made the 
motion to approve the eagle scout project. motion was 2nd by Chad Atkins, and a unanimous vote followed. The 
motion passed with a 6-0 vote.

Request to send Kyle Hausman to a CDL Certification Program 
Steve Nemeth mentioned that in 2022, a new law was created requiring all CDL applicants to complete a formal training 
course, noting that this is a new mandate for the borough and will require sending Kyle to attend the class. In 
conversations between members, there was agreement on establishing a minimum two-year employment commitment 
following completion of the CDL training so that, if the borough pays for the course, the employee remains for at least 
two years or is responsible for repayment. Dave and Steve agreed to work together on drafting this policy. Dave also 
noted that an ordinance is already being prepared for the next meeting to add a $140 fee for rental property owners who 
fail to appear for scheduled inspections and suggested that the CDL requirement could be incorporated into the same 
ordinance. Members expressed support for the two-year requirement, and Mike suggested adding a stipulation that if the 
employee fails the course, they would be responsible for the cost. John Aleszczyk made the motion to send Kyle Hausman 
to the CDL program and eventually Mark once he is eligible, motion 2nd by Michael Gerhart, and a unanimous vote 
followed. The motion passed with a 6-0 vote.
