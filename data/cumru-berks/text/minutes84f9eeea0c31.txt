D.) Capital Planning & Roadway Policy — presentation and discussion
Mr. Yost and Mr. McGuire have been working on a road improvement plan.
Mr. McGuire explained the 5-tier Pavement Condition Index (PCI) used to assess the roads.
Tier 1 = best condition

Tier 2 = good
Tier 3 = fair
Tier 4 = poor

Tier 5 = beyond serviceable life, needs total reconstruction

The majority of the Township roads fall in the Tier 3 and Tier 4 categories.

The condition is based on more than a score. Oher factors such as existing infrastructure,
winter paving and number of travelers using the road are taken into consideration.

Possible finance sources are Liquid Fuels money, grants, and low-interest loans.

He explained 7 methods of treatment and cost options for each.

The primary focus would be on long term preventative maintenance for Township roads not
state roads.

There was a discussion about aggregate or “oil and chip”.

Mr. Donnell asked about diminishing return with using Township crews. The Township
would not do “oil and chip”.

Mr. Ferrero commended Public Works for the recent road work completed by the Township
crews.

Mr. Miller explained the process for the Capital Planning & Roadway Policy. Assessments
would be done every year. A Capital Reserve Fund would be used for budgeting of projects.
Departments and the Manager would make recommendations to the Commissioners to avoid
personal bias.

The Board agreed by nodding heads for Atty. Frankowski to put this into a formal form.
