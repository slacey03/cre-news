6:00p.m. by Chairperson Barb Dietrich. Other members in attendance were Vice- Chairperson Bev

Comment #2 under the Stormwater section recommended revising the berm width to 8 ft unless
they plan on requesting a waiver. The applicant is requesting a waiver to allow the berm width to
be 5 ft. instead of the required 8 ft. Mr. Hoffman did not see any conflict with recommending this
waiver to the Board of Commissioners.

Comments #5 and #6 under the Stormwater section are standard. #5 requires that an
Operations and Management Agreement be signed and recorded. #6 requires a contribution be
made to the stormwater fund.

The Fire Department has not submitted any review comments yet.

The applicant requested a waiver from land development for this plan. Ms. Dietrich suggested
that a summary explanation of the plan should be submitted along with the waiver for
consideration by the Board of Commissioners. Mr. Leomporra agreed to submit this.

During discussion there was a question about the telecommunications line.

The Township would want the 2 properties to be annexed and this should be confirmed.

Mr. Hoffman stated that he was supportive of the requested waivers.

Ms. Dietrich asked if the Planning Commission had any further comments.
