Commissioner Miller requested Asst. Manager Kristin Yoder to discuss the proposal to
create a flat fee for refuse and recycling. Ms. Yoder discussed the administrative burden
associated with verifying household records for single and family dwellings. Furthermore
she noted there isn’t a clear correlation between household size and trash and recycling
production.

Solicitor Frankowski stated the Board would have to submit a new ordinance in
conjunction with the new refuse and recycling contract.

Commissioner Miller and President Donnell discussed the cost difference between a single
and family household.

The Board provided guidance to the Township Manager to conduct an analysis on the
refuse fees and incorporating a flat fee.

B.) Rental Property Program Development RFP — proposal results and discussion
Township Manager James reported that no proposals were submitted to the Township.

Commissioner Miller noted we received a proposal from Kraft Municipal Group.
Solicitor Frankowski clarified the Kraft proposal was designed to provide a framework
for what a rental property program could look like and not a proposal to provide rental
property services. Commissioner Miller further explained that Kraft, under their
current proposal, would review the rental ordinance, create a fee structure, and
operationalize the rental ordinance.

C.) Financial System Proposal

Commissioner Ferrero presented the results from the Township’s research into a new
financial software system. Two main contenders were Edmunds GovtTech, and
Intellitec/Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central. Highlights from the analysis
identified Edmunds GovTech as the best choice for the following reasons:

1. Lower cost
2. Comparable implementation timeline
3. Better integrated solutions and modules |

4. Comprehensive data conversion
5. Operational efficiency

Commissioner Miller inquired about the current cost of the financial system which
Township Manager James noted was approximately $30,000 annually.
