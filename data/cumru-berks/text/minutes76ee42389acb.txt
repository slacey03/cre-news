C.) Cherry Bekaert — 2025 Audit Presentation

Nick Bieber presented the 2025 audit results to the Board. The audit received a clean opinion
from independent auditors, which is the highest level that can be issued.

Some of the highlights were that revenues came in $153,000 over budget (1.5%) and the
expenses came in $90,000 under budget (0.8%). The General Fund was budgeted for $648,000
and the actual results were $411,000. The balance EOY was just under $3 million.

The recommended carryover is usually 20-30% of operating expenses. The present carryover
was 28%.

He reported some material weakness in the internal controls such as miscommunication and
delay in actual recording.

Mr. Bieber said that there were no major issues that needed to be reported.

Mr. Miller asked Mr. James and Ms. George to meet to discuss the internal controls and
report back to the Board.

D.) Atty. Beth Kohl of Barley/Snyder and Michael Hartman of Schlouch, Inc. addressed the
Board on behalf of Route 10 Realty to discuss the Route 10 TND land development sketch plan
that had been submitted to the Township.

The purpose of the discussion was to take the temperature of the Board and receive feedback
for the proposed TND sketch plan.

They presented a drawing to show the 170-acre tract with the proposed 88-acre TND land
development. The retatl/commercial development was shown along SR10 since its viability
would depend on pass by traffic.

There was discussion which included examples of small changes that could be made to the
plan.

Mr. Ferrero asked about the centrally located area of the TND for walkability and integration
of a retail area. How would the walking trails and sidewalks connect? He was told that the
problem with locating the retail in the center would be that people would have to travel through
the development to reach the retail area, which would make it more of a destination. It would be
more viable along the main road.

Ms. Rowe asked about the number of entrances. She was told that 1 full access and 2
emergency accesses were being proposed. The developers plan to talk with PennDOT about
access.

Chief Beane inquired about the possibility of connecting to Valley Stream Rd. and stated that
they would need 2 full accesses. He asked about the maintenance of the emergency accesses. He
said that the street dimensions were not adequate. He asked about the parking and fire hydrants.
Chief Beane reiterated that entrances are key and this is a lot of development for only 1 access.

This led to a discussion about a possible middle access which would be a right turn in and a
right turn out.

Mr. Donnell asked about the possible changes to the plan and said that he is happy for the
consideration of green space.. Mr. Hartman said that this is a living thing so it is changing. He
will probably submit another sketch plan based on the feedback.

Both the Police Department and Public Works had no comments at this time. Mr. Hartman
reported that he plans to talk about the sewer.

Mr. Rogosky stated that he will be coordinating directly with Mr. Hartman and he agreed to
looking at a second access.

There was a discussion about whether this development could be built profitably. Mr.
Hartman said that at this point his answer is yes, but we will know more as the project
progresses. The Schlouch team can weigh in and help to eliminate surprises.

Barbara Nagle, a resident of Pine Woods Ct., wanted to know about the effect this will have
on the school district. Mr. Donnell responded that they are working with the school district and
are thinking about the constraints of the school district. They have talked to Delta about how to
have development without overburdening the schools. Commercial and Industry are better for
the school district.

Atty. Kohl welcomed any comments or thoughts.

A.) Monthly Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Report ~ August 2026

incidents.

Mr. Ferrero asked if he could talk about the incident at the Mifflin high school football game.
Chief Winchester said that while this is location is in Shillington’s jurisdiction, Cumru officers

Shillington Borough takes the lead.

incident.

provide coverage if needed. Chief Winchester explained that sometimes shifts must be held later
or others must be called in early.
