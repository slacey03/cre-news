BEFORE THE BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
EAST ALLEN TOWNSHIP, NORTHAMPTON COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA

. IN RE CONDITIONAL USE APPLICATION OF: Dutch Holding Company
I. BACKGROUND

Dutch Holding Company (“Appellant”) owns property located at 6945 Weaversville Road,
East Allen Township, PA 18067, Northampton County Parcel ID: L5SW4 4 3 (the “Property”).
The Property is located in the Township’s Suburban Residential (SR) Zoning District. The
Property contains an existing mobile home that has been in place since at least 1975, when the
original permit for a 12’ x 56’ mobile home was issued on February 19, 1975. The mobile home
use constitutes a lawful nonconforming use of the Property, as mobile homes are not a permitted
use in the SR District under the current East Allen Township Zoning Ordinance (the “Ordinance”).

On March 10, 2026, Appellant submitted a zoning permit application to replace the existing
12’ x 56’ mobile home with a new mobile home measuring 13.3’ x 58’. On March 27, 2026, the
Township’s Zoning Officer denied the application pursuant to Section 94-33C(3)\(a) of the
Ordinance, which provides that nonconforming uses “shall not be altered, reconstructed, extended
or enlarged, except in accordance with the following provisions: Such alteration, reconstruction,
extension or enlargement shall be permitted only by conditional use from the Board of
Supervisors.” The Zoning Officer determined that because the proposed replacement mobile home
is slightly larger than the original: (13.3’ x 58’ versus 12’ x 56’), the replacement constitutes an
enlargement of an existing nonconforming use requiring conditional use approval. The reason for
the larger mobile home than what previously existed is simply due to the fact that mobile homes
are no longer made in the 12’ x 56’ size. Appellant now seeks conditional use approval from the
Board of Supervisors to permit the replacement of the existing mobile home with a newer mobile
home slightly larger in size.

Il. RELIEF REQUESTED

Appellant requests conditional use approval pursuant to Section 94-33C(3)(a)! of the
Ordinance to permit the replacement of the existing mobile home on the Property with a new
mobile home measuring 13.3’ x 58’. Section 94-33C(3)(a) provides that alteration, reconstruction,
extension, or enlargement of a nonconforming use “shall be permitted only by conditional use
from the Board of Supervisors.” The Appellant seeks to replace an, aging mobile home with a

A.’ The Proposed Conditional Use Is Consistent with the Spirit, Purposes, and

Intent of the Ordinance. (

The spirit and intent of Section 94-33C(3)(a) is to ensure that nonconforming uses do not

‘ substantially expand or intensify beyond their original scope. The requested conditional use

approval is consistent with this intent because the proposed replacement mobile home represents
a continuation—not an expansion—of the existing lawful nonconforming residential use. The
Property has been used for residential purposes via a mobile home since at least 1975, a period of
over fifty (50) years. The Appellant does not seek to intensify this use or to add additional dwelling
units; rather, the Appellant simply seeks to replace an aging structure with a modern equivalent.

The marginal increase in dimensions (1.3 feet wider and 2 feet longer) is de minimis and
does not represent any meaningful expansion of the nonconforming use. The total footprint
increase is approximately 102 square feet—from approximately 672 square feet to approximately
774 square feet. This modest increase is necessitated solely by changes in mobile home
manufacturing standards and is not an attempt to expand the use of the Property. The Appellant
would install a mobile home of identical or smaller dimensions to the original if such units were
available, but they are not. ‘

B. The Proposed ‘Conditional Use Will Not Be Detrimental to Health, Safety, or
Welfare.

Granting the requested conditional use approval will not be detrimental to the health, safety,
or welfare of the neighborhood or the Township. To the contrary, the replacement of an aging
mobile home with a hhew, code-compliant structure will enhance the health and safety of the
Property’s occupants and the surrounding community. Modern mobile homes are constructed to
current safety standards, including improved fire resistance and structural integrity. The
replacement will also improve the aesthetic appearance of the Property, benefiting both the
Appellant and neighboring property owners.

The Property is approximately one (1) acre in size (43,740 square feet) and can easily
accommodate the proposed replacement mobile home without any adverse impact on neighboring
properties. The slight increase in the mobile home’s footprint (approximately 102 square feet) fis
inconsequential on a lot of this size and will not create any issues with setbacks, coverage, or other
dimensional requirements. -

C. The Proposed Conditional Use Will Not Alter the Essential Character of the
Neighborhood.

The proposed conditional use will not alter the essential character of the neighborhood or
district. The Property has contained a mobile home for over fifty (50) years, and a mobile home
on this Property is part of the established character of the neighborhood. The replacement of one
mobile home with another mobile home of similar size maintains, rather than alters, the existing

character. The neighbors and community have long accepted the presence of a mobile home on
this Property, and the proposed replacement will be visually similar to what has existed for
decades. °

The purpose of the nonconforming use regulations is to allow lawful pre-existing uses to
continue while preventing substantial expansion. The Ordinance recognizes that it would be unjust
to require the immediate termination of uses that were lawful when established. Allowing the
Appellant to replace an aging mobile home with a modern equivalent is consistent with this
purpose and does not alter the character of the neighborhood in any meaningful way.

D. The Proposed Conditional Use Will Not Impair Adjacent Property.

The proposed conditional use will not substantially or permanently impair the appropriate
use or development of adjacent property. The mobile home will be located in essentially the same
position as the existing structure and will occupy a marginally larger footprint. Given the size of
the Property (approximately one acre), there is ample room for the replacement structure without
encroaching on setbacks or creating any impact on neighboring properties. Adjacent property
owners will see no meaningful change to their views, light, or air, and their ability to use and
develop their own properties will be completely unaffected.

E. The Requested Relief Is the Minimum Necessary.

The requested conditional use approval represents the minimum relief necessary to afford
the Appellant reasonable use of the Property. The Appellant is not seeking to expand the use of
the Property, add, additional structures, or intensify the residential use in any way. The Appellant
simply seeks to continue the lawful nonconforming residential use that has existed on the Property
for over fifty (50) years by replacing an aging mobile home with a modern equivalent.

Importantly, the Appellant has no alternative means to achieve this result. Mobile homes
of the dimensions originally permitted in 1975 (12’ x 56’) are no longer manufactured. Modern
building codes, safety standards, and manufacturing practices have resulted in slightly larger
minimum dimensions for new mobile homes. The proposed replacement (13.3’ x 58’) represents
the smallest available option that meets current manufacturing standards. The Appellant cannot
comply with the literal dimensions of the original permit because such compliance is physically
impossible in today’s marketplace.

Denying the requested conditional use approval would effectively prevent the Appellant
from maintaining a habitable residence on the Property. Such a result would constitute an
unnecessary hardship and would be contrary to the’spirit and intent of the Ordinance’s provisions
allowing for the continuation of lawful nonconforming uses. Granting the requested relief allows
the Appellant to continue the established residential use while making only the minimal
accommodation necessary to account for changes in mobile home. manufacturing standards.

grant conditional use approval to permit the replacement of the existing mobile home on the
Property with a new mobile home measuring 13.3’.x 58’. The.requested relief is consistent with
the spirit, purposes, and intent of the Ordinance; will not be detrimental to the health, safety, or
welfare of the neighborhood or Township; will not alter the essential character of the
neighborhood; will not impair adjacent property; and represents the minimum relief necessary to
afford the Appellant reasonable use of the Property.
