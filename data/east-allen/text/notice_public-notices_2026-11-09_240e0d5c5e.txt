PUBLIC NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN; NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING AND NOTICE 
OF A UCC BOARD OF APPEAL APPLICATION FOR EAST ALLEN TOWNSHIP  
 
PUBLIC NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the UCC Board of Appeals  of East Allen 
Township, Northampton County, Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, will hold a  public 
meeting on Monday, November 9, 2026,  at 7:00:00 PM  at the East Allen Township 
Municipal Building located at 5344 Nor -Bath Boulevard, Northampton, PA, 18067. The 
purpose of the meeting w ill be to conduct a public hearing for UCC Board of Appeals  
application from Luke Lichtenwalner, Agent on behalf of UGI Utilities, Inc. The UCC Board 
of Appeals  request is for the Appeal of Building Code Official or Code Official 
Determination under Chapter 87 -  Article III Uniform Construction Code specifically 34 
Pa. Code § 403.122 and specific criteria ordained in § 403.122. Appeals, Variances and 
Extensions of Time . The UGI Utilities, Inc  has filed an appeal maintaining that utility 
facilities are exempt from local regulation, pursuant to 53 P.S. Section 10619, and further 
the proposd noise-limiting structure is not a "building" for purposes of Section 10619. The 
subject property where the proposed UCC Board of Appeals  is located at 6659 Locust  
Road & 6699 Locust Road on tax parcel(s) L6-6-1A-0508 & L6-7-2-0508 at Locust Road 
near the intersection of Beth-Bath Pike (Route 512). 
 
A copy of the application can be found on www.eatwp.org or may be viewed in person at 
the East Allen Township Municipal Building, 5344 Nor-Bath Boulevard, Northampton, PA 
18067 during business hours, Monday through Friday 8:30 AM to 3:30 PM. Interested 
parties are encouraged to participate.
