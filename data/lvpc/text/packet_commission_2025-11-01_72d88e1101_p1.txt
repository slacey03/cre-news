Northampton and Carbon Counties in Pennsylvania and Warren County in New Jersey. Ms.

ACTION ITEM: Private Request #2 to Revise Official Act 537 Plan – 2951 Betz Court, 
Lowhill Township 
Ms. Myerov presented some background and context for this second private request review to 
revise the official sewage facilities plan of the Township. In response to the first request, the 
LVPC commented that expanding public utility infrastructure in this context could be detrimental 
to the Township and region.  Since the LVPC’s original review of the sewage facilities planning 
module in May 2024, the applicant and applicant’s engineer have provided a secondary 
absorption area for the proposed on-lot sewage disposal system for the site. While the provision 
of a tested secondary absorption area aligns with the FutureLV action to ‘protect the quality and 
quantity of surface water and groundwater’ (of Policy 3.2), the location of the secondary 
absorption area should not result in additional impacts to the existing natural resource features, 
including woodlands present in the area. The LVPC urges the Department of Environmental 
Protection to carefully consider the potential irreversible impacts on the environment and 
community, and to prioritize the preservation of rural areas for agriculture, conservation, and 
sustainable, low-impact development.   
Chair Amato called for a motion to accept the staff review.  
 
Commissioner Morgan made the motion, and Commissioner Ghai seconded the motion. Chair 
Amato asked for any comments or questions.  
 
Chair Amato called for affirmative votes to adopt the plan. The motion passed.
