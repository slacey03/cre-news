COURTESY OF THE FLOOR  
 
CHAIR’S REPORT 
Chair Amato introduced two new members of the LVPC staff, Dr. Subham Kharel and Faria Urmy. Both 
gave a brief overview of their background and LVPC projects they are looking forward to contributing to in 
their role.  
 
Chair Amato recognized the individuals who completed the required hours of training to receive the 
Pennsylvania Municipal Planning Education Institute (PMPEI) award for Certified Citizen Planner. Three 
individuals were recognized for their achievements in earning the Outstanding Planning Education 
Achievement Award.  
 
Chair Amato honored long-time City of Bethlehem Planning Director and LVPC Commissioner Darlene 
Heller, whose retirement was scheduled for January 31. Several Commissioners spoke kind words and 
congratulated Ms. Heller on her regional impact.

MINUTES 
Chair Amato stated that the minutes of the Thursday, December 19, 2024, LVPC meeting are in the 
meeting packet. Chair Amato asked for a motion to approve the minutes. Commissioner Glickman made 
a motion to approve the minutes and Commissioner Elbich seconded the motion.  
 
Chair Amato asked for any comments or questions. There were none. The motion passed. 
Commissioners Kelly, Klusaritz and Charles Elliott abstained.  
 
COMPREHENSIVE PLANNING COMMITTEE 
ACTION ITEM: Housing Supply and Attainability Strategy 
Ms. Seitz shared details about the upcoming Lehigh Valley Housing Supply and Attainability Strategy that 
will be released. The event will be a part of a three-part series with key stakeholders to address housing 
shortages across the Lehigh Valley.  
 
Chair Amato asked for any comments or questions. Commissioner Melnick asked about the effects on the 
local market, and it was discussed how the strategy can be used by municipalities as an informational 
tool.  
 
ACTION ITEM: Comprehensive Planning Committee Summary Sheet 
Comprehensive Planning Committee Chair Melnick briefed the Commission on the Comprehensive 
Committee Summary Sheet that featured four zoning ordinance and map amendments reviews. There 
were zoning map amendments in Bethlehem and Hanover Townships, a zoning ordinance amendment in 
Whitehall Township and a zoning ordinance and map update in Alburtis Borough.  
 
Chair Amato called for a motion to accept the summary sheet. Commissioner Morgan made the motion, 
and Commissioner Repasch seconded the motion. Chair Amato asked for any comments or questions. 
There were none.  
 
Chair Amato called for affirmative votes to accept the staff comments. The motion passed.  
 
ENVIRONMENT COMMITTEE  
INFORMATION ITEM: Greater Lehigh Valley Regional Climate Action Overview 
Ms. Myerov gave a brief synopsis of the ongoing Greater Lehigh Valley Regional Climate Action Plan. 
The Climate Pollution Reduction Grant (CPRG), a program funded by the U.S. Environmental Protection 
Agency (EPA), features four main parts. The third component of the series is in the works currently. The 
purpose of the plans and featured findings were shared.  
 
Chair Amato asked for any comments or questions. There was brief discussion about the federal funding 
freeze and it was emphasized that the LVPC would monitor the situation and report if anything drastically 
changes with the 2025 workplan.  
 
ACTION ITEM: Environment Committee Summary Sheet 
Commissioner Repasch briefed the Commission on Environment Committee’s business that included two 
Delaware River Basin Commission items involving wastewater treatment plant and discharge renewals in 
Lehigh Township and North Whitehall Township. There was one Agricultural Security Addition reviewed 
for Lower Nazareth Township.  
 
Chair Amato called for a motion to accept the summary sheet. Commissioner Molchany made the motion, 
and Commissioner Morgan seconded the motion. Chair Amato asked for any comments or questions. 
There were none.  
 
Chair Amato called for affirmative votes to accept the staff comments. The motion passed. Commissioner 
Klusaritz abstained from the item for North Whitehall Township.

NEW BUSINESS: 
PRESENTATION ITEM: Training - The Role of LVPC Commissioners 
Ms. Bradley gave an overview of the LVPC’s functions and legal authority under the Pennsylvania 
Municipal Planning Code. Ms. Seitz gave information about the Community Planning team’s responsibility 
and objectives during the land development review process. Mr. Dotta briefed the Commission on their 
roles relating to the letters and reiterated the importance of FutureLV: The Regional Plan. Chair Amato 
asked for any comments or questions. Commissioner Molchany asked for the Commission to be sent a 
copy of the training slides and LVPC staff confirmed it would be done.  
 
EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR AND MANAGING EDITOR’S REPORT:  
Ms. Bradley and Mr. Assad gave an overview of the latest Morning Call column which talked about the 
Regional Watershed Management Plan, the Greater Lehigh Valley Regional Climate Action Plan, a focus 
on the Lehigh and Northampton Transportation Authority (LANTA) Bus Rapid Transit, and the previously 
presented on Housing Supply and Attainability Strategy. Chair Amato asked for any comments or 
questions. There were none.

Chair Amato stated that the next LVPC meeting will be on Thursday, February 27 at 7 pm. Chair Amato
