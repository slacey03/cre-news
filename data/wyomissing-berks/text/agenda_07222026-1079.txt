MEMO To: Zoning Hearing Board       
FROM:  Jeff Hogg, Residential Zoning Officer 
TOPIC:  Scudder Residence – 1112 Fairview Avenue 
 
APPLICATION: 
Jonathan Scudder, owner of a property located at 1112 Fairview Ave. is requesting 
a Variance from Section 424, “Area, Yard and Height Regulations” of the Borough 
of Wyomissing Zoning Ordinance. 
COMMENTS: 
1. The property is located in the R-2 Suburban Residential District.  The property 
measures approximately 6534 sq ft and is non-conforming for the minimum 
lot size of 12,000 sq ft for a single-family detached dwelling in that Zoning 
District.  
2. The property has a frontage along Fairview and Terrace Avenues (parallel 
streets) and is bordered on both sides by other residential parcels. 
3. The existing dwelling is located approximately 4.1 feet from the eastern side 
property lines and approximately 11.89 feet from the western side property 
line, and is therefore considered non-conforming for the minimum side yard 
setback of 15’ per side required by Section 424 of the Zoning Ordinance. 
4. Currently, the property has 980 sq ft of building coverage, which is roughly 
15.1% of the total lot. This is conforming with the maximum permitted 25% 
allowed by Section 424.  
5. As an existing non-conforming building, Section 704.3 of the Zoning 
Ordinance only allows additions to be constructed where all portions of the 
addition are located within all applicable setbacks.  
6. The applicant proposes to construct a 23.25’ x 30’ addition to the rear of the 
existing home.  
7. The proposed addition would be setback 5.19’ from the eastern side property 
line and 12.31’ from the western side property line. These setbacks are 
greater than the existing home setbacks, but still less than the minimum 
required by Section 424.  
8. The proposed addition would create 698 sq ft of new building coverage 
which increases total building coverage to approximately 1678 sq ft or 25.9%.   
9. The applicant is seeking relief from Section 424 of the Zoning Ordinance to 
allow the addition to extend roughly 10’ and 3’ into the minimum side yard 
setback of 15’ per side and to exceed the maximum allowed building 
coverage of 25% by 0.9%.

10. Previous ZHB decisions of a similar nature have required relief from Section 
704.3 in addition to Section 424. Therefore, the applicant should amend the 
application at their hearing to request relief in this manner.  
11. The proposal, as submitted, appears to comply with all other requirements of 
the Borough Zoning Ordinance.  
12. Section 904 stipulates that Variances shall expire after 12 months if 
construction has not commenced, unless otherwise specified by the Zoning 
Hearing Board. If the Applicant shall need more time to commence 
construction, testimony of such should be provided.  
13. If relief is granted by the Zoning Hearing Board, the Applicant must submit 
applications for and obtain both Zoning and UCC Building Code permits prior 
to commencement of construction activities.  
 
HEARING: 
A hearing on the applicant’s request is scheduled for Wednesday, July 22, 2026, at 
3:00 PM, at Wyomissing Borough Hall.  Applicants and their representatives, if 
applicable, must participate in the hearing to present testimony and evidence.
