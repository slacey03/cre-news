PUBLIC HEARING NOTICE 
 
 
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Borough of Wyomissing Zoning Hearing 
Board, Berks County, Pennsylvania will hold a public hearing on Wednesday, October 7, 
2026 commencing at 3:00 p.m. at the Wyomissing Borough Hall, 22 Reading Blvd., 
Wyomissing PA for the purpose of considering a Zoning Application Requesting Zoning 
Relief by Kate Vandal and Fazal Vandal, (“Applicants”), with respect to property located 
at 1323 Clevland Avenue, Wyomissing PA 19610 (“Property ”), approximately .13 acres 
in the R1 - Low Density Residential District. The Applicants plan to construct a 2 story 
addition to the single family home on the Property and are requesting variances from 
Borough of Wyomissing Ordinances, as amended, Chapter 27, entitled “Zoning ”, as 
follows: (a) a 1.8% building coverage variance from  Section 404, entitled “Area, Yard and 
Height Regulations” to permit 26.8% building coverage where 25% is the permitted 
maximum building coverage ; (b) a 10.8’ side yard setback variance from  Section 404, 
entitled “Area, Yard and Height Regulations” to permit 4.29’ side yard building setback 
where the required side yard setback is 15’  and (c) a variance from Section 704, entitled 
“Nonconforming Buildings or Structures ”, Subsection 3  to permit an enlargement  and 
modification of a nonconforming building or structure , which encroaches rear and side 
yard setback requirements.  
 
A copy of the Borough of Wyomissing Zoning Ordinance, and Kate Vandal and 
Fazal Vandal’s Application, together with any plans and attachments, may be examined 
at the Wyomissing Borough Hall, 22 Reading Blvd., Wyomissing PA, during normal 
business hours. If you require special accommodations in order to attend the public 
hearing, please call th e Borough Hall at (610) 376- 7481. The Borough will make every
