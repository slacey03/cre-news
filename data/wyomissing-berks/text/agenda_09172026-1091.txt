MEMO To : Wyomissing Zoning Hearing Board 
FROM:  Nick Perilli, Commercial Zoning Officer 
TOPIC : Atonement Lutheran Church 
 
APPLICATION: 
Lutheran Church of Atonement is the applicant and owner of the property located at 5 
Wyomissing Boulevard, identified as Tax parcel ID Number 96-4396-08-79-9039. The subject 
property is located in the R1 and TC Zoning Districts. The applicant is proposing to subdivide 
the existing 2.36-acre property into two parcels. The application notes that the only proposed 
improvements at this time are to rehabilitate the existing community center to convert the 
space to a private residence. The applicant submitted a zoning map amendment request to the 
Borough to adjust the zoning district boundary of the R1 and TC boundaries. The map 
amendment was approved such that the new zoning district boundary follows the proposed 
subdivision line as shown on the included subdivision plan. The applicant appeared before the 
Board on February 25, 2026, with the same request, which the Board denied. 
 
The applicant is seeking a variance from Section 404 of the Borough’s Zoning Ordinance, to 
allow a maximum building coverage on Lot 1 of 28.6%, which is above the maximum building 
coverage permitted of 25%. The applicant is also seeking a variance from this section with 
respect to the maximum allowable paved area. The application proposes a paved area of 
41.8%, where 20% is allowed. 
 
ZONING ISSUES: 
1.  The property is located in the R-1 Low Density Residential and TC Town Center 
Residential/Office Zoning Districts.  
 
2.  The property is currently developed and consists of a place of worship, a community 
center, and associated parking areas. 
 
3.  The applicant is requesting a variance from Section 404 “Area, Yard and Height 
Regulations” to allow a building coverage of 28.6%, when 25% is the maximum 
allowed. The existing building coverage of the property before the proposed subdivision 
is 26.7%. 
 
4.  The applicant is requesting a variance from Section 404 “Area, Yard and Height 
Regulations” to allow a paved coverage of 41.8%, when 20% is the maximum allowed. 
The existing paved area of the property before the proposed subdivision is 36.9%.
