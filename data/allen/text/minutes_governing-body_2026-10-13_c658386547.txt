8. New Business:  
 
  A.  Quarry Hill Estates, Phase 2 B, Final Subdivision Plan: Mr. Joseph Zader introduced himself and 
acknowledged the attendance of the project owner and developer, Ed Deichmeister, and the project 
engineer, Lew Rauch of Lehigh Engineering Associates. He explained that while the project had been 
on hold for several years, meetings with staff and consultants have since progressed, allowing for 
recommendations from the Planning Commission. He noted that the required NPDES permit had been 
renewed and that the project is being sold in increments to  D.R. Horton, one of the largest national 
builders. Mr. Rauch further mentioned that the project was initially put in before the Township in 
2006. He generally discussed the first phase of the project and explained that they are now seeking 
completion of the second phase, which also includes a cul-de-sac extension off Howertown Road. He 
briefly noted that the proposed twin homes will be serviced by public water and public sewer. Mr. 
Rauch additionally explained that all necessary approvals, including those previously received in 
2006, have been obtained and that they are now seeking final approval to move forward with the next 
phase. Mr. Behler mentioned having requested confirmation that all comments listed in the Gilmore 
and Associates letter had been addressed. He expressed pleasure with the request, noting that no 
zoning or SALDO issues were being presented. Mr. Deichmeister noted that while all items of 
concern involved lengthy processes, they had all been addressed and successfully achieved. Mr. 
Behler made a motion to approve the Quarry Hill Estates Phase 2B Final Plan, as long as all 
comments from the Gilmore & Associates letter dated September 16th, 2026, were satisfied; seconded 
by Mr. Paul. On the motion, by roll call vote, all present Supervisors voted yes.  
 
B. Park Road Restoration - $17,986.00: Ms. Averbeck clarified that this item pertains to the road 
leading to the pocket park by Kreidersville Road, off Howertown Road, before the intersection with 
Indian Trail Road. She mentioned that a meeting with Mr. Hertz concluded with a quoted cost of 
$17,986.00 and noted that the Township would be responsible for purchasing the materials and 
providing a local dumpsite. Mr. Behler stated that the quoted amount was reasonable and agreed with 
the update, noting that the park is utilized. Mr. Behler made a motion to approve the amount of 
$17,986.00; seconded by Mr. Frack. On the motion, by roll call vote, all present Supervisors voted yes.  
 
C. Authorization to Advertise for Bids for Sale of Equipment: Mr. Behler questioned whether the 
equipment would be replaced or if the items being stored were no longer necessary. Mr. Hertz 
indicated that the majority of the items were paving equipment and noted an interest in discontinuing 
pavement work. He explained that contracting paving workers for future projects would be ideal for
