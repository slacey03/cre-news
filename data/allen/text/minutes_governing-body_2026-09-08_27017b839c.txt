D. Allen Township/Northampton Borough Municipal Authority Intergovernmental Billing & 
Collection Agreement for Sanitary Sewer – Discussion/Draft: Ms. Eckhart explained that for many years, 
Northampton Borough Municipal Authority (NBMA) had generated the combined sewer/water bill for all 
customers they serve within Allen Township without requesting compensation. She noted that this is 
changing with the addition of Willowbrook Farms, as the homes will subsequently be occupied and require 
billing. She explained that discussions were held regarding various items, including postage costs, which 
assisted with the creation of a basic agreement draft. The draft was intended to obtain consensus from the 
Board before returning with a refined document. Mr. Behler indicated that the request was reasonable and 
thanked NBMA for providing the service to the Township at no cost for many years, noting that it had been 
an immense benefit. He also stated that the invoices he receives from NBMA are very accurate and concise 
and questioned whether there would be additional payment processing options. Ms. Eckhart stated that 
NBMA held a meeting of its own where fees and other details were discussed and would eventually be 
presented to the Allen Township Board of Supervisors. She noted that while there had been a fluctuation in 
the rate, the Township would be covered because the rate from the previous cycle had not been lowered. Mr. 
Behler clarified that residents on public sewer or water are charged 115% of an invoice, with an additional

15% being a service fee to the Township for sewer equipment maintenance. He noted that because NBMA 
had not previously charged for billing services, the Township had not needed rate increases and would need 
to ensure any proposed charges are standard. Ms. Eckhart indicated that she would like to have a second 
meeting in September regarding the upcoming budget and noted that the rate for the present district would 
also need to be reviewed, as the values she had reviewed ranged from approximately 115% to 125%. Mr. 
Treadwell noted that the sewer from the project goes to Catasauqua, which invoices differently than 
Northampton, and the Township would need to ensure that the total sewer charges are adequately assessed to 
each unit so the total cost to Catasauqua, along with the maintenance requirement, is covered. Mr. Behler 
inquired about Certificates of Occupancy for the Willowbrook Farm homes, noting that the apartment 
buildings were constructed rapidly. Ms. Eckhart replied that the multi-family units would be occupied first, 
which would be beneficial because the invoices would be directed to the entity itself, similar to Willow 
Ridge Apartments. Regarding the pump station hookup, Ms. Eckhart explained that Gilmore and Associates 
would review the designs and DEP-required permitting, as there may be specific guidelines that need to be 
met. Mr. Treadwell advised the Board that as long as everyone is agreeable with the concept, he and Ms. 
Eckhart would work out the details and return with the matter for further discussion.

November 17, 2026) (Tax Map Parcel L4-3-13) (Two Lot Subdivision): Mr. Brad Rock of Lehigh 
Engineering, representing the Bohun Subdivision, explained that the property is located along 
Kreidersville Road at the intersection of Lappawinzo Drive and is connected to the Lappawinzo Fish 
& Game Club. He recalled previous subdivision plans that included five lots with waivers for lot 
width-to-depth, noting that the waiver remains in place. Mr. Rock explained that they are now 
requesting a four-lot subdivision, with an existing house on one property. He indicated that they are 
seeking approval of a preliminary plan for the four lots and final approval for the two-lot Phase I

that Mr. Bohun was interested in recouping investments made on the property, as the home was

D. Township-Based Nonprofit Organization Use – Fee Policy for Howertown Park: Ms. Eckhart 
explained that the Ordinance currently includes a fee and deposit for certain groups using Howertown 
Park. She requested the Board’s input and suggested that only a refundable deposit be required, with 
the deposit returned as long as the facility has no damages. She explained that following the agreement 
created in August 2026, this discussion would be the next step. Mr. Behler questioned the definition of 
Township-based nonprofit organizations, noting the organizations he could recall were Bath-Allen and 
Tri-Boro. Ms. Eckhart referred to the resolution and explained that Type 1 would be the Allen 
Township Youth Association, while Type II would include other nonprofit groups focused on youth 
activities. Mr. Paul questioned whether there was a fee for outside organizations, and Ms. Eckhart 
confirmed that all organizations are charged a fee with the exception of the Bath-Allen Youth 
Association. Mr. Behler suggested that the Township not withhold deposits received from Township-
based organizations involving Township resident youth but agreed that a sizeable deposit should be 
required as an incentive to prevent damage to the fieldhouse. Ms. Eckhart confirmed that the 
Township charges a fee, with the exception of the Bath-Allen Youth Association. Mr. Treadwell 
clarified that the Township would charge everyone a deposit instead of a fee, unless the Phillies 
Baseball team wanted to practice at the park, in which they would be charged a fee. Mr. Behler 
reiterated that other organizations interested in renting a pavilion or similar facility would be required 
to pay the applicable fee. Ms. Eckhart explained that although the agreement does not require names to 
be listed, the Township requests information regarding the percentage of children who reside in the 
Township within each group for statistical purposes. The Board discussed the deposit amount and 
ultimately agreed on $400.00. Mr. Behler made a motion to be revised in the fee policy for 
Howertown Park, specifically for Township based non-profit organizations to be charged a $400.00 
deposit for the year that would be refunded as long as all expectations and rules within the agreement 
signed are met; seconded by Mr. Sculley. On the motion, by roll call vote, all present Supervisors 
voted yes.

Public Comment Policy and Procedures- 
 
 
1. A period for public comment will be held at the beginning of the meeting and at the conclusion of all agenda 
business items. Any public comments or questions shall be reserved until time on the agenda. Public comments 
and questions will not be permitted during the course of the Board’s/Commission’s business items. 
 
2. Individuals who speak must give their name, address and municipality prior to speaking. 
 
3. Time limit on length of public comment or presentation will be three (3) minutes per person, per meeting. 
 
4. A speaker will only be granted one three (3) minute extension, if given, for a maximum speaking time of six (6) 
minutes, at the discretion of the Chairman. 
 
5. Comments/questions shall be directed to the Board/Commission members only. 
 
6. Procedures will be in place to maintain proper decorum for the hearing. Public participation will be allowed 
but the Board will ensure respect for all citizens and maintain order. Personal attacks and outbursts will be ruled 
out of order. The Chairman of the Board or Commission may, within this discretion, rule out of order 
scandalous, impertinent, and redundant comment or any comment the discernible purpose of which is to disrupt 
or prevent the conduct of the business of the meeting. 
 
7. Individuals with lengthy written statements may submit them for the record and provide a verbal summary of 
three (3) minutes or less. Interested persons may email public comments via email, sent to 
manager@allentownship.org until 3:00pm local time the day of each meeting or by contacting the Township 
office. Public comment received via email will be read at the outset of the meeting. If you require an auxiliary 
aid, service or other accommodation, please contact the Allen Township offices in advance. In addition, an audio 
recording of the meeting will be posted to the Township website: www.allentownship.org within 48 hours of the 
meeting.
