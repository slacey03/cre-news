Budget Work Session Meeting Minutes   
  
A budget work session of the Forks Township Board of Supervisors was held on Monday, September 28, 
2026, at the Forks Township Municipal Building, Easton, PA, and was called to order by Chairman Turner 
at 5:30 pm.   
 
Board Members: 
Dean Turner, Chairman  
Amy Blum, Vice-Chairperson 
John O’Neil, Secretary/Treasurer  
Ty Corallo, Member – member 
Derek Hughes – member - absent 
 
IN ATTENDANCE: 
Donna M. Asure, Township Manager  
Kacee Stout, Director of Finance 
 
 
The board met with various departments and agencies to discuss their needs for the 2027 budget.   
 
Travis Fedor, township mechanic, was the first person to discuss the 2027 budget requests with the 
board.  The board questioned various capital purchases that have been requested and the mechanics 
thoughts on the need for replacement.  Mr. Fedor left the work session at 6:00pm. 
 
The board then met with Daniel Morgan, Director of Public Works.  The board discussed the liquid fuels 
program and the need for additional funding for the annual road program which is the program that is 
bid out, to conduct milling and paving of various roads.  The township has a little over 12 miles of roads.  
D. Morgan is attempting to come up with a schedule that can prioritize the roads that should be milled 
and overlayed and those which can be seal coated to provide additional years of use before pavin g is 
needed. 
The following items were discussed – 
 - Trash and recycling cans – we have gone through several hundred cans this year.  They have reached 
the age where they are breaking.  To date the township has spent $21,000 and will be requesting to go 
out to bid at the next meeting.  The request is $40,000 for this line item for 2027 
 - Sewer Camera Truck – this is partially funded by a grant in the amount of $275,000.00.  Total cost is 
$462,000.00.  This request is from the utility capital fund.   
 - Sewer Tap in Fund – additional work on a major line will increase this budget to just under $1 million 
dollars. 
 - The vehicle maintenance line is being requested to be increased due to the rising cost of parts and 
maintenance on all township vehicles and large equipment 
 - The backhoe is requested to be replaced.  It is a 2015 with 8000 hours on it.  They are starting to have 
some issues.  A replacement is approximately $187,000.00 
 
The board discussed leasing some of the items to be purchased with a buy out at the end.  
D. Morgan left the meeting at 6:56 pm. 
 
Representatives from Suburban EMS were present to discuss their budget request with the board.  
Suburban is requesting the board increase their current contribution of $45,000.00 to $120,000.00.
