Budget Work Session Meeting Minutes   
  
A budget work session of the Forks Township Board of Supervisors was held on Monday, November 
2, 2026, at the Forks Township Municipal Building, Easton, PA, and was called to order by Chairman 
Turner at 5:30 pm followed by the Pledge of Allegiance.   
 
Board Members: 
Dean Turner, Chairman  
Amy Blum, Vice-Chairperson  
John O’Neil, Secretary/Treasurer  
Ty Corallo, Member – member 
Derek Hughes – member  
 
IN ATTENDANCE: 
Donna M. Asure, Township Manager  
Kacee Stout, Director of Finance 
 
The board was informed that there will be a municipal luncheon hosted by Palmer Township on 
December 9, 2026.  If any member would like to attend, please advise, and D. Asure will RSVP on your 
behalf. 
 
The board asked how Comcast is handling issues with grass that are occurring now but if there is an 
problem it may not show up until spring.  The board was informed that Comcast has been stating that 
they will come back in the spring to repair any lawn damage that is happening at this time and unable to 
be fixed due to the weather changing. 
 
The purpose of this budget work shop was to review the final draft of the budget, make final decisions on 
purchases and a possible tax increase.  
 
The board discussed what paying off the current loan with Jim Thorpe National Bank, whose final 
payment would be due in May of 2027, would do to the budget.  Completing the payoff by November 
15th of this year and a real estate tax increase of .5 mils would have the budget using approximately 
$150,000.00 of reserves versus the original $2 million dollars of reserves at the start of the budget .   
 
The board discussed several open items to finalize the budget - 
- Tax Increase – the board decided that a .5 mil tax increase will be necessary to fund the cost 
increases in personnel and services 
- Pay JTNB loan early – the board stated this would make good financial sense and asked that it be 
placed on the November 5th agenda for action 
- Fire Truck purchase – after much discussion over several workshops it was decided not to budget for 
the purchase of a fire truck due to looking for alternative ways to fund the truck, the grants currently 
in the pipeline requesting funding, working with Stockertown on their submittal for the purchase of a 
fire truck, looking at quotes from other companies and whether trucks have to be replaced at twenty 
years.  D. Hughes stated he is not opposed to looking at increasing the fire tax in the future. 
- Fire allocation – remains in the general fund at $110,000.00 
- Fire Utility truck – this will remain in the budget but will not be purchased until the use of the diesel 
sewer camera truck can be vetted once the new camera truck is received 
- Grants – the contract will remain the same level as it is currently 
- Non-Uniform agreement – will remain at an increase of 3.5% for employees with negotiations on a 
new agreement to start in February 2027.

A. Blum asked when the new garbage contract would go into effect.  It will be September 2027 and be 
included in the October 2027 utility billing.  She asked if the board would consider a discount for seniors.  
There was discussion on how this would be handled, what information would be asked of the residents 
and how other townships handle this.  D. Asure suggested asking the current trash hauler if they would 
extend their current contract four months until the end of 2027 rather then September to be able to 
discuss during budget time options for providing this benefit to the seniors of Forks Township.  The board 
will be updated when information is received. 
 
K. Stout left the workshop at 6:34pm with the board adjourning into executive session and announcing 
they would not reconvene when done.
