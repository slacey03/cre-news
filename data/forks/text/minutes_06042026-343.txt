Discussion Items:  
Tall Grass on private property – 
The board spoke about the many complaints received about tall grass in the township.  S. McGlynn stated 
that door hangers with a letter have been put on properties with tall grass to give the owner an 
opportunity to correct the problem before an enforcement notice is sent.  A property maintenance 
ordinance may assist in providing more tools to zoning to combat some of these issues.  The biggest 
challenges are the vacant properties where no owners can be found to deal with the issues. 
A. Blum asked if it is possible for the front yard to be cut but not the back yard or is that still a violation?  
S. McGlynn stated it depends upon the property as some backyards do not affect the neighbors where 
others do. 
J. O’Neil proposed to search for a local landscaper to contract with to cut tall grass on private properties. 
T. Hughes – 367 DeMaria Dr – asked about the length of time someone has to cut their tall grass?  The 
notice provides for a response within 30 days. 
 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by D. Hughes, for Donna Asure to send out an RFP for grass cutting 
services on properties not being maintained.  Under discussion, the properties that would fall under this 
category would be provided by the zoning officer as ones they have sent notices to with no response ad 
we are aware they are vacant.  The township staff will work in the meantime to cut the grass on these 
properties and the solicitor will be asked to place a lien against the property.  All in Favor.  Motion 
carried.  
 
Hearings: 
605 Kuebler Conditional Use decision: 
D. Tuner reported that the board of supervisors had deliberated on the information received during the 
conditional use hearing for 605 Kuebler and he would entertain a motion outlining the conditions for 
approval. 
D. Hughes made a motion, seconded by A. Blum to grant the application of Industrial VI Enterprises, LLC 
for 605 Kuebler Road with the following conditions –  
1. Applicant shall provide a supplemental Traffic Impact Study of the intersection of Kesslersville 
Road and Uhler Road.  In addition, the Applicant shall provide a right hand turn analysis for 
tractor trailers demonstrating legal tractor trailer turns from Kuebler Road to Kesslersville Road. 
2. A new parking analysis shall be submitted to the Township for review and approval prior to any 
tenancy of the subject property.  If it is determined by the Township that additional off-street 
parking spaces, including tractor trailer parking spaces, are required to meet the parking demand 
for such tenant or user, the additional parking areas shall be completed prior to tenancy.

Presentations: 
The Courts at Newlins – sketch plan 
Joel Weiner, Attorney, Bill Erdman, Keystone Engineer and Paul McNamara presented a sketch plan for 
the Courts at Newlins which is for two buildings of twin homes off of Newlins Road, Kesslersville Road 
and Sullivan Trail.  The original plans were presented in 2005 for this property, which presents a 
developmental challenge due to the flood plain which encompasses a majority of the center of the 
property. 
Tim Danahey-535 Paxinosa asked if it would be possible to build a bike path and/or pedestrian path in 
the floodplain.  It was explained that building in the floodplain would be prohibited. 
Robert Parella-3089 Cory Terrace expressed concerns about the limited parking, the entrances and exits 
of the community and the road extending Cory Terrace. 
Discussion also included whether an entrance onto Sullivan Trail was possible, the dangerous intersection 
that is currently Newlins Road and Sullivan Trail, the turning radius of the fire trucks and public works 
trucks and the flooding known to happen on this property and in this area. 
The developer thanked the board and will discuss the comments received.
