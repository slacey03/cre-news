EC District uses

Chairperson Klapatch created and distributed a zoning use table to assist the Planning Commission in
this discussion and explained that the Board of Supervisors would like them to review these districts
and provide them with recommendations. There was much discussion regarding the allowed uses in
these districts, and the use definitions. Mr. Klapatch summarized the uses they discussed of interest
which are solar, passenger terminals, essential services, auto repair shops, prisons, and the possibility
of merging the EC and EC2 districts. They also discussed making suggestions on updating or altering the
definitions of certain uses. The planning commission would like more specific direction from the Board
of Supervisors as to what they would like them to focus on reviewing, and they agreed that holding a
joint work session would be helpful. Chairperson Klapatch will reach out to them regarding this. Public
comment was received from Patrice Schwartzman of 523 Paxinosa Road East, she requested they
review all zoning district uses allowed and provided an example of a nail salon which is an allowed use
in the Open Space residential district where she resides. She also asked that they review the definitions
as some are outdated or too broad. Public comment was received from Amy Opitz of 409 Paxinosa
Road East who requested that they provide copies to the audience of the documents they are
reviewing and also requested they review the Open Space residential district and the allowed uses as
they may conflict with the existing character of their neighborhood. There being no further comments,
no action was taken.

5. Data Center Ordinance

Chairperson Klapatch stated that they have not received a response from the Township Solicitor to the
power and noise questions from their previous meeting. Mr. Danahey stated that he provided the
Township Solicitor for her review and guidance, his conceptual draft, tying it to the Ratepayer
protection pledge regarding electrical use of these facilities. He also suggested they review the decibel
limit on noise, the ambient low level hum needs to be addressed, Lower Macungie has a good
ordinance which he will get copies of for their next meeting to discuss. Mr. Danahey asked audience
member Patrice Schwartzman if Lafayette College has an audiologist who can assist them. Patrice
Schwartzman will find out and let them know, in the meantime she has reference material she will
email the chairperson. Chairperson Klapatch stated they will wait to receive a response from the
Township Solicitor and can discuss this further at a future meeting.
