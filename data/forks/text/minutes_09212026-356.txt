Budget Work Session Meeting Minutes   
  
A budget work session of the Forks Township Board of Supervisors was held on Monday, September 21, 
2026, at the Forks Township Municipal Building, Easton, PA, and was called to order by Chairman Turner 
at 5:37 pm.   
 
Board Members: 
Dean Turner, Chairman  
Amy Blum, Vice-Chairperson 
John O’Neil, Secretary/Treasurer  
Ty Corallo, Member – absent 
Derek Hughes - member 
 
IN ATTENDANCE: 
Donna M. Asure, Township Manager  
Kacee Stout, Director of Finance 
 
 
The work session was used to discuss the overall 2027 budget, various department requests and the 
different funds.    
 
K. Stout explained to the board that the documents they received concerning the budget contains all the 
needs and wants from the various departments.  The largest funds are the General Fund and the General 
Capital Fund.   
 
Over the next several work sessions, the board will meet with all the departments from the township to 
discuss their requests.  We will then need to know from the board what capital, what personnel and what 
increases in line items they will consider placing in the 2027 budget.  We will also need to know if the 
board is considering a real estate or a fire tax increase for 2027.  To provide everything that everyone is 
requesting in the General Fund it will take $1.8 million dollars to balance.  The board asked what one mil 
brings in and the response is $625,000.00 based on the current assessment.  They discussed a part time 
human resource person and asked the township manager to provide ideas of how another employee 
could assist with her job. 
 
The board discussed going after every possible grant that is out there and what that entails.  We have 
several grants in the pipeline and must budget to front the grants if awarded.  They discussed the 
decrease in the cable franchise funding stream due to residents using streaming platforms rather than 
cable, they discussed police overtime and budgeting for grants in the capital fund. 
 
The board discussed many of the capital requests and will each request will be further explored when the 
board meets with the various departments? 
 
D. Turner spoke to the board about getting sponsors to build a venue on township property that could be 
used for functions that could eventually turn into a revenue source. 
 
There are still outstanding unknowns for the budget such as the cost of health insurance, the sewer 
authority billing and salaries.  
 
K. Stout left the work session at 6:53pm and the board adjourned into executive session for personnel 
issues and will not reconvene.
