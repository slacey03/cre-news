The Courts at Newlins Road — sketch plan
Newlins Road W., Easton, PA 18040

Paul A. McNemar, PE of Rettew appeared before the Planning Commission. He explained that they
presented a sketch plan to the Planning Commission on March 26, 2026 and appeared before the
Board of Supervisors on June 4, 2026 to obtain their feedback. He confirmed they are in receipt of the
Gilmore and Associates letter dated September 18, 2026. They have subsequently redesigned the plan
to address the feedback they received and are seeking the Planning Commissions comments regarding
this redesign before making a formal land development submission. They have addressed parking
concerns and extended Cory Terrace; they are aware that they must obtain floodplain permits for this
extension. There was discussion regarding sidewalks, the floodplain, steep slopes, parking, road width,
lighting at intersections for bus stop safety, stormwater facility design options, and recreation trail
connectivity. Public comment was received from Robert Parella of 3089 Cory Terrace who described
the stormwater history of this parcel, he provided photographs for Mr. McNemar to review and then
shared them with the Planning Commission. There being no further comments, no action was taken.

EC District uses — for discussion

Vice-Chairperson Menkowski stated that the Board of Supervisors have directed them to review the EC
zoning districts and provide them with recommendations if a use should be added or removed, he read
Purposes of the district §200-11.G Employment Center District which describes the intent. Mr. Heck
stated that he would like to receive clear direction from the Board of Supervisors as to what they
would like them to achieve. Mr. Konecke agreed and would like to come up with a plan on how to go
about this review at their next meeting and would like to find out from the Board of Supervisors what
their specific concerns are as it would be helpful so that they will be prepared to have an informed
discussion. Mr. Danahey agreed that they should come up with a process on how to go about this and
make the documents available to the public, Mr. Konecke replied that the ordinance is online and
available for public view. Donna Asure, Township Manager who was in attendance stated that she will
get clear direction from the Board of Supervisors. Public comment was received from Patrice
Schwartzman of 523 Paxinosa Road East, stated that it would be helpful if the Board of Supervisors
attend their next work session meeting to go through the EC District with them so that the Planning
Commission is aware of what they are asking of them and provide examples.
