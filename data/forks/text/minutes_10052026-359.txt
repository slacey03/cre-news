Budget Work Session Meeting Minutes   
  
A budget work session of the Forks Township Board of Supervisors was held on Monday, October 5, 
2026, at the Forks Township Municipal Building, Easton, PA, and was called to order by Chairman Turner 
at 5:30 pm.   
 
Board Members: 
Dean Turner, Chairman  
Amy Blum, Vice-Chairperson 
John O’Neil, Secretary/Treasurer  
Ty Corallo, Member – member 
Derek Hughes – member  
 
IN ATTENDANCE: 
Donna M. Asure, Township Manager  
Kacee Stout, Director of Finance 
 
The board met with members of the Forks Township fire company to discuss the requests they have 
made to the township which includes the following purchases - a new pumper fire truck, a chief’s vehicle, 
a pickup truck and a trailer for the cascade system.     
 
The board discussed information provided by Chief Michael Vinci on the need to purchase the new 
pumper, the timeline to do so, why refurbishing the apparatus is not an option, funding sources and 
growing demands on the fire company.   
 
Chief Vinci also recommended that the board start planning for a staffing plan for the future of the fire 
service and the need for possible incentives, such as paying members to staff the fire house during 
certain hours, forgiving real estate taxes, regionalization, and other incentives.  He also stated that a true 
capital plan needs to be developed for the purchases of all the aging apparatuses as well as consideration 
of a second fire station in the northern part of the township.   
 
The discussion included National Fire Protection Association standards which is what the fire company 
uses to define the need to replace a vehicle.  The fire company is opposed to refurbishment as is A. Blum 
as it takes the truck out of service for 6-12 months and does not include the latest technology. 
 
Discussion on funding included the use of the fire tax and allowing the fund to grow, with the argument 
being that it takes 40-56 months to receive a new truck.  The board asked whether the fire company 
would be willing to use any of its funds towards the purchase.  How do they get paid back, at what 
interest rate, and can the funds controlled by the fire company be used for that?  The Community Fire 
Company of Forks Township is a 501c3 which would not allow the purchase of the fire truck and whose 
mission for use of this fund is retention and recruitment purposes.  Their tax documents state they have 
almost $1 million but some of that is in capital assets.  The Fireman’s Relief Fund has about $1 million 
dollars and the fire company may be willing to assist the township through that fund if it is allowed.  They 
have already used this fund to buy equipment to outfit the current trucks and will do the same in the 
future. 
 
The fire company representatives and Kacee Stout left the workshop at 6:50pm with the board 
adjourning into executive session for personnel issues and will not be reconvening the workshop.
