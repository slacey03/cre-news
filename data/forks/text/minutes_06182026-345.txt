Hearings: (Ordinances) 
Ordinance 415 – Zoning Text Amendment – growth management 
D. Turner opened the hearing at 7:03 pm. 
Lisa Pereria, Township Solicitor, gave a brief overview of Ordinance 415.  She advised the ordinance had 
been properly advertised two times and copies were sent to the Forks Township Planning Commission 
and the Lehigh Valley Planning Commission.  Forks PC voted 4-1 to recommend approval.  The LVPC has 
provided a staff review for the ordinance. 
It was found during the review that the protection of woodlands as an environmentally sensitive area to 
be protected was accidentally omitted from the draft ordinance.  This was not intended and will be added 
back in. 
There was no public comment and no discussion from the board. 
 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by T. Corallo, to authorize the solicitor to advertise the amendment to 
the ordinance to add woodlands mistakenly deleted in the original draft and continue the hearing until 
July 2, 2026. All in favor.  Motion carried. 
 
Data Center Ordinance – Ordinance 416 – 
D. Turner opened the hearing at 7:09 pm. 
Lisa Pereria, Township Solicitor, gave a brief overview of Ordinance 416.  She advised the ordinance had 
been properly advertised two times and copies were sent to the Forks Township Planning Commission 
and the Lehigh Valley Planning Commission.  Forks PC voted 4-1 to recommend approval.  The LVPC has 
provided a staff review for the ordinance. 
During the hearing there was testimony that the decommissioning language in the ordinance is not what 
was agreed to by the planning commission.  The compromise reached by the PC is not reflected in the 
ordinance.  The board was provided with the minutes from the PC meeting where the ordinance language 
was drafted and the ordinance reflects, word for word the language read into the minutes by the PC and 
voted on.  Testimony also was heard as to not allowing data centers at all.  It was explained that the law  
requires the township must allow for every use somewhere in the township.  Residents expressed 
concerns about the increased water and power usage as well as the air pollution that would be 
generated. 
 
D. Turner closed the hearing at 7:27 pm. 
 
T. Corallo made a motion, seconded by A. Blum, to adopt Ordinance 416, an ordinance amending the 
regulations for Data Centers. Under discussion, the board stated this should go back to the planning 
commission for clarification.  Roll Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, yes; 
D. Turner, yes. Motion carried.
