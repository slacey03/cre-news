Ms. Pereira stated the ordinance will amend Chapter 49, which governs the board of supervisors.  It was 
advertised as required in the Morning Call.  Two sections will be amended, and one will be repealed.  The 
first section deals with supervisors’ compensation.  Instead of changing the ordinance each time the 
legislature updates the second class township code, this change will document that the supervisors will 
be paid based on the second class township code as amended by the legislature from time to time.  The 
second change will amend the prohibition of supervisors working for the township, to prohibit full time 
employment by a current supervisor.  The section that will be repealed is a moratorium of two years 
before a former supervisor can work for the township.   
 
D. Turner closed the hearing at 7:07 pm. 
 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by T. Corallo to adopt Ordinance 417, amending Chapter 49, Board of 
Supervisors, in the Forks Township Code of Ordinances. Roll Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. 
Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, yes; D. Turner, yes.  Motion carried.
