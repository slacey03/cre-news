Budget Work Session Meeting Minutes   
  
A budget work session of the Forks Township Board of Supervisors was held on Monday, October 19, 
2026, at the Forks Township Municipal Building, Easton, PA, and was called to order by Chairman Turner 
at 5:30 pm followed by the Pledge of Allegiance.   
 
Board Members: 
Dean Turner, Chairman  
Amy Blum, Vice-Chairperson  
John O’Neil, Secretary/Treasurer  
Ty Corallo, Member – member 
Derek Hughes – member  
 
IN ATTENDANCE: 
Donna M. Asure, Township Manager  
Kacee Stout, Director of Finance 
 
The purpose of this budget work shop was to discuss the entire 2027 draft budget and all requests from 
each department which make up the draft budget.  If everything requested for 2027 is approved the 
budget will need to use over $2 million dollars of the township’s reserve funds.   
 
The board took action on a few items prior to budget discussion. 
 
IT service proposal: 
The board reviewed a proposal from Infradapt for continued IT support beginning January 1, 2027, 
through December 31, 2030.  There is a $1400/month increase in the monthly cost due to adding ten 
mobile data units in the police vehicles and the need for a new server at the police department next year. 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by A. Blum, to approve the proposal from Infradapt, LLC for IT 
support and services, beginning January 1, 2027 through December 31, 2030 in the amount of 
$9,382.16/month, plus $3000 as a one-time set up fee for new server at the police department.  No 
payment is due until January 2027. Roll Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. Hughes, yes; J. 
O’Neil, yes; D. Turner, yes. Motion carried. 
  
VOIP Phone services: 
The board reviewed a proposal from Infradapt to provide Voice over IP (VOIP) phone services.  The 
proposal will reduce the monthly phone charges by $1300/month. 
 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by T. Corallo, to approve the proposal from Infradapt to provide 
Voice Over IP phones services, effective December 1, 2026, in the amount of $1465.40/month, plus a 
$2,800 onetime set up fee.  Roll Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, 
yes; D. Turner, yes. Motion carried. 
 
Vouchers payable: 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by D. Hughes, to approve the invoice to Infradapt in the amount 
of $4,265.40 for the set up and first month of VOIP phone service.  Roll Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. 
Corallo, yes; D. Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, yes; D. Turner, yes. Motion carried.

look at revenue and opportunities for growth –

- Zoning – permit and hearing fees 
- Police – will there be revenue from the motor safety unit? 
 
The board discussed the need for a real estate tax increase which has not been done since 2017.  The 
cost of providing the services to the township residents is drastically increasing.  A ½ mill tax increase on 
the average home would be about $47/year, or $3.92/month. 
 
The board discussed the general fund and the budget requests that would affect that fund such as 
personnel requests.  The board had already received the cost for employee health care for 2027 which 
has increased by 19.5%.  D. Turner filled the board in on a meeting held with Stockertown Borough 
council President.  The borough has no funds that they could use to increase the annual payment to the 
township of $15,000 which has been in place since 2006 but they are willing to work with the township 
staff and grant writers to apply for a truck which if successful they would lease back to the township.  
 
There was discussion on raising the fire tax but with the needs of the general budget this increase would 
not help with those shortfalls.  J. O’Neil left the workshop at 6:30pm. 
 
The purchase of a fire truck looked at many possibilities and scenarios, such as when to order, grants 
already applied for, and internal loans.  The board was informed that with the recently applied for LSA 
grants, no funds could be expended on the purchase of a vehicle prior to the grant award announcement 
date next spring, or the grant would be considered void.  The board would like to see quotes from other 
companies for the fire truck the fire company is interested in purchasing. 
 
The board went through each capital fund, general, utility, recreation, sewer tap in and liquid fuels and 
discussed each request that was made for 2027.  Some requested items are going to have to be pushed 
out to the next year in order to balance the budget. 
 
The board would like additional information from Suburban EMS as was talked about in their budget 
workshop.  They also added $25,000 to the general fund for the payment of a multi-municipal comp plan 
that may move forward next year. 
 
The board will be provided with the additional information requested during the workshop as their next 
budget workshop on Monday, November 2, 2026. 
 
The board discussed the need for additional workshops to discuss the budget but believe the one 
remaining workshop should be enough to finalize the draft 2027 budget. 
 
Having no further business the board adjourned the workshop at 8:18pm.
