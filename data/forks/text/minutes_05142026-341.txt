NEW BUSINESS:

1. Hillwood Warehouse — Conditional Use
605 Kuebler Road, Easton, PA 18040

Shawn Gallagher of Buchannan Ingersoll & Rooney PC, Keith Ottes, PE, PP, CFM of Langan, AnnMarie
Vigilante of Langan, and Jason Ascher of Hillwood appeared before the Planning Commission. Mr. Gallagher
stated that they are in receipt of the Gilmore and Associates review letter dated May 7, 2026, the Zoning
Office review memo dated April 21, 2026 and they have also submitted response letters to Forks Township
regarding these two documents. Mr. Gallagher provided the Planning Commission with a slide presentation
and reviewed the property history explaining it was formerly a manufacturing plant which has been removed,
he described their proposed warehouse which would have loading docks, stormwater detention basin details,
parking plan details, landscaping, and a traffic study. After discussing this proposed plan at length, Mr. Hera
made a motion recommending the following conditions be considered by the Board of Supervisors.
