Public Comment: –  
Emilio Montesdeoca – 1201 Friendly Tavern Lane spoke to the board about issues he is having with the 
neighboring property.  He stated that there are homeless people living in cars on the property, there are 
rodents and trash on the property.  He stated that he has been to the zoning office and nothing has been 
done.  He wants enforcement.  This will be reported to the zoning officer. 
 
Gene Parziale, 1904 Quail Court spoke to the board about a letter he sent to the board before the August 
6th meeting, concerning the funding of the Forks Township fire company.  Below is a summary of the 
letter: 
• All funds allocated by the township or raised by the fire company should be exclusively used for 
the acquisition and maintenance of essential fire apparatus and lifesaving equipment  
• In light of fire tax consider reviewing, revising or canceling prior funding allocation that do not 
directly support front-line emergency readiness
