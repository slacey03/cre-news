Presentations:  
Growth Management ordinance – 
Andrew Schwartz of EPD spoke to the board about the changes to the possible zoning text amendment 
that was discussed the last time he was before the board.  The changes are for the farmland preservation 
district.  A development of single family detached homes would be able to have lot sizes which would go 
from the current 3 acres to 2 acres. There would be open space requirements.  Cluster housing would be 
permitted with a different open space requirement.  The ordinance would clean up terminology such as 
adding agricultural uses to the open space land, which will allow farming activity to continue to take place 
on this land that has very valuable prime agricultural soils.

Homes at Forks   - conditional/final approval        
The project was before the board tonight for consideration of conditional/final approval.  Representatives 
of the project were present to explain that items have been addressed, a recommendation to approve 
was received by the township planning commission and they would like to move forward. 
A representative of Habitat for Humanity spoke to the board about the criteria for ownership of the 
homes.  The program is grant funded with all money going back into the homes.  The homes are at 
market rate, so they will be sold for approximately $350,000.00.  The owner goes through a credit check 
with a certain credit score required, a debt to income level requirement and the requirement for each 
adult in the home to contribute 250 hours working on the home being built.  Habitat holds the mortgage 
and has the right of first refusal if someone moves out.  Chances of foreclosure average around 1%.  The 
owner is required to know the township ordinances and do everything every other home owner is 
expected to do. 
A representative for Thomas Dietzler spoke on his behalf that he is concerned about the 8 trees being 
planted as that is not enough, and the water and sanitary sewer easement, which is on the plan, but Mr. 
Dietzler believes is not correctly depicted. They would like the easement removed.  Mr. Lawler, Keystone 
Engineering, stated that the Elizabethtown gas easement is shown as per all the information found during 
the planning process.  He stated he is not comfortable removing from a plan an easement that is there. 
L. Pereira, solicitor for Forks Township stated that our ordinance requires easements to be shown.  
T. Corallo stated it sounds like a great program, but he again wanted his concerns about the exit onto 
Kesslersville Road noted.  It is a dangerous curve, people speed and the site distance is not good.  
 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by D. Hughes, to approve the request for preliminary/final conditional 
approval of the Homes at Forks residential development project contingent upon meeting all conditions in 
the final Gilmore & Associates review letter dated 3-4-26.  Roll Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. 
Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, yes; D. Turner, yes.  Motion carried
