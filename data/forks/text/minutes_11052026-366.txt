Department Reports:  
Fire –  
D. Turner congratulated Mr. Vinci on his next endeavor as he is leaving his position as Fire Chief. 
Mr. Vinci reported they have a very active group making calls. 
561 call YTD - Non-Emergency – 269 calls. 
They are working on two grants to replace the thermal imaging camera and the aging SCBA gear. 
 
Parks & Recreation –  
Rachel Sulzbach reported they had a very successful and well attended Big Rig Roundup on October 7th. 
Trunk or Treat – October 23 approximately 400 attendees. 
Preparations are underway for the Holiday Festival and Tree Lighting scheduled for December 4th. 
Grant funding for park improvement and beautification projects continue to be pursued. 
The facilities continue to experience significant utilization. 
 
 Public Works –  
Daniel Morgan stated the busy season is starting with leaf collection and will continue until December     
11, 2026. 
Road crew have been patching Newlins Rd East to make plowing safer. Work was done on Short Rd. on 
the shoulder. 
Public Works and Facilities have worked together to replace the sidewalks around the Police Station. 
Comcast is patching the roadways they have opened, and the board was assured that he is inspecting the 
work being done. 
The pump station underwent the annual cleaning and maintenance of the pumps.

Department heads were released from the meeting at 7:31 pm. 
 
Hearings –  
PMRS Ordinance – Ordinance 418 and amendments 
D. Turner opened the hearing at 7:32 pm.  
Lisa Pereira gave a brief overview of the Ordinance.  This is an amendment to the plan, for the employee 
contributions, which PMRS requires an ordinance.  She reported the ordinance had been properly 
advertised. 
Having no discussion or questions, D. Turner closed the hearing at 7:33 pm. 
 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by D. Hughes, to adopt Ordinance 418, amending the non-uniform 
defined benefit pension plan administered by the Pennsylvania Municipal Retirement System. Roll Call 
Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, yes; D. Turner, yes.  Motion carried. 
 
Amendments – 
J. O’Neil made a motion, seconded by T. Corallo, to approve the Plan Amendment with PMRS for the 
Forks Township Defined Benefit plan to reduce the required member contribution to 5% for 2027. Roll 
Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, yes; D. Turner, yes.  Motion carried.

Flock Camera Discussion: 
The township stated they are aware of the issues with the Flock Cameras which is why this is on the 
agenda this evening.  The township contracted for three cameras, two are installed and one is not.  The 
contract ends next April but the board would like them removed now. 
Gerd Viede – 800 Margaret - expressed his concern with having the cameras in the township and said he 
was very uncomfortable with them being installed.  He is happy seeing the board removing them. 
 
T. Corallo made a motion, seconded by A. Blum, to authorize the township manager to provide notice 
to the Flock Company that the township desires the two installed cameras to be removed within 60 
days and by notice terminating the agreement. Roll Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. 
Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, yes; D. Turner, yes.  Motion carried.
