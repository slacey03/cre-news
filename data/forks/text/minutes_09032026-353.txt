Meeting Minutes 
  
A work session of the Forks Township Board of Supervisors was held on Thursday, September 3, 2026, at 
the Forks Township Municipal Building, Easton, PA.  Chairman Turner opened the meeting at 7:01 p.m., 
followed by the Pledge of Allegiance.

Supervisors’ Comments:   
D. Turner –  
Mr. Turner reported that the board was informed by Chief Buckley that there have been a number of car 
thefts in the area and advised everyone to lock car doors and not leave keys or valuables in cars. 
Marilyn Hessinger – 1416 Derhammer attended the public meeting hosted by Lafayette College 
concerning the trail study that has been conducted and funded by a grant for the Trailway System 
between Lafayette College and the surrounding communities, including ways to get to Metzgar Field . Mrs. 
Hessinger said there was discussion on having a trail from College Hill to safely walk and bike to Park 
Plaza and other Easton and Forks Township areas.   
Mr. Turner stated there will be a Lehigh Valley Planning Commission meeting on Oct. 1 concerning data 
centers.    
 
A. Blum – none 
 
J. O’Neil – Absent  
 
T. Corallo – none

Referenced a public comment made at the last meeting concerning a sidewalk from the new apartment 
complex up Sullivan Trail to the Uhler Road intersection.  He would like the discussion to continue in the 
future about installing this sidewalk that he understands is currently not going to be constructed.    
 
Announcements:

issues. No decisions were made in executive session.

03 required by PennDOT.   Roll Call Vote: A. Blum, yes; T. Corallo, yes; D. Hughes, yes; J. O’Neil, absent;
