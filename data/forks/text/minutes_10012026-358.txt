Presentations: 
Village at Fox Crossings: 
The Engineers presented a plan change for Phase II of this development with the concept of eliminating 
the cut through of Braden Boulevard which would stop truck and high speed traffic in this residential 
area.  The plan change, which the township staff and professionals believe can be handled as a minor 
amendment to the final as built plan, will reduce impervious surface and have the internal roads of this 
development remain private to be maintained by the HOA.  A. Blum asked about the parking for the 
homes.  It was explained that some homes have garages, there is no on street parking, and the 
driveways are large enough for two cars.  After discussion with the board the supervisors are agreeable 
to the applicant moving forward with submitting land development plans based on their concept plan and 
approving the staff recommendations of a minor plan amendment which will be on the final plan.      
 
Victaulic – zoning text amendment and sketch plan: 
Robert Donchez, Chris Williams and Walt Sloyer representing Victaulic presented a sketch plan and 
possible text amendment to expand onto property adjacent to their current facility with the concept to 
use for research and development.

It was explained that this will be a research and development center for their product which is sold
