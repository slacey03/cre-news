NEW BUSINESS:

1. Riverview Estates phase VII — Final Land Development
Frost Hollow Road, Easton, PA 18040

Phil Malitsch of Tuskes Homes and Don Peters of Bohler Engineering appeared before the Planning
Commission, requesting acceptance today, so that the Township Engineer review may begin, he further
explained that this plan received Township approval in 2004 and they are modifying the lots from the previous
plan to accommodate architectural designs. Public comment was received from the following:

e Karen Roof of 2521 Napa Drive — she asked the applicant if their stormwater will use the basin in her
community, Mr. Malitsch confirmed they will not.

e Deborah Hunter of 901 Frost Hollow Road — advised the applicant that the land owner names of the
properties surrounding this development are not accurate. She also stated that the drainage currently
slopes towards Frost Hollow road and there is a drainage grate in front of her neighbor’s house that
gets blocked during storms. She expressed concerns regarding the one lane bridge, bus stops and the
increase in traffic, with approximately two hundred additional vehicles entering this development. Mr.
Malitsch stated that PennDOT has already performed their application review of potential hazards and
sight distance and they have received their permit from them.

e Joan Heywood of 905 Frost Hollow Road — expressed concerns regarding increased noise, traffic and
displaced wildlife.

e Dan Hunter of 901 Frost Hollow Road — asked for clarification on the acceptance procedure, Mr. Heck
explained that tonight they are here to accept the application and plans submitted to the Township,
which will begin the review process and the applicant will return before the planning commission for
further review and discussion.

e Patrick Lincoln of 1819 Meadow Lane Drive — asked for further explanation of the land development
application process, Mr. Heck provided a description of the review process. Mr. Lincoln recommended
the applicant send out notifications via regular mail instead of certified.

e David McManus of 2421 Napa Drive — asked the applicant to confirm that Tuskes is the applicant and
builder for this development, Mr. Malitsch confirmed they are the equitable owner, under agreement.
He also stated that in New Jersey the school buses would drive into the community to pick up the
children. Vice-Chairperson Menkowski explained that the bus stop locations are determined by the
School District.

e Brian Simet of 2445 Napa Drive — inquired about the mailing notification procedure, Mr. Heck
answered his question.

e Joseph Malham of 2453 Napa Drive — he is in receipt of his certified letter notification; he stated that
the mail carrier had many more in their possession for delivery.

Mr. Malitsch addressed the mailing concerns raised at this meeting and stated that they will review their
mailing list and resend a second notification certified and standard mail to all property owners within three
hundred feet of this development. Mr. Hera asked for clarification on who completed the checklist that was
submitted with the application, it was confirmed that the applicant completed the checklist. He stated that
they checked off that certified receipts were submitted confirming the property notification, in his opinion
they are not included, haven’t been received or executed in a proper manner. Mr. Heck made a motion to
accept this application, motion seconded by Mr. Danahey, Mr. Hera voted nay because some residents stated
they were not notified of this application prior to this meeting , motion carried 3-1-0.
