Presentations: 
Stormwater Utility Program -  
Lee Stinnett, Esq from Saltzman Hughes and Michael Callahan of HRG Engineering were present to 
discuss the latest court cases involving storm water fees and how the township might move forward with 
this program. 
The discussion involved the latest PA Supreme Court ruling brought against West Chester Borough by 
West Chester University.  Mr. Stinnett discussed the opinions entered by the Judges and how they might 
affect moving forward with a stormwater fee.  There are other ongoing or recently decided court cases 
that are being monitored and reviewed.  Either way the MS4 permit and requirements are mandated by 
the state and “requires an adequate funding mechanism”.  In other words an unfunded mandated by the 
state legislature.   
The board asked if they could proceed with the original concept of working with HRG and Salzman 
Hughes to set up an authority and put the program in place. 
Michael Callahan stated that HRG can once again get the process moving forward and within the next 
few weeks set up the kick off meeting that had been put on hold a few months ago. 
The board had approved moving forward with a Stormwater Utility Program at the March 5, 2026, 
meeting.  The board gave direction to the township manager to move forward with this program.

Discussion Items: 
Fire Company discussion: 
Mike Vinci, chief of the fire company, reported that year to date they are at 374 calls which puts them on 
a path to exceed the number of calls in 2025. 
He discussed the structure of the fire department, the fire company and the relief association and the 
role each plays in providing fire services to Forks Township and other municipalities, the number of 
apparatuses currently in use, the need to increase the number of apparatuses, the length of time it take 
from ordering a fire truck to delivery and the need to order a fire truck in the very near future and 
another one right behind that. 
The board thanked the fire company for presenting the information and talked about the need to keep 
open communication going forward.  Further discussion will take place at upcoming meetings and during 
the budget process.
