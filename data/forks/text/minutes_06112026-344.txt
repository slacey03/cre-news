1. Country Meadows - Conditional Use Hearing— recommendations to the Board of Supervisors

Thomas Ludgate, PE, of Ludgate Engineering Corporation and Carl W. Helman, Director of Building

Design of Country Meadows appeared before the Planning Commission. Mr. Ludgate confirmed they are in
receipt of the Gilmore and Associates, Inc., review letter dated June 4, 2026, and the Zoning Office memo
dated June 4, 2026. He went on to state that he submitted a response letter to the Township dated June 10,
2026, and they intend to comply with all comments in both letters. There was discussion pertaining to lighting,
stormwater basins, the access road and parking. Chairperson Klapatch stated that they had discussed sidewalk
installation when they appeared before them last year for land development, Mr. Helman explained that they

3. Zoning Ordinance text amendment — review and provide recommendation to Board of
Supervisors

Chairperson Klapatch explained that the Board of Supervisors are seeking their recommendation
regarding this amendment. Mr. Heck stated that he does not feel that the Planning Commission should make a
favorable recommendation for adoption of this amendment. He further explained his reasons for this are that
the ordinance was not relayed to the property owners located in the Farmland Protection district which has a
direct affect on the value of their land. Also in his opinion, this amendment does not match what is stated in
‘he comprehensive plan. Specifically, Mr. Heck referenced page 90, Chapter 7: Action Plan, Objective: 'Protect
agriculture, natural resources, and open space through the use of regulatory provisions,’ and the

corresponding Action Item: 'Explore opportunities for preservation of land through purchase of land and/or

sewer map does not go into the Farmland Protection zone. Mr. Klapatch disagrees with Mr. Heck’s statement
regarding the comprehensive plan as it states, “review and evaluate current ordinance provisions”, also this
amendment was publicly advertised, and they may attend the Planning meeting and Supervisors meeting next
week. Mr. Heck stated that it is not possible for a landowner in the Farmland Protection zone to read the
agenda item titled 'Zoning Ordinance Text Amendment — Review and Provide Recommendation to the Board
of Supervisors’ and reasonably understand that the Commission would be discussing a matter that may
jirectly affect the value of their land. Mr. Menkowski stated that they have discussed this proposed
amendment at length for the past few years. Mr. Danahey stated that what has been presented to them
provides landowners with additional options. After further discussion, and no public comment, a motion was
made by Mr. Konecke to recommend the denial of this anendment to the Board of Supervisors, no one
seconded this motion, motion failed. Mr. Menkowski made a motion to recommend approval of this
amendment to the Board of Supervisors, motion seconded by Mr. Hera. Mr. Konecke voted nay. Motion
carried 4-1-0.
