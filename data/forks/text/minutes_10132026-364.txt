Budget Work Session Meeting Minutes   
  
A budget work session of the Forks Township Board of Supervisors was held on Tuesday, October 13, 
2026, at the Forks Township Municipal Building, Easton, PA, and was called to order by Chairman Turner 
at 5:30 pm followed by the Pledge of Allegiance.   
 
Board Members: 
Dean Turner, Chairman

Derek Hughes – member  
 
IN ATTENDANCE: 
Donna M. Asure, Township Manager  
Kacee Stout, Director of Finance 
 
The purpose of this budget work shop was to meet with various departments to discuss their needs for 
the 2027 budget. 
 
Parks & Recreation – Rachel Sulzbach, Director, was present to discuss the requests she had put into the 
draft budget.  The following were highlights of the discussion – 
- Revenues are budgeted as slightly higher as seems to be the trend.  Many programs being weather 
dependent 
- Expenditures are slightly increased due to required training for staff to keep their pesticide and 
arborist licenses, as well as increased travel for the Director and Manager to attend an out of state 
conference. 
- Capital discussion took place going over the equipment needed to maintain the community center 
and the park 
- The pole barn was discussed and budgeted at $125,000.  Vendors have been contacting and 
awaiting responses. 
R. Sulzbach left the workshop at 5:55pm.  The board would like to explore raising the rates of the rentals 
to outside organizations.  Information will be gathered and reported back to the board.  
 
Prior to the arrival of M. Border, the board discussed next week’s budget workshop where they would like 
to see various scenarios of funding, the impact of the expiration of the trash contract, the fire company’s 
request for a new fire truck and the various needs and requests by township departments.  
understanding that next year the trash contract is expiring and the reality of a major increase  
 
Facilities – Matt Border, Director, arrived at 6:06pm to discuss the facilities budget with the board.  
- The board reviewed the aging infrastructure of the buildings’ various components such as the HVAC 
systems, public rooms at the community center, the roofs, etc.   
- Discussion took place surrounding the increases to the general fund due to rising costs of electricity 
and fuel.   
- Currently there is a process in place to budget money in various line items to “save” for the need for 
large purchases.  With the aging of the HVAC units though they appear to be working well at this 
time, units must start to be replaced annually to avoid emergency replacements.    
- Facilities is a large budget within the capital fund but necessary to keep the buildings running 
efficiently. 
M. Border left the workshop at 6:55pm.

Police – Chief Buckley was present to discuss the police budget with the board.   
- He spoke to the board concerning increases in the budgets for training, ammunition and contracted 
services.  There are new MPOETC requirements for fire arms training, there must be training on less 
lethal weapons and more which will drastically increase the ammunition line.  Chief Buckley believes 
we can save money on ammunition by shopping around as there are less expensive places to 
purchase what we need then where we are currently buying from. 
- There is a new vehicle in the budget and the upfitting, though budgeted at $20,000 it is believed 
that it will come in much less than that.  The vehicle being retired will be used for defensive driving 
training and then sold. 
- Upgrades to the required P25 radios were discussed with the township having recently received a 
grant of $200,000 for the purchase.  Total should be about $300,00.00. 
- The department is in need of Body Worn Cameras as the current BWC’s are becoming inoperable .  
Quotes are being received to include car cameras. 
- Personnel requests were discussed with the request to add a full time administrative assistant, two 
officers in order to start a traffic unit and a Deputy Chief.  The traffic unit will require two officers 
attending Motor Carrier School in order to become certified but will then be able to conduct truck 
inspections throughout the township. 
- Capital items were discussed such as a new range target carrier system for the firearms range, 
drones, tasers and bullet proof vests. 
 
The board entered into executive session at 8:01pm with Chief Buckley and announced they will not 
reconvene.
