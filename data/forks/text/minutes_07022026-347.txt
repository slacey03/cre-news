Hiring and Swearing in of Police Officers: 
Chairman Turner stated that this evening we have the pleasure of welcoming two new police officers to 
the Forks Township Police Department.  Mr. Turner introduced Craig Buckley, newly hired Chief of Police

welcome to both the new officers, the officers in attendance and all those present.

Robert Parella- 3089 Cory Terrace spoke to the board about the roundabout being installed along Sullivan

supervisors is asking them to do.  D. Turner explained that the board of supervisors has been asking that

uses are still logical to be in those districts.  To do that they would need to see what the intent of each

Discussion Items: 
FTAA – overtime charges for using police: 
Raphael Margolin the president of the Forks Township Athletic Association spoke to the board about 
paying by the FTAA for the use of police officers as security.  The football commissioner had previously  
made a decision, due to an incident last year, to require security at all football games.  The township 
charges $100/hour for the use of township police officers who would be on overtime.  The commissioner 
is reviewing his prior decision and now it is more of a recommendation.  J. O’Neil asked how many 
children that are in the league are Forks Township residents.  The board was told that approximately 
75% of those involved are not township residents.  The board asked why the township taxpayers should 
front the bill for poor behavior.  T. Corallo asked why the registration fee could not be raised so fund 
security at the games if that is what is needed.  Mr. Margolin asked that perhaps the officers on duty 
could drive by a little more often during the games.  The consensus of the board was that the fee 
charged, per our fee schedule, was not going to be lowered or waived.
