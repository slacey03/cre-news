Board Reports: 
Planning Commission -  
Matt Menkowski – 
The planning commission at its last meeting had the developer for Riverview Phase 7 present for 
acceptance of their plan.  There was quite a discussion on the property owners being notified of the 
project.  The developer nor the township can be held liable for the United States Postal Service not 
delivering mail.  It was stated that the applicant had provided proof of mailing in their submission packet.   
D. Turner stated that the Ordinance notifications may need to be updated for Developers. 
Mr. Menkowski also stated that the planning commission was asked to review the uses in the various EC 
Districts, but it was not on the draft agenda and then it was on the final agenda. 
 
Tim Danahey –  
Spoke to the board about providing the members in the audience the same documents that the members 
of the commission are looking at.  It would be helpful if they had Chapter 200, which is the entire zoning 
ordinance, as well as the chart put together by Anthony Klapatch of the EC district and uses. 
D. Turner stated that it would not be cost effective to make copies for the audience.  The documents 
change all the time and it would be hard to know who had what version of that document.  The TV’s can 
be used to show the documents to the audience which is why they were installed in the first place.   
D. Turner also attempted to explain that the board of supervisors have asked the planning commission to 
look at the intent of each EC District and then look at the uses listed in those districts.  Do the uses still 
match the intent?   Should some be removed, or added only in certain EC Districts or are there uses that 
are not listed that would make sense to be in that district?  When the township created the Industrial 
District (ID) a couple of years ago, many uses were removed and put only in the ID, does that still make 
sense or should some be moved back to one or all of the EC Districts?
