NEW BUSINESS:

1. Kanakarat Realty Lot Line Adjustment
1309, 1313, 1315 Richmond Road, Easton, PA 18040 (12/09/26)

Mr. Peter Terry of Benchmark Engineering and Mr. Jay Johnson, property owner, appeared before the
Planning Commission. Mr. Terry provided the Planning Commission with a brief description of their proposed
lot line adjustment explaining that one of the property lines goes through an existing structure and they would
like to correct that. He further explained that there is an existing auto repair garage and existing residential
structures, their plan would separate the auto repair business from the residential property. Mr. Danahey
asked the applicant what their intent is for this property, such as further development. Mr. Terry explained
this is to remedy the current property line issues. Mr. Danahey asked him to confirm that this will be two lots,
not three, Mr. Terry confirmed that is correct. There being no public comment, Mr. McIntosh made a motion
to accept this application, Mr. Konecke seconded the motion, motion carried 5-0-0.

DISCUSSION ITEMS:

1. EC District uses — there was discussion regarding waiting to review these uses until the Board of
Supervisors appoints a new member to fill their vacancy and they appoint a new chairperson. The Planning
Commission was in unanimous agreement to postpone their review. Mr. McIntosh made a motion to table this
discussion, motion seconded by Mr. Knoecke, motion carried 5-0-0.

NON-AGENDA ITEMS:

Mr. Konecke asked what the procedure is now that the chairperson has resigned and they have a member
vacancy as well on the Planning Commission. There was discussion on when and how to choose a new
chairperson. Mr. Menkowski explained that the Board of Supervisors are currently accepting applications and
interviewing candidates for the vacancy. Mr. Konecke asked if they have a written procedure, Mr. Heck shared
the by-laws with him from 2022. Mr. Mcintosh stated that he would rather wait until the Board of Supervisors
appoint a new Planning Commission member before appointing a new chairperson. Mr. Konecke agreed with
him and stated that he would like to make a recommendation to the Board of Supervisors that they appoint
Mr. Heck as a full voting member. Mr. McIntosh agreed. Mr. Menkowski agreed that they should wait to have
five voting members to reorganize. Mr. Danahey stated that they serve at the pleasure of the Board of
Supervisors, he is reluctant to vote for a new chairperson, as they do not know what is being considered. Mr.
McIntosh made a motion to table electing a chairperson, there being no public comments, motion seconded
by Mr. Konecke, the motion carried 5-0-0.
