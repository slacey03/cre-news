My goal in this process, my goal trajectory in this process has been to create some sort of Ordinance for public oversight of surveillance technologies, and then to have that reference, a surveillance use policy, which would live, would be a living document within the administrative handbook, outlining the criteria and guardrails that departments within the city would adhere to related to anything with surveillance.
