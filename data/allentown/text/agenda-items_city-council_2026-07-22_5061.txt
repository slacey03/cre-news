Ms. Vicky Kistler stated members of City Council, the administration supports good Public Health Legislation and they are supporters of Fair Labor Practices.  The city of Allentown administration does not support Bill 54 - 2026 which would amend the city's health code to require all businesses within the city limits to provide Paid Sick Leave to employees.  She stated that as you are aware, this ordinance was brought to you as a Charter Citizen Initiative Referendum.  It was not proposed by the city of Allentown's city administration, Allentown's City Council, any administrative board, commission or third party agency that functions under the health bureau or Lehigh County.  As such, there is no research, investigations, studies or data collected by the city or the city affiliated public health organization that shows the ordinance should protect the public health or assist in disease prevention and control.  There are currently over 20,500 active in city license businesses that the Health Bureau would be required to monitor for compliance and enforcement.  The city does not currently have staffing or administrative processes in place to comply with this ordinance. Implementation of this ordinance would require significant investment and additional staffing which may result in doubling or tripling the city's business license fees and/or property tax increase.  The measures will be in addition to the cost of the businesses after this benefit.  She stated finally many of the people who live in Allentown, do not work in Allentown and many people who work in  Allentown live elsewhere.   Approximately, 80 percent of our residents in the workforce, leave the city for their employment and conversely, approximately 80 percent of the jobs in the city are filled by commuters that live outside the city.  This ordinance will protect the public health or assist in disease prevention and control for this community. If compelled to implement the ordinance, they believe it will put the city at a competitive disadvantage with neighboring communities and expanding new small business opportunities in the city.  The administration believes that it would be more appropriate for this ordinance to be implemented and managed at a county or state level or government.  Thank you.\par
