City of Allentown - City Council - 2026-11-18 5:30 PM

Roll Call
ADJOURN
   Council meetings are held on the first and third Wednesday of each month beginning at 6:30 pm in Council Chambers.  For copies of the agenda or meeting announcements, please visit our website at www.allentownpa.gov  or contact the Clerk at Michael.Hanlon@allentownpa.gov to receive an email notice of the meetings.
15-8751 Bill 85
Amending the 2026 Capital Fund Budget to provide for an increase of Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($200,000.00) to fund a completion of a feasibility study evaluating the conversion of select one-way streets to two-way streets. This grant is fully reimbursable and once the project has been completed, the City will be reimbursed.
Budget Review
15-8788 Bill 95
Amending the 2026 Capital Fund Budget to provide for an increase of One Million Nine Hundred Thousand Dollars ($1,900,000.00) to fund the upgrade of the school zone flashers at Twenty-Six (26) locations throughout the city. This grant is funded through the Transportation Alternatives Set Aside Program (TASA).
15-8786 Bill 86
An Ordinance of the City of Allentown, County of Lehigh and Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, establishing and adopting the Solid Waste Enterprise Fund Budget for 2027 and amending Article 1131, Municipal Waste Storage, Collection and Disposal by maintaining the Solid Waste Fee at Seven Hundred Forty ($740) Dollars.
15-8783 Bill 88
An Ordinance of the City of Allentown, County of Lehigh, and Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, adopting the Liquid Fuels Fund Budget for 2027.

15-8787 Bill 90
An Ordinance of the City of Allentown, County of Lehigh and Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, establishing and adopting the Stormwater Fund Budget for 2027 and amending Article 393.15 – Stormwater Utility Fee by maintaining the Stormwater Utility Fee at Twenty ($20) Dollars per Five Hundred (500) square feet of impervious surface.
