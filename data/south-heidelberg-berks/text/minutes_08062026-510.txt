MUNICIPAL AUTHORITY OF THE TOWNSHIP OF
SOUTH HEIDELBERG, BERKS COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA
August 6, 2026

The Board of the Municipal Authority of the Township of South Heidelberg met for their general meeting at
the Municipal Building. The meeting was called to order by Mr. Gerhart, at 6:00pm. Mr. Gerhart then led
everyone in the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag. Mr. Gerhart announced that the meeting was being recorded
to ensure the accuracy of the taking of meeting minutes.

Present were Jon H. Gerhart, Thomas Schultz, Jeff Pawling , Ryan Beissel, Members; Andrew George of Kozloff
Stoudt, Authority Solicitor; Kevin M. Conrad, P.E. of Spotts, Stevens & McCoy, Inc., Authority Engineer; Kim
Auman, clerk; (missing: Kevin Eberhart and Sean McKee, Township Manager)

Chairman Gerhart asked for public comment: NONE

Motion:
ON a motion by Mr. Pawling and seconded by Mr. Schultz, it was unanimously voted to approve the minutes
of the 6/4/2026 Meeting.

ENGINEER’S REPORT:

1-3 Aspen Ave — SHTwp Municipal Authority gave the approval for the lateral extension and will be having RS
Excavating do the work. Mr. Lanza has the quote from RS Excavating but did not have the exact amount at the
meeting.

ON A MOTION by Mr. Gerhart and seconded by Mr. Pawling it was unanimously voted to approve the lateral
extension work for 3 Aspen Avenue.

2 - ISCO Samplers — The Board discussed that the ISCO Samplers had arrived and the Roadcrew was going to
be trained. Also Ms. Auman will be setting up an account with SPL Laboratories.

3 — Sag in Sewer Main at Redners — Discussed moving forward with the project to repair the sag which would
have included preparing Plans and Specifications. This was before we knew about the sinkhole!

4 - Tapping Fee — Spring Township — We discussed updating the Tapping Fee for the Spring Township

District. The Authority agreed to move forward with this, but we will need final actual construction costs from
Ethos West so that we can calculate a reimbursement for new customers connecting to the sewer main they
are constructing.

SOLICITORS REPORT:

Mr. George discussed the Improvements Agreement for Ethos West. The financial security amount is
$438,097.25. Also, part of the Agreement includes off-site force main developer reimbursement language that
deals with the Authority collecting a reimbursement fee from new users of the main and remitting the
reimbursement to the developer. This obligation will last for 10 years after the new line is dedicated.


ON A MOTION by Mr. Gerhart and seconded by Mr. Beissel, it was unanimously voted to approve and sign the
Ethos West Improvements Agreement.

TOWNSHIP MANAGER’S REPORT: N/A

ROAD CREW MUNICIPAL REPORT:
Mr. Lanza went over the work done in June and July for the Municipal Authority by the Roadcrew.

Old Business: NONE

New Business: Ms. Auman presented information on Portnoff Law Associates; to utilize their services for
delinquent sewer accounts.

ON A MOTION by Mr. Gerhart and seconded by Mr. Schultz it was unanimously voted to approve and sign the
agreements pending review of said agreements by Mr. George.

Motion:

ON a motion by Mr. Schultz and seconded by Mr. Beissel, it was unanimously voted to ratify the payment of
bills since 6/4/26 and authorize the payment of bills dated 7/2/26 and 8/6/2026.

Motion:

ON a motion by Mr. Gerhart and seconded by Mr. Pawling, it was unanimously voted to receive and file the
Treasurer's Report.

PUBLIC COMMENT: NONE

Motion:

Mr. Pawling made a motion to adjourn, the motion seconded by Mr. Schultz, it was unanimously voted to
adjourn the meeting at 7:00pm.

Kevin R, Eberhart, Secretary
