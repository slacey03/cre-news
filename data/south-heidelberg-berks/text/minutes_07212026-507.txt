SOUTH HEIDELBERG TOWNSHIP
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
Bnet WORKSHOP & GENERAL BUSINESS MEETING-7/21/2026

CALL

The Board of Supervisors of the Township of South Heidelberg met at the Municipal Building for the
monthly general business meeting. The meeting was called to order by Chairman Musante at 9:00 AM, on
July 21, 2026, followed by the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS TOWNSHIP STAFF & ADVISORS

TOWNSHIP REPORTS
TOWNSHIP MANAGER
Mr. McKee provided the following updates:

RACP/Community Funding Grant. Mr. McKee provided an update on the RACP grant. Mr. McKee stated
that the Township has been awarded the grant in the full amount requested of $1 million. Mr. McKee
stated that he would be drafting a press release and also would send thank you responses to Senator
Gebhard and Representative Weaknecht.

Krick Lane Extension Update. Mr. McKee stated that PADOT has provided the various clearances and the
Township can proceed to put the project out for bid. Mr. McKee stated he would be working with
Engineer Anderson on getting the bid advertised in the nest couple weeks.

Ethos West Update. Mr. McKee stated that an on-site pre-construction meeting was held with Ethos and
then a second meeting with Ethos and the construction management group for the project occurred at
the Township. Mr. McKee stated that Public Works Manager Bill Lanza and Chief Hook were part of the
meeting with the focus on communication and addressing concerns if and when they arise. Mr. McKee
provided an overview of the upcoming construction schedule as well as a draft of a citizen’s information
packet regarding the project.

Police Services Contracts. Mr. McKee stated that he and Chief Hook are working on setting up meetings

with the municipalities regarding the police contract renewals. Mr. McKee stated the existing contract
ends December 31, 2027.

Schuylkill Highlands Grant. Mr. McKee stated the Township has been awarded the Schuylkill Highlands
Grant which will be used to study Township open spaces and areas that can be transitioned to meadows.

TOWNSHIP ENGINEER
Mr. Anderson provided the following updates:

Krick Lane Extension Bid. Mr. Anderson stated that the bid for Krick Lane Extension will be issued in the
next couple weeks. Mr. Anderson stated that interested contractors would have approximately 1 month
to submit their proposals and then the typical bid opening and tabulation would occur in August with an
award sometime in September via action by the Board.

2026 Road Projects. Mr. Anderson stated that the oil and chip/slurry seal application would be underway
in Heidelberg Run West and Laurel Ridge Road. Mr. Anderson stated that a double oil and chip would be
completed on Laurel Ridge and then an oil and chip followed by slurry seal on all roadways in Heidelberg
Run West.

Mr. Anderson asked if there were any questions. The Board had no questions.

TOWNSHIP SOLICITOR
Mr. George provided the following updates:

Logistics/Data Center Ordinance. Mr. George stated that the Joint Planning Commission met on June 29"
and recommended approval of the ordinance. Mr. George stated that each of the municipalities of the
southwestern berks joint zoning ordinance will need to schedule hearings and then adopt the ordinance.
Mr. George stated most of those hearings and adoption would occur in August.

Lower Heidelberg Township Zoning Amendment C-2 District. Mr. George stated that the Solicitor for
Lower Heidelberg Township has distributed a draft ordinance to amend the zoning map of Lower
Heidelberg Township. Mr. George stated that the amendment reclassifies a number of properties along
Route 422 to I-1 or C-2 along with an amendment to permit a medical/surgical hospital as a conditional
use in the C-2 district. Mr. George stated the amendment was briefly discussed at the joint planning
commission meeting and would be brought formally before the joint planning commission at a future
date.

SHPD/Heidelberg Township Police Addendums/Agreement/Resolution. Mr. George stated that the
Alden on 6" Development located in Heidelberg Township and Marion Townsihp has a majority of the
homes falling in Marion Township. Mr. George stated that because the majority of the homes are in
Marion Township, that an agreement between Heidelberg Township and Marion Township to provide
police services to those homes located in Heidelberg Township has been agreed upon. Mr. George stated
that South Heidelberg Township would need to pass an addendum allowing Tulpehocken PD to service
the homes in Heidelberg Township. Mr. George stated that action on this item would be on the general
business meeting agenda.

Mr. George asked the board if there were any questions. The Board had no questions.
