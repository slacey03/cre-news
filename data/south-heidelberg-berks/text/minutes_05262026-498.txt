TOWNSHIP

CALL TO ORDER/PLEDGE

The Board of Supervisors of the Township of South Heidelberg met at the Municipal Building for the
monthly general business meeting. The meeting was called to order by Chairman Musante at 7:00 PM, on
May 26, 2026 followed by the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.

MEETING ATTENDEES

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS TOWNSHIP STAFF & ADVISORS
Chairman, John N. Musante Township Manager, Sean A. McKee
Vice Chairman, Jon Gerhart Public Works Manager, Bill Lanza
Secretary, Tom Vida Police Chief, J. Matthew Hook

Township Solicitor, Andrew George, Esq.
Township Engineer, Scott Anderson

MEETING MINUTE APPROVAL
A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and carried unanimously by the Board to
approve the meeting minutes of the April 28, 2026 Board of Supervisors General Business Meeting.

PUBLIC COMMENT
No public comment.

NEW BUSINESS

4 Way Stop Sign Ordinance. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Vida and carried unanimously by
the Board to adopt Ordinance 295 which will establish an all-way stop intersection on Wernersville Road at the
intersection of Wernersville Road, Balthaser Road and Roxberry Drive.

Towing Contracts. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and carried unanimously by the
Board to execute a two-year renewal of existing towing contracts with Spitler’s Towing and Arndt’s Towing.

Heidelberg Township — Alden Development Intergovernmental/Police Agreement. A motion was made by Mr.
Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and carried unanimously by the Board to execute intergovernmental and police
agreement relating to the Alden Development in Heidelberg Township. The agreement allows a small section of
homes located in Heidelberg Township to receive police services from another municipality due to the location of
and the fact that the development is largely serviced by another municipality.

Public Works Promotion. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Vida and carried unanimously by
the Board to promote current Public Works Laborer Michael Trainer to Assistant Public Works Manager at a per
hour wage of $27.50.

Library Funding. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Musante and carried unanimously by the
Board to increase library funding to $23,000 per year which equates to a $3 per capita rate for 2026. The existing
budgeted library funding amount is $19,390 with the requested 2026 amount increasing by $3,610.

M&T Bank Lot 2. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Musante and carried unanimously by the
Board to approve M&T Bank — Lot 2 Final Subdivision Plan conditioned upon meeting the requirements of the
Township Engineer’s review letter dated April 6, 2026. The planning commission recommended approval of the
plan at it’s last meeting.

Shared Street Project — Old West Penn Avenue. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Vida and
carried unanimously by the Board to authorize award of the Old West Penn Avenue Road project to Martins Paving,
Ince and acknowledge the cost of $20,273.85 for the portion of Old West Penn Avenue located in South Heidelberg
Township.

Walters Avenue Bridge Project. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Musante and carried
unanimously by the Board to award the Walter’s Avenue Bridge Project to Herbert, Rowland and Grubic, Inc (HRG)
based on the proposal dated March 13, 2026. The Township received 3 proposals for both professional services and
construction costs and HRG’s proposal is as follows:

- Engineering Costs: $122,600

- Construction Costs: $498,000 (Aluminum Box Culvert
