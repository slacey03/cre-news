CALL TO ORDER/PLEDGE
The Board of Supervisors of the Township of South Heidelberg met at the Municipal Building for the

monthly general business meeting. The meeting was called to order by Chairman Musante at 7:00 PM, on
June 23, 2026 followed by the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.

NEW BUSINESS

GENERAL BUSINESS

Ethos West Final Land Development Plan (continued)

A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Vida and carried unanimously by the Board to
approve the Ethos West Final Land Development Plan conditioned upon meeting the Township Engineer’s
Review letter dated June 2, 2026.

Ethos West Improvements Agreements. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart
and carried unanimously by the Board to authorize execution of Improvements Agreements relating to
the Ethos West Development.

Lehman Subdivision Time Extension. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Vida and
carried unanimously by the Board to accept a time extension for the Lehman Subdivision Plan until
September 15, 2026 as submitted by Hoffert Surveyors.

Berks Auto Collision. Engineer John Hoffert was present to discuss the Berks Auto Collision plan. The
plan initially called for an expansion of the existing building to create a second building. Mr. Hoffert
stated that due to family issues the applicant would not be proceeding with the second building and
discussed alternative plans for the site relating to Berks Auto Collision. A Notice of Violation was issued
which is the purpose of Mr. Hoffert along with the attorney for Berks Auto Collision Mark Koch being
present at the meeting. The request from Mr. Hoffert and Attorney Koch was for a 6-month period to
submit a preliminary plan for review and a request to pause the notice of violation during that period.
The Board discussed and a motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Musante and carried
unanimously by the Board to grant a 6-month timeframe to submit a preliminary plan to include
submission and approval of an NPDES permit relating to site work at the property.

Police Cadet Program Hire. Chief Hook discussed the Cadet program and introduced candidate Chad
Werner. Mr. Werner addressed the Board and provided his background and discussed his path to
enrolling in the police academy. Chief Hook stated that the department had completed all preliminary
background work on candidate Werner and recommended hire into the cadet program. A motion was
made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Vida and carried unanimously by the Board to Potential to hire
Chad Werner as a South Heidelberg Township Cadet as part of the Police Cadet Program.

Police Cadet Program Agreement. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Vida and carried
unanimously by the Board to authorize the execution of the Police Cadet Program Agreement with
candidate Chad Werner.

Police Sale of Equipment. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and carried
unanimously by the Board to authorize sale of decommissioned police equipment to Accredited Security,
Naples, Florida for an estimated sale of $2,047.50 with the following items being sold:

17 X2 Tasers ($100.00 each) = $1,700.00

10 brand new batteries ($10.00 each) = $100.00

91 cartridges ($5.00 per set of 2) = $227.50

PA Commission on Crime and Delinquency (PCCD) Grant Resolution. A motion was made by Mr.
Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and carried unanimously by the Board to adopt Resolution 13-2026.
AUTHORIZING THE PREPARATION AND SUBMISSION OF A BYRNE JUSTICE ASSISTANCE GRANT SINGLE
