SOUTH HEIDELBERG TOWNSHIP
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
GENERAL BUSINESS MEETING - 8-25-2026

TOWNSHIP

CALL TO ORDER/PLEDGE

The Board of Supervisors of the Township of South Heidelberg met at the Municipal Building for the
monthly general business meeting. The meeting was called to order by Chairman Musante at 7:00 PM, on
August 25, 2026 followed by the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.

MEETING ATTENDEES

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS TOWNSHIP STAFF & ADVISORS
Chairman, John N. Musante Township Manager, Sean A. McKee
Vice Chairman, Jon Gerhart Public Works Manager, Bill Lanza
Secretary, Tom Vida Police Chief, J. Matthew Hook

Township Solicitor, Andrew George, Esq.
Township Engineer, Scott Anderson

MEETING MINUTE APPROVAL

A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Musante and carried unanimously by the Board to
approve the meeting minutes of the July 21, 2026 Board of Supervisors Workshop and General Business
Meeting and the July 27, 2026 Board of Supervisors General Business Meeting.

PUBLIC COMMENT
No Public Comment.

PUBLIC HEARING
A Public Hearing was held concerning a Zoning Ordinance Amendment of the Southwestern Berks Joint
Zoning Ordinance pertaining to Data Centers, Truck Terminal and Logistics.

NEW BUSINESS

GENERAL BUSINESS

Southwestern Berks Joint Zoning Amendment Ordinance 296. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart,
seconded by Mr. Vida and carried unanimously by the Board to adopt Ordinance 296 amending the
Southwestern Berks Joint Zoning Ordinance of 2004 to define the terms data center, data center
equipment and data center accessory uses...truck terminal, warehouse/logistics. The ordinance
establishes the data center use in the 13 Industrial District.

Pro-Grade Supplies/SuperQuartz LLC, Plan Re-Affirmation. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart,
seconded by Mr. Musante and carried unanimously by the Board to re-affirm the Preliminary/Final Plan
for Pro-Grade Supplies.

SG America Final Escrow Release. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Vida and
carried unanimously by the Board to authorize Final Escrow Release for SG America in the amount of
$57,833.00. The Township Engineer issued a letter dated August 14, 2026 recommending the release of
escrow.

Martins Paving Payment Authorization. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Vida and
carried unanimously by the Board to Authorize Payment in the amount of $20,273.85 to Martin Paving Inc
for a double oil and chip road paving on half of Old West Penn Avenue. The Township Engineer reviewed
and recommended authorizing payment.

Human Resources Administration. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Vida and
carried unanimously by the Board to appoint Kim Auman and Wanda Keener to the roles of Human
Resources Coordinator with a salary increase of $1.00 per hour. Kim Auman will be overseeing all Human
Resources with support from Wanda Keener relating to police/uniformed matters.

Towing Contract. Action regarding the towing contract as it relates to Ardnt’s Towing was delayed
however the owner of Ardnt’s Towing, Will Ardnt was present and asked if he could address the Board.
Mr. Ardnt provided background on his issues with a citizen of Robesonia which resulted in an incident in
the Borough regarding a trailer parked in front of the house of the citizen Mr. Ardnt has had a dispute
with. Mr. Ardnt stated that he was peacefully protesting and expressing his right to free speech. Mr.
Arndt made various accusations regarding the handling of the incident and his concerns regarding how
the incident was handled. Mr. McKee provided a response to Mr. Arndt’s accusations followed by a
rebuttal by Chief Hook. There was no action regarding the status of Mr. Ardnt’s towing contract as part of
the meeting.

TOWNSHIP REPORTS
WERNERSVILLE LIBRARY
Director Cathy Martin was present and provided updates regarding the Library.

TOWNSHIP MANAGER
Mr. McKee referenced his monthly report and stated he had nothing further to report.

PUBLIC WORKS MANAGER

Mr. Lanza referenced his monthly report and provided updates on Bensing Road milling and report. Mr.
Lanza stated that the asphalt zipper experienced issue with its clutch and the clutch replacement is being
sent out by Asphalt zipper. Mr. Lanza stated he and his team would install the new clutch and resume
repairs on Bensing once the clutch has been replaced. Mr. Lanza had nothing further to report.

POLICE REPORT

Chief Hook referenced his report to include 184 calls for service last month. Chief Hook provided update
on the transition of pathfinder which is the new record keeping system for the PD. Chief Hook also
referenced the upcoming meeting with members of Wernersville Borough to discuss the police services
contract as well as meetings with the county regarding the radio replacement program. Chief Hook had
nothing further to report.

TOWNSHIP ENGINEER REPORT
Mr. Anderson referenced his monthly report and had nothing further to report.

TOWNSHIP SOLICITOR REPORT

Mr. George referenced his monthly report and reminded by Board that the Joint Planning Commission
Meeting will be held August 26, 2026 to discuss the amendments presented by Lower Heidelberg
Township. Mr. George had nothing further to report.

PUBLIC COMMENT

Don Boulanger RE: Credit Card Purchases. Mr. Boulanger asked what the Township credit cards are used
for and who has those cards. Mr. McKee stated that the cards are setup to pay utilities and that is the
main function but they are also used for other purchases such as office supplies. Mr. McKee stated that
each Township vehicle has a gas credit card for use for that particular vehicle. Mr. McKee stated that he,
Chief Hook and Mr. Lanza have non gas related credit cards and that the Township credit card statements
are part of the yearly audit. .

ADJOURNMENT
A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and carried unanimously by the Board to
adjourn the meeting at 8:27pm.
