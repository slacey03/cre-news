9. Ethos West Hours of Work Extension Request.  Potential action regarding a request from 
Matt Nunn, Ethos Development concerning an extension of work hours.  Ordinance 263 
states in section 3.B. that construction hours are between 7:00AM and 6:00PM Monday 
through Saturday.  Ethos is seeking an extension of work hours from 7:00AM to 8:00PM 
Monday through Friday and 7:00AM to 7:00PM on Saturday until December 31, 2026.
10. 342 N. Galen Hall Road Holding Tank Agreement.  Action to authorize execution of a 
Holding Tank Agreement for 342 N. Galen Hall Road. The property owner is proposing the 
installation of a 1,500 gallon holding tank.  Sewage Enforcement Officer Quinn Haller has 
reviewed the proposal/agreement and approves what is being proposed.
11. Alden on Sixth Police Services Agreement.  Action to authorize execution of a Police 
Services Agreement for Alden on Sith.  Alden on Sixth is a 61 home neighborhood located 
mostly in Marion Township with approximately 16 to 18 homes to be located in Heidelberg 
Township.  The location of those homes, distance and other factors would make it difficult 
for the SHPD to service and thus the agreement allows those homes in Heidelberg Township 
to receive police services through the police department of Tulpehocken.
