BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
GENERAL BUSINESS MEETING - 7-28-2026

TOWNSHIP

CALL TO ORDER/PLEDGE

The Board of Supervisors of the Township of South Heidelberg met at the Municipal Building for the
monthly general business meeting. The meeting was called to order by Chairman Musante at 7:00 PM, on
July 28, 2026 followed by the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.

MEETING ATTENDEES

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS TOWNSHIP STAFF & ADVISORS
Chairman, John N. Musante Township Manager, Sean A. McKee
Vice Chairman, Jon Gerhart Public Works Manager, Bill Lanza
Secretary, Tom Vida Police Chief, J. Matthew Hook

Township Solicitor, Andrew George, Esq.
Township Engineer, Scott Anderson

MEETING MINUTE APPROVAL
A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and carried unanimously by the Board to
approve the meeting minutes of the June 23, 2026 Board of Supervisors General Business Meeting.

PUBLIC COMMENT
No Public Comment.

NEW BUSINESS

GENERAL BUSINESS

Police Hire — Lydia Griffith. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Vida and carried unanimously by
the Board to hire Lydia Griffith as a full-time Police Officer — Class II, subject to one-year probationary period and
this hire is based on the South Heidelberg Township Police Department providing police coverage to Heidelberg
Township, Wernersville Borough and Robesonia Borough.

Ethos Final Plan Approval — Decision Order. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and
carried unanimously by the Board to authorize execution of Decision and Order for the Final Land Development

Plan Approval of the Ethos West Project. The Decision and Order summarize all aspects of the plan approval to

ensure all items covered as part of land development are documented in this formal order.

Ethos West Improvements and Stormwater Agreements. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr.
Vida and carried unanimously by the Board to authorize execution of Improvements Agreement and Stormwater
Agreement relating to the Ethos West Development.

Ethos West Quitclaim Deed of Dedication. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Musante and

carried unanimously by the Board to authorize execution of Quitclaim Deed of Dedication to convey land for the
purposes of widening Krick Lane.

Ethos West Deed of Dedication Resolution. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and
carried unanimously by the Board to adopt Resolution 14-2026. The resolution accepts the quitclaim deed of
dedication as part of the Ethos West Project.

Ethos West Hours of Work Extension Request. A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Musante and
carried unanimously by the Board to grant a request from Matt Nunn, Ethos Development concerning an extension
of work hours relating to Ordinance 263 from 7:00AM to 8:00PM Monday through Friday and 7:00AM to 7:00PM
on Saturday until October 31, 2026.

342 N. Galen Hall Road Holding Tank Agreement. . A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart, seconded by Mr. Musante
and carried unanimously by the Board to authorize execution of a Holding Tank Agreement for 342 N. Galen Hall
Road. The property owner is proposing the installation of a 1,500 gallon holding tank. Sewage Enforcement Officer
Quinn Haller has reviewed the proposal/agreement and approves what is being proposed.

Alden on Sixth Police Services Agreement. A motion was made by Mr. Musante, seconded by Mr. Gerhart and
carried unanimously by the Board to authorize execution of a Police Services Agreement for Alden on Sith. Alden
on Sixth is a 61 home neighborhood located mostly in Marion Township with approximately 16 to 18 homes to be
located in Heidelberg Township. The location of those homes, distance and other factors would make it difficult for
the SHPD to service and thus the agreement allows those homes in Heidelberg Township to receive police services
through the police department of Tulpehocken.

TOWNSHIP REPORTS

WERNERSVILLE LIBRARY

Director Cathy Martin was present and provided updates regarding the Library’s summer reading program
and stated the program had over 200 registrations in June.

TOWNSHIP MANAGER

Mr. McKee referenced his monthly report. Mr. McKee stated that he has been made aware that the
Township will receive a DCNR C2P2 Grant Award in the amount of $250k. Mr. McKee stated the Township
was seeking $500k, however $250k was awarded based on a competitive grant cycle. Mr. McKee stated
he had nothing further to report.

PUBLIC WORKS MANAGER

Mr. Lanza referenced his monthly report and provided updates on paving work and other activities. Mr.
Lanza stated that street sweeping in advance of the oil and chip work would be done on August 17%
followed by the oil and chip application. Mr. Lanza stated he had nothing to report.

POLICE REPORT

Chief Hook referenced his report to include 173 calls for service last month. Chief Hook stated as a
reminder that National Night Out will be held on August 4, 2026 at the Township Building and stated the
event will be in cooperation with the Sinking Spring PD. Chief Hook asked if there were any questions and
had nothing further to report.

TOWNSHIP ENGINEER REPORT

Mr. Anderson referenced his monthly report provided updates concerning Berks Auto Collision,
Heidelberg Crossing Stormwater Pipe Repairs and the Krick Lane Extension project/bid. Mr. Anderson had
nothing further to report.
