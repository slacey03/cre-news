4. Southwestern Berks Joint Zoning Amendment Ordinance 296.  Action to adopt Ordinance 
296 amending the Southwestern Berks Joint Zoning Ordinance of 2004 to define the terms 
data center, data center equipment and data center accessory uses…truck terminal, 
warehouse/logistics.  The ordinance establishes the data center use in the I3 Industrial 
District.  
5. Pro-Grade Supplies/SuperQuartz LLC, Plan Re-Affirmation.  Action to re-affirm the 
Preliminary/Final Plan for Pro-Grade Supplies.   SuperQuartz LLC has purchased the lot in 
the South Heidelberg Industrial Park that was previously submitted by Pro-Grade Supplies. 
6. SG America Final Escrow Release.  Action to authorize Final Escrow Release for SG 
America in the amount of $57,833.00.  The Township Engineer issued a letter dated August 
14, 2026 recommending the release of escrow.
7. Martins Paving Payment Authorization.  Action to Authorize Payment in the amount of 
$20,273.85 to Martin Paving Inc for a double oil and chip road paving on half of Old West 
Penn Avenue.  The Township Engineer reviewed and recommended authorizing payment.
8. Human Resources Administration.  Action to appoint Kim Auman and Wanda Keener to 
the roles of Human Resources Coordinator with a salary increase of $1.00 per hour. Kim 
Auman will be overseeing all Human Resources with support from Wanda Keener relating to
police/uniformed matters.
