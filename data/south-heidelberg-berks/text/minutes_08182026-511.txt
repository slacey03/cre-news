SOUTH HEIDELBERG TOWNSHIP
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS
WORKSHOP & GENERAL BUSINESS MEETING-8/18/2026

CALL RD

The Board of Supervisors of the Township of South Heidelberg met at the Municipal Building for the
monthly general business meeting. The meeting was called to order by Chairman Musante at 9:00 AM, on
August 18, 2026, followed by the Pledge of Allegiance to the Flag.

MEETING ATTENDEES

BOARD OF SUPERVISORS TOWNSHIP STAFF & ADVISORS
Chairman, John N. Musante Township Manager, Sean A. McKee
Vice-Chairman, Jon Gerhart Police Chief, J. Matthew Hook
Secretary, Tom Vida Public Works Manager, Bill Lanza

Township Solicitor, Andrew George, Esq.

Township Engineer, Scott Anderson
PUBLIC COMMENT
Anthony Tucci, Western Berks Ambulance RE: Update. Mr. Tucci was present to review updates regarding
Western Berks Ambulance including discussion on a new website and new taxing structure based on mill.

TOWNSHIP REPORTS

TOWNSHIP MANAGER

Mr. McKee provided the following updates:

2027 Budget. Mr. McKee stated that the 2027 budget will be presented to the Board as part of the
October 2026 Workshop Meeting. Mr. McKee stated that the initial draft of the budget is complete and
that he will be meeting with Chief Hook and Public Works Manager Bill Lanza to review before presenting
the draft budget to the Board.

Human Resources Policy Manual. Mr. McKee stated that Kim Auman and Wanda Keener have fully
assumed their new roles as overseeing Human Resources for the Township. Mr. McKee stated an update
to the Employee Handbook and Policy Manual will be produced soon for the Board’s review and approval.

Ethos Updates and Citizen Communication. Mr. McKee reviewed with the Board the construction
schedule provided by Ethos and stated that Ethos will be providing the weekly schedule showing the
upcoming four weeks of activities. Mr. McKee also reviewed the various efforts made to communicate
with citizens and provide information to the public.

Krick Lane Extension Update. Mr. McKee stated that the project is out for bid with the bids due prior to
the September Workshop meeting. Mr. McKee stated the bids will be tabulated and reviewed with the
Board with the hope of beginning the project in October.

Grant Updates. Mr. McKee provided updates on the Township’s grant efforts to include the RACP grant
and an upcoming meeting with Senator McCormick’s office. Mr. McKee stated that the public works LSA
grant announcements will occur sometime in September.

Mr. McKee requested an Executive Session to discuss police services and human resources salary

POLICE CHIEF

Chief Hook reviewed his monthly report and provided the following updates:

Big Boy Train Event. Chief Hook reviewed police coverage and efforts relating to the Big Boy Train event
that occurred in the various municipalities serviced by the SHPD. Chief Hook stated that additional
Officers were on duty to handle each of the various railroad areas and ensure citizen safety during the
trains route through our area.

National Night Out. Chief Hook stated that National Night Out was held on August 4" at the Township
building. Chief Hook stated that members of the SHPD and Sinking Spring Borough PD collaborated on
the event and that the event was well attended.

Cadet Werner Update. Chief Hook provided an update on Cadet Chad Werner and stated that Cadet
Werner continues to excel in his academy activities.

Chief Hook requested an Executive Session to discuss an issue with the towing contract.

TOWNSHIP REPORTS
PUBLIC WORKS
Mr. Lanza reference is monthly report.

Bensing Road Milling. Mr. Lanza stated that public works will be conducting milling and repair on Bensing
Road in the upcoming weeks.

Mr. Lanza asked if there were any questions. The Board had no questions.

TOWNSHIP ENGINEER
Mr. Anderson provided the following updates:

Krick Lane Extension Bid. Mr. Anderson stated that as reported by Mr. McKee the bids for the Krick Lane
Extension will be received on August 31° and then reviewed with the Board in September.

ProGrade Supply Re-Affirmation/Changes. Mr. Anderson stated that the plan for ProGrade supplies will
need re-affirmation but also stated that the parcel has been sold to a new entity. Mr. Anderson stated
there have been some minor changes to the plan. Mr. Anderson stated the changes are small and do not
require the plan to be resubmitted to the planning commission. Mr. Anderson did state that the engineer
for the new entity would be attending the general business meeting to review the plan and changes with
the Board.

SG America Escrow Release. Mr. Anderson stated that he has received an escrow release request from
SG America. Mr. Anderson stated that he has reviewed the request and is recommending approval of
Escrow Release #3 in the amount of $57,883.

2026 Road Projects. Mr. Anderson provided an update on the slurry seal work in HRW development as
well as the double oil and chip on Laurel Ridge Road. Mr. Anderson also stated that the joint road project
on Old West Penn Avenue with Lower Heidelberg Township is complete and an approval to pay the
Township’s share of the work will be forthcoming.

Mr. Anderson asked if there were any questions. The Board had no questions.

TOWNSHIP SOLICITOR
Mr. George provided the following updates:

Logistics/Data Center Ordinance. Mr. George stated that a hearing will be held to adopt the
Logistics/Data Center Ordinance at the general business meeting. Mr. George stated that Wernersville
Borough and Lower Heidelberg Township will also be conducting hearings to adopt the ordinance.

Lower Heidelberg Township Zoning Amendment C-2 District. Mr. George stated that a Joint Planning
Commission meeting would be held at Lower Heidelberg Township on August 26 at 7pm to discuss the
proposed amendments. Mr. George stated that Supervisor Vida and Planning Commission Chairman
Behling would be in attendance representing South Heidelberg Township.

Short Term Rental Ordinance. Mr. George stated that Lower Heidelberg Township has presented another
zoning amendment relating to short term rentals. Mr. George stated that he has forwarded information
on the proposal to Manager McKee and Engineer Anderson for review and stated future discussion at the
Joint Planning Commission and also with the Board would occur at a future date.

Mr. George asked the board if there were any questions. The Board had no questions.

PUBLIC COMMENT
No public comment.

ADJOURNMENT
A motion was made by Mr. Gerhart seconded by Mr. Vida and carried unanimously by the Board to
adjourn the meeting at 9:58 AM.
