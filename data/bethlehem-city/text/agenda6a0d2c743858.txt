In Re: Appeal of Michael Italiana
Property: 1865 Cloverleaf Street
Date: July 29, 2026

PROJECT NARRATIVE

Applicant owns an existing home in the City of Bethlehem with an attached front porch.
Applicant seeks to enclose the attached front porch; however, he has been advised by the City’s
permit coordinator that enclosing the existing front porch would constitute an encroachment into
a required side yard. Attached is the e-mail from the permit coordinator explaining the nature of
the encroachment.

Also attached are an aerial photo of the subject property demonstrating the unique

physical shape of the Subject Property, and a photo showing the existing porch.
