Narrative Re: 1431 Monocacy Street, Bethlehem, PA 18018

Applicants are the owners of 1431 Monocacy Street, Parcel
ID Number N6SW3B 5 4. The lot is an existing 7,800 square foot
lot which contains one single family residence.

Applicants seek to subdivide the Property into two (2)
3,900 square lots and construct a new single family dwelling on
the new lot and maintain the keep the existing dwelling on the
other lot. The Property is located in between the RT
Residential Zoning District and the IP Institutional Plan Zoning
District in the RS Zoning District.

Historically, the Property was subdivided into two (2) lots
as shown on the City Block Plan. This block and neighborhood
were designed to accept high density residential development.
The original City Block Plan evidences the same intended
development.

The Property is surrounded by the following properties which
all have lots which do not conform with the RS Zoning District
and conform and align with the proposed development of the

The proposed development will provide an additional and new
single-family dwelling within the City of Bethlehem.

The relief set forth in the Application will not harm or
impair the use or development of surrounding properties and will
be in conformity with the existing neighborhood.

The hardship is not created by Applicants as the Property
is already undersized and nonconforming with the appropriate


zoning district. There are unique circumstances with this
Property as the house is developed on one side of the Property
leaving the proposed second lot vacant, originally its own lot.
The current development is indicative of the original and
intended development of the Property in two lots.
