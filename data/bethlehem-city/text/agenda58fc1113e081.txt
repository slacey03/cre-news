3. (26-007 LD) – 26060009 – 701-719 N NEW STREET, 14 W NORTH STREET and 11-15 W 
GARRISON STREET – MAJOR LAND DEVELOPMENT PLAN – Ward 9, Zoned CB, plans 
dated June 16, 2026. 
The Applicants propose to demolish all existing structures, and construct a five-story structure, as viewed 
from W North Street, or six-story structure, as viewed from W Garrison Street. The missed use structure 
would contain 80 off-street/garage parking spaces, 3,300 SF of commercial (retail) space with outdoor 
seating, 82 multi-family dwellings and tenant amenities including a two-story lobby, lounge, 2,250 SF 
gym, common courtyard, +/- 8,500 SF rooftop deck and +/- 1,850 SF rooftop dog park. 
 
4. (26-008 LD) –26080002– 2184 INDUSTRIAL DRIVE – MAJOR LAND DEVELOPMENT PLAN – 
Ward 13, Zoned PI, plans dated August 13, 2026. 
The Applicants propose to demolish all existing structures and site features, and construct a two-story, 
approximate 65,590 SF Data Center, 61,018 SF area for a substation, 17,159 SF area for medium voltage 
switchgear, two 11’ x 50’ loading spaces, ten off-street parking spaces, access roadways, lighting, 
landscaping, underground stormwater management facilities and other site appurtenances. 
 
The Applicants also seek waivers from the Subdivision and Land Development Ordinance, Section 
1349.03.E.9.a, to exceed the maximum 3:1 ratio for fill slopes, and 1349.19.G, to provide one deciduous 
or evergreen tree for every 4,000 SF of impervious surface.
