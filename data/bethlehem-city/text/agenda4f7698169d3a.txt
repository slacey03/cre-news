DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS – BUREAU OF ENGINEERING 
INTEROFFICE MEMORANDUM 
 
TO:  Cathy Fletcher, Director of Planning & Zoning 
    
FROM:  Adam J Herbold, Project Engineer  
 
SUBJECT: Driveway Width Waiver Request – (26-008 Land Development) – 2184 
Industrial Dr 
 
DATE:  October 28, 2026 
             
 
The property owner is proposing to construct a 65,590 SF data center and supporting 
infrastructure, replacing the existing 40,653 SF industrial building previously located on 
this site. We have received a request for relief from Article 1349.12.A of the Codified 
Ordinance from Mary Bachert, Project Manager with Bohler engineering on behalf of 
the Boyle Construction, Inc. dated August 13, 2026. The proposed driveway entrance 
widths of 100 ft. & 120 ft. along Avenue B & Industrial Dr respectively exceed the 35 ft. 
maximum set by the ordinance.  
 
A 35 ft. driveway entrance would not provide adequate width for larger vehicles to 
enter and exit the site safely. Driveways exceeding 35 ft. have previously been 
permitted within industrial areas across the City for properties which require access by 
larger vehicles, including along Ave A & Ave B just to the north of the site. 
 
In view of the above information, the Department of Public Works supports a waiver of 
§1349.12.A along Industrial Dr & Ave B. provided the driveways are constructed as 
directed by the City Engineer’s office with ADA curb ramps and crosswalks.
