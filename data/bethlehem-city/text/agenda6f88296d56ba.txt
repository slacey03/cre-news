RE: Request for Zoning Permit — Food Truck Operation

To Whom It May Concern:

| am writing to respectfully request consideration and approval of a zoning permit to operate my food truck
service, DEldealCreationsFood, at 3200 Commerce Center Blvd, Bethlehem, PA 18015.

The purpose of this food truck service is to provide convenient, affordable, and accessible meals to
employees and individuals working in the surrounding area. Many employees have limited time during their
workday to obtain a meal and may otherwise need to leave the workplace and travel to another location.

By providing food directly at or near the workplace, employees will have a convenient option to purchase
meals without having to make additional trips away from the property. This can help reduce unnecessary
vehicle travel during work hours, minimize time spent dealing with traffic, and allow employees to return to
work within their scheduled break periods.

Providing an on-site food option may also contribute to a safer and more efficient work environment.
Employees who would otherwise need to drive to another location during a short break can obtain their
meals locally, reducing the need for additional trips on busy roads and helping them avoid potential delays
caused by traffic, parking, and travel time.

Our goal is to operate the food truck in a professional, clean, and responsible manner while complying with
all applicable zoning, health, safety, parking, sanitation, and operational requirements.

We will make every effort to maintain the surrounding area in a clean and orderly condition, properly
manage trash and waste, and operate in a manner that is respectful of the property, neighboring
businesses, employees, and the surrounding community.

We respectfully ask that the zoning department consider the convenience and benefits this food service can
provide to the employees and businesses in the area. We believe that providing accessible meals at this
location can serve a practical need while supporting a safe and efficient workplace environment.


Thank you for your time and consideration of our request. We would be happy to provide any additional
information or documentation required as part of the zoning permit application.
