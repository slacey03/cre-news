|, Martha Santos Maga, residing at 520 4th Avenue, Bethlehem, Pennsylvania 18018, submit this
document as a formal description of the home child care services provided at my residence, in
accordance with the regulations and standards of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.

Home child care, also known as Family Child Care, is a child care service operated within a private
residence where children are cared for in a safe, supervised, and nurturing home environment. The
purpose of this service is to provide quality care for children while supporting their overall development
and well-being. ,

The home child care program is designed to promote the physical, emotional, social, and cognitive
development of children through age-appropriate activities. Daily activities may include structured and
unstructured play, early learning activities, reading, arts and crafts, music, rest periods, and outdoor
play, when weather and safety conditions permit.

The home environment is organized to meet child care safety standards and is arranged to be
child-friendly. Appropriate safety measures are implemented to protect children from potential hazards,
and children are supervised at all times while in care.

The program maintains consistent daily routines, including scheduled meal and snack times, rest
periods, and hygiene practices. Meals and snacks provided comply with basic nutritional guidelines,
and special dietary needs or allergies are accommodated as communicated by parents or guardians.

Due to the smaller group size typical of home child care, individualized attention is provided to each
child, allowing for close observation of developmental progress and personalized care. Open
communication with parents or guardians is encouraged to ensure consistency between the child’s
home environment and child care routines.

This home child care program is operated in compliance with applicable Pennsylvania Department of
Human Services (DHS) regulations for home-based child care and is intended to meet all required
health, safety, and supervision standards.

This document is provided for licensing and official purposes within the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania.

Applicant Name: Martha Santos Maga
Address: 520 4th Avenue, Bethlehem, PA 18018

|, Martha Santos Maga, hereby submit this statement in support of my application for an in-home day
care and to demonstrate compliance with the requirements established by the Bureau of Planning &
Zoning.

1. Residential Appearance of the Dwelling

The dwelling will retain its residential appearance, with no exterior modifications made to accommodate
the day care use, except for minor cosmetic or safety inprovements if necessary.

2. Operation by Permanent Resident

The day care will be actively operated by a permanent resident of the dwelling, who will be responsible
for the daily supervision and management of the facility.

3. Outdoor Play Area Requirement

If seven (7) or more children are cared for, a minimum of 200 square feet of fenced outdoor play area
will be provided, or an outdoor play area within 400 feet of the residence will be available for use.

4. Use Within a Place of Worship
The child day care will operate solely within the residence and not within a place of worship.
5. Compliance with State and Federal Regulations

The day care will comply with all applicable state and federal regulations, including obtaining any
required registration or license from the Pennsylvania Department of Public Welfare or its successor
agency.

6. Fencing of Outdoor Areas

Any outdoor areas routinely used for play will be secured with appropriate fencing to ensure the safety
of the children.

7. Sanitary Disposal of Diapers

Appropriate methods will be used to contain and dispose of used diapers in a sanitary manner,
including sealed containers and regular disposal, ensuring no nuisance to neighbors.

8. Safe Pick-Up and Drop-Off Area

A safe and convenient area will be provided for the pick-up and drop-off of children, minimizing traffic
disruption and ensuring safety at all times.


Additional Statement:

The operation of the in-home day care will not create any fire, toxic, or explosive hazards and will
maintain a safe and healthy environment for children and the surrounding community.
