RE:  Proposed Zoning Text Amendments: Data Centers 
 
 
Attached is a draft zoning text amendment that amends the City’s Zoning Ordinance to establish 
regulations for Data Centers. 
 
The proposed amendment to Article 1302 adds a definition for "Data Center," which is not 
currently defined in the Zoning Ordinance. 
 
The proposed amendments to Article 1305 establish the zoning districts in which Data Centers are 
permitted. Data Centers would not be permitted in the Light Industrial (LI) or Planned Industrial 
(PI) Districts and would be permitted by Special Exception (SE) in the Industrial (IN) District, 
including the Industrial Overlay (IN-O) District. 
 
The proposed amendments to Article 1322 establish regulations specific to the Data Center use. 
These provisions require applicants to submit applicable plans, studies, evaluations, and 
mitigation strategies at time of application and establish site design standards, including minimum 
setbacks based on the characteristics of nearby properties, as well as maximum building height, 
minimum yard, minimum buffer yard, and maximum building coverage requirements. 
 
This item is placed on your August 25th Planning Commission Agenda for review and 
consideration. Pursuant to the Pennsylvania Municipalities Planning Code, the Planning 
Commission is responsible for reviewing proposed zoning text amendments and providing a 
recommendation to City Council.
