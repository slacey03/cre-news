



  
BILL NO.    
ORDINANCE NO. 2027 -    
AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF BETHLEHEM, COUNTIES 
OF LEHIGH AND NORTHAMPTON, COMMONWEALTH OF 
PENNSYLVANIA, AMENDING THE ZONING ORDINANCE 
OF THE CITY OF BETHLEHEM A/K/A THE ZONING CODE, 
PART 13 OF THE CODIFIED ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF 
BETHLEHEM, BY ADDING A NEW USE DEFINED AS 
“HEALTHCARE ADAPTIVE REUSE” AND ADDING 
PROVISIONS TO ADDRESS ADAPTIVE REUSE OF EXISTING 
INSTITUTIONAL USES IN THE RS ZONING DISTRICT. 
 
THE COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF BETHLEHEM HEREBY ORDAINS AS FOLLOWS: 
SECTION 1: Adaptive Reuse Provisions in the RS Zoning District.  
 
In Section 1304.01 Allowed Land Uses in Primarily Residential Districts, add "Healthcare 
Adaptive Reuse (S. 1322)" to Section 1304.01(b((3) Institutional/Semi-Public Uses and insert the 
letter "P" in the column for the RS district and the letter "N" in the columns for all other districts.  
 
Add a new subsection 1322.03(y) "Healthcare Adaptive Reuse" to Section 1322.03 Additional 
Requirements for Certain Uses as follows:  
 
1322.03(y) Healthcare Adaptive Reuse. 
 
(1) The “Healthcare Adaptive Reuse” use is intended to allow existing healthcare buildings to 
be converted to different healthcare uses, as provided in this subsection. 
(2) This subsection shall only apply to properties in the RS zoning district having a lot size of 
one acre or greater that are occupied by an existing lawful Assisted Living Facility or 
Personal Care Home. 
(3) The use of qualifying properties may be adapted and changed for use as and for: a) in-
patient and/or out-patient behavioral health services and/or b) a drug and alcohol treatment 
facility licensed by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania that addresses substance abuse 
disorders.   
(4) The total number of resident beds shall not be increased from the number of beds that 
currently lawfully exist in the Assisted Living Facility or Personal Care Home. 
(5) The height of existing buildings shall not be increased from the building height existing at 
the time of adoption of this subsection. The building setbacks that exist at the time of 

  
adoption of this subsection shall not be reduced where a setback abuts a lot line of an 
existing dwelling. 
(6) The number of off-street parking spaces shall not be reduced below the number of spaces 
that lawfully exist on the property. 
(7) Prior to occupancy, the facility operator shall provide proof of receipt of applicable 
required licenses from agencies of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, prior to occupancy 
of the facility. 
SECTION 2. All subsections in Article 1322 following new subsection 1322.03(y) shall be 
renumbered accordingly. 
SECTION 3. All Ordinances and parts of Ordinances inconsistent herewith be and hereby are 
repealed. 
SECTION 4. The provisions of this Ordinance Amendment are declared to be severable, and if 
any provision, sentence, clause, section, term, phrase or part of this Ordinance Amendment is for any 
reason found to be unconstitutional, illegal or invalid, then such unconstitutionality, illegality or 
invalidity shall not affect or impact any of the remaining provisions, sentences, clauses, sections, 
terms or parts of this Ordinance Amendment.
