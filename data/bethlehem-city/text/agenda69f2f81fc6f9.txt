Dear City of Bethlehem Zoning Board,

The proposed use of 824 8th Avenue as a behavioral health and community-support office
represents an appropriate and beneficial transition from the property's existing nonconforming
use to another nonconforming office use. We respectfully request approval of the Special
Exception pursuant to §1323.07 and §1325.07 of the City of Bethlehem Zoning Ordinance. The
property is already improved as a multi-level office building, with multiple access points and
approximately 10 on-site parking spaces, making continued professional office use a practical

reuse of the existing property.

Under §1323.07(c), an applicant must demonstrate that the proposed change will be /ess
objectionable in its external effects than the existing nonconforming use, specifically considering
traffic, noise and other environmental effects, storage and waste disposal, and appearance. The
property's prior approved use involved a construction/tradesperson headquarters. In comparison,
the proposed behavioral health office would be a substantially quieter, lower-impact use. There
would be no construction equipment, material staging, heavy commercial activity, unusual
fumes, dust, vibration, or industrial waste associated with the proposed operation. The existing
building and professional appearance of the property would be maintained and improved as

necessary.

The proposed use is also consistent with the broader standards of §1325.07(b). Most importantly,
it would serve the interests of Bethlehem and the welfare and convenience of the community.
The facility would expand access to critical behavioral health, mental health, substance-use and
community-support services for youth, adults and families in Bethlehem. These services can be
life-saving—helping residents receive intervention, treatment and support within their own
community rather than facing unnecessary barriers to care.

The project would also provide a meaningful economic benefit. We anticipate creating
approximately 10 new in-person positions and an additional 20 virtual employment
opportunities. This investment therefore allows an existing Bethlehem property to remain
productive while simultaneously generating employment and expanding access to essential

healthcare and community services.

The location is particularly appropriate for this purpose. The property offers visibility and
convenient access to major routes into and through Bethlehem; the listing identifies convenient
