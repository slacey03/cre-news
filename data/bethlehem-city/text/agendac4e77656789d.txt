The above-referenced plan has been reviewed by the appropriate City offices. A Sketch Plan lacks much of the
information necessary for a thorough evaluation and has been reviewed only for general conformance to City of
Bethlehem standards to the extent permitted by the limited detail provided. Additional comments will be provided
when a more detailed plan is submitted for review.

At this point we offer the following sketch plan comments:

1. Zoning Relief. The Applicant shall submit an Appeal Application for the following relief:

a. A Variance to eliminate the requirement for a principal commercial use on the front street level;
Ref. 1305.01(a) footnote 1.

b. A Dimensional Variance to reduce the lot area per dwelling, 1,200 SF/DU required, 666 SF/DU
proposed, or 10 dwellings permitted, 18 dwellings proposed; Ref. 1306.01(b)(3)(a). See also
Comments #2 and #3 below.

c. A Variance to reduce the minimum required off-street parking, 32 spaces required, 17 proposed;
Ref. 1319.01(a)(1)(ii). Street parking is not available on Stefko Boulevard; therefore, the parking
calculation is 1.75 spaces per dwelling unit.

2. Workforce Housing incentives are not available for parcels located in the CG (General Commercial)
Zoning District. [1307.01].

3. Workforce Housing incentives are not available for projects creating less than 20 Dwelling Units.
[1307.02].

4. Provide architectural elevations or rendered perspectives for each fagade and label exterior finishes (this
item should be submitted in paper or e-format prior to the Planning Commission Meeting). The
applicants are encouraged to provide masonry on the facades facing Peters Street and Croft Street.

5. The City commends the Applicants for proposing “Workforce-Focused” affordable housing. For
information on the City’s Housing Policies and Incentives, contact Emily Folenta at 610.419.0616, or

efolenta@bethlehem-pa.gov.
