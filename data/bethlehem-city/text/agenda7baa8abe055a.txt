I looked at most recent plan, and based on the information available to me, I have the
following comments:
You will a use variance for lot size in a Steep Slope area (minimum 4 acres).
[1316.01(c)(1)]
It does not appear that any relief will be needed for maximum building coverage (25%),
but a calculation was not included on the plan.
An additional variance for impervious surface may be required based upon the quantity
and severity of existing slopes (15%-25%, 25%-35% and/or over 35%). A full
measurement and inventory of the slopes will need to be prepared.
Your engineer should prepare a mature tree inventory.
Your engineer should prepare impervious surface inventory and calculation. Your
engineer should do a building coverage calculation.
Not necessarily a zoning issue, but resolution of the stormwater flow through the lot will
require approval by City Engineering prior to issuance of any eventual permits.
 
It is the suggestion of the Planning Bureau that you address the above comments and submit a
more detailed sketch plan to the Planning Commission for review
