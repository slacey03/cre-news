Joe,
 
I looked at the plans you submitted, and I am unable to tell you full extent of the relief you
would need based on the information submitted. What I can ascertain you need is:
A use variance to construct a two-family detached dwelling in the RS zoning district.
[1304.01(b)(1)]
A use variance for lot size in a Steep Slope area (minimum 4 acres). [1316.01(c)(1)]
A variance for retaining walls higher than 5’. [1316.01(e)]
Assuming you were successful in receiving the above use variances, you would need to
specifically request the ZHB endorse your proposed 8’ side yard setback; otherwise ZO
section 1306.07(b) will require you to comply with the dimensional requirements for
“Other Allowed Uses”  in the RS district; those requirements mandate 25’ side yards.
Additional variances for impervious surface, building coverage slope disturbance may also be
necessary. My comments only apply to zoning and may be incomplete. Other City
departments are may have additional comments and requirements.
 
I would also require any official submittal to address the comments below (for a complete
review):
The plan only calls out 15-25% slopes and 25%+ slopes; there is no note specifically
excluding  35% slope areas.
Your engineer should prepare a mature tree inventory.
Your engineer should prepare impervious surface inventory and calculation. ZO section
1316,01(c)(1) will limit you to 10% impervious coverage, and the City Engineer’s office
will need to approve your proposed method of establishing a “porous paving”.
Your engineer should do a building coverage calculation.
My recollection is that you previously suggested constructing the building on piers; one
or two elevation drawings would be helpful if this is still the intent..
Your engineer should prepare a table specifying the total of each category of steep
slopes on the site, as well as calculating the total disturbance of each category
