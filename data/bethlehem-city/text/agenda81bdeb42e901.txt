Benchmark Civil Engineering Services, Inc. has reviewed the Transportation Impact Assessment
prepared by TPD and dated May 8, 2026 for the Autumn Estates in the City of Bethlehem and
Bethlehem Township with respect to City of Bethlehem Ordinance No. 2025-39.
We offer the following comments.
1. The Transportation Impact Assessment utilizes the 12th Ed. of the ITE Trip Generation
Manual for the proposed 65 lot single family residential development (19 lots in the city
of Bethlehem and 46 lots in Bethlehem Township) generates a volume of traffic which
requires a limited transportation impact study to address pedestrian safety concerns. 
2. The Transportation Impact Assessment satisfies the ordinance requirements in that the
proposed development proposes sidewalks to provide connectivity for pedestrian
movements and safety within the development and the adjacent roadway network. 
3. The study indicated that adequate sight distance is available at the proposed driveway
intersection opposite the existing Cottage Drive intersection with Township Line Road.
