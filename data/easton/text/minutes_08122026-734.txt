Crystal Rose, Chairperson of Planning and Codes: Council is being asked to authorize a 
contract with THA Consulting, Inc. for Phase One of a citywide parking system evaluation.  
• Phase One will assess the City’s existing parking system, including:  
o Parking inventory and existing conditions  
o Parking occupancy and utilization  
o Residential parking  
o Stakeholder engagement  
o Recommendations for operational improvements  
• The study is intended to provide better data before the City considers future parking 
investments or policy changes.  
• If Phase One is successful, staff anticipates returning next year with a proposal for Phase 
Two, which could address future parking demand, pricing strategies, technology and 
enforcement, zoning recommendations, public engagement, revenue optimization, and 
implementation planning.  
• Phase One is expected to be completed by December 2026, if approved.  

• Mrs. Rose also praised new Planning and Zoning Director Samantha McClean, noting 
her continued engagement with community members, commissions, and the Zoning 
Hearing Board. The full meeting packets are now being posted online, which was 
described as a positive step for transparency.  
• The next Zoning Hearing Board meeting is scheduled for Monday, August 17, at 6:00 
p.m., with the agenda and meeting packet available on the City’s website.  
 
Frank Graziano, Chairperson of Public Safety: no report 
 
Julie Zando-Dennis, Chairperson of Public Works: 
   Four resolutions and two ordinance 
changes will be introduced for approval.  
Three resolutions concern development at 364 East Berwick Street and involve standard 
agreements holding the developer responsible for stormwater runoff and related infrastructure.  
The fourth resolution would approve a cooperation agreement with the Karl Stirner Arts Trail, 
under which the City would manage construction for a project funded by a $25,000 Hotel Tax 
Grant.  
One ordinance would require utilities that excavate City streets to be accountable for properly 
repairing the streets.  
The second ordinance would clarify that on-duty City vehicles may be exempt from parking 
regulations while conducting City business.
