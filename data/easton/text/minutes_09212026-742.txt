1. 813 Reynolds St 
Description: The applicant, Brynlee Lippincott, on behalf of River of God Fellowship Church, 
requested variances from § 595-34(O)(1) to permit two additional signs where one sign per 
frontage is permitted, to permit a freestanding sign where freestanding signs are not 
permitted in the South Side zoning district, and to exceed the maximum permitted total sign 
area. The application identified 136 square feet of proposed signage where six square feet 
is permitted. Fifty adjacent property owners were notified, the hearing was advertised, and 
staff confirmed that the property was posted.  
 
Applicant Presentation: Maureen Romans, a member of the church council, and Jeremy 
Cressman, church treasurer, appeared on behalf of the applicant. Ms. Romans presented 
background information on the property and church, including its history, site configuration, 
photographs, and signatures from nearby residents supporting the variance request. The 
materials were entered as Exhibit A-1. She explained that River of God Fellowship Church 
shares the building with a Spanish-speaking congregation and that the sign provides church 
identification, contact information, and service information for both congregations. The sign 
is positioned near the front corner of the property and oriented toward Cheston Elementary 
School and the nearby residential development rather than directly toward neighboring 
residential properties.

Ms. Romans described the church as a community-oriented congregation of approximately 150 
members. She stated that the church serves as an emergency evacuation location for Cheston 
Elementary School and operates community programs including a food pantry, blessing box, 
holiday food assistance, and support for school families. She stated that the freestanding sign 
helps identify the church and its English- and Spanish-language services to nearby residents 
and school families. The representatives explained that the church building is set substantially 
back from the roadway and that a wall sign mounted on the building would be more difficult to 
read from the street.  
 
The Board discussed how the size of the sign was selected, whether comparable freestanding 
signs exist in the South Side district, the distance between the roadway and the church building, 
the orientation of the sign, lighting, and how the sign is secured. The applicant stated that the 
vinyl sign was sized to make information for both congregations visible from the street and has a 
degree of transparency. The representatives acknowledged that the sign had been installed 
without zoning approval because they did not realize that a non-permanent vinyl sign was 
subject to the sign regulations. They stated that the sign has been in place for approximately 
two years and is not illuminated.  
 
During the hearing, the Board and staff clarified that the 136-square-foot figure identified in the 
application appeared to include the open area below the sign panel. Because open portions of 
the supporting frame are not included in sign area, the actual sign face was estimated during 
the hearing to be approximately 63 square feet. The representatives stated that the overall 
frame was approximately 8.5 feet wide by 8 feet high.  
 
The Board discussed whether the existing posts were sufficiently secured. Staff cited § 595-
34(F)(6)(b), which requires a freestanding ground sign to be supported and permanently placed 
by embedding, anchoring, or connecting the sign in a manner that incorporates it into the 
landscape or architectural design scheme. The church representatives stated that the posts had 
been driven into the ground and agreed that they could add concrete or another means of 
permanent anchoring. The applicant also confirmed that no lighting is proposed and that neither 
congregation intends to install different signage under the approval.  
 
Public Comment: There was no public comment on this item. 
 
Board Action: Approved the requested variances subject to the conditions that the sign 
posts be permanently anchored and that the approval apply only to the current church 
use or a successor church use. (Motion passed 5-0)
