5. 309 West Wayne Avenue – Special Exception for I4 Large Accessory Structure  
Staff Presentation and Recommendation: Staff presented a report and recommendation on 
a proposed I4 Large accessory structure permitted by Special Exception in the College Hill 
(CH) zoning district at 309 West Wayne Avenue as detailed in the staff report in the October 
7, 2026 Meeting Packet. Staff recommended that the Planning Commission recommend 
approval of the special exception request subject to conditions.  
 
Applicant Presentation: The applicant, Mark Ricker, appeared before the Commission and 
provided additional drawings depicting the proposed garage and site layout. Mr. Ricker 
explained that the garage would be positioned approximately 17 feet from the side of the 
house and would connect to the existing driveway through a short stone driveway extension 
leading to the garage doors. He reviewed the submitted sketches, site information, and 
construction estimates and described how the proposed garage would function with the 
existing driveway and parking areas on the property. 
 
Commissioner Discussion: Commissioners discussed the height and location of the 
proposed garage. Staff confirmed that the proposed structure height of approximately 16 
feet complies with the zoning ordinance requirement permitting large accessory structures 
up to 20 feet in height. 
 
Discussion also focused on the existing site layout, including the driveway circulation 
pattern, retaining wall, fencing, and existing improvements on the property. Commissioners 
asked questions regarding the property's parcel configuration. Mr. Ricker explained that 
although the property is identified through multiple tax parcels, it is held through a single 
deed. Commissioners discussed the implications of future parcel separation and whether 
the detached garage could remain if ownership configurations changed. Staff explained that 
the structure is permitted because it functions as an accessory structure to the principal 
residential use and that any future attempt to subdivide or establish the garage as a 
principal use on a separate lot would require zoning review and compliance with applicable 
ordinance standards at that time. 
 
Public Comment: There was no public comment on this item.
