1. 309 W. Wayne Avenue 
Description: The applicant, Mark Ricker, on behalf of himself and Joan Ricker, requested a 
special exception pursuant to § 595-12(C)(7) to construct an I4 Large Accessory Structure in 
the College Hill zoning district, Block Class B. Fifty-nine adjacent property owners were 
notified, the hearing was advertised, and the property was posted. The Planning 
Commission recommended conditional approval.  
 
Applicant Presentation: Mr. Ricker presented plans, a lot map, construction information, and 
photographs of the property. He explained that the proposed garage would be located near 
the front of the house and would provide space for his trucks. The proposed garage would 
be approximately 16 feet high, comparable to the height of the house. Mr. Ricker stated that 
it would be used for personal vehicle storage and not for business. Staff confirmed that the 
proposal meets the applicable setback requirements. 
 
Public Comment: There was no public comment on this item.

2. 61 N. Locust Street 
Description: The applicant and property owner, Gary Skelly, requested a variance from        
§595-17(E)(2) to permit a four-foot side yard setback for a prefabricated 12-by-20-foot 
garage shed on a gravel bed, where a 10-foot setback from any property line is required. 
Twenty-two adjacent property owners were notified, the hearing was advertised, and the 
property was posted. 
 
Applicant Presentation: Mr. Skelly stated that the proposed location was the only feasible 
place on the property for the garage. He explained that the garage would help protect his 
vehicle from falling branches from mature trees on the property, which had previously 
caused damage to fencing, the roof, and a metal railing. He stated that the neighboring 
commercial building has a solid wall facing the proposed garage, that there would be 
sufficient room for access and maintenance, and that gutters and leaders would direct roof 
drainage away from the neighboring property. No adjoining property was available for 
purchase to help meet the required setback. The garage will be used for vehicle and bicycle 
storage and will have electricity for an automatic garage door.  
 
Public Comment: There was no public comment on this item.  
 
Board Action: Approved the requested variance. (Motion passed 4-0) 
 
 Motion: Mr. Loebsack 
 Second: Ms. Vulcano-Hall 
 Ayes:  Mr. Civitella, Mr. Loebsack, Ms. Thomas and Ms. Volcano-Hall  
 Nays:    None 
 
3. 254 W. Nesquehoning Street  
Description: The applicant and property owner, William Valencia, requested variances 
associated with converting a single-family dwelling to a two-family dwelling. The requests 
concerned the existing six-foot built-to line where three feet is required; an existing three-
foot side yard setback where five feet is required; street trees; an existing driveway 
approximately 50 feet wide where a maximum width of 10 feet is required; an existing four-
space parking area that backs into a public street; and a zero-foot side yard setback for the 
existing parking area. Fifty-six adjacent property owners were notified, the hearing was 
advertised, and the property was posted. The Zoning Officer noted that the proposed and 
required column headings on the application information sheet were reversed.

Applicant Presentation: Mr. Valencia explained that he intended to create two apartments 
within the existing house through interior work, including framing, drywall, and installation of 
a kitchen. He stated that the exterior conditions and four rear parking spaces existed when 
he purchased the property and would not be changed. He provided proposed floor layouts 
and a photograph of the property. After confirming that there was room for street trees, Mr. 
Valencia agreed to withdraw the street-tree variance request and work with the City Forester 
to determine whether street trees are required. 
 
Public Comment: There was no public comment on this item. 
 
Board Action: Approved the requested variances, with the exception of the withdrawn 
street tree variance, subject to the following conditions: 
• The applicant shall work with the City Forester to determine whether a street tree 
or trees are required and plant street tree(s) if they are required. 
• The four parking spaces shall be marked to provide four nine-foot spaces with 
equal spacing between them. 
• The written leases for the rental units shall assign two of the four parking spaces 
to each unit. 
(Motion passed 4-0) 
 
 Motion: Mr. Civitella 
 Second: Mr. Loebsack 
 Ayes:  Mr. Civitella, Mr. Loebsack, Ms. Thomas, and Ms. Vulcano-Hall 
 Nays:    None 
 
4. 812 W. Grant Street 
Description: The applicant and property owner, Saul Riera, requested a variance from                     
§595-14(F)(6) to retain an existing paved driveway approximately 80 feet wide at the rear of 
the property, where a maximum driveway width of 10 feet is required. Thirty-six adjacent 
property owners were notified, the hearing was advertised, and the property was posted. 
 
Applicant Presentation: Mr. Riera appeared with his son, Arnold Riera, who assisted with the 
presentation. They explained that the paving was installed by a contractor working on a 
neighboring driveway and that the contractor had not obtained the required permits. The 
family subsequently applied for the zoning permit and variance. They stated that the paving 
replaced a gravel area that required frequent maintenance and that the paved area would 
not be used for parking; vehicle parking would remain inside the fenced area at the rear of 
the property. The Board discussed measures to discourage parking on the paved area. 
 
Public Comment: There was no public comment on this item.

Board Action: Approved the requested variance, subject to the following conditions: 
• Four reflective “Private Property — No Parking” signs shall be installed along the 
fence line. 
• The existing asphalt may be maintained during its useful life but may not be 
replaced with asphalt at the end of its useful life, except for a driveway no wider 
than 10 feet, at which point the property shall comply with the ordinance. 
(Motion passed 4-0) 
 
 Motion: Mr. Civitella  
 Second: Mr. Loebsack 
 Ayes:  Mr. Civitella, Mr. Loebsack, Ms. Thomas and Ms. Vulcano-Hall 
 Nays:    None
