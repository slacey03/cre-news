Luis Campos, City Administrator: The Economic Development Department recently completed 
a mid-year budget forecasting review as part of ongoing check-ins with all departments. 
Preliminary findings identified several budget items being closely monitored: 
• A previously reported $200,000 healthcare billing from last year will impact this year's 
budget.  
• The city expects a $70,000 revenue shortfall because a developer chose not to use RACP 
funds at the Confluence project. The funds will be redirected to another city project, but 
the related commission will not be received this year.  
• Fire department overtime is currently projected to exceed budget by $200,000–$300,000, 
though it is being monitored.  
A more comprehensive budget update will be presented later. 
Additionally, an update was provided on the parking study RFP. Bids have been received, the 
evaluation process is underway, and meetings with consultants are scheduled. While the project 
budget is expected to be very tight, the city plans to move forward because of the study's 
importance and will provide more detailed updates as the process continues.
