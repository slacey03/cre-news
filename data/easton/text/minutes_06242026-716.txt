Ken Brown, Chairperson of Finance:  n/a 
 
Frank Pintabone, Chairperson of Economic Development: the DCED coordinated and led 
commercial real estate tour in partnership with the LV Chamber. In partnership with 
Northampton County DCED and Lehigh Small Business Development Center we held 2nd annual 
taste of the food industry technical assistance panel at the quadrant. 
Hosted Main Streets EV Committee, met with PennDot and Trust for public lands regarding 
TASA grant for the Highline acquisition. 
Completed a state DCED survey regarding opportunity zones. Attended CPACE event. Attended 
a webinar about outdoor recreation funding opportunity. Met with county parks department 
about possible funding support for the Highline. 
Worked with West Ward Community initiatives, Main Street and Farmers Market to develop a 
pilot trolley transportation program to assist bringing visitors from West Ward and Southside. 
City received $235,000 from the PHFA Aging to place home exterior renovations.

Attended trophies and treats. Juneteenth parade and celebration. Rose on the River and Project 
Easton’s Graduation. Two emails she shared with the Mayor.  Discussed a dumping issue and 
pedestrian safety by Larry Holmes Drive.

last Wednesday. Staffing will be at 63. Parking officers are well staffed.
