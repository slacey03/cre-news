A public hearing will be held for the following zoning appeals:
ZHB-26-41 | 254 W. Nesquehoning St.
William Valencia, who requests variances from the following sections of the Easton Zoning Ordinance 
(the “Ordinance”) to convert an A1 Single-Family Detached to a A4 Two-Family Detached:
1. §595-14(D) for a 6’ existing build-to line (3’ build-to line req.);
2. §595-14(D) for an existing 3’ side yard setback (5’ side yard setback req.);
3. §595-14(F)(3) for no street trees (street trees planted in accordance with Chapter 554 and as 
determined by the City Forester req.);
4. §595-14(F)(6) for an existing driveway width of 50’ installed without approvals (10’ max. 
driveway width req.); 
5. §595-14(J)(2) for an existing 4 space off street parking installed without approvals that backs 
into a public street (off street parking for 3 or more vehicles shall not be designed to require or 
encourage vehicles to back into a public street req.); and 
6. §595-15(J)(3) for an existing 4 space off street parking with 0’ side yard setback (4’ side yard 
setback req.).
The property is located in the West Ward zoning district – Block Class C.
ZHB-26-46 | 309 W. Wayne Ave.
Mark Ricker, who requests special exception to construct an I4 Large Accessory Structure, under §595-
12(C)(7) of the Ordinance. The property is located in the College Hill zoning district – Block Class B.
ZHB-26-47 | 61 N. Locust St.
Gary Skelly, who requests a variance from §595-17(E)(2) of the Ordinance for a 4’ side yard setback for
an I3 Small Accessory Structure (10’ setback req.). The property is located in the River Corridor zoning 
district – Block Class B.
ZHB-26-48 | 812 W. Grant St.
Saul Riera, who requests a variance from §595-14(F)(6) of the Ordinance for an existing 80’ wide 
driveway installed without approval (10’ max. driveway width req.). The property is located in the South 
Side zoning district – Block Class B.
All persons interested or aggrieved may appear at the indicated time and place and be heard.
