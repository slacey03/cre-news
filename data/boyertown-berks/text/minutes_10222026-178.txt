BOYERTOWN PLANNING COMMISSION 
MINUTES 
October 22, 2026 
 
 A meeting of the Boyertown Planning Commission was called to order at 7:00 
p.m. by Chairman Charles Neubauer. In addition to Mr. Neubauer, commission members 
present were Frank Deery, Charles Fryer, Barry Christman and Glenn Trate.    
 
 Also present were Christopher Hartman, solicitor; Patricia Loder, Borough 
Manager; Scott Miller, SSM Engineering, Alex Barlow, Howell Engineering; Stephen 
Shanahan, 3 Dog Garage, and one interested citizen. 
    
  Mr. Trate made motion to approve the minutes of the March 30th, 2026 meeting.  
The motion was seconded by Mr. Deery, and approved.   
 
Mr. Neubauer then recognized Mr. Barlow, Howell Engineering; and Mr. Miller, 
SSM, Engineering, to review changes to the site improvements proposed to made at 230 
East Second Street. The proposed use of the property is for interior vehicle storage.  The 
previous use of the property was warehousing/storage.  The applicant Big Road, LLC is 
proposing to redevelop the vehicle storage lot with rain garden and detention bed, as well 
as a parking lot with 8 off-street parking spaces on the 1.55 acre (gross) tract.  The site is 
located at 230 East Second Street and 221 South Franklin Street and is bound by a 20-
foot wide alley to the west, East Second Street to the north, South Franklin Street to the 
east, and residential buildings to the south.  Access is provided via a two lane driveway 
on South Franklin Street. The property is zoned LI – Light Industrial.  Borough Council 
granted a waiver of Land Development on April 6, 2026 conditioned upon compliance 
with LTL Consultants zoning review, and stormwater and building permit reviews.   
 
 Mr. Miller and Mr. Barlow advised that due to infiltration issues on the site  
development of the site has changed from the plan that was initially submitted to the 
Borough.  SSM Engineering and LTL reviewed the revised plan for compliance with the 
Stormwater Ordinance and the Zoning Ordinance.  Mr. Barlow advised that Big Road 
LLC will comply with all comments in LTL’s zoning review letter dated September 21st, 
2026 and SSM’s Stormwater review letter dated October 14, 2026.   
 
Mr. Barlow submitted waiver requests for consideration.  The waivers requested 
have been reviewed by SSM Engineering.  Each request is made where literal 
enforcement of the stormwater ordinance will exact undue hardship because of peculiar 
conditions pertaining to the land.  Mr. Barlow noted that the waivers, if granted, will not 
be contrary to the public interest, and the purpose and intent of the stormwater ordinance 
will be observed.   
 
Waivers are requested from the following Sections of the Stormwater Ordinance; 
Section 165-21 C & D; 165-26.A (3); 165-26.A (10); and 165-26.E.  Mr. Miller advised 
that SSM recommends approval of the waivers as requested.   
 

 
Following discussion, Mr. Deery made a motion, second by Mr. Trate to 
recommend Borough Council grant the requested waivers.  The motion was approved. 
 
Mr. Trate made a motion, second by Mr. Fryer, to recommend Borough Council 
reaffirm waiver of Land Development conditioned upon the applicants compliance with 
LTL Consultant’s zoning review letter dated September 21st, 2026; and SSM 
Engineering’s Stormwater review letter dated October 14th, 2026.  The motion was 
approved.   
 
There being no further business to discuss, a motion to adjourn the meeting at 
7:15 p.m. was made by Mr. Trate and seconded by Mr. Fryer.  The next meeting of the 
Planning Commission is scheduled to be held on Thursday, November 19th, 2026.
