Public Comments 
 
Mr. Deery called for public comment and recognized Paul Eckert, who queried if Council 
had made a decision regarding right-of-way weed spraying by the Borough for 2026.  Mrs. Loder 
stated that the decision Council made previously to discontinue weed spraying is still in place.  
He then queried who makes the decisions on the purchase of vehicles for the Eastern Berks 
Regional Police Department, and Mrs. Loder explained that it is the Eastern Berks Regional 
Police Commission and they usually discuss vehicles in August or September. 
 
Mr. Deery then recognized Karen Stauffer, who queried what the Recreation Fund is, and 
who controls it.  Mrs. Loder explained that the fund is where the proceeds from events like Fun 
Days are placed to then be used for improvements and projects for the Community Park.  
Borough Council makes the decisions regarding the use and allocation of those funds.  Ms. 
Stauffer queried if the Recreation Fund could be used towards the Community Pool, as the Pool 
is part of the Park.  Mrs. Loder clarified that the money in the Recreation Fund is not money 
derived from taxes, and reiterated that Borough Council has allocation authority over those 
funds.

Mr. Deery then recognized Denise Trout, who expressed her concern over the amount of 
unchecked weeds growing at the rear of her property that borders the Colebrookdale Railroad 
tracks at Shaner Street.  She reported that the Railroad people cleared out the area once last year 
and have not returned since that time.  Mrs. Loder explained that the Borough has no authority 
over the railroad property, as the land is actually owned by the Berks Redevelopment Authority.  
Mrs. Loder advised she would reach out to Josh Alderfer and Nathaniel Guest with the 
Colebrookdale Railroad about the situation.  
 
Mr. Deery then recognized Susan Eddinger, who queried if a decision had been made 
regarding the trap, neuter, and release situation for the feral cats.  Mrs. Rhodes explained that 
while nothing has been officially put in place, she reached out to some organizations that 
perform those services and Mrs. Loder was in touch with one organization about developing a 
contract for a voucher program.  More discussion will occur at the next meeting of the Public 
Communication Committee on July 1st, and anyone interested is welcome to attend.  Ms. 
Eddinger also wanted to express her concern to Council that the Boyertown Area Multi-Service 
is changing its daily hot meal Meals on Wheels delivery program to a twice-a-week delivery of 
frozen dinners.  The program is being handled by Helping Harvest and begins July 1st.      
 
 There being no further public comment or regular business to conduct, Council recessed 
to executive session to discuss a lease of real property.   
 
 At the conclusion of the Executive Session, the Council meeting reconvened at 8:15 p.m.  
Mr. Hartman announced that in executive session Council discussed a real property lease and 
that no official action by Council is required at this time.  There being no further business to 
conduct, a motion to adjourn the meeting at 8:15 p.m. was made by Mr. Neubauer and second by 
Mr. Cascino.  The next meeting of Boyertown Borough Council will be held on Monday, July 
6th, 2026, beginning at 7:00 p.m.
