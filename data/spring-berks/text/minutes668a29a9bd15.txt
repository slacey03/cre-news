PUBLIC SAFETY

A. Police Department — Police Accreditation Discontinuance and Consultant Usage — Chief
Brock stated that, after internal review, he is requesting authorization from the Board to
withdraw from the Pennsylvania Law Enforcement Accreditation Company ( PLEAC)
process and contract with the Daigle Law Group for policy review and implementation. Chief
Brock stated that Daigle Law Group is a National Law firm that provides best practices and
policies, specifically for Police Departments. Chief Brock reviewed the history of the
accreditation process and the current process, noting that the current process is redundant and
that changing it will free up manhours and save the Township a considerable amount of
money. The initial contract with the Daigle Law Group is $25,000.00, and they will review all
current policies; the Department will have training and testing, and after that, costs are not
mandated; they are on an as-needed basis. Chief Brock noted that the time officers currently
spend on the process amounts to approximately $150,000.00 annually, as well as the costs to
pay the Accreditation Commission to allow the Department to be accredited, pay the
assessors, pay for their hotels, meals, and proofs that the Department has to do. In the long
term, there will be definite cost savings to the Department.

Motion to approve withdrawing from the PLEAC Accreditation Process and contract with the
Daigle Law Group was made by Mr. Stuck and seconded by Mr. Zeoli. Upon roll call, all
Supervisors present voted affirmatively, MOTION CARRIED.
