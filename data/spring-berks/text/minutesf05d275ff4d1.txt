Police Building Renovation Project
Mr. Groller updated the Board on the process the Township has been pursuing for bidding,
construction, and repair of the police building, noting that there have been water issues at the
location for many years. Mr. Groller said that the Township is looking to replace the HVAC
Unit on the 2"? floor, upgrade the security camera system, and upgrade the sign on SR. 724,
among other things. Mr. Groller stated that the Township was awarded an LSA Grant for the
Police Building, totaling approximately $500,000.00, which expires in June 2027. Mr. Groller
briefed the Board on the bids that were received and the significant higher cost bid for the
project than the engineer's estimate. There would be additional engineering costs if the Board
opts to have an Engineering review to scale back the project. After discussion, the Board stated
they would like to review the grant application and the bid proposal for the project.
