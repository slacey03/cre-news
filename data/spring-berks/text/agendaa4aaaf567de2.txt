OPEN TO THE FLOOR:

John Martucci, 249 Keats Drive, stated that he is seeking approval to install a fence on his
property, noting that the Township denied his permit application due to a drainage easement that
is located in the back of his property. Mr. Martucci said that due to the location of his property,
at the corner of Keats Drive and Andrew Drive, he does not see an issue with installing a fence at
that location. Mr. Martucci indicated that most of his neighbors have fences in their backyard,
going down the property line, noting that the easement starts at the sidewalk and continues down
everyone’s backyard.

Mr. Royer questioned whether the other fences were permitted. Mr. Wozniak confirmed they
were possibly issued before 2004, noting that the Township does not issue permits in easements,
and said that during property transfer inspections, items identified that need to be removed or go
through the permitting process for approval are identified at that time. Mr. Martucci indicated
that they installed a temporary fence to let the dogs out, and that they would like to install a
wrought-iron fence, much like their neighbors.

Mr. Wozniak stated that the Township has a procedure that a resident may attend an
Infrastructure Committee (IC) meeting to discuss their particular easement and waiver request.
Occasionally waivers may be recommended for approval for certain structures such as fences or
sheds, and after review, the IC’s the recommendation is forwarded to the Board of Supervisors.
This easement is over a surface swale, there are no underground features, and the swale takes
water down to an inlet box approx. 7-8 properties down. Mr. Reichert indicated that the
easements are to be kept open, so water flows properly. Mr. Wozniak confirmed the fee for the
waiver is in the Township’s approved fee schedule, at a cost of $500.00, which is a simpler
procedure than going to the Zoning Hearing Board for a Variance, which has a fee of $1,500.00.

At the conclusion of the discussion, the Board directed Mr. & Mrs. Martucci to request the
waiver with the Infrastructure Committee (IC). The next IC Meeting is scheduled for Tuesday,
August 4"", at 8:30 a.m.

7. Contract Award- Colony Park Tennis Court Reconstruction-Ms. Houck presented two (2)
quotes, one submitted by AstroTurf Corporation in the amount of $128,125.00 and Keystone
Sports Construction in the amount of $156,172.39, for the reconstruction of the tennis courts
at Colony Park, noting that the project will be processed through the Commonwealth of
Pennsylvania’s CoStar Program. Ms. Houck explained that the difference between the two (2)
quotes is that the gold standard for Keystone Sports Construction is that they plan to put down
construction pads across the park. The drawing showed that they would enter the parking lot,
go across the turf at the park, and cross the Township’s sanitary sewer lines with their
equipment.

Mr. Becker confirmed for Mr. Royer that since both vendors are through CoStar, the Board
may select either bid. Ms. Houck indicated that the courts were installed in the 1970’s, and
there have been many repairs over the years, noting that these are the only two (2) tennis
courts in the Township. The Board indicated they would like revisions to the quotes to include
the following:
e Two (2) sets of lines (contrasting colors) painted on the courts, one for tennis, and one for
pickleball, using tennis nets.

e Cost revision for AstroTurf Corporation to use construction pads (Or guarantee to repair
turf after the project is complete).

Ms. Houck indicated that the performance bonds were not included in the quotes, and that
both vendors said that the performance bonds would add a 1.5% cost increase. Mr. Becker
stated that since both vendors have CoStar Contracts, they must follow State law, and they are
bound to the CoStar Contract and must provide a performance bond, indicating that the
CoStar Contract should be reviewed. After the discussion, the Board tabled this item until the
requested revisions to the quotes are received.
