ZONING REQUESTS:

2275 Reading Avenue — Special Exception Request

The subject property is a .77-acre commercial property located within the Neighborhood
Convenience (NC) Zoning District at the northeast corner of Reading Avenue and West
Wyomissing Blvd within West Wyomissing Development.

Since the last time the property was reviewed by the Planning Commission, a new owner
has purchased the property. The property currently consists of the former and currently
closed Matt Steaks, an apartment above the former Matt Steaks, Unit 3 (now Vacant)
which was the site of the former Salon, a vacant Contractors Office, Custom Creations
Auto, a warehouse, and the former Dolly’s Delites (subject area of the current SE Request).


The applicant has requested that the Zoning Hearing Board grant a special exception to permit
the operation of an automobile service station for motorcycles within the existing building
formerly occupied by Dolly’s Delights. The Motorcycle Service Station would occupy Unit 6
and Unit 7 Totaling 2,585 SF.

The applicant states that the motorcycle service station will be for the sale and installation of
motorcycle accessories. All work will be performed within closed interior service bays. The
application states that loud noises and excessive revving will be prohibited. Applicant further
states that no motorcycle parts or disassembled motorcycles will be stored outdoors and that off-
street parking will be provided.

The applicant’s description closely matched that of the definition of Automobile Service Station
in the Township Zoning Ordinance which is a Special Exception Use in the NC District.

In 2009 Custom Creation Auto had applied for a special exception use and was granted Special
Exception approval with conditions in place.

The applicant has applied to the Zoning Hearing Board for special exception approval. The
hearing is scheduled for Wednesday, September 23.

The applicant, Mr. Johnny Morales, began to speak about his shop and how it really does not
include any repair operations. The purpose of the business is to display and install accessories as
they are purchased. There will be no storage of unrepaired motorcycles since the purpose of the
shop is to sell and install custom, high-end accessories. No oil changes, sparkplug replacement or
any maintenance of a motorcycle will be performed on the premises. If your bike breaks down
and needs repair, I’m not your guy, says Mr. Morales. All installations will occur in their
enclosed shop.

Mr. Zaborowski asked if the automobile shop that is present on the site is conforming to the
Ordinance and he was told that they consistently violate the Ordinance by storing inoperable or
unlicensed vehicles outside beyond the maximum 7-day period.

Mr. Zaborowski explained the options available to the Planning Commission regarding the
subject Special Exception. The PC and Approve, deny, make no recommendation or
approve/deny with conditions. Prior to the motion, Mr. Becker asked Mr. Morales if he had
representation for the Zoning Hearing in which Mr. Morales responded, yes.

A Motion was made by Mr. Ulrich and seconded by Ms. Ehrlich to recommend the Zoning
Hearing board approve the Special Exception request for a motorcycle service station. Upon roll
call all members present voted affirmatively. MOTION PASSED.

802 Snyder Road (Bryla) Annexation/Subdivision

The subject property 802 Snyder Rd is a 14,386 square foot lot located on the south side of
Snyder Road between Gring Drive and Wassner Drive in the Whitfield Sec 1 development. 2303
Wassner Drive is a 10,698 square foot lot located adjacent and perpendicular to 802 Snyder Rd.
The applicant proposes to subdivide 6,386 sq feet from the existing 14,386 square foot lot and
Annex it onto the property known as 2303 Wassner Drive located perpendicular to 802 Snyder
which is owned by the same owner as 802 Snyder Rd.

Since the plans show a chicken pen at the rear corner of 2303 Wassner Drive which is a violation
of the MDS Zoning District that does not allow the keeping of fowl on land less than 1 acre. A
plan cannot be approved in violation of the Township Zoning Ordinance. The applicant can go
before the Zoning Hearing Board and request relief for this or remove the chickens. Since the
chickens were pets of the son who no longer resides at the property, the owner said they would
remove the chickens from the property. A note has been added to the plan.

The applicant has requested a waiver of the plan scale since the plans are drawn at a scale of
1”°=20-feet instead of the required 1”=50-feet. Since the 1”=20-feet provides more detail the
Township staff supports this change.

A motion to recommend the Board of Supervisors approve a waiver of plan scale was made by
Mr. Weisman and seconded by Mr. Ulrich. Upon roll call all members voted affirmatively.
MOTION PASSED.

Mr. Hoffert requested final plan approval based on the review letter of August 25", and the
updated note that was provided on the plans that will be reflected in the minutes from today.

A Motion was made by Mr. Ulrich seconded by Mr. Weisman recommending the Board of
Supervisors approve the Bryla Final Plan of 802 Snyder Rd conditioned upon items 1-4 in the
Engineering review letter dated August 25, 2026. Upon roll call all members voted affirmatively.
MOTION PASSED.
