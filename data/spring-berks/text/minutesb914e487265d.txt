9. EWO-Entech Engineering-Digester Conversion Feasibility Study

Mr. Conrad explained to the Board that there are large capital expenses for the
treatment plant in the upcoming years and that the digesters that hold the sludge until
ready for processing are already having issues. One digester has a big mushroom top
which collects the methane gas and is currently experiencing issues. Staff would like
Entech Engineering to do a Feasibility study on how the sludge is processed at the
treatment plant (aerobic vs anerobic) and determine which makes more sense for the
Township. The Township currently has anerobic digestors which produce the
methane which has to be collected or burned off. If we switched to aerobic the
digestors wouldn’t have to have the lids that collect methane and or the methane
burner. This item was discussed at the Infrastructure Committee who recommended
that the Board of Supervisor approve the Feasibility study.

Irrigation Subtraction meter request — Broadcast District

The Township has received a request from Broadcast District Developer for the
apartment buildings specifically regarding subtraction meters for the watering of green
spaces around the buildings so the water usage does not appear on their sewer bill.

Mr. Groller explains that there used to be a water subtraction meter program but has been
discontinued due to the amount of staff time needed to run the program. Mr. Groller
continues to explain that Adelphia Seafood has one for the ice making process at their
facility and Wilson School District has a subtraction meter for their cooling towers at
multiple schools and there are several others who are grandfathered under the old
program. The residential program was discontinued because it was burdensome on the
staff due to the number of requests and the fact that the bills have to be individually
processed separately from the other sewer bills. A question was asked if other developers
have made the same request. Mr. Wozniak answered no, and he suspects that if we open
the door, other developers and apartment communities may submit requests.

Motion to Act on the request to allow subtraction meter was Tabled by Chairperson
Royer because the Board would like additional information and more time to make a
decision.

2. 802 Snyder Rd —Subdivision/Annexation Conditional Plan Approval
802 Snyder Rd is a 14,386 sq ft lot located on the south side of Snyder Road between Gring

Drive and Wassner Drive in Whitfield Sec 1 Development. The applicant proposes to
subdivide 6,386 sq feet from the existing 14,386 square foot lot located at 802 Snyder Rd
and Annex it onto the rear of the property known as 2303 Wassner Drive. Both 802 Snyder
and 2303 Wassner Drive are owned by the same people. 802 Snyder Rd will be left with an
8,000 square foot lot which is the minimum lot size requirement in the Moderate Density
Suburban (MDS) District. 2303 Wassner will go from a 10,898 square foot lot to a 17,084
square foot lot after the annexation. Both lots meet the minimum lot size, lot width, and the
lot depth to width ratio requirements of the MDS District. During the plan review it was
noticed that there is a chicken pen at the back corner of the property at 2303 Wassner
Drive. The Zoning District does not allow the keeping of fowl on a lot smaller than 1 acre
and the structure does not meet setbacks. The SALDO does not allow a plan to be approved
in violation of the Zoning Ordinance. Township staff talked with the applicant, and they
have agreed to remove the Chickens from the property which is also noted on the plan.

The applicant is seeking Final Plan Approval for the Subdivision /Annexation Plan for 802
Snyder Rd. The Planning Commission at their public meeting held on Thursday,
September 3rd, 2026, voted to recommend that the Board of Supervisors approve the Bryla

1.The applicant remove the chicken and chicken pen from the property.
