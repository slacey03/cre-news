3) TAC Recommendations

a. No Parking-Eastbound Lane of Grandview Blvd—Mtr. Reichert stated that the Wilson School
District (WSD) made a formal request to the Township to prohibit parking on the eastbound
lane on Grandview Boulevard between Fairview Street and Riegel Avenue. Currently, there
is no parking at the location at certain times of the day. With students parking along that area,
it makes it difficult for bus traffic, emergency vehicles, and any larger vehicles to get
through. The TAC Committee requested that Mr. Simcox reach out to residents in the area to
ensure the change would not take parking away from the residents that live there.

Mr. Simcox stated that he sent letters out to the residents and the responses he received were
positive. He had reached out to fire and emergency services, which indicated the difficulty
navigating that road with students parking there. Now that the Performing Arts Center is
complete, there is ample parking for the students.

b. No Parking on Dorchester Avenue— Mr. Reichert stated this request is due to the new
elementary at Lincoln Park. The request is to make this no parking, as previously done for
Amherst Avenue, as this area will be a drop-off location for parents taking their kids to
school, and children crossing at the location on either side, which creates a visibility issue
with vehicles parked at that location. Mr. Simcox said that the Township making no parking
on Amherst Ave was a great success and indicated receiving a lot of positive feedback from
residents, noting that the WSD supports prohibiting parking on Dorchester Avenue.

c. S.A. Comunale Loading Area/Hrs.— Mr. Reichert stated that this request from S.A. Comunale,
located on Spring Street, was made because they have limited space for loading and
unloading. They do have some parking spaces in the back, but it is difficult for larger trucks
to access.

The request is to make the front of their business a loading zone only area, Monday through

d. No Parking on Hillvale Avenue —Mr. Reichert stated that this request is to make the section
of Hillvale Avenue and Spohn Road a no-parking area, as indicated on the exhibit. Earlier
this year, a stop sign was installed at the intersection, and as a result, there are offsets around
the stop sign that limit parking. Mr. Reichert indicated the home on the corner has started
parking on the lower side of their driveway, towards the intersection where the traffic light is
located. When you come around the curve, you have to drive around their vehicles to get
around to move back into the right turn lane, noting it also creates a visibility issue when you
are at the stop sign coming down toward the traffic light, as it is difficult to see past their
vehicles to see traffic coming up from the intersection.

The recommendation from the TAC Committee was to make the entire section from the
intersection of Shillington Road all the way up to the driveway, (where the stop sign is
located), as a no-parking area. The resident at that location does have a driveway, a garage,
and there is parking further up around the curve. Mr. Reichert indicated that when school
starts, that intersection gets a lot busier, with a lot more vehicles stacking at the light.

Mr. Conrad said that he stopped in, spoke to the resident, and discussed the situation and
why this is being recommended to the Board. Mr. Conrad stated the resident was very
understanding, and they had stopped parking at that location.

Motion to approve the four (4) areas requested for no-parking (items a-d), as recommended by
the TAC Committee, was made by Mr. Stuck and seconded by Mr. Wertz.

Mr. Reichert confirmed for Mr. Becker that the no-parking in front of S.A. Comunale is to
make that area in front of the business a loading zone only during the hours of Monday
through Friday from 8:00 a.m.- 4:00 p.m., and they will not park their business vehicles in
the loading zone. Residents will be able to park there during off-hours and weekends.

Upon roll call, all Supervisors present voted unanimously. MOTION CARRIED
