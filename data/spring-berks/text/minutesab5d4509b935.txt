TOWNSHIP DIRECTORS

A. Zoning/Building Codes Department

1. Public Gathering Permit-Wilson Junior Soccer Club-Mr. Wozniak stated that the
Township received the permit application for the annual tournament last week;
therefore, they did not meet the thirty (30) day requirement to submit a Public
Gathering Permit. Mr. Wozniak stated that the first order of business is whether the
Board will accept the late permit application.

Motion to accept the Public Gathering Permit application submitted by the Wilson Junior
Soccer Club was made by Mr. Stuck and seconded by Mr. Zeoli. Upon roll call, all
Supervisors present voted affirmatively, with Mr. Royer abstaining.

MOTION CARRIED 4-0-1

Mr. Wozniak stated that four (4) vendors are still completing the Township’s insurance
requirements, reviewing the open required permits and fees that have not been submitted.
The soccer tournament is scheduled for October 10" & 11". Ms. Houck asked whether
the park permit and keys to the parking lot should be issued to Wilson Soccer Club if the
Food Truck Vendors do not meet Township requirements, because their permit
application states they will have these vendors at the event. After discussion, the Board
agreed to have Ms. Houck revise the Wilson Soccer Club Permit to state that no food
trucks are permitted if the vendors do not meet the requirements by October 7", 2026.

Motion to authorize issuance of the Public Gathering Permit if all requirements are met and
all required fees are paid to the Township by October 7", 2026 was made by Mr. Stuck and
seconded by Mr. Wertz. Upon roll call, all Supervisors present voted affirmatively, except for
Mr. Royer, who abstained. MOTION CARRIED 4-0-1.

Letter from Resident— Speeding Issues on Montello Road— The Board received a letter from a
resident about traffic concerns on Montello Road. Mr. Conrad confirmed that South Heidelberg
Township installed an additional speed limit sign. After reviewing the issue with the
Transportation Advisory Committee (TAC), staff will install oversized speed limit signs in that
area. Mr. Conrad confirmed to Mr. Stuck that the speed humps in Sinking Spring Borough were
installed recently, and there is no word yet on how it will affect snow plowing. Chief Hackman
added that emergency vehicles are slowed more than they need to be in an emergency, and that
speed humps are bad for the trucks; additionally, ambulances are bumpy to begin with a patient
in the back, so going over speed humps is not a good scenario.

Mr. Groller stated that at everyone’s seats is communication from District #50 from PennDOT
regarding the Dwight Street Bridge.
