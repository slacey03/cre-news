CALL TO ORDER: Chairperson Royer called a regular business meeting of the Board of

AGENDA AMENDMENTS: None

OPEN TO THE FLOOR:

Todd Hiester, a lifelong resident of the Township at 508 Albert Drive, thanked Mr. Groller and
Randy Fultz for their time prior to the meeting listening to his concerns. Mr. Hiester gave a
summary of the history of sinkhole issues on Albert Drive, noting that there was a period of 6-7
years in a row when sinkholes occurred. Mr. Hiester stated that his intent is to be proactive
before any additional digging or repairs are done in the area, so that he can access his driveway
with his motorhome and car trailer. Mr. Groller confirmed that the Township is awaiting receipt
of the Study Results from Geo-Tech and their recommendation. Mr. Royer thanked Mr. Hiester
and said that residents will be kept informed.

Mr. Royer recessed the regular meeting to open the duly advertised scheduled Public Hearing
for Raising Cane’s Restaurants LLC.

Mr. Becker stated that Raising Cane’s Restaurants LLC requested an additional continuance. Mr.
Becker entered as exhibits- Exhibit 2, the request of a continuance submitted by Raising Cane’s
Restaurants LLC’s Attorney from the last meeting to hold the Hearing this evening, and Exhibit
3, the request for a continuance from their Attorney submitted for this evening with a request to
continue the Hearing on Monday, June 22", 2026, at 7:15 p.m., at the Township Administrative
Building.


Motion of approval to reconvene the Public Hearing for Raising Cane’s Restaurants LLC on
Monday, June22™ at 7:15 p.m. at the Township’s Administrative Building was made by Mr.
Stuck and seconded by Mr. Wertz. Upon roll call, all Supervisors present voted affirmatively.
MOTION CARRIED

Mr. Royer recessed the Public Hearing and reconvened the regular Board of Supervisors
Meeting.

8. Sign & Paving Recommendation- Chapel Hill Road

Mr. Conrad noted that the residents who live beyond the curve on Chapel Hill Road
previously attended a Board Meeting to discuss their concerns about speeding and incidents
involving their home and vehicle. LTAP went to the site with Mr. Conrad and Mr. Fultz and
provided a recommendation to the Township. LTAP recommends adding signage and
delineator posts in that area, as well as a slow speed insignia at the turn to alert motorists. Mr.
Conrad stated that the Transportation Advisory Committee reviewed the recommendation this
morning and recommended approval of the additional signage.

9. No Parking Recommendation- Amherst Avenue

Mr. Reichert briefed the Board on the background of this area, noting the curve that leads up
to the former Lincoln Park Elementary School. The road is very narrow, and if cars are parked
on both sides, you cannot see around the curve. After the school went dormant, the Township
removed the no-parking signs in that area. Mr. Reichert stated that since the school will be
used again, it is recommended that this area be posted as a no-parking zone. The
Transportation Advisory Committee reviewed the request and recommended that the Board
authorize making this area a no-parking zone.
