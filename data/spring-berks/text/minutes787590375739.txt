C. Engineering/Planning Department
1. 1600 Whitfield Boulevard-Variance Request Rear Yard Setback — Ms. Bensinger presented

the applicant’s request for the property located at the southwest corner of the intersection of
Whitfield Boulevard and Dalin Drive in the Whitfield Sec 4 Development. The applicant
requested a variance from the rear yard setback requirement to allow enclosure of the
existing porch for a kitchen addition. At their Public Meeting held on October 1‘, 2026, the
Planning Commission voted to recommend that the Zoning Hearing Board approve the
Variance Request.

Motion to recommend that the Zoning Hearing Board approve the Variance Request for 1600

Whitefield Boulevard was made by Mr. Stuck and seconded by Mr. Wertz. Upon roll call, all

Supervisors in attendance voted unanimously. MOTION CARRIED

2. 10 Beaver Road-Self Storage — Mr. Reichert presented the applicant's requests for 10 Beaver
Road.

a) Sidewalk Waiver — Section 503(C)(G)(12) requires installation of sidewalks along
Township road frontage. In this case, no viable location/connection exists for a new
sidewalk along Beaver Road, and the applicant has requested a waiver of this
requirement. At their Public Meeting on October 1‘, 2026, the Planning Commission
voted to recommend that the Board of Supervisors approve this request.

Motion to approve the Sidewalk Waiver request from Section 503(C)(G)(12) was made by Mr.
Stuck and seconded by Mr. Royer. Upon roll call, all Supervisors present voted affirmatively.
MOTION CARRIED

b) Conditional Plan Approval-Mr. Reichert presented the request and history of the
project. Mr. Reichert said that the new proposed plan includes a total of 109 units,
noting it is slightly different from the initial plan submitted in 2023. Mr. Reichert
briefed the Board on discussion with the applicant regarding two (2) paper streets that
cut through the property, noting a requirement will be noted on the plan stating that the
applicant is responsible for the paper streets and the Township will be “held
harmless”.

At their public meeting held on October 1*, 2026, the Planning Commission voted to
recommend that the Board of Supervisors approve the Preliminary Plan conditioned on
the comments in the September 25", 2026 Engineering review letter.

Motion to approve the Preliminary Plan for 10 Beaver Road-Self Storage conditioned upon all
comments are addressed in the September 25", 2026 Engineering review letter was made by Mr.
Stuck and seconded by Mr. Wertz. Upon roll call, all Supervisors present voted affirmatively.
MOTION CARRIED
