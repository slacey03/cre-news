CALL TO ORDER: Chairperson Royer called the Budget Meeting of the Board of Supervisors
to order at 5:55 p.m. on Monday, October 26", 2026, in the Public Meeting Room of the
Township of Spring Administration Building, located at 2850 Windmill Road, Spring Township,
Berks County, Pennsylvania.

ROLL CALL: Upon roll call, Supervisors Lauren Robertson, George Stuck III, Michael Wertz,
and Jesse Royer were present. Supervisor Zach Zeoli was unable to attend. Also present were
Township Manager John Groller, Assistant Township Manager Dean Murray, Township
Secretary Mary Rossi, GIS Engineering & Planning Coordinator Jen Bensinger, Information
Systems Manager Jeremy Baez-Reyes, Fire Chief Colin Hackman, Deputy Fire Chief Johnathan
Fritz, Parks and Recreation Director Judy Houck, Public Works Director Craig Conrad, Director
of Zoning & Codes Enforcement Aaron Wozniak, Director of Engineering & Planning Jason
Reichert, Police Chief Stephen Brock, and Solicitor Dan Becker. Sheryl Lynn Sonon joined the
meeting via TEAMS at 6:39 p.m.

BUDGET MEETING: Mt. Groller explained the budget review timeline. There was discussion

on proposed budget numbers for the Fire Department, Parks and Recreation Department, Public

Works, and the Police Department. Key discussion points were staffing, operating expenses, and
capital expenses.

After discussion, the Board agreed to meet on Thursday, November 5 at 5:00 p.m. (Before the
7:00 p.m. Planning Commission Meeting) to review the proposed budget. Ms. Rossi will
advertise the meeting.

REGULAR MEETING:
Mr. Royer reconvened the regular Board of Supervisors meeting at 7:07 p.m.

Attendees: In addition to those listed above, Toni Tucci of Western Berks Ambulance, Todd
Hiester of Albert Drive, and Ron Lewars of Fritztown Road attended.

AGENDA AMENDMENTS: None


OPEN TO THE FLOOR:
Todd Hiester, 508 Albert Drive- Mr. Hiester requested a status on the study results for Albert
Drive.

Ron Lewars- 983 Fritztown Road-Mr. Lewars addressed the Board concerning his trash hauler,
J.P. Mascaro & Sons Inc. leaving toters in the road. Mr. Lewars noted the Recycling Company
the Township contracts with does a nice job. Mr. Lewars stated that about 4 weeks ago, the toter
was left in the road; a truck came by, smashed into the toter, spun out, hit his lamp post, and
broke his light. Mr. Lewars contacted J.P. Mascaro, and they sent the video to their insurance
company, who admitted no liability. Mr. Lewars provided a video to the Township and stated
that if the Township could get him a new light, that would be great.
