1. 4190 Shimerville Rd & 4135 Mill Rd Lot Line Adjustment 
 
Planning Coordinator Miller read the prepared letter regarding the lot line adjustment: 
 
At their meeting on September 28th, 2026 the Planning Commission reviewed the Frank & 
Deborah A. Kuklis, 4190 Shimerville Road & William S. Wall, 4135 Mill Road Lot Line 
Adjustment. The Planning Commission discussed the road Right of way along the property 
involved and recommends the Board accept the dedication along Shimerville Road and an 
easement area now shown on the plan in the intersection of 4190 Shimerville Road (shown on 
the attached). The Planning Commission also recommended approval of the Lot Line 

 
 
Page 3 of 4 
Adjustment plan contingent on addressing the staff municipal review letter comments, Lehigh 
Valley Planning Commission approval and payment of any outstanding fees. Currently the 
applicants Engineer has addressed staff comments and the LVPC has responded favorably.   
If you chose to accept the dedication of right of way along Shimerville Road and the easement 
area shown on the attached sheet on the 4190 Shimerville Road property, any and all required 
legal documentation should be reviewed/completed and approved by the Township Solicitor 
along with review and approval of the new deed descriptions for both 4190 Shimerville Road & 
4135 Mill Road, and any required additional notes on the plan that may be required by the 
Solicitor.   
Approval can be made contingent on addressing the items above along with payment of any 
Township fees acquired in the process. 
 
Planning Coordinator Miller referred to the map included for the Board’s review. Applicant William 
(Bill) Wall, and Martin (Marty) Smith PE with Pany & Lentz Engineering were in the audience to 
answer questions regarding the lot line adjustment. Supervisor Ashbrook asked what the reasoning 
was for the lot line adjustment. Marty explained it is an adjustment between the two properties to 
equalize areas for the houses. There was a brief discussion with the Board and Planning Coordinator 
Miller regarding bridge work and clean up needed at the corner of Mill Road.  
 
MOTION: Supervisor Zgura made a motion to accept the lot line adjustment of 4190 Shimerville Road 
and 4135 Mill Road contingent on addressing items addressed in the letter dated October 15, 2026, 
and payment of any Township fees acquired in the process. Solicitor Fisher added dedication to the 
Township for the ultimate right-of-way along 4190 Shimerville Road shown in the plan and 5-foot 
easement on 4190 Shimerville Road and 9-foot easement along 4135 Mill Road as shown in the plan. 
Supervisor Ashbrook seconded the motion. Hearing no questions or comments, all were in favor, 
motion carried.
