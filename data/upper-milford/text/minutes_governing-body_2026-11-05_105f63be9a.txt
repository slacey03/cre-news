2. Approval of Joint Municipal Salt Bid  
 
Manager Carter read the prepared memo: 
 
Over the past two winters we have had problems with salt deliverers when the demand for 
road salt is high, and the suppliers are getting low on their stockpile.   
 
To make sure that the Township has an adequate supply of road salt I have joined in on the 
Lehigh County Consortium joint salt bid. The Salt Bid was advertised by South Whitehall 
Township; the lowest bidder was Eastern Salt Company at $87.90 per ton.     
 
I would like the Board to approve the Township part of the joint salt bid for 200 tons from 
Eastern Salt Company at a per ton price of $87.90. 
 
Supervisor Zgura asked what our primary salt price is. Manager Carter explained we use Costars, 
priced at $85 per ton. Costars would be used first; Eastern Salt Company would be used as a backup 
if we are low on our allotment or supply is not coming in. There is no minimum order. 
 
MOTION: Supervisor Zgura made a motion to accept the joint salt bid for Eastern Salt Company for 
$87.90 per ton. Supervisor Mohr seconded the motion. Hearing no questions or comments, all were 
in favor, motion carried.

4. Discussion about John Deere 5100M Roadside mower repairs 
 
Manager Carter read the prepared memo: 
 
The 2017 John Deere 5100M used for mowing along the sides of the roads and is equipped 
with a Tiger Wildkat side shift 75-inch flail head mower was purchased for $106,655.80 in 2017 
new. 
 
The tractor started to give us problems; once it warmed up, warning lights would come on and 
the tractor itself would not move forward or backwards.  Lee Marks consulted with Deere 
Country, and they said it would be best for us to bring the tractor into them to diagnose what is 
going on with the tractor. After running some tests, it was determined that the tractor was 
losing pressure in the rear, but in order to find out what was going on in the inside of the rear 
axle, the rear axle had to be split open. In order to do that, the cab and mower had to be 
removed from the tractor to complete the diagnostics. 
 
Once the rear was disassembled, Deere Country found that one the bearing for the axle had 
broken and was spinning in the center housing of the rear and caused metal fragments to 
enter the hydraulic system of the tractor causing the pressure loss.  They also found that the 
differential had also been damaged. (please see pictures attached)    
 
We have received a service estimate to fix it with the diagnostic fee included in the amount of 
$32,670.44. After seeing the price to fix, we asked about other options. Trading in the tractor 
as is; we were given a price of $6,000. Fixing it and then trading it in on a new one we were 
given an estimate of $24,000 to $34,000. The amount given to replace the tractor and mower 
was $256,009.62. 
 
Supervisor Zgura asked for clarification, there was a short discussion on the options and pricing. 
Manager Carter mentioned the recommendation from Lee Marks is to spend the money to fix the 
equipment, run it and hope it does not happen again. If it happens again, we would know what the 
problem is, and try to sell as is rather than fixing again. Supervisor Zgura mentioned it is only 10 
years only. Both Supervisor Mohr and Zgura agreed it would be best to fix the equipment.  
 
MOTION: Supervisor Mohr made a motion to spend the estimate of $32,670.44 to fix the John Deere 
5100M Roadside Mower. Supervisor Zgura seconded the motion.
