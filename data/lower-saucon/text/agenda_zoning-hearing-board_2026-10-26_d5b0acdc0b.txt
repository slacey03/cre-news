Mory Variance, 09-26 – William and Kellee Mory, 1917 Dartford Road, Bethlehem, 
PA 18015, time limit waived  
 Zoning Officer Tyler Mitch was sworn in and testified that Applicant is seeking 
relief from §180-35 Yard Requirements and §180-127.3 Impervious and building 
coverage charts for the purpose of constructing a two-car garage, breezeway, and 
covered patio.  The Township took no position regarding this request.  Property is 
located in the R40 Rural Suburban District.   
 Paul Harak, Esq. presented an overview of the Application and relief sought by 
the Applicant explaining that Applicant intended to remove the existing shed on the 
property and replace the shed with a two-car door garage, breezeway, and patio.   
 William Thomas Barlow, P.E., was recognized as an expert in engineering and 
land surveying.  Mr. Barlow explained the submitted plans, which were marked as 
Exhibit A-1.  Mr. Barlow testified that the setback of the existing shed is non-conforming 
at 9.88 feet.  The proposed garage would be setback 6.23 feet, which is well-short of 
the required 30-foot setback.  The current building coverage is 11.2%, which is non-
conforming as it exceeds the 8% allowed by the Zoning Ordinance. The proposed plan 
would increase this coverage to 13.6%.  Mr. Barlow explained that the parcel at 1917 
Dartford Road is smaller than many of the parcels in the surrounding area and contains 

Page 3 of 5 
 
a ranch-style home while many of the surrounding homes are two stories.  The size and 
shape of the lot constrain Applicant’s ability to construct a usable garage that can be 
connected to the home.   
 William Mory was sworn in and testified regarding the size of the lot, confirming 
that it is smaller than most of the surrounding lots and contains a smaller home than 
many of the surrounding lots, as it is a ranch-style home.  The Lentzs own the home 
behind the Mory lot and sent in a letter of support for the project and plan.  Mr. Hock, a 
direct neighbor of the Morys, testified at the last hearing regarding his support of the 
project and plan.  Mr. Mory explained that the existing two car garage does not fit two 
modern cars and the lawnmower.  He also testified regarding the need for the 
breezeway to protect household members from the weather between the home and the 
garage.  The garage and breezeway project includes coverage to the existing patio.  Mr. 
Mory developed the plan with the intention of matching the current exterior of the home 
so it does not appear to be a separate structure.     
 No other members of the community wished to be heard on the Mory matter.      
 Mr. Gorzynski motioned to grant the relief in the form of both variances 
requested.  Mr. Easley seconded and the motion was passed 3-0.   
Steely Variance 11-26 - Rickey and Timothy Steely, 2485 Applebutter Road, 
Hellertown, PA, 18055 - 10/23/2026  
 Zoning Officer Tyler Mitch was sworn in and testified that Applicant is seeking 
relief from §180-23(A) Front Yard Setback Line and Lot Line to construct a shed on an 
existing concrete pad.  The Township took no position regarding this request.  Property 
is located in the RA Rural Agriculture.   

Page 4 of 5 
 
 Matthew Goodrich, Esq. represented the Applicant and provided a summation of 
the relief sought.  Attorney Goodrich explained that a barn previously existed near the 
concrete pad and that barn was closer to the property line than the proposed shed.  As 
the concrete pad is pre-existing, there is no increase to impervious coverage.  The 
parcel of land is quite small at a quarter of an acre and it is not possible to construct a 
shed on the parcel and meet the required setbacks.   
 Timothy Steely was sworn-in and testified regarding the history of the property.  
The property currently operates as part of Red Barn Auto, which has been in business 
for 47 years.  A previous structure, an old barn, was removed from the parcel in 2022.  
The proposed shed was setback further than the previous barn.  The shed will match 
the other buildings on the properties.  The cement pad remains from previous housing.  
The Applicants wish to construct a shed on the property to store antique cars.  Mr. 
Steely testified that the property is surrounded by land owned by the Steelys.  The 
combined Steely property is surrounded on three sides by property owned by the 
landfill.  The Boardmans reside across the street at 2473 Applebutter Road.   
 Gregory Boardman was sworn-in and testified regarding his support for the 
project.  Mr. Boardman has owned the property across the street for approximately ten 
years and does not object to the proposed shed.   
 No other members of the public wished to be heard on this Application.   
 Dr. Carolan motioned approved to the requested relief from the setback 
ordinance.  Mr. Gorczynski seconded the motion and it passed 3-0.
