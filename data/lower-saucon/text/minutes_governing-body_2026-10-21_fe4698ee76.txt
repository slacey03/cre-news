 A. HELLERTOWN AREA LIBRARY (YT: 11:21) 
 
Ms. Opthof-Cordaro welcomed Ali Finkbeiner from the Hellertown Area Library to give a presentation 
and proposals for next year. 
 
Ali Finkbeiner thanked Council for having her here this evening.  She has been the Director of the 
Hellertown Area Library (HAL) for a little over two years now.  She thanked the Township for their 
support of the HAL.  Some highlights for this year so far: 
 
• They received an anonymous donation of $50,000. 
• Installed a new patio as part of our “Pave the Way” project. That was a fundraiser that started 
pre-COVID and they were finally able to wrap that up.  
• They were a recipient of the Main Street grant through the Chamber and they will be putting 
two new benches outside on the new patio.   Through that $50,000 donation, they were able to 
get new furniture for the basement and new chairs for the staff.  
• Purchased a mini split for the basement because the heating system wasn’t the greatest.   
• Got the outside professionally landscaped, which also was another big highlight for the library.  
• Their biggest accomplishment that they are working on is transferring over to a new circulation 
cataloging software through the Pennsylvania Integrated Library System called PALS. 
Migration date is set for December 3, 2026. The migration was paid for through a Library 
Services Technology Act grant.   
• They rebar coded over 33,000 items in the library and issued and continue to issue new patron 
cards.  
• The best part of this transition is hopefully in 2027 they will be participating in resource sharing, 
and it's going to be a better experience for patrons and for staff. It's just a more sophisticated 
system.  They were at a resource sharing meeting with this shared catalog. Our patrons are going 
to have access to almost 1.5 million items to borrow.  In April 2025 through 2026, 275,000 
items were shared throughout libraries in Northampton and Lehigh County and items placed on 
hold will be delivered directly to the Hellertown Area Library for easy pick up.  This delivery 
system is provided through our district library, which is the Bethlehem area public library. 
• Until everybody's trained and they are comfortable with everything, she’s looking at resource 
sharing to start sometime in 2027.  
• Some statistics for 2026 so far, the Township has 2,859 card holders right now.  The end of 
January 2025, there were 1,864 card holders. 
• The township so far has circulated 24,181 items, and as a point of reference, as in September of 
2025, they had 23,003 items circulated, so almost 1000 more items. 
• Having a library card beyond books available at the Hellertown Library, patrons can check out 
museum passes, theme backpacks for youth.  They have Tony players and Tony figures and 
Yodo players and Yodo cards, which are just different ways for children to listen to stories other 
than on CD.   They have ukuleles, a seed library, computers, faxing, scanned email, copying 
and printing through our district with your Hellertown Library card. In Bethlehem, you can have 
access to interlibrary loan cloud library, which is e-books and e-audio, and Kanopy, which is a 
streaming service which has a side for adults and a side for children. 
• Through the PA Power Library with your library card, you have access to OverDrive and Libby, 
which is also e-audio and e-audio and e-books.  
• You have Cloud Library, which is e-audio only.  
• You have research databases.  
• You have a foreign language learning database s you can tap into and there's fabulous job 
resources through the PA Power Library.   These are all resources that you have access to with 
your Hellertown Area Library card.
