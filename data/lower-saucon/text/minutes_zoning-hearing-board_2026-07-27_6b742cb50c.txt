Hearings 
Hendricks/Ochse Variance, 06-26 – Brian Hendricks and Samantha Ochse, 3641 
Old Philadelphia Pike, Bethlehem, PA 18015, exp. 8/23/2026 

Page 2 of 4 
 
 Zoning Officer Tyler Mitch was sworn in and testified that Applicant is seeking 
relief from §180-128(C) – structure setbacks in order to construct an attached deck at 
the rear of the existing single-family home located in the R12 zoning district.   
 Township Council submitted a letter of confirming they were not taking a position 
on the requested variance on July 16, 2026.   
 Mr. Hendricks was sworn in and testified regarding the plan.  Mr. Hendricks 
explained that the placement of the home on the property, as well as the location of the 
back door makes it possible to construct a deck and meet the required setback.  The 
back door faces south and is four feet off the ground, currently with concrete steps.  The 
proposed deck will face one of the walls of the next-door neighbor’s garage, so it does 
not create any privacy concerns.   
 No members of the community were present to be heard on the request.     
 Mr. Carolan moved for the approval of the requested variance, Mr. Jauregui 
seconded and the motion passed by a vote of 4 to 0.   
Royse Variance, 08-26 – Eric and Teather Royse, 1483 Colesville Road, 
Bethlehem, PA 18015, exp. 8/25/2026 
Zoning Officer Tyler Mitch was sworn in and testified that Applicant is seeking 
relief from §180-35(C) – Yard Requirements in order to construct an addition on an 
existing single-family home located in the R-40 Zoning District.    
 Township Council submitted a letter of confirming they were not taking a position 
on the requested variance on July 16, 2026.   
Attorney Samuel Morgan appeared on behalf of the Applicant and provided 
opening remarks and comments before proceeding with testimony.   

Page 3 of 4 
 
 Mr. Royse was sworn in and testified regarding the revised plan.  Mr. Royse 
explained that they have owned the home for twenty years and wish to construct an 
addition to allow for comfortable accommodations when their now adult children visit 
with their significant others.  The existing home is an 1870’s farmhouse and the Royses 
intend to keep as much of the character of the home as possible with the addition.  In 
order to maintain that character, they are seeking to add an addition that matches the 
current structure and does not require the removal of very established trees.  The 
addition will include an enhanced master suite and great room.  The architect presented 
several options, but in order to maintain the property’s trees, the existing patio, and 
view, as well as maintain the same profile of the existing home, the yard requirements 
cannot be met without the requested variance.   
The requested variance is less than the current non-conforming yard of the 
home.  The existing setback is 22.8 feet and the requested setback is 23.9 feet.  This 
addition will keep the profile of the home the same with the difference being due to the 
fact that the home is not parallel to the road and the front of the home does not face the 
street.  The sight line regarding egress or ingress to the home will be unchanged.  Ms. 
Royse testified that she spoke with the neighbors about the plans and no concerns were 
mentioned.   
No members of the community were present to be heard on the request.     
 Mr. McGovern moved for the approval of the requested variance, Mr. Jauregui 
seconded and the motion passed by a vote of 4 to 0.
