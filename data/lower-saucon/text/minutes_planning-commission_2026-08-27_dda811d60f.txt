A. Review of Proposed Ordinance for Impervious Coverage for recommendation to Council 
 
Chris Nagy stated we  have a couple of miscellaneous items, business items. Item A is the review of 
proposed ordinance for improvements coverage for recommendation to Council. 
 
Andrew Youssef stated if you guys don't mind, Brien Kocher from our office actually prepared a nice 
summary of this ordinance amendment and sent it to Jay Finnigan. So, if you guys don't mind, I'm just 
going to read it off to you, so it gives you a nice synopsis of what this ordinance revision entails. So,  
in 2010 and 2011, Township Council was reviewing several Zoning Hearing Board requests for  a 
variance of exceeding the allowable impervious cover for lots within several developments. Township 
Council created the impervious cover table in section 180 -127.3 to address concerns of residents that 
their initial zoning permit application submitted generally by builders, was not leaving enough 
impervious coverage on the lot to allow for further development of the lot, such as decks and pools. As 
a result of residents' concerns, Township Council indicated that they supported a reduction in allowable 
impervious cover for the initial permit applications generally submitted by builders. So this was around 
the 2010 to 2011 mark, hence why I'm reading this because I wasn't around during that time. Although 
Township Council's intent was to protect future residents' development of their lot by limiting the 
builder's initial impervious coverage request, a chart in section 127.3 also contained significant 
reductions in the overall allowable impervious cover by enacting an  overall site impervious cover 
provision, which included impervious cover associated with the development's roads. It also 
established the maximum impervious cover amount that the residential building could occupy. As a 
result of this table created in 2011 , we have seen that many residents experienced restrictions on the 
development of their lot, which we believe was not the intent of Township Council in 2011. We have 
also seen an example of a sketch plan submitted by a developer in an R20 zoning district, which shows 
that the lots within the development may not be developed to any reasonable extent by complying with 
the requirements in this table contained in Section 127.3. As a result, Township staff at Township 
Council's direction has prepared an amendment to the Township Zoning Ordinance, which would 
eliminate this table in Section 127.3, and return to the impervious coverage requirements in place at 
the time of the creation of this table within each zoning district. To address Township Council's 
concerns regarding the builder's initial permit application. We have added language that if the initial 
building permit application is submitted by a builder, they are permitted a maximum of 85% of the 
zoning district's impervious cover. We have also included language with which reiterate s that the 
impervious coverage created on the lot must not exceed a subdivision's assumption of impervious cover 
related to the design of the subdivision stormwater management facilities. That being said, if you have 
any questions, I know I just threw a lot at you guys, but feel free to ask.
