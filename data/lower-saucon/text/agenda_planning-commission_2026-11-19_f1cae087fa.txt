Section 3. Inserting § 180-127.4. Planned Residential Development. 
 
Chapter 180 is hereby amended to insert the following: 
 
§ 180-127.4. Planned Residential Development. 
 
A. Definitions. 
 
“Gross area of the development” means the total area, expressed in acres, within 
the exterior boundaries of the contiguous lot or lots proposed for the P.A.D., 
measured before deduction of floodplain, steep slope, wetlands, other natural-
resource protection land, required open space, required recreation area, easements, 
or areas devoted to buildings, parking, driveways, or internal streets. Gross area 
does not include land within an existing or proposed public street right-of-way. 
 
“Dwelling-unit density” means the maximum number of dwelling units permitted, 
calculated by multiplying the gross area of the development by the permitted 
dwelling units per acre. A fractional result shall be rounded down to the next whole 
dwelling unit.

3 
 
B. Additional requirements. 
 
 (1) Public water and sewer are required. 
 
 (2) Waste disposal areas shall require a planting screen in accordance  
  with the requirements of this chapter and Chapter 145. 
 
 (3) Buffer yards are required around the perimeter of the site in   
  accordance with § 180-97, except in the required front yard.  
 
 (4) Fifteen percent of the gross area of the development shall be provided  
  and maintained as open space, in addition to the recreation fee   
  required by Chapter 145, Subdivision and Land Development. The  
  open space area shall contain a clubhouse available to all residents of  
  the P.A.D. The open space area shall also include elements of active  
  recreation (e.g. swimming pool, tennis courts, etc.), and a walking  
  path.  
 
 (5) No single structure shall exceed 225 feet in length. 
 
 (6) No single structure shall contain more than 36 dwelling units. 
 
 (7) No garden-apartment structure shall be closer than 20 feet to another  
  structure. Accessory parking lots or parking garages shall be no closer 
  than ten (10) feet to a garden-apartment structure. 
 
 (8) Minimum landscape stock. 
  
  (a) P.A.D. structures shall be landscaped with minimum landscape  
   stock  of one deciduous tree and three shrubs per dwelling unit. 
 
  (b) All stock is to be of nurseryman quality and in accordance with  
   Chapter 145, Subdivision and Land Development. 
 
 (9) Variations in roofline and setbacks in building facades shall be   
  provided. 
 
C. Lot and Density requirements. 
 
 (1) Area: minimum 14 acres.

 (2) Width: minimum 250 feet. 
 
 (3) Impervious coverage: maximum 70%. 
 
 (4) Height: maximum 50 feet and maximum 3 habitable stories. 
 
 (5)   Maximum dwelling-unit density: 18 dwelling units per acre of gross  
  area of the development, calculated in accordance with Subsection  
  A. 
 
D. Yard requirements. 
 
(1) Front yard: minimum 50 feet. 
 
(2) Side yard: minimum 25 feet. 
 
(3) Rear yard: minimum 25 feet. 
 
E. Natural resource mitigation alternative.  
 
An applicant proposing a P.A.D. may elect to develop under this subsection. An 
applicant proceeding under this subsection shall be exempt from the natural 
resource protection rates of § 180-95.B and may exceed the net buildable site area 
permitted by § 180-95.C(2)(c) (Permitted net buildable site area), using a greater 
area of natural resource protection land than would be otherwise permitted by the 
resource protection standards contained in § 180-95 herein (an “excess resource 
utilization”), provided the applicant dedicates land to the Township for 
preservation in an amount equal to the excess resource utilization as provided 
below. 
 
(1) The excess resource utilization shall be the difference between (a) the 
resource protection land calculated under § 180-95.C(2)(b); and (b) the 
applicant's proposed resource protection land, which is the difference 
between the base site area pursuant to § 180-95.C(2)(a) and applicant's 
proposed buildable site area. The Township may accept a dedication in 
excess of what is required. 
 
(2) The applicant shall submit drawings and calculations clearly showing the 
resource protection land, the proposed resource protection land, applicant's 
proposed buildable site area, excess resource utilization, and the minimum

use in accordance with this subsection. The submission shall include an

(3) The land proposed for dedication need not contain the same environmental 
resources as the resource protection lands on the subject property, but it

(a) be located within Lower Saucon Township;

not acquire sufficient qualifying land on commercially reasonable 
terms and conditions. 
 
(b) If the applicant demonstrates that, after a good faith effort, they were 
unable to obtain, on commercially reasonable terms and conditions,
