Hearings 
Midlam Variance, 05-26 – Roy and Lauren Midlam, 2022 Springtown Hill Road, 
exp. 7/14/2026 

Page 2 of 3 
 
 Zoning Officer Tyler Mitch was sworn in and testified that Applicant is seeking 
relief from §180-95B(4)(b) – Floodplain soils in order to construct a single-family home 
on the property located in the R-4 Zoning District.   
 Applicant previously sought relief from the Zoning Hearing Board for the a 
substantially similar project and was denied.  The relief included a higher amount of 
disturbance, as well as a variance for environmentally sensitive woodlands.  The 
revised plan does not require a variance for the woodlands and reduces the disturbance 
of floodplain soils from .528 acres to .482 acres.    
 Township Council submitted a letter of support on June 22, 2026 confirming their 
support of the relief sought after presentations made to council about the plan including 
the Applicant’s plans regarding the barn.   
Attorney Joseph Bubba appeared on behalf of the Applicant and provided 
opening remarks and comments before proceedings with testimony.   
 Mr. Midlam was sworn in and testified regarding the revised plan.  Mr. Midlam 
explained that the property contains existing structures including a barn, cottage, and 
horse arena, as well as some other small outbuildings.  The property also contains 
natural resources including a pond, stream, and wetlands.  The Midlams wish to 
construct a single-family residence on the property.  Mr. Midlam testified to the plans to 
ultimately preserve and restore the barn, as well as perform needed maintenance on 
the cottage and remove the horse arena.  Mr. Midlam explained his desire to preserve 
the natural resources on the property including adding native trees.  He testified to the 
reasons for the placement of the home and why the placement of the home would 

Page 3 of 3 
 
cause the least disturbance to the property, but also allow for safe egress and ingress.  
Mr. Midlam also provided letters of support from neighbors.  
 Scott Pasterski, Engineer, was sworn in and testified to the particulars of this 
property including its unique shape and the existing historical elements, natural 
resources, and varied topography.  Mr. Pasterski explained the difficulty regarding drive-
way placement due to pre-existing safety concerns regarding traffic.  He also provided 
more in-depth information regarding floodplain soils.   
 One member of the community asked a question about sewer and well 
placement and that was addressed by the engineer.   
 Mr. Easley moved for the approval of the requested variance, Mr. McGovern 
seconded and the motion passed by a vote of 5 to 0.
