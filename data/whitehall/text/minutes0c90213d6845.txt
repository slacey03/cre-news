G.    REPORTS OF PUBLIC OFFICIALS 
     
        Commissioner STAKES stated his disappointment in the passing of LVHN rezoning. 
          He and Deb Rosene met with County Farmland Preservation and they have identified several farms that are  
          Candidates for farmland preservation.  
          He noted his concern with clearcutting of trees with new development and will start working on an  
          ordinance to prevent that from happening in the future. 
          Lastly, he explained the reasoning behind the referendum being placed on the ballot since there has been  
          much controversy about it:  this is not about a person, but rather to have oversight over the position that  
          collects the taxes for the Township. 
 
        Commissioner FOX noted it is fire prevention month. She commented that regarding the referendum she  
         feels it should be up to the people of the Township. 
 
        Commissioner ATIYEH has a concern with pedestrian safety from WAWA down to the intersection at  
         Mickley Rd Corner.  If possible, he is requesting that additional lighting be added in that area as it seems  
         people are walking on the roadway. 
  

  Page 4 
 
        Commissioner CHRISTMAN asked if any information was found on regulating Ebikes.  Attorney Gross  
         stated that he did receive an information sheet from PennDOT and there are very limited options since it 
         is primarily regulated by the State.  There are a few things that could be approved by ordinance but cannot 
         supersede the state regulations and also will be difficult for the police to enforce.  Lastly, Commissioner 
         Christman stated that he voted for the LVHN rezoning because he felt the good outweighed the bad. 
 
        Commissioner SNYDER thanked Attorney Gross for meeting with the election board to make sure that the   
          referendum verbiage is clear. The State has recommended that municipalities go to a hired position and he 
          agreed that there would be more oversight with a hired professional.  Next, he stated concerns with the 
          upcoming budget and items that will need to be addressed upon receiving it.  He would also like to attend 
          the next Community Council meeting with the School District and asked that he be informed of the date. 
           
        Commissioner SLONAKER stated his reasons for revisiting the referendum since he knows first hand that  
         years ago that there were more duties and taxes collected than there are now.  He also noted to be safe being  
         outside with shorter daylight hours coming.   Lastly, he noted the Halloween Parade on Thursday, 
         October 22nd. 
 
        President WARREN noted fire prevention month so it’s a reminder to change or check your smoke detector              
           batteries.  Senator Miller has proposed legislation for the use of radar by municipal police. Budget 
           Hearing dates were discussed. 
  
        Mayor MARX noted the following:  PFC Krapf Ceremony and thanked all agencies involved. 
        Water Street status: 4 speed humps installed, roadway is being seal coated and pavement markings will be  
        done and upgraded lighting, twelve additional parking spots are being provided as well.   Jefferson Street  
        hvac system install has been started with a smaller unit.  October 22nd Police Dept becomes officially  
        accredited.  Thanked Sen Miller & Rep. McNeill for their help in Whitehall receiving 1 Million LSA grant  
        for a rescue pumper and another $350,000 for a milling machine.  Livestreaming has been setup at the  
        request of the Board and a panic button installed. Senator Miller also helped the Township received another  
        $75,000 toward the Police Training Facility.  Sixth Street Bypass was cleaned and brush removed. Whitehall  
        will team up with PennDOT at the end of the month to cleanup MacArthur Road starting at Rt 329 and  
        continuing to Allentown border. Please watch out for deer along MacArthur Road & S. Church Street.   
        Chestnut Street Barn redo is almost complete with a new roof last year and this year the entire structure  
        painted and a new hex sign is being designed and completed.  Halloween Parade is October 22nd and Trick or  
        Treat is October 30th.
