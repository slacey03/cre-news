F.    OTHER 
 
       DISCUSSION ITEM 
 
       Coplay Creek (K. Snyder & J. Warren)   
       President WARREN stated that back in June a sink hole opened in the creek and a professional geologist  
        assessed the situation and advised that it should be fixed.  Bids are being acquired and hopes that by next 
        meeting to have a path forward in place.  
       Commissioner SNYDER questioned the report’s findings.  Elisa Highley of KCE stated that the report noted 
        that the hole should not just be filled in as it should have other remediation measurers.  Commissioner  
        SNYDER has concern by doing that it could affect the aqua structure underneath. 
       President WARREN noted that if this is deemed an emergency repair there are avenues to obtain funding as  
        this would be a costly endeavor.
