DISCUSSION ITEM 
 
1.  Water Street 
      Commissioner SNYDER noted the different options that have been discussed.  He also noted a document that 
      was supplied by KCE that shows photos of different sections of Water Street and the widths at each section  
      from one end to the other.  He noted that there is photo that depicts a parking area that is owned by the   
      Township that has 14 spaces.  If angle spaces were preferred, it would take it down to 11 spaces.  The  
      standard traffic lane is 12 ft.  With that information Commissioner SNYDER proposed that Water Street, on a  
      trial basis, go to a one way street, going from north to south and adding a lane for parking (approximately 8  
      ft.) and a lane for walking (remainder of area). 
      Commissioner SLONAKER noted that speed bumps/humps could potentially cause an accident. 
      Elsa Highly of KCE advised that the Board should be cautious about installing speed bumps due to how close 
      the houses are on the street.  They also could cause drainage issues.   
      Commissioner STAKES supported the idea of the one way scenario on a temporary basis.  Eventually he  
      would like to see it opened back up to two way once it is deemed safe. 
      Attorney GROSS advised that the Mayor had the authority to close the street originally.  He also has the  
      authority to temporarily reopen the street one way.  If it was to become permanent, a more extensive process  
      would have to occur.   
      Commissioner CHRISTMAN asked if the one way would be better going south to north to help alleviate  
      traffic on Eberhart Road. 
      Kyle Kern of 3166 N. 5th Avenue suggested making Water Street two way until the guard rail, then down to  
      one way closer to the box culvert with stop signs.
