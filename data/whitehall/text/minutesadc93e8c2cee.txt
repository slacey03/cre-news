            8.   RESOLUTION NO. 3444 
                  TITLE: A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WHITEHALL  
                               TOWNSHIP, LEHIGH COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA AUTHORIZING THE  
                               INVESTIGATION, PREPARATION, COMMENCEMENT OF PRELIMINARY            
                               REVIEW AND OTHERWISE INITIATING PUBLIC DISCOURSE REGARDING  
                               THE SALE OF TOWNSHIP-OWNED REAL PROPERTY IDENTIFIED AS THE  
                               RANGE ROAD PROPERTIES INCLUDING THE CAMERON TRACT 
 
        President WARREN read Resolution No. 3444 to the Board. 
                        
      Commissioner SLONAKER moved to approve, seconded by Commissioner CHRISTMAN 
 
                  Commissioner CHRISTMAN feels that this item is worth looking into as the adjacent property 
                  was approved for a warehouse facility and the funds from the sale of this property would help 
                  out the budget and to help alleviate a possible tax increase. 
 
                  Commissioner SNYDER explained that the Township did not seek this out.  A real estate company 
                  reached out to Attorney Gross and asking if the Township would be interested.  An executive session  
                  was held with the Board members and it was decided to have the company submit a proposal.  That  
                  was received and the Board determined that it was time to bring it to the residents.  The proposal  
                  numbers ranged from $12 to $20 million.   Commissioner SNYDER noted that he feels this is an  
                  opportunity to get Eberhart Road and the intersection of Eberhart and MacArthur Roads fixed and  
                  upgraded as it is a PennDOT roadway and working with a development project would ensure those  
                  upgrades get done. 
 
                  Commissioner STAKES stated it’s not wise to put a value on open space and the property should   
                  remain open space and the Township should take their time in considering what the best option would  
                  be for this parcel. 
                                              
                   President WARREN commented that selling the Township’s open space goes against his beliefs.  He 
                   mentioned numerous new residential developments that will be bringing in revenue.  There was also a  
                   proposal many years ago to refurbish the area which housed soccer fields. He also noted that 
                   there was a fire study done and it was stated that if there was ever a reason to consolidate depts, it  
                   would be necessary to have a central location, Range Road would be the location for it. 
 
                   Commissioner ATIYEH asked to have the timeline explained if the Board approved this resolution. 
                   Attorney GROSS stated that the Township is not legally allowed to sell property by resolution.  There 
                   is a state process where it has to be done by public auction or sealed bid.   
 
                   Lori Jarvis of 3347 S 3rd Street stated concerns on this proposal and does not support it. 
                   LoriAnn Fehnel of 3107 N. 3rd Street stated concerns and does not support the proposal. 
                   Deb Rosene of 4408 N. Church Street stated concerns and does not support the proposal. 
                   Joni Todesko of 424 4th Street feels if the property is sold the money will be used for a rec center. 
                   Dennis Yoo of 3206 S Front Street noted that the comprehensive plan should re redone every 5 years. 
                   Deb Martin 4104 Redbud Drive East also does not support the proposal. 
                   Mike Evans of 3476 West Columbia Street asked what the money will be used for if property is sold  
                    and also questioned reassessments. 
                   Dave Hassick of 3795 Dogwood Drive feels if the proposal goes through the money should be put in a  
                    fund and seriously considered the best use of it. 
                   Jennifer Yenser of 3486 Ringer Road does not agree with the proposal. 
                   Anthony Kopack of 3763 Dogwood Drive asked who submitted the proposal. 

  Page 7 
 
                   Stephanie Falcone of 3434 Lehigh Street is not in favor of this proposal as well. 
                   
                  SEVEN Commissioners were present and three voting “yes” (SNYDER, CHRISTMAN & ATIYEH)  
                  and four voting “no” (SLONAKER, FOX, STAKES & WARREN).  Resolution No. 3444 was denied.

           10.   RESOLUTION NO. 3446 
                  TITLE: A RESOLUTION OF THE BOARD OF COMMISSIONERS OF WHITEHALL  
                               TOWNSHIP, LEHIGH COUNTY, PENNSYLVANIA CONFIRMING THE PROPERTY  
                               IDENTIFIED AS JEFFERSON PARK AS THE LOCATION FOR THE PROPOSED  
                               RECREATION CENTER  AND AUTHORIZING THE INVESTIGATION,  
                               PREPARATION, COMMENCEMENT OF PRELIMINARY REVIEW AND OTHERWISE  
                               INITIATING PUBLIC DISCOURSE OF THE SAME. 
 
        President WARREN read Resolution No. 3446 to the Board. 
                        
      Commissioner SLONAKER moved to approve, seconded by Commissioner WARREN 
 
                  Commissioner SNYDER stated that with all the past studies that have been done over the last two  
                  years regarding a possible rec enter, he has always been under the impression that Jefferson Street was  
                  the intended location.  He added that 50% of Whitehall’s population lives in the Fullerton area and it  
                  would not require transportation to get there which would make it more likely for the center to be  
                  utilized heavier. 
 
                  President WARREN commented that while Jefferson Street does need to be revitalized, he has heard 
                  from many people that if this idea would move forward, the center should be centrally located.  There  
                  are other concerns that it may not work at Jefferson such as parking/zoning requirements. 
 
                  Commissioner’s CHRISTMAN, STAKES AND FOX all noted that they do not feel this is the time to 
                  move forward with the proposal.   
 
                  Commissioner ATIYEH defended the project, stating that the Fullerton area does not have much to 
                  offer as far as recreation goes any longer.  He added that a rec center would not only benefit kids, but  
                  seniors as well.  Not offering programs for children in the Fullerton area will possibly lead them into  
                  other things that are not positive. 
                                              
                  SEVEN Commissioners were present and four voting “yes” (SNYDER, SLONAKER, CHRISTMAN  
                  & ATIYEH) and three voting “no” ( FOX, STAKES & WARREN).  Resolution No. 3446 was  

  Page 8 
 
                  approved.
