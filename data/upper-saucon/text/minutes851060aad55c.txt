b. Williams Zoning Agreement — 5090 East Valley Road
Motion made by Supervisor Farrell and seconded by Supervisor Foley to approve

the Agreement with Thomas P. Williams setting forth the terms and conditions
under which the Township will refrain from objecting to the Zoning Appeal filed
by Mr. Williams pertaining to the property at 5090 East Valley Road; and to
authorize the Township Manager to execute said Agreement on behalf of the

Township.

Supervisor Dolan asked why this property could not just be rezoned, or why at
least there couldn’t be a discussion regarding rezoning, since it is not suitable for
its current zoning classification. Manager Beil responded that while the property
could be rezoned, it would not fit Mr. Williams’ timeline and would deprive the
Township of the benefits it is getting under the pending agreement. Supervisor
Dolan then asked hypothetically what zone it would be rezoned to if needed.
Manager Beil responded that since the area surrounding the property is R-2, then
it would likely be rezoned to R-2. He also pointed out that given that the
Township has little commercially zoned property, such rezoning would not
benefit the Township.

Supervisor Dolan asked which residential zoning district the proposed agreement
would fit into best. Manager Beil responded that the proposed agreement does not
necessarily fit any of the existing residential zoning districts, though it would
most closely fit the R-3 district requirements. Supervisor Dolan pointed out that
this seemed like a roundabout way of rezoning a commercial property for
residential uses.

Solicitor Dinklelacker clarified that the agreement simply states that the Township
won’t object to Mr. Williams’ appeal. This is conditional on Mr. Williams
following through with the conditions laid out in the agreement and proving the
need for the variance to the Zoning Hearing Board. Solicitor Dinklelacker also
agreed with Manager Beil’s concern of losing more commercially zoned property.

Supervisor Dolan agreed that it makes sense to put residential homes on the
property since they are near a residential zone. Manager Beil added that since
there could be undesirable, commercial uses on the property, this agreement is
beneficial since it gives the Township some control over what is located on the

property.

Manager Beil invited Mr. Williams to speak on the matter and explain his plans.
Mr. Williams said that he is looking for Township support on the project. He

DIRECTION / DISCUSSION ITEMS

a. Meeting Minutes and Posting Audio Recording of Meetings on Township Website
Supervisor Farrell mentioned that he discussed with Supervisor Dolan on how to
improve the meeting process, specifically by posting the audio recordings of the
Board meetings on the Township Website. Additionally, Supervisor Farrell said
that the current budget process would likely result in hiring someone to take
meeting minutes. Supervisor Dolan added that any such changes would require a
change to each meeting’s “Notification” message. Supervisor Dolan made clear
that a decision to post meeting recordings does not inherently rule out the future
possibility of livestreaming meetings.

Assistant Manager Leonard indicated that an upgraded AV system would be
beneficial in a variety of different settings. He noted that the system could be used
outside of Board meetings. He also mentioned that given the AV system’s age and
the current inability to get compatible parts for it, next year’s budget process may
need to consider a complete AV system upgrade for the Public Meeting Room.

Supervisor Farrell asked how long it would take for a meeting recording to go
live, and Assistant Manager Leonard responded that 24 hours would be
reasonable. Supervisor Dolan indicated that any editing of these recordings would
be to trim any silence at the very beginning or end of the recordings.

Supervisor Farrell said that posting the Board meeting recordings would not
require an independent resolution by the Board. He also mentioned that the
proposed budget allocates funds for a full-time Executive Assistant whose role
would encompass detailed minute taking. Assistant Manager Leonard clarified
that this position’s title and job description have yet to be finalized.

Supervisor Dolan advised that posting the recordings could allow the minutes to
be more succinct, shifting the responsibility for interpreting speakers’ comments

b. Review Agenda for Zoning Hearing Board Meeting on October 5, 2026
Manager Beil said that there were 4 appeals on the docket for the Zoning Hearing
Board October 5“ Meeting. He said that all 4 involve residential properties, three
of which involve setbacks, with the fourth one involving an increase in
impervious coverage. He mentioned that all appeals seem relatively routine.

Supervisor Dolan understood that notification guidelines were established to
prevent the encroachment of a neighbor’s property, but wanted confirmation
regarding the understanding that it is each neighbor’s responsibility to speak up
against or in favor of these appeals rather than that of the Board’s. Manager Beil
confirmed, adding that neighbors within 300 feet of the zoning appeal receive
written notice, that the property involved in the appeal is required to place signage
indicating such involvement, and that notice of the appeal is published in the

newspaper.

Supervisor Farrell said that Board representation is not needed for the appeal
hearing regarding the Williams Zoning Agreement.
