Tammy Clemens of 6725 Blue Church Road South expressed concern about a serious
mosquito problem in her neighborhood. She stated she recently attended an Environmental
Advisory Council meeting to raise this issue but was advised the Council could not assist and
was referred to the Board of Supervisors. Ms. Clemens specifically expressed concern that
the detention basins in the Strawberry Hill and Traditions of America Locust Valley
developments were not being adequately treated and were significant contributors to the

A brief discussion ensued regarding Lehigh County’s mosquito contro] program. It was noted

Ms. Clemens expressed concern that developers were not complying with the Township’s
restrictions on construction work hours. She reported that construction work was occurring
on Sundays in violation of these restrictions and that she had reported the violation to the
police. She asked what consequences violators faced for not complying with the Township’s
requirements. Mr. Beil explained the police and code officers have discretion to issue either a
warning or a citation and stated that he was unsure which action had been taken in this

particular instance.

Ms. Lang confirmed the Zoning Officer issued a strongly worded written warning to the
developer of the Strawberry Hill Development, advising that any future violations of the
Township’s work hours ordinance would result in enforcement action.

ADDITIONAL BUSINESS

Supervisor Wagner expressed concern that the rose garden at Hopewell Park was not being
properly maintained and described its condition as an embarrassment. Mr. Beil explained the
Township’s lease with the Rose Garden Society makes the Society responsible for the
ongoing maintenance of the rose garden. Mr. Beil stated he would notify the Society of the
garden’s condition and request that immediate action be taken to address the lack of

maintenance.

Supervisor Wagner asked whether the lease contains provisions addressing what would
happen if the rose garden were abandoned. Mr. Beil stated that he would review the lease and

report back to Supervisor Wagner.

Supervisor Wagner reported he received correspondence from the Mayor of Coopersburg
Borough requesting a meeting to discuss opportunities for regional partnerships and
