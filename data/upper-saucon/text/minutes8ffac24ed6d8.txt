At approximately 6:59 p.m., the Board returned from Executive Session.

Tammy Clemens of 6725 Blue Church Road South indicated her desire to address the Board,
even though she arrived late to the meeting and missed both public comment periods.

Ms. Clemens expressed concerns regarding truck traffic and speeding on Blue Church Road
South. She said the existing “No Trucks” signs are too small and asked the Board to consider
installing larger signs. She also asked the Board to consider authorizing an engineering study
of Blue Church Road South to identify ways to improve safety. She noted that different
sections of Blue Church Road South have different speed limits and recommended
establishing a consistent speed limit for the entire stretch of Blue Church Road South. She
cited the proposed crosswalk near the intersection of Blue Church Road South and Mountain
Laurel Drive as a reason to consider reducing the speed limit on Blue Church Road South.

Ms. Clemens also said that construction work at the Strawberry Hill Subdivision is occurring
on Sundays and fairly late at night in violation of the Township’s work-hours ordinance. She
said the Zoning Officer should investigate these violations.

Mr. Beil suggested that Ms. Clemens contact the Police Department and report work-hours
violations when they occur, noting police officers are available to respond at all times,
whereas the Zoning Officer is not available during early mornings, evenings and weekends.
Ms. Clemens said she did not want to report the violations to the Police Department because
her family was not in favor of her doing so and she would prefer to contact the Zoning
Officer. She also expressed the opinion that the Police Department has more important
matters to address than work-hours violations. She recommended that the Township take a
stronger stance on violations of the work-hours ordinance.

It was the consensus of the Board to authorize an engineering study of the Township portion
of Blue Church Road South to determine an appropriate and consistent speed limit and
whether larger road signs are warranted. Upon completion, the study will be shared with
PennDOT so that consideration can be given to making the PennDOT portion of Blue Church
Road South consistent with the Township portion.
