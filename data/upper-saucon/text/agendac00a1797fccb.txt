a. Resolution No. 2026-19 – Recognizing the 20th Anniversary of Promenade Saucon Valley

sincere congratulations to the owners, management team, staff and tenants of Promenade

PennDOT 
Consideration of a motion to adopt Resolution No. 2026-20 approving the conveyance of

Transportation in connection with the construction of the new police building; and 
authorizing the Township Manager to execute all documents necessary to effectuate the 
conveyance.

9. MOTIONS 
 
a. Request for Payment – New Police Building (Mechanical Contract) – Payment  
Application 1 
Consideration of a motion to authorize payment in the amount of $31,306.50 to Myco 
Mechanical, Inc. for work completed on the New Police Building Project. 
 
b. Request for Payment – New Police Building (General Construction Contract) – Payment  
Application 2 
Consideration of a motion to authorize payment in the amount of $25,876.08 to Gordon H. 
Baver, Inc. for work completed on the New Police Building Project. 
 
c. Request for Payment – North Branch Sanitary Sewer Interceptor Project (Contract 1 - Site 
Work & Sanitary Sewer Installation) - Payment Application 1 
Consideration of a motion to authorize payment in the amount of $519,899.40 to DOLI 
Construction Corporation for work completed on the North Branch Sanitary Sewer 
Interceptor Project. 
 
d. Release of Funds – Strawberry Hill Subdivision – Security Release Request 3 (Sanitary 
Portion) 
Consideration of a motion to authorize the release of construction security in the amount of  
$1,100.00 for the Strawberry Hill Subdivision in accordance with the Township Engineer’s 
recommendation made by letter dated October 6, 2026. 
 
e. Separation and Retirement Agreement with Thomas Beil 
Consideration of a motion to approve the Separation and Retirement Agreement between 
Thomas Beil and the Township whereby the parties agree to mutually end their employment 
relationship, as per Mr. Beil’s indication that he will retire effective July 1, 2027; and to 
authorize the Chairman of the Board of Supervisors to execute said Agreement, subject to 
any minor modifications deemed appropriate by the Alternate Township Solicitor.     
 
f. Appointment of Assistant Township Manager 
Consideration of a motion to appoint Thomas Beil as Assistant Township Manager on a 
temporary basis to assist with the transition to a new Township Manager.  
 
g. Appointment of Township Manager 
Consideration of a motion to appoint Patrick Leonard as Township Manager, effective 
immediately. 
 
h. Employment Agreement for Township Manager 
Consideration of a motion to approve the Employment Agreement between Patrick 
Leonard and Upper Saucon Township setting forth the terms and conditions under which 
Patrick Leonard will perform the duties of Township Manager, and to authorize the 
Chairman of the Board of Supervisors to execute said Agreement on behalf of the 
Township.
