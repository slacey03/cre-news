11. MOTIONS 
 
a. Appointment of Assistant Director of Roads and Properties (Trainee) 
Consideration of a motion to appoint Christopher Craig as Assistant Director of Roads and 
Properties (Trainee) at a starting hourly rate of pay of $40.22, contingent upon Mr. Craig’s 
acceptance of the conditional offer of employment as set forth in the Township Manager’s 
letter dated July 13, 2026.   
 
b. Review Comments on Salisbury Township’s Comprehensive Plan 
Consideration of a motion to approve the review comments pertaining to Salisbury 
Township’s Draft Comprehensive Plan, as detailed in the memorandum dated July 8, 2026, 
prepared by Trisha Lang, Community Development Director, and to authorize submission 
of those comments to Salisbury Township for their consideration. 
 
c. Appointment of Sean Cunningham as Sewage Enforcement Officer 
Consideration of a motion to appoint Sean Cunningham (SEO #04274) of Hanover 
Engineering Associates as a Sewage Enforcement Officer for Upper Saucon Township, with 
authority to act on behalf of the Township in the administration and enforcement of the 
Pennsylvania Sewage Facilities Act; and to reaffirm the prior appointments of Christopher 
A. Taylor (SEO #03138), Jacob A. Schray (SEO #03134), Paul Gallagher Saba (SEO 
#04167), Scott J. Brown (SEO #01716), and Luke E. Eggert (SEO #04090) as Sewage 
Enforcement Officers for the Township. 
 
d. Release of Funds – Good Shepherd Rehabilitation Hospital – Security Release Request 9 
Consideration of a motion to authorize the release of construction security in the amount of 
$38,296.50 for the Good Shepherd Rehabilitation Hospital Project in accordance with the 
Township Engineer’s recommendation made by letter dated June 3, 2026. 
 
e. Release of Funds – Strawberry Hill Subdivision – Security Release Request 2 
Consideration of a motion to authorize the release of construction security in the amount of 
$550,646.69 for the Strawberry Hill Subdivision in accordance with the Township 
Engineer’s recommendation made by letter dated July 7, 2026. 
 
f. Award Mechanical Construction Bid for New Police Building  
Consideration of a motion to award the Mechanical Construction Bid for the New Police 
Building to Myco Mechanical, as the lowest bidder, in the amount of $1,234,000.00.  Note:   
The Board previously awarded the General Construction, Plumbing Construction, and 
Electrical Construction contracts for the New Police Building at its June 8, 2026 meeting. 
The Mechanical Construction bid was tabled at that time due to uncertainty regarding the 
availability of certain materials and the potential use of substitute materials. Those concerns 
have since been resolved, and staff now recommends awarding the Mechanical 
Construction contract to Myco Mechanical. 
 
g. Release of Funds – Brookhill Estates Subdivision a/k/a Yamnicky – Security Release 
Request 1  
Consideration of a motion to authorize the release of construction security in the amount of 
$141,387.07 for the Brookhill Estates Subdivision in accordance with the Township 
Engineer’s recommendation made by letter dated July 1, 2026.
