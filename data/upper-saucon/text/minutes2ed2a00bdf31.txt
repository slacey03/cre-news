PUBLIC COMMENT

Gary Ginter of 4621 Spring Drive asked the Board to lower the speed limit on Spring Drive
from 35 mph to 25 mph. He cited several reasons in support of the requested reduction,
including compliance with State law and PennDOT regulations, the residential nature of
Spring Drive, and concerns regarding traffic conditions and pedestrian safety. Mr. Ginter
provided the Board with a packet containing 18 exhibits supporting his request. He asked the
Board to forgo an engineering study and immediately lower the speed limit on Spring Drive
to 25 mph, which he stated is permitted under State law. He said an engineering study is
unnecessary and would be a waste of taxpayer dollars. Mr. Ginter also noted that several
other Spring Drive residents were present at the meeting in support of the requested speed
limit reduction. He thanked the Supervisors for considering his request and asked the Board
to provide a response within the next 10 business days.

BADGE PINNING CEREMONY FOR NEW POLICE SERGEANT AUSTIN

BARTHOLOMEW

Police Chief Fritts introduced Austin Bartholomew, who was recently promoted from Patrol

BADGE PINNING CEREMONIES FOR NEW POLICE DETECTIVES THOMAS

DELLA SALA AND LORA MARTIN

Officers Thomas Della Sala and Lora Martin recently assumed their new roles as Detectives
with the Police Department. Police Chief Fritts introduced Detectives Della Sala and Martin
and highlighted their backgrounds, qualifications, and professional accomplishments. Badge
pinning ceremonies were held to recognize their accomplishments and formally welcome
them to their new roles, with their families participating in the ceremonies.

Chief Fritts thanked the Supervisors for allocating time on their meeting agenda to recognize
these important career milestones and he thanked everyone in the audience for their

attendance.

Award Mechanical Construction Bid for New Police Building
Motion made by Supervisor Farrell and seconded by Supervisor Dolan to award the
Mechanical Construction Bid for the New Police Building to Myco Mechanical, as the lowest

bidder, in the amount of $1,234,000.00.

Chairman Inglis noted that the Board previously awarded the General Construction,
Plumbing Construction, and Electrical Construction contracts for the New Police Building at
its June 8, 2026 meeting. The Mechanical Construction bid was tabled at that time due to
uncertainty regarding the availability of certain materials and the potential use of substitute
materials. Those concerns have since been resolved, and staff now recommends awarding the
Mechanical Construction contract to Myco Mechanical.

The motion was approved by a vote of 5 to 0.
