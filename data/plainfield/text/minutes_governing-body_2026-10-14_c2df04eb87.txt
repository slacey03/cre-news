PLAINFIELD TOWNSHIP
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS MEETING
October 14, 2026

The monthly meeting of the Plainfield Township Board of Supervisors was held on Wednesday
October 14, 2026, at the Plainfield Township Municipal Building located at 6292 Sullivan Trail,
Nazareth, PA 18064.

Executive session was held from 6:00 P.M. to 6:25 P.M. regarding personnel, no action taken.
Chairman, Glenn Borger, called the meeting to order at 6:25 P.M.

The Pledge of Allegiance was performed.
ROLL CALL:

The following supervisors answered Roll Call: Chairman, Glenn Borger, Vice Chairman, Ken
Field, Supervisor, Nolan Kemmerer, Supervisor, Jane Mellert and Supervisor, Paul Levits.

Also present were Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, Solicitor, David Backenstoe, Esq,
Administrative Coordinator/Secretary, Amanda Haney and Transcend Finance, Jake Stahl and
Scott Boehret.

Slate Belt Regional Police Department was present for adherence to Public Meeting Rules and
Procedures Policy.

1. Grand Central Sanitary Landfill (Waste Management) Monthly Update —
Adrienne Fors:

Adrienne Fors reported that an end-of-life letter had been delivered to the
Township and provided an update regarding capping a portion of Cell 19. She
also announced an electronic waste collection event scheduled for November 7,
from 9:00 a.m. to noon.

2. Morgan Stanley Irrevocable Trust Fund Update — Paul Azzaretto:

A representative from Morgan Stanley presented an update on the Plainfield
Township trust, reporting a value of just under $16,558,000 and a yield of
approximately 3.8%. The representative discussed a difficult quarter for the bond
market, concerns regarding inflation, and the outlook for interest rates, which
were expected to trend downward over the longer term.

. Discussion of 2027 Plainfield Township Budget Parameters:

Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, presented the draft 2027 budget using the
current tax rate of 5.5 mills and requested Board direction regarding proposed
expenditures, project priorities, and any adjustment to the tax rate. Discussion
addressed road infrastructure needs, projected fund balances, equipment needs,
possible proposed interfund transfers, and the effect of such using alternative tax
rates. Board members questioned further investment in Mud Run Road and
discussed grant funding for other road projects. Supervisor, Jane Mellert,
questioned a difference of approximately $14 million between the assessed
property values used by the Township and the assessed value currently used
within the Slate Belt Regional Police Department budget. Township Manager,
Paige Stefanelli, stated that the Township’s figure had been provided by the
Northampton County Assessment Office and would forward the correspondence
for review. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, will work with Chief, Jonathon
Hoadley, to ensure the assessment is accurate in the police budget.

Board members discussed holding a budget workshop and reviewing proposed
expenditures in greater detail. Resident, Alan Gumeny, offered historical revenue
and expenditure information and recommended evaluating spending priorities.
Ms. Stefanelli, explained that the draft included both necessary expenditures and
requested projects, including a proposed $25,000 interfund transfer for fire
apparatus.


ACTION: Motion was made by Glenn Borger and seconded by Paul Levits
to have Board members review the draft budget before the next meeting,
prepare lists of items they wish to retain or remove, and return to compare
their recommendations. Prior to the vote, Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if
there were any comments from the governing body or the public. Motion
approved. Vote 5-0.

Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, will request the reports notated from the

7. Local Share Account Grant Award for Plainfield Township — FYT:

Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, reported that the Township had been
awarded a $200,000 Local Share Account grant and an additional $110,000 for
P25 Radios for the Plainfield Township Volunteer Fire Company. She advised
that authorization had been given to proceed with purchasing P25 radios and
discussed possible purchasing of the P25 Radios for the Public Works Department
and the Slate Belt Regional Police Department. Supervisor, Jane Mellert,
questioned why grant funds were being received through the Township rather
than directly by the Fire Company. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli,
indicated it was explained that the Fire Company could not apply directly for the
Local Share Account grant and that the application had to be submitted through
the municipality.

C. Resolutions:
1. Resolution No. 2026-25 — 2026 Budget Amendment to Appropriate Additional

Funds for Police Protection Services Provided by Slate Belt Regional Police
Department:



The Board discussed Resolution 2026-25 authorizing supplemental funding for
the Slate Belt Regional Police Department for the 2026 Fiscal Year. Police Chief,
Jonathon Hoadley, explained that the shortfall involved increased health insurance
costs and anticipated grant revenue that did not materialize. He outlined corrective
measures, including removing anticipated grant revenue from the budget,
reviewing health insurance options, holding additional budget meetings, and
consulting with an accounting service. Board members expressed concerns
regarding the timing of the funding request, financial oversight, and the effect on
taxpayers. Supervisor, Jane Mellert, requested additional financial information
and questioned compliance with the charter agreement. Supervisor, Ken Field,
stated that safeguards and additional accounting review were being pursued by the
Police Commission to prevent a recurrence. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli,
indicated that the Township and Slate Belt Regional Police Commission are in
compliance with the Charter Agreement pertaining to this matter.

ACTION: Motion was made by Ken Field and seconded by Nolan Kemmerer
to approve Resolution 2026-25, providing an allocation of $330,907 from the
General Fund for police expenses. Prior to the vote, Chairman, Glenn Borger,
asked if there were any comments from the governing body or the public.
Motion approved. Vote 4-0-1, with Jane Mellert abstaining due to requesting
information in a letter dated September 22, 2026.

. Resolution No. 2026-27 — Local Share Account Statewide Grand Application
Submission:

The Board considered Resolution 2026-27 for a Local Share Account grant
application requesting $486,476 for a leaf vacuum truck and a crack sealing
machine. Discussion addressed equipment needs, availability through the Council
of Governments, potential leaf collection services, and storage capacity.
Supervisor, Nolan Kemmerer, expressed concern about acquiring additional

7. Consideration of Advertisement for Ordinance No. 440 pertaining to a Proposed Fire
Apparatus Fund:

The Board discussed proposed Ordinance No. 440 to establish a sinking fund for
future fire apparatus purchases. Discussion addressed limiting expenditures to
specified apparatus, including ladder trucks, tanker trucks, and pumper trucks, rather
than routine repairs or operating expenses. Township Solicitor, Dave Backenstoe,
explained that disbursements would require Board approval. Board members also
discussed incorporating financial reporting requirements into the ordinance.

ACTION: Motion was made by Glenn Borger and seconded by Ken Field to
table this matter and revise the proposed ordinance to include financial
reporting requirements and specify eligible apparatus purchases. Prior to the
vote, Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments from the
governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.
