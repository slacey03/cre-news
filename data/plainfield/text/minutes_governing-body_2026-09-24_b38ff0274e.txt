1. Resolution No. 2026-25- 2026 Budget Amendment to Appropriate Additional
Funds for Police Protection Services Provided by Slate Belt Regional Police
Department.

The Board discussed Resolution No. 2026-25 to amend the 2026 budget by
appropriating additional funds for police protection services provided by the Slate
Belt Regional Police Department.

Supervisor, Jane Mellert, requested an executive session with Chief of Police,
Jonathon Hoadley, to ensure all Board members were fully informed and
referenced the approaches taken by Wind Gap and Pen Argyl. Solicitor, David
Backenstoe, supported the request stating it would lawfully fall under a new
category that was adopted regarding discussing issues which would affect
emergency preparedness, protection, public safety and security.

The Board recessed into executive session at 6:16 p.m. and reconvened the public
meeting at 6:51 p.m.

SOLICITOR’S REPORT —- (DAVID BACKENSTORE, ESQ.)

1,

Consideration of Approval for Intermunicipal Agreement with Wind Gap Municipal
Authority (WGMA):

Solicitor, David Backenstoe, reviewed the proposed intermunicipal agreement with
the Wind Gap Municipal Authority to provide service to the Jaindl property known as
The Cube at Wind Gap. He explained that the facilities were privately owned and that
the Township would have no routine maintenance or financial obligation. He also
discussed the Township’s responsibilities should issues arise within Plainfield
Township. Supervisor, Jane Mellert, questioned the agreement’s designation language
and whether other properties were included.

ACTION: Motion was made by Paul Levits and seconded by Nolan Kemmerer
to approve the intermunicipal agreement with the Wind Gap Authority. Prior to
the vote, Chairman Glenn Borger asked if there were any comments from the
governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.

CITIZEN’S AGENDA/NON-AGENDA (Only persons who have signed the Sign-In
Sheet by 6:15 PM will be allowed to speak. There is a 3-minute time limit for speakers):

Laurie Levits expressed concern about perceived disrespect between Board members and
residents during meetings. She referenced the posted rules of conduct and asked how
violations are addressed. She also requested that Township meetings be televised.

Solicitor, David Backenstoe, explained that complaints concerning Board conduct may be
submitted to the Board or Chairman. He stated that the Chairman may address disruptive
public conduct by asking an individual to sit down or leave and, when necessary,
requesting police assistance. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, clarified that the rules
governing Board members are separate from those governing public participation.

4. Paul Levits - discussed internal processes and communications, expressing concern

5. Jane Mellert - requested that the record reflects that she had asked to give a Board

Ms. Mellert asked about tree removal notices sent to residents. Township Manager,
Paige Stefanelli, stated that one notice had been issued to Mr. Finken following
investigation of a complaint. The Board discussed responsibility for trees near the
road right-of-way, diseased trees that could fall onto the roadway, and
communication with affected property owners. Solicitor, David Backenstoe,
explained that the Township’s adopted ordinance provides procedures for addressing
these matters. Ms. Stefanelli noted that the notice included an appeal process and that
the tree should have been flagged.

Ms. Mellert also expressed concern that she had not been permitted access to the
Township office since taking office in January and stated that Board members should
be able to walk through the office.
