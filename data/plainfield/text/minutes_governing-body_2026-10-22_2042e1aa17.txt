. Discussion of Easement Purchase through Northampton County Farmland

Preservation- Kerr Farm- $376.000.00:

The Board discussed the proposed $376,000 contribution toward an agricultural
conservation easement for Kerr Farm through Northampton County Partnership
Program for Agricultural Conservation Easements. Discussion addressed prior
Township commitments, available preservation funds, the farm-ranking process,
and state, county, and municipal funding. Resident, Carlton Michaels, commented
on funding availability and factors affecting preservation priorities and per-acre
values.

ACTION: Motion was made by Nolan Kemmerer and seconded by Glenn
Borger to approve the Kerr Farm contribution. Prior to the vote, Chairman,
Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments from the governing body or
the public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.

Discussion of 2027 Plainfield Township Budget Parameters:

The Board began reviewing the proposed 2027 budget and discussed revisions to
staffing, equipment, and project allocations. Paige Stefanelli reported that the
$90,000 paving allocation and proposed Ford Ranger purchase had been removed,
with continued use of the F-550 dump truck planned. The revised Public Works
staffing proposal included one additional full-time employee and one regular part-
time employee.


The Board reviewed budget line item 342.200 for rent charged to the Slate Belt
Regional Police Department. Supervisor, Jane Mellert, requested the rental rate
per square foot, which was identified as $13.

ACTION: Motion was made by Nolan Kemmerer and seconded by Ken Field
to reduce Slate Belt Police Departments rent to $0. Prior to the vote,
Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments from the
governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 3-2, with Jane Mellert
and Paul Levits opposed.

ACTION: Motion was made by Ken Field and seconded by Glenn Borger to
increase budget line item 410.230 for heating fuel from $5,000 to $10,000.
Prior to the vote, Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments
from the governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.

The Board reviewed employee cellphone expenses under budget line 402.320.
Supervisor, Jane Mellert, questioned whether all employees required Township-
issued cellphones, Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, explained that their use
primarily related to Right-to-Know requests.

ACTION: Motion was made by Nolan Kemmerer and seconded by Paul
Levits to remove the Finance Director’s cellphone expense from the budget.
Prior to the vote, Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments
from the governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.

Supervisor, Jane Mellert, moved to reduce budget line 401.460 to $2,500. The
motion failed for lack of a second.

The Board discussed bank fees under budget line 402.390, including costs
associated with Positive Pay and fraud protection.

ACTION: Motion was made by Jane Mellert and seconded by Nolan
Kemmerer to reduce budget line 402.460 to $1,500.00. Prior to the vote,
Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments from the
governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.

The Board reviewed proposed technology and records management expenses,
including equipment replacement, SmartGov accounts, and conversion of
Township records to digital documents. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli,
explained that the proposed records conversion allocation reflected a project
budgeted but not completed during the current fiscal year.

The Board discussed the proposed addition of one full-time and one regular part-
time Public Works employee. Public Works Director, William Hughes, cited
staffing shortages affecting road maintenance, equipment operation, flagging,


snow removal, and employee safety. Board members discussed workload,
contracting alternatives, union requirements, and the cost of wages and benefits.

ACTION: Motion was made by Jane Mellert and seconded by Paul Levits to
remove the proposed additional full-time position pending further evaluation
of staffing needs and the overall budget. Prior to the vote, Chairman, Glenn
Borger, asked if there were any comments from the governing body or the
public. Motion failed. Vote 2-3, with Glenn Borger, Ken Field and Nolan
Kemmerer opposed.

The Board reviewed budget line 430.232, noting that expenditures had already
exceeded the existing $21,000 allocation.

ACTION: Motion was made by Ken Field and seconded by Nolan Kemmerer
to increase budget line item 430.230 from $6,000 to $10,000.00. Prior to the
vote, Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments from the
governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.

ACTION: Motion was made by Nolan Kemmerer and seconded by Ken Field
to increase the allocation to $30,000.00. Prior to the vote, Chairman, Glenn
Borger, asked if there were any comments from the governing body or the
public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.

The Board also discussed water expenses under budget lines 430.236 and
430.366. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, agreed to clarify the distinction
between bottled water and utility water charges.

The Board reviewed budget line item 454.386 for portable toilet rentals and
discussed reducing the number of units rented from November through March.

The Board reviewed the Liquid Fuels budget and accounting process. Supervisor,
Jane Mellert, provided examples of prior reporting formats and questioned
allocations appearing in both the General Fund and Liquid Fuels budgets.
Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, explained that the current approach
followed PennDOT guidance, improved project tracking and financial reporting,
and did not duplicate expenses.

The Board discussed reimbursements from Northampton County for equipment
rentals and related work under an existing agreement. Township Manager, Paige
Stefanelli, stated that the Township performed the work when staff time was
available.

Supervisor, Paul Levits, questioned the $210,000 allocation for other equipment
under budget line 430.740. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, referenced the
equipment spreadsheet previously provided to the Board and explained that the
proposed amounts used the highest quotes received to allow for anticipated costs.


The Board reviewed proposed recreation facility expenses under budget line
454.610, including certified playground inspections, accessibility requirements,
and improvements at the Grove. Supervisor, Jane Mellert, suggested reducing the
allocation to between $50,000 and $75,000. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli,
and Public Works Director, William Hughes, discussed required inspections and
compliance needs, while Supervisor, Ken Field, requested complete cost estimates
and clarification of responsibilities between the Township and the Farmers
Association. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, advised that additional quotes
were being obtained.

The Board reviewed a proposed monthly expense of $607 under budget line
407.310. Board members requested further discussion with Scott Edwards
regarding the services provided. Township Solicitor, Dave Backenstoe, suggested
arranging a conference call.

The Board discussed the proposed Fire Apparatus Fund allocation, including an
additional $25,000. Supervisor, Jane Mellert, requested the Fire Company’s
financial records before further consideration. Township Manager, Paige
Stefanelli, advised that the requested information was due by noon on November
5.

The Board reviewed proposed road engineering expenses under budget line
408.310, including Engler Road, Mud Run Road, and Hahn Road. Township
Engineer, Mike Muffley, explained that the Engler Road work had not progressed
to final design and discussed limitations on signing and sealing another engineer’s
work.

Motion was made by Glenn Borger and seconded by Ken Field to remove
Mud Run Road and substitute Hahn Road under budget line 439.610. Prior
to the vote, Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments from
the governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 5-0.

Motion was made by Glenn Borger and seconded by Ken Field to revise
budget line 408.310 to reflect 20 hours for preparation of a sketch plan. Prior
to the vote, Chairman, Glenn Borger, asked if there were any comments from
the governing body or the public. Motion approved. Vote 4-1, with Jane
Mellert opposed.

The Board discussed the remaining budget shortfall, road infrastructure needs,
and options for balancing the budget through expenditure reductions, interfund
transfers, or a tax increase. Township Manager, Paige Stefanelli, explained that
the budget draft excluded approximately $2 million in Waste Management
revenue previously used to balance the budget, in preparation for the anticipated
loss of that revenue when the landfill closes. Supervisor, Jane Mellert, requested a
revised budget and updated police figures before determining the tax rate. No
final tax rate was established.


Resident, Carlton Michaels, commented on the continuing shortfall and the need
to fund road improvements through transfers or increased taxes.
