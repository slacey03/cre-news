ENGINEER REPORT: Matt Evans 
1) Act 14 Notice - Keystone has asked a few times to see the plans with no success. 
Without the plans it is difficult to review and comment or determine any impact. 
Engineers Evans will draft a letter to be reviewed by the Board and Solicitor. 
2) Road Opening Excavation Permit - Standards, Applications and Security 
Measures – Engineer Evans discussed the road opening ordinance, number 255. 
There are requirements around restoration, when work can take place, overlaying, 
and poles. The township may want to add bonding into the ordinance. Supervisor 
Huratiak spoke about updating the application or ordinance to make it clear to the 
staff to get documentation regarding the scope of work and what triggers a higher 
review. Engineer Evans will prepare an application that ties into the ordinance, adds 
language about elevating a review, and requiring something on the applicant’s 
letterhead that speaks to the scope of work and restoration. 
3) GGH Investment LLC – Lot Consolidation – The lot consolidation plan for 359 & 
363 Blue Valley Drive was discussed. Discussed were continuing existing use, 
permitted and non-permitted uses,  driveway, parking lot, ADA compliance, sizing of 
the septic, which was for the single-family home, etc. The proposed use is vague. 
The property does meet  lot consolidation.  The waiver is not applicable.   
Supervisor Huratiak motioned to approve the lot consolidation with the condition 
of satisfying all the comments of the engineer’s review letter and adding a note, 
with the input and review of the township professionals, on the plan clarifying the 
existing use of this parcel and the structures shown on the lot consolidation plan. 
Vice Chairman Tolino seconded. 
   VOTE 3-0-0
