CORRESPONDENCE: 
1) Blue Valley Farm Show – A letter was received regarding a 40x60 pole building they 
would like to put up to house an Agricultural Museum. They were told by our Zoning 
Officer they would need a land development plan.  The Blue Valley Farm Show is 
requesting that the Board waive the requirement for land development. It was 
discussed whether it qualified for an agricultural exemption. The Blue Valley Farm 
Show representative stated they are looking for guidance on how to proceed. The 
Blue Valley Farm Show is a commercial entity.  It is easier to obtain waivers on 
SALDO rather than waiving land development altogether. A land development plan 
should be filed.
