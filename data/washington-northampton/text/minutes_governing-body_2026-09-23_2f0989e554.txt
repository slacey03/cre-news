WASHINGTON TOWNSHIP 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS’MEETING 
September 23, 2026 - 3:30 PM 
 
 
CALL TO ORDER: Chairman Smith called the meeting to order at 3:51 pm.  Vice Chairman 
Tolino, Supervisor Huratiak, Secretary/Treasurer Cifuentes, and Engineer Evans were also 
present. There was one person in the audience. 
 
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE:  Recited 
 
CORRESPONDENCE:  None 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: Jeff Mulitsch voiced a complaint that his right to enjoy his property in 
peace has diminished. He stated this is due to his neighbor giving permission for others to 
ride ATVs and dirt bikes on his property and that the noise is continuous and disturbing the 
enjoyment of his own property. The Board discussed that as this is private property, there 
was little action they could take. The Board agreed to send a letter to the property owner 
quoting Ordinance 287 and 149, highlighting that the complaint received states the noise is 
excessive and frequent. Mr. Mulitsch submitted a written complaint.  
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  Robert Smith 
1) LSA Grant Resolution to be adopted –  
Engineer Evans discussed the LSA Grant application in the amount of $1,000,000 for 
the proposed public works facility.  He provided a preliminary cost estimate, plans, 
and a letter of support from Representative Ann Flood.  He anticipates additional 
support letters from Congressman Ryan MacKenzie and State Senator Lisa Boscola. 
Additional grants may be applied for. Various aspects and details of the proposed 
project were discussed. Recently approved grants were also discussed: $100K for a 
road truck and $100K for the Washington Township Volunteer Fire Company tanker. 
Chairman Smith motioned to adopt Resolution 18-2026, a resolution among 
Washington Township and the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania Department of 
Community and Economic Development and the Commonwealth Finance Authority to 
facilitate and assist in obtaining the requested Northampton and Lehigh Counties 
Local Share Account Grant in the amount of $1,000,000.00, to improve the public 
works complex. Vice Chairman Tolino seconded. 
   VOTE 3-0-0   
 
2) Handicap Spot- for 1504 Pine St., Pen Argyl, PA – Discussed.  The Board asked that 
the office reach out and request a written request, along with proof of handicap 
status, and upon receipt would approve reasonable accommodation of painting the 
curb blue. 

Supervisor Huratiak made a motion to approve the reasonable request for 1504 Pine 
Street, Pen Argyl to paint the curb blue upon the resident providing a written request 
and documentation of a handicap placard.  Chairman Smith seconded. 
   VOTE 3-0-0 
 
3) 2026 Trick-Or-Treat Date – The date and time of trick or treat  in the township was 
discussed. 
Chairman Smith motioned to celebrate Halloween and set the trick or treating on 
October 31, 2026, from 5pm – 8pm. Vice Chairman Tolino seconded. 
   VOTE 3-0-0  
      
 
ADJOURNMENT: 
Chairman Smith motioned to adjourn the meeting at 4:39 pm. Supervisor Huratiak 
seconded. 
   VOTE 3-0-0
