WASHINGTON TOWNSHIP

CALL TO ORDER:   Chairman Smith called the meeting to order at 9:02 am.  Also in 
attendance were Vice Chairman Tolino, Supervisor Huratiak, Secretary/Treasurer 
Cifuentes, Police Chief Krome, Road Foreman Hester and Township Bookkeeper 
Shetler. There were 0 attendees. 
 
PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE: Recited 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT: None 
 
BOARD OF SUPERVISORS:  Robert Smith 
1) Propane Tank Proposals – Secretary/Treasurer presented proposals from 
Laudenslager, Nolts, Deiter Brothers and Tolino’s Fuel. Also submitted was a 
proposal to excavate and set up the tanks from RFE Enterprises. The Board 
discussed the various proposals.   
Chairman Smith motioned to approve Deiter Brothers to install and hook up two 
1000-gallon underground propane tanks, less the propane charge of $2,990.40, as 
the township is under contract with another provider for propane.  Supervisor 
Huratiak seconded. 
   VOTE 2-0-1 
 
2) Medical Plan Renewal – December 1 – Secretary/Treasurer Cifuentes presented a 
benefits proposal from PCI, Pennsylvania Chamber Insurance.   Our representative 
at PCI put together a competitor analysis looking at the other available carriers’ 
plans/rates vs. the renewal we received from Capital Blue Cross.  The Board of 
Supervisors reviewed the  information. 
Chairman Smith motioned to renew the current plan with Capital Blue Cross. Vice 
Chairman Tolino seconded. 
   VOTE 3-0-0 
 
3) 2026 Budget Planning - Secretary/Treasurer Cifuentes presented the draft budget 
document.  The draft budget included three (3) years of historical data and actual 
YTD data for 2026 through October 6, 2026.  The initial framework for the 2027 
Budget was also included. The draft budget to be discussed today is a starting point 
and will change as we receive additional information.  Secretary /Treasurer Sharon 

Cifuentes reviewed the content of each of the income and expense categories in the 
2027 draft budget for the General Fund.
