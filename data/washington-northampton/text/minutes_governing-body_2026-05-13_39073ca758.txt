COMMUNITY CONVERSATION ABOUT ZONING: Shannon Callouri of Hailstone Economic 
spoke about zoning and Plan Slate Belt. Plan Slate Belt is a project Washington Township is 
involved in and is a multi-municipal comprehensive plan. Eight communities in our region 
came together to develop this plan.  Shannon discussed the project goals, how we prepare 
and plan for the future, and how zoning ties in. The next step is to update the zoning 

ordinances of the participating communities. It was then opened up to the public  for 
questions and feedback. 
 
Chairman Smith announced that prior to the start of the meeting a twenty-minute
