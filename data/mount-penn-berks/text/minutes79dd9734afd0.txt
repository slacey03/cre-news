June 11, 2026 Special Voting Meeting 
Meeting minutes 
The Mount Penn Borough Council met for a Special Voting Meeting on June 11, 2026, at 
Mount Penn Borough Hall, 200 N. 25th Street, Reading, PA 19606. Council President Rick 
Lombardo called the meeting to order at 6:31 PM and adjourned at 7:22 PM. 
 
Council President Rick Lombardo, Council Vice President Christine Dise, Council Member 
Thomas Baer, Council Member Kelly Dudash, Council Member Michael Kindlick, and Council 
Member David Korinchock were present. Mayor Ryan Maurer and Council Member Troy 
Goodman were absent. Staff present was Borough Manager Hunter L. Ahrens. 
Meeting Opening 
The meeting was called to order at 6:31 PM. All present were asked to rise for the Pledge of 
Allegiance, which was led by Mr. Lombardo. 
Borough Council entered an Executive Session at 6:32 PM to discuss potential litigation. 
Council returned from the Executive Session at 6:54 PM. 
Discussion & Updates on Conveyance of MPBMA 
Lease Agreement Negotiations: Council noted that productive conversations with the 
Authority were ongoing and that a successful lease agreement was being pursued. In light of 
this progress, Council indicated it would not be seeking any additional offensive legal action 
beyond what was necessary to defend against the suit filed by Saint Lawrence. 
 
Allan Myers Construction Contract: Council reviewed the outstanding dispute with Allan 
Myers regarding additional costs incurred during the project. Allan Myers has claimed 
approximately $98,000 in costs attributable to conditions encountered in the field, while the 
Borough has calculated liquidated damages of at least $82,000, representing 82 days. 
Negotiations have narrowed to a figure of approximately $50,000 needed to satisfy the 
project. Council discussed potential means of closing the gap, including the addition of 
Ochre Street resurfacing work for which the Authority would contribute approximately 
$18,000.  
 
The possibility of authorizing a change order to perform the entirety of Ochre Street at a 
total cost of approximately $56,000 was discussed, with the Authority's share being 
approximately $18,000 and the Borough's share approximately $30,000 from the general 
fund. Concerns were raised regarding whether such a change order would exceed bid 
thresholds and legal authorization limits, and it was determined that Borough Solicitor 
Klonis should be consulted before any action is taken. Council agreed to place the matter on 
the agenda for the June 30, 2026 Business Meeting pending legal guidance.

PENNVEST Loan: Council discussed the importance of the PennVEST loan, noting that the 
loan is for $2,830,000 at a rate of 1.75% over 20 years, compared to an alternative scenario 
of 4.35% over 25 years, which would result in approximately $900,000 in additional interest 
and $1,300,000 in total additional cost. Council emphasized that resolving the conveyance 
and securing the PENNVEST loan is a critical financial priority, with substantial completion of 
the tank project anticipated and loan repayment expected to begin in October. 
 
Council acknowledged that legal costs associated with the Authority dispute are expected to 
result in attorney fee expenditures of approximately $100,000 for the year, representing a 
significant budget overrun. Council noted that if the conveyance is not completed and 
associated revenues do not materialize by January, a tax increase may need to be 
considered. 
 
Council discussed whether to hold the scheduled June 23, 2026 Workshop Meeting, noting 
that the Borough Solicitor would be on vacation that week. It was agreed to revisit the 
decision by the following Thursday. 
 
Council noted that proposed dates of June 29th and June 30th had been submitted to the 
Authority for a joint meeting, with no response yet received. Council emphasized the 
urgency of scheduling this meeting given a July 28th deadline. 
Authorize Counsel to Enter a Defense in Legal Action with St. Lawrence Borough 
Council adopted Resolution 25-26, authorizing Borough Counsel to enter a defense against 
the legal action filed by Saint Lawrence and to take such additional legal steps as necessary 
in defending and responding to that suit. 
Motion by Christine Dise, seconded by Michael Kindlick, to adopt Resolution 25-26, 
authorizing Counsel to enter a defense in the legal action brought by Saint Lawrence and to 
take any other legal action deemed necessary by Counsel in furtherance of that defense. 
Motion carried unanimously. 
Authorize Counsel to Execute Legal Action 
Council determined that offensive legal action against the Mt. Penn Borough Municipal 
Authority was not necessary at this time given the status of lease negotiations. This item 
was tabled.
